## Cluster A — just judge / break cross / swine / jizya

### Bukhari 3448

Collection-level content:
- Jesus son of Mary descends;
- acts as just judge;
- breaks the cross;
- kills swine;
- removes/abolishes jizya;
- abundance follows.

Collection chain as presented:
- Bukhari's immediate chain;
- Salih;
- Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri;
- Sa'id b. al-Musayyib;
- Abu Hurayra.

### Muslim 155a

Closely overlapping content:
- Jesus descends as just judge;
- breaks cross;
- kills swine;
- removes jizya;
- abundance.

Collection chain as presented:
- al-Layth;
- Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri;
- Sa'id b. al-Musayyib;
- Abu Hurayra.

### HAD-DESC-V1-001

Proposition:
Bukhari 3448 and Muslim 155a preserve closely related forms of one major
Jesus-descent report cluster.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_COLLECTION_LAYER`

### HAD-DESC-V1-002

Proposition:
Because Bukhari and Muslim both contain the report, they automatically provide
two fully independent early origins.

Status:
`NOT_ADMITTED`

Reason:
the displayed chains converge through Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri -> Sa'id b.
al-Musayyib -> Abu Hurayra. Collection multiplicity is not the same thing as
independent origin.

Guard:

`BUKHARI_PLUS_MUSLIM != INDEPENDENT_DOUBLE_ATTESTATION_BY_DEFAULT`

## Cluster B — "your imam is among you"

### Bukhari 3449

Content:
Jesus descends "among you" while "your imam is among you."

Displayed chain:
- Ibn Bukayr;
- al-Layth;
- Yunus;
- Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri;
- Nafi', mawla of Abu Qatada;
- Abu Hurayra.

Bukhari also notes follow-up transmission by 'Uqayl and al-Awza'i.

### Muslim 155d/e/f

Muslim preserves variants of the same broad saying:
- Jesus descends;
- an imam/leader is among the community;
- one variant says Jesus leads "as one among you";
- a transmitter-level explanation interprets this leadership through God's
  Book and the Prophet's sunna.

Displayed variants use multiple routes through Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri and Nafi'
to Abu Hurayra.

### HAD-DESC-V1-003

Proposition:
The "imam among you" saying exists as a variant cluster rather than one fixed
English sentence.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_COLLECTION_LAYER`

### HAD-DESC-V1-004

Proposition:
The explanatory statement in Muslim 155f about leading according to the Book
and sunna is necessarily identical in historical layer to the core prophetic
saying.

Status:
`NOT_ADMITTED`

Reason:
the page itself marks an explanatory transmitter-level comment by Ibn Abi
Dhi'b. Matn and explanatory gloss must remain distinguishable.

## Cluster C — Jabir / refusal to lead prayer

### Muslim 156

Content:
- a group of the community remains on truth until the end;
- Jesus son of Mary descends;
- their commander invites Jesus to lead prayer;
- Jesus declines, saying some among them are leaders over others.

Displayed chain:
- Hajjaj b. Muhammad;
- Ibn Jurayj;
- Abu al-Zubayr;
- Jabir b. 'Abd Allah.

## Hostile self-check

Potential overclaim:
"Bukhari and Muslim agree, therefore the report is independently doubled."

Result:
`REJECTED`.

Potential overclaim:
"All descent hadith say Jesus leads the prayer."

Result:
`REJECTED`.

The collection layer preserves variant leadership formulations, and Muslim 156
explicitly has Jesus decline.

Potential overclaim:
"All details travel together as one fixed hadith."

Result:
`REJECTED`.

The motif matrix shows different bundles.

Potential overclaim:
"Shared al-Zuhri transmission proves fabrication."

Result:
`REJECTED`.

A shared node is a transmission fact to investigate, not by itself a verdict.

Potential overclaim:
"A separate Jabir route proves every Abu Hurayra detail."

Result:
`REJECTED`.

It supports the broader descent motif at collection level, not every detail.

## Testament constraint

The current Book VI wording already fits this evidence ceiling:
canonical hadith collections give Jesus a more detailed eschatological role
than the Qur'an alone.

If later expanded, Book VI may say:
- multiple canonical report clusters preserve Jesus's descent;
- "break the cross" belongs to one major cluster;
- "imam among you" has its own variant cluster;
- Muslim also preserves a Jabir report where Jesus declines to lead prayer;
- the collection-level diversity should not be flattened into one quotation.

Book VI should not say:
- every descent report contains every motif;
- Bukhari/Muslim automatically equal independent double attestation;
- canonical status settles historical origin;
- one variant's transmitter gloss is necessarily part of the earliest matn.

# Jain Diachronic / Sectarian Source Boundary V1

Status: ACTIVE RESEARCH CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open a Jain research lane without collapsing:
- Mahāvīra's historical teaching context;
- Śvetāmbara canonical tradition;
- Digambara authoritative textual tradition;
- later systematic philosophy;
- monastic and lay practice;
- medieval manuscript witnesses;
- modern global Jain self-description.

Core rule:

ŚVETĀMBARA_CANON != PAN_JAIN_CANON.

A second rule:

EXTANT_JAIN_TEXT != VERBATIM_MAHĀVĪRA_TRANSCRIPT.

## 1. Research labels

"Jainism" is a useful research-family label.

Every proposition must still identify where relevant:
- Śvetāmbara;
- Digambara;
- sub-sect;
- monastic versus lay;
- canonical versus post-canonical;
- historical layer;
- current organization/community.

No later systematic doctrine is silently projected into the earliest teaching
layer.

## 2. Mahāvīra chronology

Mahāvīra is the twenty-fourth Jina/Tīrthaṅkara of the current cycle in Jain
tradition and is the historically central teacher for the extant Jain
traditions.

Traditional dates and modern historical dating are not identical evidentiary
categories.

Current rule:

TRADITIONAL_MAHĀVĪRA_DATE != SECURE_EVENT_CHRONOLOGY_BY_DEFAULT.

A dedicated historical-source packet is required before exact-life chronology is
used comparatively.

## 3. Oral transmission / redaction / witness clocks

For every early Jain text distinguish:

A. CLAIMED_TEACHING_TIME
B. ORAL_TRANSMISSION_LAYER
C. COMPOSITION_OR_COMPILATION_LAYER
D. CANONICAL_REDACTION_LAYER
E. SURVIVING_MANUSCRIPT_WITNESS

These dates can differ by centuries.

Therefore:

CANONICAL_REDaction_DATE != TEACHING_EVENT_DATE.

And:

LATE_MANUSCRIPT != LATE_COMPOSITION_BY_DEFAULT.

## 4. Śvetāmbara canon

Current scholarship describes the Śvetāmbara āgama corpus as preserving
canonical literature redacted/codified through long oral and scholastic
transmission, with the major Vallabhī redaction conventionally placed in the
mid-fifth century CE.

Important early textual families include:
- Ācārāṅga / Āyāraṅga;
- Sūtrakṛtāṅga / Sūyagaḍaṅga;
- Uttarādhyayana / Uttarajjhayaṇa;
- Bhagavatī / Viyāhapannatti;
- disciplinary/ritual texts.

Current rule:

ŚVETĀMBARA_ĀGAMA =
PRIMARY_TEXT_FOR_ŚVETĀMBARA_CANONICAL_TEACHING,
with internal layering controls.

It is not:
an uncontested canon for all Jains.

## 5. Digambara canon-loss position

Digambara tradition does not accept the extant Śvetāmbara āgamas as a preserved
canonical transmission of Mahāvīra's original canon.

Important early authoritative Digambara textual strata include:
- Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama;
- Kaṣāyapāhuda;
- later systematic and commentarial works.

Current classification:

ORIGINAL_CANON_LOSS =
DIGAMBARA_SECTARIAN_HISTORICAL_CLAIM.

It must not be converted into a neutral project fact without independent
historical argument.

Likewise:

ŚVETĀMBARA_CANON_PRESERVATION_CLAIM =
SECTARIAN_TRADITIONAL_POSITION_PLUS_TEXTUAL_HISTORY,
not automatically neutral fact.

## 6. Canon divergence is evidence

The disagreement itself is positive data about:
- authority;
- memory;
- sectarian identity;
- textual transmission;
- canon formation.

Current candidate axes:

DOCUMENTARY_MEMORY_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_SECTARIAN_CANON_DIVERGENCE.

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_SCRIPTURAL_AUTHORITY.

## 7. Tattvārthasūtra

The Tattvārthasūtra is a major early systematic Jain philosophical text with
authority across sectarian boundaries, though commentarial traditions and
interpretations differ.

Current use:
CROSS_SECT_SYSTEMATIZATION_CONTROL.

It must not be used to erase differences in canonical inheritance.

## 8. Manuscript evidence

Surviving medieval/early-modern Jain manuscripts, including numerous
Śvetāmbara canonical and sectarian works, provide:
- MANUSCRIPT WITNESS;
- scribal;
- visual;
- commentarial;
- ritual-use evidence.

A sixteenth-century Kalpasūtra manuscript, for example, is primary material
evidence for a sixteenth-century witness of that text.

It is not:
a sixth-century-BCE manuscript of Mahāvīra's words.

## 9. Monastic / lay distinction

Jain ethics and ritual practice distinguish:
- ascetic renouncer commitments;
- lay vows/limited restraints;
- devotional and ritual practices;
- community support of renouncers.

Current guard:

MONASTIC_MAHĀVRATA != LAY_ANUVRATA.

No universal "Jain practice" cell should merge them.

## 10. Living Jain sources

Current Jain organizations are valid evidence for:
- contemporary self-description;
- current pedagogy;
- current ethical emphases;
- diaspora institutions.

They are not automatically primary evidence for:
early Jain doctrinal history.

## 11. Comparison hold

No Jain cell enters global synthesis until:
- sectarian scope is explicit;
- textual layer is named;
- witness/redaction distinction is preserved;
- monastic/lay scope is explicit;
- exact source supports the neutral axis.

Current status:

JAIN_CROSS_TRADITION_COMPARISON = HOLD.

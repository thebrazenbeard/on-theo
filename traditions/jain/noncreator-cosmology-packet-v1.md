# Jain Non-Creator Cosmology Packet V1

Status: CLASSICAL COSMOLOGY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind the Jain world-origin/cosmology model without reducing it to the negative
statement "Jains do not believe in a creator god."

Core rule:

NO_CREATOR
is not the full model.

The positive cosmological proposition is:

BEGINNINGLESS_UNCREATED_COSMOS_WITH_ETERNAL_SUBSTANCES_AND_CHANGING_MODES.

## 1. Beginningless cosmos

Current scholarly descriptions of Jain cosmology consistently treat the cosmos
as:
- without a first creation event;
- without a creator god;
- not dependent on a transcendent sustaining agent;
- structured by eternal ontological constituents.

Current axis:

WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL =
BOUNDED_BEGINNINGLESS_UNCREATED_COSMOS.

This is not:
CREATION_EX_NIHILO.

It is not:
EMERGENCE_FROM_ABSOLUTE_NOTHING.

It is not:
DIVINE_ARTIFACT.

## 2. Eternal substances / changing modes

Classical Jain ontology models reality through eternal substances (dravya) whose
modes/conditions change.

Core systematic categories include:
- jīva;
- pudgala / matter;
- dharma as medium of motion;
- adharma as medium of rest;
- ākāśa / space;
- kāla / time in later/classical six-substance enumeration.

Current result:

ONTOLOGICAL_CONTINUITY =
ETERNAL_SUBSTANCE_WITH_MUTABLE_MODE.

Therefore:

CHANGE
does not require
CREATION_OF_SUBSTANCE.

And:

DESTRUCTION_OF_A_MODE
does not imply
ANNIHILATION_OF_REALITY.

## 3. Loka / aloka

Classical Jain cosmology distinguishes:

LOKA
- the cosmos/universe-space in which the relevant substances of embodied,
  mobile/resting existence occur;

ALOKA / ALOKĀKĀŚA
- the infinite space beyond the inhabited/structured cosmos, characterized by
  absence of the media enabling movement/rest found in loka.

Current classification:

COSMOS =
BOUNDED_STRUCTURED_REGION_WITHIN_INFINITE_SPACE.

This is ontologically different from:
"the entire universe is spatially infinite in all respects."

## 4. No creator does not mean no gods

Jain traditions include:
- devas;
- heavenly beings;
- liberated Jinas as perfected beings;
- devotional practice.

But none is required as:
CREATOR_OF_COSMOS.

Current guard:

DEITY_PRESENCE != CREATOR_THEISM.

And:

JINA_VENERATION != CREATOR_WORSHIP.

## 5. Causation without first cause

The classical model does not require a first temporal cause for:
- cosmos;
- jīva;
- matter;
- karmic process.

Current result:

FIRST_CAUSE_REQUIREMENT =
NOT_ADMITTED_IN_CLASSICAL_JAIN_COSMOLOGY.

Beginningless karmic association is treated as a condition from which liberation
can occur without specifying a first historical bonding event.

This does not mean:
"Jainism proves an actual infinite past."

It is the internal metaphysical model.

## 6. Cosmic time

Jain cosmology includes very large cyclical temporal structures, including
ascending and descending ages in relevant inhabited regions.

Current classification:

COSMIC_TIME_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CYCLIC_WITHOUT_COSMIC_CREATION_EVENT.

Important:

CYCLIC_TIME
does not mean
THE_WHOLE_COSMOS_IS_RECREATED_EACH_CYCLE.

The cycles concern changing historical/cosmic conditions within an already
beginningless reality.

## 7. World origin versus world process

This distinction matters for the global world-origin schema.

Jainism answers the origin question by denying that:
THE_COSMOS_HAS_AN_ORIGIN_EVENT.

It instead supplies:

WORLD_PROCESS_MODEL =
BEGINNINGLESS_CONTINUOUS_TRANSFORMATION.

Therefore:

NO_ORIGIN_EVENT
is itself a positive world-origin classification.

## 8. Relation to human existence

Humans are not ontologically unique created beings in this cosmology.

They are one class of embodied jīvas within a larger field of conscious beings,
including nonhuman animals and divine/heavenly beings.

Current result:

HUMAN_ONTOLOGICAL_EXCEPTIONALISM =
NOT_CENTRAL_TO_CLASSICAL_JAIN_MODEL.

But:
HUMAN_BIRTH
can have special soteriological importance in selected Jain teachings because of
capacity for disciplined liberation practice.

That second proposition requires dedicated source control before global use.

## 9. Liberation does not exit reality into creator

The liberated jīva is not absorbed into a creator deity.

Liberation is modeled as:
- cessation of karmic bondage;
- end of rebirth;
- perfected jīva.

Current guard:

LIBERATION != RETURN_TO_CREATOR.

This aligns with the non-creator cosmology rather than functioning as an
exception to it.

## 10. World-origin comparison value

The Jain case is highly discriminating against a universal creator model.

Current comparison proposition:

A_RELIGIOUS_COSMOLOGY_CAN_BE_FULLY_STRUCTURED_MEANINGFUL_AND_POPULATED_BY_DIVINE_BEINGS_WHILE_DENYING_A_CREATOR_AND_DENYING_ANY_FIRST_CREATION_EVENT.

This is stronger than:
SOME_RELIGIONS_DO_NOT_FOCUS_ON_CREATION.

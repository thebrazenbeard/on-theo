# Jain Diachronic Foundation Packet V1

Status: INTERNAL FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish the initial Jain evidence architecture before admitting any global
comparison cells.

## 1. Jainism is not a Hindu or Buddhist sub-branch

Jain traditions participate in the broader South Asian religious/philosophical
environment but preserve independent:
- teacher lineages;
- textual corpora;
- monastic institutions;
- ontology;
- karma models;
- liberation paths.

Current rule:

SHARED_SOUTH_ASIAN_VOCABULARY != DOCTRINAL_IDENTITY.

Terms such as:
- karma;
- mokṣa/liberation;
- saṃsāra;
- dharma;
- yoga
require Jain-specific semantic control.

## 2. Historical teacher versus cosmic Jina tradition

Mahāvīra functions in both:
- historical reconstruction of early Jainism;
- a much larger Jain cosmic Tīrthaṅkara framework.

Current rule:

HISTORICAL_MAHĀVĪRA
and
COSMIC_TĪRTHAṄKARA_MODEL
are related but analytically distinct.

No project claim about prior Tīrthaṅkaras' historicity is admitted from
traditional chronology alone.

## 3. Canon memory is already a major research result

Śvetāmbara and Digambara traditions differ fundamentally over preservation of
the early canon.

This makes Jainism unusually valuable for:

DOCUMENTARY_MEMORY_MODEL.

Potential subtype:

SECTARIAN_CANON_PRESERVATION_DIVERGENCE.

Questions include:
- what counts as surviving teaching;
- what was lost;
- which texts preserve authority;
- how oral transmission becomes redacted text;
- how sectarian identity governs canon.

## 4. Jīva / self

Jain philosophy strongly distinguishes:
- jīva / living self;
- ajīva / nonliving categories;
- embodied/transmigrating condition;
- purified/liberated self.

Current axis:

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE.

A dedicated lexical/source packet is required before admission.

## 5. Karma

Jain karma theory is not treated as generic South Asian moral causation.

Current scholarship and Jain systematic texts model karma in relation to:
- jīva;
- material/bondage processes;
- passions/action;
- rebirth;
- purification;
- liberation.

Current axes:

CAUSAL_ORDER_TYPE =
STRONG_CANDIDATE.

FATE_CONSTRAINT_MODEL =
CANDIDATE.

POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_REBIRTH_LAYER.

No cross-tradition cell yet.

## 6. Liberation

The path toward liberation is bound to:
- right view/faith;
- knowledge;
- conduct;
- ascetic discipline;
- reduction/removal of karmic bondage.

Current axis:

TRANSFORMATION_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_SELF_PURIFICATION_LIBERATION.

Again, do not translate automatically into:
"salvation"
without qualification.

## 7. Non-creator cosmology

Jain philosophical/cosmological traditions describe a beginningless universe
without a creator god.

Current axis:

WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_NON_CREATOR_BEGINNINGLESS.

This is highly discriminating for the existing world-origin test.

But a source-specific primary-text packet is required before admission.

## 8. Cosmic time

Jain cosmology includes large cyclic temporal models.

Current axis:

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL is NOT the right default axis.

Potential axis:

COSMIC_TIME_MODEL
or
WORLD_TERMINUS_RENEWAL_MODEL,
depending exact source propositions.

Do not confuse:
ritual calendar
with
cosmic temporal structure.

## 9. Ahiṃsā and vows

Current sources strongly support the centrality of non-harm/nonviolence.

But practice is differentiated:
- ascetic mahāvratas;
- lay anuvratas;
- dietary/ritual disciplines;
- modern ethical/ecological reinterpretations.

Current axis candidate:

ETHICAL_RITUAL_DISCIPLINE_MODEL.

OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL is not assumed.

## 10. Ascetic / lay relation

Jain lived religion combines:
- radical renunciation as normative religious ideal;
- lay community life;
- support relations;
- ritual;
- business/household worlds;
- partial vows and ascetic practices.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_DUAL_LAY_RENOUNCER.

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL =
CANDIDATE_LAY_RENOUNCER_INTERDEPENDENCE.

## 11. Deities

Jainism includes deities and devotional practice in many historical/current
contexts.

But:

DEITY_PRESENCE != CREATOR_GOD.

And:

TĪRTHAṄKARA_VENERATION != CREATOR_WORSHIP.

SACRAL_PRESENCE_MODEL remains HOLD pending source decomposition.

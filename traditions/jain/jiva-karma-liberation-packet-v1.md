# Jain Jīva / Karma / Liberation Packet V1

Status: CLASSICAL SYSTEMATIC ONTOLOGY-SOTERIOLOGY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind a source-controlled Jain model of:
- self (jīva);
- karmic influx/bondage;
- rebirth;
- stoppage/shedding;
- liberation;

without projecting the fully systematized classical account into every earliest
Jain textual stratum.

Core rule:

CLASSICAL_TATTVĀRTHASŪTRA_SYSTEM != EARLIEST_JAIN_DOCTRINE_IN_FULL_FORM_BY_DEFAULT.

## 1. Primary systematic control

The Tattvārthasūtra is the principal V1 control because:
- current scholarship dates it broadly c. 150–400 CE, with other reconstructions
  around c. 350–400 CE;
- it is the oldest extant systematic Jain treatise in Sanskrit;
- it organizes themes inherited from earlier canonical traditions;
- it has authority in both major Jain traditions despite recension/commentary
  differences.

Evidence class:
PRIMARY TEXT / CLASSICAL SYSTEMATIC TEXT,
mediated here through current scholarly edition/translation.

## 2. Liberation architecture

Tattvārthasūtra 1.1 gives the path through:
- right view;
- right knowledge;
- right conduct.

Tattvārthasūtra 1.4 organizes key categories as:
- self;
- non-self;
- karmic influx;
- bondage;
- stoppage;
- destruction/shedding;
- liberation.

Current classification:

TRANSFORMATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_KARMIC_PURIFICATION_LIBERATION.

The mechanism is not:
divine forgiveness
or
creator rescue.

It is a structured transformation of the jīva's karmically bound condition.

## 3. Jīva / ajīva

Classical Jain philosophy distinguishes:
- jīva — living/self;
- ajīva — nonliving categories.

Current scholarship treats the jīva–ajīva division as one of the earliest and
most persistent Jain ontological distinctions, although later systematic
classification develops over time.

Current axis:

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CLASSICAL_JĪVA.

At current ceiling:
- jīva is an active subject/self;
- cognition/experience are central characteristics;
- embodied states are not identical with the self's purified condition.

Do not translate jīva simply as:
"immortal soul"
without proposition-level controls.

## 4. Active moral subject

The Jain self is not a passive witness in the classical system.

Current scholarly reconstruction emphasizes:
- activity matters;
- ethical responsibility matters;
- the self can bind karma;
- the self can stop and shed karma.

Current result:

JĪVA =
ACTIVE_MORAL_CAUSAL_SUBJECT
in the classical systematic layer.

This sharply distinguishes the model from philosophies where the deepest self
is wholly inactive.

## 5. Karma as more than moral bookkeeping

In classical Jain theory, karma is not merely:
- divine reward/punishment;
- abstract moral law;
- memory of action.

It is treated as involving subtle material processes that can bind to the jīva.

Current axis:

CAUSAL_ORDER_TYPE =
BOUNDED_KARMIC_MATERIAL_CAUSATION.

Important:
"Karmic matter" is a technical Jain ontological claim.

It is not a modern physics claim.

## 6. Influx and bondage

Tattvārthasūtra categories distinguish:
- āsrava — influx;
- bandha — bondage;
- saṃvara — stoppage;
- nirjarā — shedding/destruction;
- mokṣa — liberation.

Current causal sequence:

ACTIVITY / PASSION
->
KARMIC_INFLUX_AND_BINDING
->
EMBODIED / CONDITIONED EXISTENCE

with disciplined practice enabling:

STOPPAGE
+
SHEDDING
->
LIBERATION.

Exact causal details vary across commentarial traditions and later analysis.

## 7. Eight karma classes

Classical Tattvārthasūtra-based systematics distinguish eight major kinds of
karmic matter, including karmas affecting:
- knowledge;
- perception;
- delusion;
- energy/obstruction;
- feeling;
- body/status;
- lifespan;
- social/species status.

Current status:

EIGHT_KARMA_SYSTEM =
BOUNDED_CLASSICAL_SYSTEMATIC.

Do not infer that the exact mature eightfold schema is verbatim Mahāvīra-era
formulation.

## 8. Rebirth

Classical Jain karma theory explicitly connects karmic condition to:
- embodied existence;
- future birth;
- lifespan;
- state/type of embodiment.

Current axis:

POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL =
BOUNDED_REBIRTH_UNTIL_LIBERATION
for the classical systematic layer.

This is not one linear route after death.

It is:
REPEATED_EMBODIMENT_CONDITIONED_BY_KARMA.

## 9. Liberation is release from rebirth and karmic bondage

Liberation requires:
- no new influx/bondage;
- removal/shedding of bound karma;
- purified jīva;
- end of karmically driven rebirth.

Current axis:

TRANSFORMATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PURIFICATION_FROM_BOUND_TO_LIBERATED_JĪVA.

Current scholarship on classical Jain liberation also treats the liberated jīva
as disembodied and spatially located at the realm/summit of liberated beings.

This latter spatial-final-journey proposition is admitted only as:
CLASSICAL_TATTVĀRTHASŪTRA_COMMENTARIAL_LAYER.

## 10. Postmortem path decomposition

The Jain model requires distinguishing:

REBIRTH_PATH
- karmically conditioned transition into another embodied state;

LIBERATION_PATH
- final release after destruction of binding karma;

LIBERATED_STATE
- no further karmically conditioned rebirth.

Therefore:

POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL
must permit
CYCLIC_REBIRTH_PLUS_TERMINAL_LIBERATION.

## 11. Karma and fate

Karma constrains present/future embodiment and experience.

But the active jīva can:
- alter conduct;
- stop influx;
- shed karma;
- pursue liberation.

Therefore:

KARMA != FATALISM.

Current axis:

FATE_CONSTRAINT_MODEL =
PARTIAL_CONSTRAINT_WITH_TRANSFORMATIVE_AGENCY.

A dedicated fate/free-agency packet would still be needed for a global bounded
cell.

## 12. Natural and karmic causality

Current Jain philosophical scholarship distinguishes and relates:
- karmic causation;
- natural/material transformation;
- multiple causal conditions.

Current result:

KARMIC_CAUSALITY != TOTAL_CAUSAL_MONISM.

The project will not explain every event through karma simply because karma is
important.

## 13. Sectarian commentary control

Śvetāmbara and Digambara traditions:
- share major Tattvārthasūtra architecture;
- preserve differing recensions/commentaries;
- can disagree on specific doctrinal implications.

Therefore:

CROSS_SECT_SYSTEMATIC_OVERLAP
!=
IDENTICAL_COMMENTARIAL_DOCTRINE.

Each contested detail requires recension/commentary attribution.

## 14. Earlier textual strata

classical Tattvārthasūtra scheme.

Current guard:

TATTVĀRTHASŪTRA_CAN_SYSTEMATIZE_EARLIER_ELEMENTS_WITHOUT_PROVING_THE_FULL_SYSTEM_EXISTED_AT_THE_EARLIEST_STAGE.

The project therefore records:

- later systematization;
separately.

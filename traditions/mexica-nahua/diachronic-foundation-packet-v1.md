# Mexica / Nahua Diachronic Foundation Packet V1

Status: INTERNAL FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open the Mexica/Nahua lane without using "Aztec religion" as a timeless,
homogeneous container.

## 1. Mexica imperial religion is one bounded Nahua historical formation

INAH's Templo Mayor documentation securely anchors a late Postclassic Mexica
religious-political center at Tenochtitlan.

Current foundation proposition:

MEXICA_TENOCHTITLAN_IMPERIAL_RITUAL_SYSTEM =
BOUNDED_LATE_POSTCLASSIC_FORMATION.

It is not:
- all Nahua religion;
- all central Mexican religion;
- all living Nahua tradition.

## 2. Material and textual sources must remain independent

Templo Mayor archaeology provides:
- buildings;
- offerings;
- stratigraphy;
- sculpture;
- sacred precinct organization;
- biological/material remains.

Florentine Codex provides:
- Nahuatl text;
- Spanish text;
- Indigenous images;
- colonial encyclopedic organization.

Codices provide:
- pictorial/calendar/tribute/historical records.

Current rule:

ARCHAEOLOGY
!=
COLONIAL_TEXT
!=
PICTORIAL_MANUSCRIPT.

Agreement can strengthen reconstruction.

One source cannot silently repair another's gaps.

## 3. Religious and political space are materially entangled

INAH explicitly identifies the Templo Mayor precinct as the center of Mexica
religious and political life.

Current internal candidate:

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL.

But the precise mechanism must be source-bound:
- sacred ruler;
- temple-state administration;
- conquest/tribute;
- sacrifice;
- calendrical festival;
- urban cosmology

must not be assumed to be one mechanism.

## 4. Templo Mayor is a sacred-space system

Current INAH interpretation identifies the Templo Mayor as:
- urban/cosmic center;
- associated with cardinal directions;
- linked to sky/earth/underworld vertical order;
- dedicated to Tlaloc and Huitzilopochtli.

This motivates:

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL / SACRAL_PRESENCE_MODEL candidates.

Guard:

MODERN_ARCHAEOLOGICAL_INTERPRETATION_NE_PRIMARY_TEXTUAL_COSMOLOGY_BY_ITSELF.

## 5. Offerings require material context

Templo Mayor excavation continues to recover large structured offerings.

A 2026 INAH report on Offerings 186, 187, and 189 describes coordinated deposits
associated with the reign of Motecuhzoma Ilhuicamina and objects brought from
other regions.

Current method result:

OFFERING_DEPOSIT =
MATERIAL_ACTION_WITH_CONTEXT.

Not:

OFFERING_DEPOSIT = KNOWN_RECIPIENT_EFFECT_OR_RECIPROCITY_BY_DEFAULT.

This prepares:
OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL.

## 6. Florentine Codex is collaborative colonial knowledge production

Getty's Digital Florentine Codex documents named Nahua intellectual
participation.

Current proposition:

FLORENTINE_CODEX_IS_A_COLLABORATIVE_COLONIAL_NAHUA_FRANCISCAN_MANUSCRIPT.

This blocks two simplistic models:

SAHAGUN_WROTE_IT_ALONE.

and:

IT_IS_AN_UNMEDIATED_PRECONQUEST_NAHUA_TEXT.

The Nahuatl/Spanish/image layers can preserve different perspectives.

## 7. Sacred time is a major source domain

Florentine Codex Book 2 and Codex Borbonicus preserve festival/calendar
material.

This motivates:

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL.

The Maya lane already demonstrated why sacred time deserves its own neutral
axis.

Mexica/Nahua evidence is an independent test, not a derivative copy of the Maya
model.

## 8. Divination and omen systems require separate categories

Florentine Codex:
- Book 4 divinatory arts;
- Book 5 omens/prognostications;
- Book 7 astral/natural philosophy.

Do not collapse:
- divination;
- omen interpretation;
- calendar day-sign systems;
- astronomy;
- prophecy.

Potential axis:
KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL.

## 9. Living Nahua religion is not imperial Mexica residue

Current INPI material documents regional living traditions involving:
- maize;
- rain;
- land;
- deceased persons;
- sacred mountains/caves;
- Catholic saints;
- mayordomias;
- community festivals;
- ceremonial music.

Current proposition:

LIVING_NAHUA_TRADITIONS_ARE_DIVERSE_CONTACT_SHAPED_PRESENT_TENSE_RELIGIOUS_SYSTEMS.

Not:
MODERN_AZTEC_RELIGION.

## 10. New comparative pressure

Mexica/Nahua material strengthens already emerging neutral axes:
- SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL;
- RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL;
- SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL.

as a possible decomposition of OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL if human/animal
sacrifice, auto-sacrifice, and deposited objects prove too heterogeneous.

That new axis is only a candidate.

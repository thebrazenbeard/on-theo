# Templo Mayor Material and Ritual Packet V1

Status: LATE POSTCLASSIC MEXICA MATERIAL-EVIDENCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind Templo Mayor / Huey Teocalli material evidence independently of the
Florentine Codex and other colonial texts.

Core rule:

MATERIAL_EVIDENCE != COMPLETE_THEOLOGY.

A second rule:

SACRED_POLITICAL_CENTER != ONE_UNIVERSAL_MEXICA_DOCTRINE.

## 1. Site identity and scope

INAH identifies Templo Mayor as:
- the center of political and religious life in Mexica Tenochtitlan;
- the principal monument within a larger sacred precinct;
- a late Postclassic complex, with the principal archaeological sequence
  associated with 1325-1521 CE.

The precinct includes or included:
- the double Templo Mayor;
- House of the Eagles;
- Ehécatl temple;
- tzompantli;
- ballcourt;
- Calmecac;
- other ritual/political buildings.

Evidence class:
MATERIAL EVIDENCE / ARCHAEOLOGICAL CONTEXT / HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION.

Current proposition:

TEMPLO_MAYOR_PRECINCT =
RELIGIOUS_POLITICAL_INSTITUTIONAL_CENTER_OF_MEXICA_TENOCHTITLAN.

Guard:

MEXICA_TENOCHTITLAN_CENTER != ALL_NAHUA_RELIGION.

## 2. Dual sanctuary

INAH identifies the principal double sanctuary as dedicated to:
- Tláloc, associated with rain/agriculture;
- Huitzilopochtli, Mexica patron and war-associated deity.

The north/south architectural pairing is directly material.

Current axis:

SACRAL_PRESENCE_MODEL =
BOUNDED_ARCHITECTURAL_DEITY_LOCALIZATION.

This means selected sacred/divine relations are materially localized through
distinct sanctuaries.

It does not mean:
- deity presence is exhausted by temple architecture;
- all Mexica theology is dualistic;
- Tláloc/Huitzilopochtli are the only central divine figures.

## 3. Sacred urban center and cosmographic interpretation

INAH public interpretation describes Templo Mayor as:
- the symbolic center/umbilicus of the city;
- a convergence of major causeways;
- a place where four earthly directions and vertical cosmic levels were
  conceptually related.

Current classification:

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL =
BOUNDED_URBAN_SACRED_CENTER.

Evidence is:
MATERIAL URBAN FORM + HISTORICAL/ARCHAEOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION.

Guard:

MODERN_COSMOGRAPHIC_INTERPRETATION != PRIMARY_TEXTUAL_STATEMENT_BY_ITSELF.

## 4. Monumental myth-ritual relation

The Coyolxauhqui monolith was found at the foot of the Huitzilopochtli stair.

INAH explicitly relates:
- its dismembered female imagery;
- the Huitzilopochtli/Coyolxauhqui Coatepec narrative;
- ritual remembrance within the precinct.

Current proposition:

ARCHITECTURE_ICONOGRAPHY_AND_COLONIAL_MYTHIC_RECONSTRUCTION_CAN_CONVERGE_ON_A_BOUNDED_RITUAL_MYTH_RELATION.

But the evidence classes remain separate:
- monolith = MATERIAL EVIDENCE;
- location = ARCHAEOLOGICAL CONTEXT;
- named myth = TEXTUAL/HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION.

Guard:

ICONOGRAPHIC_FIT != MYTHIC_TEXT_PROVED_BY_OBJECT_ALONE.

## 5. Repeated rebuilding and ritual institutionalization

INAH records seven major enlargements of the Templo Mayor.

Current implication:

RITUAL_ORDER_AND_POLITICAL_INSTITUTION_WERE_STABILIZED_THROUGH_REPEATED_MONUMENTAL_REBUILDING.

The rebuilding sequence shows:
- continuity of central site;
- political investment;
- architectural renewal.

It does not prove one unchanged ritual program through all stages.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_MONUMENTAL_RENEWAL_LAYER.

## 6. Structured offerings

Templo Mayor excavations have recovered numerous structured ritual deposits.

INAH museum interpretation states that many displayed objects derive from
offerings containing:
- greenstone;
- masks;
- human remains;
- vessels;
- flora;
- fauna;
- other valuable materials.

A 2026 Proyecto Templo Mayor report on Offerings 186, 187, and 189 describes:
- coordinated deposition in one major event;
- 83 Mezcala-style figurines across related offerings;
- shells and marine materials;
- seeds;
- copal;
- bitumen;
- sawfish remains;
- other objects;
- an interpretation linking some figurines to war booty acquired under
  Motecuhzoma Ilhuicamina.

Evidence class:
MATERIAL EVIDENCE + ARCHAEOLOGICAL RECONSTRUCTION.

Secure proposition:

MEXICA_STATE_RITUAL_COULD_MOBILIZE_LARGE_STRUCTURED_MULTI_MATERIAL_OFFERING_DEPOSITS.

## 7. Offering mechanism remains decomposed

Material deposits establish:
- deposition;
- selection;
- spatial organization;
- chronology;
- provenance in some cases.

They do not automatically establish:
- exact recipient;
- exact requested outcome;
- reciprocal contract;
- verbal prayer;
- theological rationale.

Therefore:

OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_MATERIAL_MEXICA_LAYER.

Required future schema fields:
- deposit;
- recipient if independently identified;
- source of object;
- ritual context;
- asserted outcome if textually attested;
- reciprocity status.

## 10. Bodily sacrifice requires its own control

INAH site interpretation materially and historically associates the precinct
with:
- tzompantli;
- sacrificial stone;
- captive sacrifice.

This is sufficient to establish:

BODILY_SACRIFICE_IS_A_REAL_MEXICA_RITUAL_DOMAIN.

But it should not be collapsed into every other offering.

Candidate decomposition:

SACRIFICE_AND_BODILY_OFFERING_MODEL.

Reason:
- auto-sacrifice;
- captive/human sacrifice;
- animal sacrifice;
- object offering;
- botanical offering;
- food offering

may have different recipients, mechanisms, and political meanings.

No universal sacrifice theory is admitted in V1.

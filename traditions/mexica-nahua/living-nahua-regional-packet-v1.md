# Living Nahua Regional Diversity Packet V1

Status: MODERN REGIONAL TRADITION / DIVERSITY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind current public Nahua religious evidence without treating living communities
as unchanged survivals of Mexica imperial religion or as one uniform "Nahua
religion."

Core rule:

LIVING_NAHUA != MODERN_MEXICA_IMPERIAL_RELIGION.

A second rule:

REGIONAL_SIMILARITY != ONE_UNIFORM_NAHUA_SYSTEM.

## 1. Current national diversity

INPI's current Catálogo Nacional de Pueblos y Comunidades Indígenas y
Afromexicanas identifies Nahua communities across a wide geographic range and
explicitly attributes their dispersion and diversity to different historical
processes including:
- migrations;
- returns;
- wars/conquests;
- trade routes;
- regional settlement histories.

The catalogue emphasizes diversity of:
- linguistic variants;
- cultural expressions;
- territorial/community forms.

Current proposition:

NAHUA_IS_A_WIDE_LIVING_PEOPLE_CATEGORY_WITH_MAJOR_REGIONAL_VARIATION.

This alone blocks the equation:

NAHUA = MEXICA.

## 2. Milpa Alta / central Mexican sacred-land layer

INPI's Milpa Alta ethnography describes:
- an inseparable/mystical relation with land;
- caves and mountains as sacred/divine places;
- named sacred mountains/caves;
- elders going to sacred places to petition for community, family, or self;
- strong Catholic presence;
- mayordomía organization around saints;
- multiple later Christian denominations.

Evidence class:
MODERN TRADITION / PUBLIC INDIGENOUS-INSTITUTIONAL ETHNOGRAPHIC SYNTHESIS.

Current axis:

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL =
BOUNDED_MILPA_ALTA_REGIONAL_LAYER.

Contact dimension:
CATHOLIC_MAYORDOMIA + LOCAL_SACRED_GEOGRAPHY.

Guard:

MILPA_ALTA != ALL_NAHUA.

## 3. Puebla / Sierra balance and domestic ritual

INPI's Puebla ethnography describes:
- current religious practices shaped through colonial Christian contact;
- domestic/private rites;
- specialist ritual practitioners outside ordinary Catholic priestly office;
- mayordomía;
- ritual responses to birth, death, marriage, house construction,
  maize planting, rain petition, healing;
- ritual speech;
- Catholic images;
- flowers, incense, candles, tobacco, drink and other ritual materials.

Current project classification:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PUEBLA_DOMESTIC_CATHOLIC_CONTACT_LAYER.

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PUEBLA_DOMESTIC_BALANCE_LAYER.

The INPI page uses "syncretism" as a summary term.

ON_THEO decomposes that label into:
- Catholic image/institutional participation;
- domestic specialist practice;
- agricultural ritual;
- healing;
- local materials;
- oral ritual.

Guard:

SYNCRETISM_LABEL != SUFFICIENT_MECHANISM_ANALYSIS.

## 4. San Luis Potosí / Huasteca maize-cycle layer

INPI describes San Luis Potosí Nahua ritual activity as strongly governed by the
growth and maturation cycle of maize.

It also records continued ritual use of sacred hills/caves in the context of
long Christian contact.

Current axis:

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL =
PARTIAL_AGRICULTURAL_CYCLE_LAYER.

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL =
PARTIAL_MAIZE_LAND_COMMUNITY_LAYER.

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_SACRED_HILL_CAVE_LAYER.

This is not the same system as Mexica tonalpohualli/veintena ritual time.

Therefore:

AGRICULTURAL_SACRED_TIME != IMPERIAL_CALENDAR_IDENTITY.

## 5. Morelos / agricultural rites and mayordomía

INPI's Morelos ethnography describes:
- agricultural rites;
- petitions for water;
- processions to sacred places;
- saint-centered cofradía/mayordomía structures;
- seed blessing;
- observation of moon/timing for sowing, watering, harvesting;
- current Catholic and Indigenous religious forms.

Current classification:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
PARTIAL_MORELOS_LAYER.

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL =
PARTIAL_AGRICULTURAL_ASTRONOMICAL_TIMING_LAYER.

Guard:

MOON_AGRICULTURAL_TIMING != BORBONICUS_TONALPOHUALLI_BY_DEFAULT.

## 6. Jalisco / ancestor and place relation

INPI's Jalisco Nahua ethnography describes:
- living presence of ancestors / "señores viejitos";
- caves, hills, springs, and waterways as key places of contact with ancestors;
- ancestors/owners related to animals, springs, and surrounding nature;
- oral tradition as identity continuity.

Current axis:

MORTUARY_RELATIONSHIP_MODEL =
PARTIAL_LIVING_ANCESTOR_PLACE_LAYER.

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_ANCESTOR_CONTACT_GEOGRAPHY.

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL =
PARTIAL_ANCESTOR_MORE_THAN_HUMAN_RELATION.

This is a living regional proposition.

It is not evidence for Mexica imperial afterlife doctrine.

## 7. Regional contact histories differ

Living Nahua religious history includes:
- Catholic saints;
- mayordomías/cofradías;
- sacred mountains/caves;
- agricultural cycles;
- domestic specialists;
- ancestors;
- Christian denominations;
- local political/community institutions.

The balance differs regionally.

Therefore:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL
must be
REGION_SPECIFIC.

No single "Nahua syncretism" model is admitted.

## 8. Sacred time has multiple living forms

Current living regional evidence includes:
- maize growth cycles;
- rain petition;
- saint/festival calendars;
- lunar/agricultural timing;
- death/funerary cycles;
- community office cycles.

This strengthens:

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL

while simultaneously requiring decomposition.

Current subtypes:
- AGRICULTURAL_CYCLE;
- LITURGICAL_SAINT_CYCLE;
- LUNAR_AGRICULTURAL_TIMING;
- FUNERARY_COMMUNAL_CYCLE;
- IMPERIAL/PICTORIAL_DIVINATORY_CALENDAR as historical layer.

## 9. Continuity cannot be inferred from resemblance alone

Some living Nahua practices resemble colonial or preconquest evidence.

But:

RESEMBLANCE != DIRECT_UNCHANGED_TRANSMISSION.

Required continuity evidence includes:
- regional history;
- language/source continuity;
- colonial documentation;
- institutional lineage;
- material/textual connections.

Current axis:

CONTINUITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_REGIONAL_CONTINUITY_WITH_CHANGE.

## 11. What is not admitted

Not admitted:
- one living Nahua theology;
- living Nahua as unchanged Mexica religion;
- one pan-Nahua mayordomía system;
- one pan-Nahua sacred geography;
- Jalisco ancestor relations as Mexica afterlife doctrine;
- current maize ritual as unchanged imperial festival practice;
- "syncretism" as a complete explanatory mechanism.

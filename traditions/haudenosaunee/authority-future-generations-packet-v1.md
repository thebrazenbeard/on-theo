# Haudenosaunee Authority and Future-Generations Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC GREAT-LAW / GOVERNANCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Deepen the current public Great Law governance layer without converting living
Confederacy self-description into an exact reconstruction of every historical
period.

## 1. Distributed authority

Current HCCC public sources describe at least four distinct authority domains:
- Grand Council;
- Chiefs;
- Clan Mothers;
- Faithkeepers.

These offices are related but not interchangeable.

Current proposition:

HAUDENOSAUNEE_AUTHORITY =
DISTRIBUTED_MULTI_OFFICE_RELATIONAL_GOVERNANCE.

## 2. Clan Mothers

HCCC publicly states that:
- Chiefs are selected by Clan Mothers;
- Clan Mothers monitor Chief conduct;
- Clan Mothers can remove Chiefs who fail responsibilities;
- titles are embedded in clan structures;
- Clan Mothers have naming and marriage-related responsibilities in public
  current descriptions.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CLAN_MOTHER_TITLE_AUTHORITY.

This demonstrates political authority that is not simply male-monarchical.

It does not mean all Haudenosaunee women's authority is exhausted by the Clan
Mother office.

## 3. Chiefs

Current public Confederacy sources describe Chiefs as:
- representatives of clans/nations;
- accountable to the welfare of people;
- Grand Council participants;
- expected to embody peace and collective responsibility.

Current classification:

CHIEF_AUTHORITY =
REPRESENTATIVE_SERVICE_TITLE_WITH_GREAT_LAW_CONSTRAINT.

Not:
SOVEREIGN_MONARCH.

## 4. Faithkeepers

Current public Confederacy/Oneida sources identify Faithkeepers as:
- spiritual advisors;
- cultural/ceremonial officers;
- organizers of Longhouse/community ceremonial timing;
- people who help maintain cultural continuity.

Current classification:

FAITHKEEPER_AUTHORITY =
SPIRITUAL_CEREMONIAL_CULTURAL_OFFICE.

Guard:

FAITHKEEPER != PRIEST_AS_GENERIC_CROSS_TRADITION_EQUIVALENT.

## 5. Grand Council

HCCC public materials describe Grand Council as:
- composed of Chiefs from the member nations;
- requiring nation representation;
- operating through deliberation/consensus;
- governed by Great Law constraints;
- preserving nation roles in the metaphorical longhouse.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CONFEDERACY_COUNCIL_LAYER.

## 6. Nation autonomy

Onondaga sovereignty materials state that each nation retains authority over its
internal affairs while participating in Confederacy government.

Current proposition:

CONFEDERACY_AUTHORITY != CENTRALIZED_NATION_ABSORPTION.

This matters for comparison with:
- empire;
- monarchy;
- centralized priesthood;
- federal-like confederacy analogies.

Project caution:
modern political analogies are explanatory only and must not replace Indigenous
self-description.

## 7. Great Law spiritual-political relation

Onondaga publicly describes Great Law mandates as:
- spiritual;
- moral;
- social.

HCCC publicly describes the Confederacy as founded through the Peacemaker and
Creator-connected Great Peace tradition.

Current axis:

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PUBLIC_SACRED_MORAL_CONSTITUTIONAL_ORDER.

This is stronger than saying religion merely "influences" politics.

But:

GREAT_LAW_CURRENT_THEOLOGICAL_LANGUAGE != PROOF_OF_ONE_UNCHANGED_PRECONTACT_WORDING.

## 8. Future generations

Onondaga public government material states that leaders should use Good Mind in
decisions not only for the present but for seven generations.

Current project classification:

FUTURE_GENERATION_OBLIGATION =
BOUNDED_ONONDAGA_CURRENT_PUBLIC_FORMULATION.

This can inform:
- RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL;
- POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL;
- moral/temporal responsibility.

Guard:

SEVEN_GENERATIONS != UNIVERSAL_HAUDENOSAUNEE_FORMULA_ALL_SOURCES_ALL_PERIODS.

## 9. Current Chiefs and Clan Mothers

The HCCC currently publishes officeholders by nation.

This supplies:
- living institutional continuity;
- nation-level titles/clans;
- current office status.

It does not prove:
- unchanged personnel structure in every historical period;
- identical present implementation across all communities.

## 10. Authority and sacred duty

HCCC current public language explicitly calls Chief appointment a sacred duty
or frames it through Great Law/Creator responsibilities.

Current conclusion:

POLITICAL_OFFICE_CAN_BE_PUBLICLY_FRAMED_AS_SACRED_MORAL_SERVICE.

This differs from:
DIVINE_KINGSHIP.

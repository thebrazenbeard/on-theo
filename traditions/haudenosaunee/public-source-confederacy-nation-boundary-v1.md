# Haudenosaunee Public-Source / Confederacy-Nation Boundary V1

Status: ACTIVE LIVING-CONFEDERACY RESEARCH CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open Haudenosaunee research without collapsing:
- Confederacy-level law/governance;
- six distinct member nations;
- nation-specific ceremonial traditions;
- later Longhouse religious movements;
- United States and Canadian governance contexts.

Core rule:

HAUDENOSAUNEE_CONFEDERACY != ONE_UNDIFFERENTIATED_RELIGIOUS_NATION.

A second rule:

CONFEDERACY_LEVEL_SOURCE != NATION_SPECIFIC_SOURCE.

## 1. Naming and political structure

Preferred shared label:
HAUDENOSAUNEE.

Historical/external labels such as:
- Iroquois Confederacy;
- League of Five Nations;
- Six Nations

remain source/historiographic labels.

The Confederacy's current public self-description identifies:
- Mohawk;
- Oneida;
- Onondaga;
- Cayuga;
- Seneca
as the original five member nations;
- Tuscarora as a later member.

Each nation retains its own council/internal affairs while the Grand Council
addresses shared Confederacy matters.

Therefore:

CONFEDERACY_UNITY != NATION_GOVERNANCE_ERASURE.

## 2. Great Law layer

Current Haudenosaunee Confederacy Council and Onondaga Nation public sources
describe the Great Law/Great Peace as:
- foundation of Confederacy government;
- spiritual, moral, social, and legal order;
- peace/consensus framework;
- source of roles for Chiefs, Clan Mothers, and other offices;
- connected in current public tradition with the Peacemaker.

Evidence class:
MODERN TRADITION / CURRENT INDIGENOUS POLITICAL-LEGAL SELF-DESCRIPTION /
HISTORICAL TRADITION.

The exact historical founding date is NOT fixed by this lane.

The Confederacy public source itself states the exact date is unknown / time
immemorial in its public framing.

Guard:

CURRENT_GREAT_LAW_SELF_DESCRIPTION != DATABLE_PRECONTACT_TRANSCRIPT.

## 3. Nation-specific evidence

Evidence from:
- Onondaga Nation;
- Oneida Nation;
- Seneca/Ganondagan;
- Mohawk communities;
- Cayuga communities;
- Tuscarora communities

must remain attached to the nation/community that supplied it unless a shared
Confederacy source independently generalizes it.

Example:

ONONDAGA_THANKSGIVING_ADDRESS_VERSION
!=
ONE_CANONICAL_HAUDENOSAUNEE_TEXT.

Onondaga explicitly states every Haudenosaunee nation has its own version.

## 4. United States / Canada context

Haudenosaunee communities and governance institutions span the present
U.S.-Canada border.

Current public institutions can include:
- traditional Confederacy councils;
- elected/band/tribal governments;
- reserve/reservation governments;
- national councils;
- heritage institutions.

These structures must not be collapsed.

A modern elected council and the traditional Haudenosaunee Confederacy Council
are distinct institutional evidence unless a source says otherwise.

## 5. Clan Mothers, Chiefs, Faithkeepers

Current Confederacy and nation sources publicly describe:
- Clan Mothers selecting/deposing Chiefs;
- Chiefs as Grand Council/nation representatives;
- Faithkeepers as spiritual/cultural officers;
- distinct responsibilities and authority domains.

This establishes public institutional categories.

It does NOT authorize:
- reconstruction of restricted ceremonial procedure;
- one identical nation-level implementation;
- one timeless office structure without diachronic control.

## 6. Thanksgiving Address

Public nation sources establish:
- use before meetings, gatherings, ceremonies and community business;
- relational thanksgiving through people, Earth, waters, plants, animals,
  celestial beings/powers, and Creator in selected versions;
- speaker/nation variation;
- a purpose of bringing minds together.

Current evidence class:
MODERN TRADITION / PUBLIC CEREMONIAL-RELATIONAL TEXT.

ON_THEO will analyze:
- public structure;
- relational sequence;
- social function;
- variation.

It will not manufacture one fixed canonical wording.

## 7. Seasonal ceremonial practice

Oneida Nation public sources describe:
- ceremonies following seasonal/agricultural cycles;
- Faithkeepers determining timing through observation of agriculture, moon,
  and seasons;
- public categories for thanksgiving, harvest, planting, death feast, and other
  ceremonies.

This is high-level public ceremonial evidence.

ON_THEO may compare:
- seasonality;
- office/timing structure;
- thanksgiving/relational function.

ON_THEO does NOT need to reproduce ceremonial procedure.

## 8. Later Longhouse / Handsome Lake history

Public Oneida materials mention Handsome Lake within current ceremonial
tradition.

The historical Handsome Lake / Gaiwiio / Longhouse movement is a later,
contact-era/revival/reform layer and MUST NOT be reverse-projected into:
- Confederacy founding;
- Great Law origin;
- all precontact Haudenosaunee religion.

Current status:

HANDSOME_LAKE_LONGHOUSE_HISTORY = SEPARATE_FUTURE_LANE.

## 9. Wampum

Public Onondaga sources describe wampum strings/belts as used for:
- ceremonies;
- carrying messages;
- recording historical events;
- treaty ratification;
- titles/office.

Wampum is DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE / MATERIAL-DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE.

Guard:

WAMPUM != ALPHABETIC_TEXT.

A specific belt's meaning must be source-bound.

## 10. Knowledge / representation boundary

Current Confederacy public statements explicitly challenge fraudulent
appropriation and unauthorised claims to names/identity/teachings.

ON_THEO therefore follows:

PUBLIC_EDUCATIONAL_SOURCE != AUTHORITY_TO_SPEAK_AS_HAUDENOSAUNEE.

Research describes public evidence; it does not claim ceremonial or political
voice.

## 11. Comparison hold

No Haudenosaunee proposition enters cross-tradition synthesis until:
- Confederacy versus nation scope is explicit;
- present institution versus historical reconstruction is explicit;
- public ceremonial evidence is separated from procedure;
- later Longhouse/Handsome Lake layers are separated;
- exact neutral axis is source-admitted.

Current status:

HAUDENOSAUNEE_CROSS_TRADITION_COMPARISON = HOLD.

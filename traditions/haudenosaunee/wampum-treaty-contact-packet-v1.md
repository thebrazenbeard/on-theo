## 2. Two Row / Guswenta as living treaty principle

Current Onondaga public materials present the Two Row Wampum / Guswenta as:
- peace/friendship relation;
- two peoples traveling side-by-side;
- separate but equal ways of life/governance/religion;
- non-interference / neither steering the other's vessel;
- an ongoing living treaty principle.

Current scholarly political/legal literature also treats Two Row principles as
important to Haudenosaunee sovereignty and relational non-interference.

Current project classification:

TWO_ROW_TREATY_PRINCIPLE =
BOUNDED_LIVING_HAUDENOSAUNEE_CONTACT_RELATION.

This is valid as current/living tradition whether or not one specific alleged
1613 written Dutch document is authentic.

## 3. Tawagonshi 1613 written-document controversy

A purported Dutch written treaty text associated with Tawagonshi/1613 became
public in the twentieth century through L. G. Van Loon.

Major historical/linguistic scholarship has challenged that written document:
- Gehring, Starna, and Fenton argued in 1987 that it was not genuine;
- Hermkens, Noordegraaf, and van der Sijs (2013) concluded linguistic
  anachronisms and anglicisms show the text was forged in the twentieth century.

Onondaga Nation has also published Robert Venables's defense of the document and
the wider oral tradition, arguing the written-document question does not exhaust
Haudenosaunee treaty memory.

Current source-critical result:

TAWAGONSHI_WRITTEN_DOCUMENT_1613 =
DISPUTED_WITH_STRONG_SCHOLARLY_FORGERY_FINDING.

TWO_ROW_ORAL_WAMPUM_TREATY_TRADITION =
INDEPENDENT_LIVING_EVIDENCE.

Therefore:

FORGED_OR_DISPUTED_EUROPEAN_DOCUMENT
DOES_NOT_EQUAL
FORGED_INDIGENOUS_TREATY_TRADITION.

Current Onondaga sources treat it as:

- connected with wampum/treaty memory;
- still politically meaningful.

## 9. Handsome Lake as separate contact response

Oneida public history places Handsome Lake / Kaliwihyo in a later contact-era
crisis.

The Good Message:
- addresses harms associated with colonial contact;
- reaffirms traditional culture;
- reaffirms Great Law;
- becomes part of later Longhouse ceremonial life.

Current status:

LATER_REFORM_REVITALIZATION_LAYER.

This is distinct from:
- Two Row diplomacy;

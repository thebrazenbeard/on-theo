# Haudenosaunee Great Law, Thanksgiving, and Relational Order Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC CONFEDERACY / NATION FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish a first bounded Haudenosaunee internal reconstruction from current
Confederacy and nation-controlled public sources.

## 1. Confederacy order is both political and sacred/moral in current sources

The Haudenosaunee Confederacy Council publicly describes:
- the Peacemaker;
- Great Peace/Great Law;
- Grand Council;
- law and values;
- relation among law, society, and nature.

Onondaga Nation independently describes sovereignty under the Great Law's:
- spiritual mandates;
- moral mandates;
- social mandates.

Current axis:

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CURRENT_CONFEDERACY_GREAT_LAW_LAYER.

Mechanism:
SACRED_MORAL_SOCIAL_LAW_AS_POLITICAL_CONSTITUTIONAL_ORDER.

This is a current living self-description.

It does not fix:
- exact origin date;
- one ancient written constitution;
- unchanged office practice across all centuries.

## 2. Nation sovereignty remains inside Confederacy structure

Current Confederacy/Onondaga sources state:
- each member nation governs internal affairs;
- Grand Council addresses shared matters;
- Onondaga holds central-fire role;
- Confederacy unity does not eliminate nation sovereignty.

Current proposition:

POLITICAL_ORDER =
NESTED_NATION_AND_CONFEDERACY_AUTHORITY.

This is important comparative evidence because centralized empire is not the
only sacred-political structure.

## 3. Clan Mothers and Chiefs

Current public Confederacy sources identify:
- Clan Mothers as title holders with authority to select and remove Chiefs;
- Chiefs as clan/nation representatives accountable to the people;
- office titles embedded in clan structure;
- decision-making obligations toward collective welfare.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CURRENT_MULTI_OFFICE.

Authority is distributed rather than monopolized.

## 4. Faithkeepers

Current Confederacy and Oneida sources describe Faithkeepers as:
- spiritual/cultural advisors;
- ceremonial officers;
- people responsible for maintaining public ceremonial cycles/Longhouse
  responsibilities;
- officers acting alongside Clan Mothers and Chiefs.

Current result:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CHIEF_CLAN_MOTHER_FAITHKEEPER_LONGHOUSE_LAYER.

Guard:

PUBLIC_OFFICE_DESCRIPTION != CEREMONIAL_PROCEDURE.

## 5. Thanksgiving Address

Onondaga Nation publicly states every Haudenosaunee nation has its own version
of the Thanksgiving Address.

The Address is described as used:
- before meetings;
- before ceremonies;
- before gatherings;
- to bring minds together;
- to acknowledge people and more-than-human relations.

Selected public versions give thanks through:
- people;
- Mother Earth;
- waters;
- plants/medicines;
- animals;
- birds;
- winds;
- celestial powers/beings;
- Creator.

Current axis:

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PUBLIC_THANKSGIVING_LAYER.

Important:

RELATIONAL_SEQUENCE != ONE_SYSTEMATIC_ONTOLOGY.

## Why this control exists

The Christianity lane already does several things well: it separates source chronology, warns against harmonization, distinguishes later doctrine from the historical Yeshua, and labels systems analogies as speculative.

Its weak point is provenance granularity.

The seven lane files are prose syntheses. `sources.md` is a useful reading list, but a URL in a reading list is not the same thing as a proposition bound to:

- an evidence class;
- an exact source or witness;
- a composition date versus physical-witness date;
- an access state;
- supporting and opposing evidence;
- a confidence ceiling;
- an exact research branch/head.

Since this lane was created, later ON_THEO branches have built much stronger registry-style controls for several Christianity-adjacent questions. Those later branches must neither be ignored nor silently treated as merged into this older lane.

## Evidence-layer rule

For any consequential Christianity proposition, preserve the distinction among:

- `PRIMARY_TEXT`: what an identified text says;
- `MATERIAL_EVIDENCE`: manuscript/inscription/archaeological evidence;
- `HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`: what likely happened historically;
- `LATER_TRADITION`: later accounts of earlier events or persons;
- `SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`: attributed modern analysis;
- `PROJECT_INFERENCE`: ON_THEO's own inference;
- `SPECULATIVE_MODEL`: systems/metaphysical analogy;
- `UNKNOWN`: unresolved.

A proposition may cross layers only by stating the inference explicitly. Canonical status, theological importance, or later acceptance does not promote a claim into historical fact.

## Three-clock rule

Where materially relevant, keep separate:

1. claimed-event date;
2. composition/redaction date;
3. physical witness/manuscript date.

A medieval manuscript can preserve older material. Its physical date does not become the tradition's composition date. Conversely, an old composition hypothesis does not establish that a particular surviving witness contained the same wording.

### Birth, paternity, and infancy

State: `DEEP_SUCCESSOR_RESEARCH_AVAILABLE_NOT_IMPORTED`.

This is the most developed successor area. Exact-head research now separately controls:

- Mark 6:3 textual wording and variant evidence;

- Mark 6:3 does not itself establish a first-century biological-paternity rumor;
- John 8:41 does not name Mary, Panthera, or an alternative biological father;
- later rabbinic Panthera/Pandera evidence must not be counted as multiple independent eyewitnesses merely because it appears in multiple textual locations;
- a medieval witness date must not be substituted for the date of the tradition it preserves;
- direct Celsus-to-rabbinic lineage remains unestablished.

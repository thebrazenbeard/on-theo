# Ifá Knowledge Governance / Specialist Authority Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC STRUCTURAL KNOWLEDGE-GOVERNANCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind Ifá specialist authority, training and interpretation without reconstructing
restricted initiatory content or treating babaláwo as a centralized priesthood
for all Yorùbá religion.

Core rule:

SPECIALIST_AUTHORITY != TOTAL_RELIGIOUS_AUTHORITY.

## 1. Specialist interpretive role

UNESCO publicly describes Ifá as a system in which:
- signs are generated through divination;
- the signs are interpreted by a diviner;
- the diviner is commonly identified as babaláwo / Ifá priest;
- the interpretation guides important individual or collective decisions.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_IFÁ_SPECIALIST_INTERPRETIVE_AUTHORITY.

Authority here is:
- corpus-based;
- sign-based;
- learned;
- situational/consultative.

It is not:
sovereign authority over all Yorùbá religion.

## 2. Training

UNESCO safeguarding documentation explicitly identifies:
- complex knowledge transmission;
- intergenerational and peer transmission;
- training of future Ifá practitioners;
- formal safeguarding/teaching projects.

Wándé Abímbọ́lá's published corpus work also treats training of Ifá priests as a
distinct topic.

Current axis:

KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE_DIMENSION =
BOUNDED_TRAINING_AND_INTERGENERATIONAL_TRANSMISSION.

## 3. Memorization and interpretive competence

The Ifá system requires more than knowing the 256 odù names/signatures.

Specialist competence includes:
- recognizing generated signs;
- maintaining a substantial verse repertoire;
- matching relevant verse/precedent to the consultation;
- interpreting the verse in the current situation;
- identifying recommended response/action where source-public.

Therefore:

INDEX_KNOWLEDGE != INTERPRETIVE_MASTERY.

This is a key authority distinction.

## 4. Corpus is distributed across specialists

The oral corpus has no single complete living human copy.

Different specialists can hold:
- overlapping repertoires;
- lineage/local variants;
- different verse selections.

Current result:

KNOWLEDGE_AUTHORITY_CAN_BE_DISTRIBUTED_WITHOUT_A_SINGLE_COMPLETE_CANON_HOLDER.

This does not imply:
- no standards;
- no training;
- no recognized expertise.

## 5. Public knowledge / specialist knowledge distinction

Public sources make the following safely comparable:
- odù structure;
- specialist role;
- broad transmission model;
- public functions of divination;
- broad content domains.

The project does not need to reconstruct:
- full initiatory grades;
- private lineage procedures;
- complete sacred verse repertories;
- restricted ritual knowledge.

Current rule:

COMPARISON_READINESS_DOES_NOT_REQUIRE_MAXIMUM_DISCLOSURE.

This independently reinforces the Diné knowledge-governance control.

## 6. Authority is practical and interpretive

A consultation does not function simply by retrieving a memorized text.

The specialist:
- generates/reads the sign;
- selects/recites relevant precedent;
- interprets;
- relates precedent to present circumstances.

Current classification:

IFÁ_AUTHORITY =
PERFORMATIVE_INTERPRETIVE_EXPERTISE.

This differs from:
- manuscript custodianship;
- hereditary political authority;
- prophetic revelation by possession.

## 7. Institutionalization

The system demonstrates durable institutionalization through:
- specialist training;
- recognized role identity;
- material practice technologies;
- repeated consultation structure;
- intergenerational transmission.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_SPECIALIST_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTION.

But:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION != CENTRALIZED_BUREAUCRACY.

## 8. Oracular-power distinction

UNESCO publicly contrasts Ifá sign interpretation with forms of divination based
on possession or individual oracular power.

Current comparison pressure:

DIVINATION_AUTHORITY_CAN_RESIDE_IN
FORMAL_SIGN_CORPUS_INTERPRETATION
rather than
MEDIUMISTIC_POSSESSION.

This must not be generalized beyond the UNESCO-described Ifá layer.

## 9. Practitioner-scholar mediation

Wándé Abímbọ́lá is methodologically important because his work occupies more than
one evidentiary position:
- trained scholar;
- major interpreter/editor of Ifá literature;
- practitioner/ritual specialist within the tradition.

Therefore:

PRACTITIONER_SCHOLAR_SOURCE =
EMIC_AND_ANALYTIC_MEDIATION.

It is not:
unmediated total tradition.

Each proposition still needs work/edition/page control when used deeply.

## 10. Safeguarding and modern institutional change

UNESCO safeguarding efforts sought to:
- formalize knowledge transfer;
- create training infrastructure;
- document verses;
- sustain the tradition under social pressures.

Current axis:

CONTINUITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_INSTITUTIONAL_SAFEGUARDING_WITH_CHANGE.

Modern schooling/documentation can preserve a tradition while also altering
transmission form.

Therefore:

FORMALIZED_SAFEGUARDING != UNCHANGED_TRADITIONAL_TRANSMISSION.

## 11. Knowledge-governance comparison contribution

Ifá adds a distinct subtype:

SPECIALIST_INTERPRETIVE_ORAL_CORPUS_AUTHORITY.

Compare later with:
- Diné conditional/restricted knowledge governance;
- Lakota community-governed disclosure;
- Haudenosaunee wampum/office memory;
- Inca khipukamayuq specialist material records.

Shared question:
WHO_MAY_INTERPRET_TRANSMIT_AND_AUTHORIZE_KNOWLEDGE?

Not:
ONE_PRIESTHOOD_MODEL.

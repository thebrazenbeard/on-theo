# Yorùbá Relational Òrìṣà / Ancestor Packet V1

Status: HISTORICAL-RELATIONAL CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind a wider Yorùbá religious layer outside Ifá by focusing on:
- relations between devotees and òrìṣà;
- ancestor/community relation;
- local cult variation;
- historical pantheon change;
- civic and political embedding.

Core rule:

ÒRÌṢÀ_RELATION != ONE_FIXED_PANTHEON_THEOLOGY.

A second rule:

ANCESTOR_RELATION != ONE_POSTMORTEM_PATH.

## 1. Deep historical frame

Recent Oxford scholarship describes Yorùbá indigenous religion as evolving over
roughly two millennia alongside political, economic, cultural and social
history.

It emphasizes:
- individual/community relations to divinity;
- ancestor veneration;
- emergence and consolidation of pantheon structures;
- kingship;
- civic/intellectual religious practice.

Current classification:

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_DIACHRONIC_YORÙBÁ_FIELD.

The comparison unit is not a timeless doctrine.

## 2. Devotee–òrìṣà relation

Karin Barber's work on an Okuku Yorùbá setting describes a strong relational
logic in which:
- òrìṣà prestige/power is sustained through devotees' attention;
- devotees seek benefits/protection;
- religious attachment can be personal and relational;
- òrìṣà identities can proliferate, merge and be locally differentiated.

Current result:

DEVOTEE_ÒRÌṢÀ_RELATION =
RECIPROCAL_SOCIAL_RELATIONAL_MODEL
in the selected ethnographic/intellectual layer.

Important guard:

HUMAN_ATTENTION_SUSTAINS_ÒRÌṢÀ
does NOT mean:
"Yorùbá people merely invent gods" as a reductive outsider claim.

It is a source-interpreted account of relational constitution.

## 3. Òrìṣà multiplicity and local manifestation

Barber's work on oríkì and òrìṣà stresses that oral genres participate in:
- differentiating manifestations;
- maintaining identity;
- merging/overlap;
- communication with òrìṣà.

Current classification:

ORAL_GENRE_CAN_PARTICIPATE_IN_DIVINE_IDENTITY_MAINTENANCE.

This links:
RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL
and
DOCUMENTARY_MEMORY_MODEL.

But:
ORÍKÌ != IFÁ CORPUS.

They remain distinct oral genres.

## 4. Local pantheon variation

Andrew Apter's Ekiti work documents substantial variation in:
- local ritual pantheons;
- cult organization;
- local forms/names of òrìṣà;
- relation between cult and sociopolitical organization.

Current result:

LOCAL_PANTHEON_VARIATION =
POSITIVE_HISTORICAL_AND_ETHNOGRAPHIC_DATA.

Therefore:

ONE_PAN_YORÙBÁ_PANTHEON_MAP =
NOT_ADMITTED.

## 5. Pantheon change through history

Ogundiran's diachronic reconstruction explicitly treats pantheon development as
historically emergent and shaped by sociopolitical/political-economic events.

Current axis:

CONTINUITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CHANGE_RECONFIGURATION.

This is stronger than:
UNCHANGED_SURVIVAL.

## 6. Ancestor relation

Current historical scholarship treats ancestor veneration as a major dimension
of the relationship between:
- individuals;
- communities;
- divinity;
- historical memory.

Current axis:

MORTUARY_RELATIONSHIP_MODEL =
PARTIAL_ANCESTOR_RELATIONAL_LAYER.

This supports:
THE_DEAD_CAN_REMAIN_SOCIAL_RELIGIOUS_REFERENTS.

It does NOT yet support:
- one Yorùbá afterlife geography;
- one soul ontology;
- one universal ancestor-making process;
- one ordinary-human postmortem destination.

## 7. Ancestor / òrìṣà boundary

Historical and ethnographic scholarship can describe overlap among categories
such as:
- deified ancestors;
- cultural heroes;
- local powers;
- òrìṣà.

But no project rule assumes:
ALL_ÒRÌṢÀ_ARE_ANCESTORS
or
ALL_ANCESTORS_BECOME_ÒRÌṢÀ.

Each case requires source control.

## 8. Civic religion

Ogundiran characterizes Yorùbá indigenous religion as a civic spiritual
practice with intellectual/dialogical dimensions.

Apter shows ritual and religious knowledge can configure:
- political authority;
- social difference;
- resistance;
- sovereignty.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
PARTIAL_CIVIC_CULT_AND_LOCAL_POLITICAL_EMBEDDING.

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL =
PARTIAL_STRONG_CANDIDATE.

A dedicated kingship packet is still required before bounded admission.

## 9. Sacred kingship pressure

Oxford history of precolonial Yorùbá states describes Ilé-Ifẹ̀'s foundational
political tradition around Odùduwà as sacred king and later mythic ancestor,
with political/religious models replicated across city-states.

Current project status:

SACRED_KINGSHIP =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_NOT_YET_FULLY_BOUND.

Do not infer:
- one kingship theology across all Yorùbá states;
- that later mythic genealogy is transparent first-millennium history;
- that "Yorùbá" was an unchanged precolonial pan-ethnic self-label.

## 10. Relational-order decomposition

The current Yorùbá layer contributes at least four distinct relational forms:

DEVOTEE_ÒRÌṢÀ_RELATION
ANCESTOR_COMMUNITY_RELATION
CULT_TOWN_POLITICAL_RELATION
ORAL_GENRE_DIVINE_IDENTITY_RELATION

These can overlap.

They must not be collapsed into:
GENERIC_RECIPROCITY.

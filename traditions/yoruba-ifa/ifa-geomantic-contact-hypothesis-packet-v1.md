# Ifá / Geomancy Contact Hypothesis Packet V1

Status: TRANSMISSION-HYPOTHESIS CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Test the common claim that Ifá's sixteen-sign architecture derives from
Arabic/Islamic geomancy without allowing formal similarity to establish a
specific transmission history.

Core rule:

SHARED_SIXTEEN_FIGURE_ARCHITECTURE != DIRECT_BORROWING_PROVED.

A second rule:

SIGN_SYSTEM_HISTORY != IFÁ_VERSE_CORPUS_HISTORY.

## 1. Comparative formal resemblance

Ifá uses:
- sixteen basic binary figures;
- 256 derived/composite odù.

Arabic/Islamic geomancy and multiple African systems also use families of
sixteen four-position binary figures.

Current classification:

FORMAL_STRUCTURAL_RESEMBLANCE =
STRONG.

This makes historical relationship a legitimate question.

It does not determine:
- direction;
- date;
- carrier;
- independent development versus diffusion.

## 2. Scholarly diffusion hypothesis

Karin Barber's Routledge overview describes the sixteen-sign geomantic form as
widespread and commonly believed to have originated in Arabic Muslim cultures,
while emphasizing that the attached poetic corpus is distinctively Yorùbá.

Other scholarship traces long-standing contact between Arab-Islamic geomancy
and African sixteen-figure systems.

Current project classification:

ARABIC_ISLAMIC_FORM_TRANSMISSION_TO_WEST_AFRICA =
SERIOUS_SCHOLARLY_HYPOTHESIS.

Not:
ESTABLISHED_EXACT_IFÁ_GENEALOGY.

## 3. Bascom's caution

William Bascom explicitly compares Ifá with Islamic geomancy but declines to
settle the ultimate origin question from formal parallels alone.

Important differences he identifies include:
- figure names;
- ordering;
- computational procedure;
- instrument use;
- verse attachment;
- ritual/sacrificial context;
- lack of Ifá's verse corpus in the locally attested Muslim sand-divination
  comparison.

Current project result:

FORM_SIMILARITY_HAS_NONTRIVIAL_DIFFERENCES.

Therefore:
STRUCTURAL_MATCHING_DOES_NOT_CLOSE_THE_TRANSMISSION_CASE.

## 4. Atímì / Islamic geomancy among Yorùbá

Bascom documents a distinct Muslim sand-cutting practice among Yorùbá Muslim
diviners:
- identified with Arabic geomancy;
- Arabic-derived figure names/order;
- distinguished locally from Ifá;
- probably relatively recent in that observed form.

Current significance:

DIRECT_ISLAMIC_GEOMANCY_CONTACT_IN_YORÙBÁ_CONTEXT =
ESTABLISHED_FOR_ATÍMÌ/SAND_DIVINATION.

But:

ATÍMÌ_CONTACT
!=
PROOF_ATÍMÌ_CREATED_IFÁ.

The co-presence of two systems is contact evidence, not origin proof.

## 5. Corpus asymmetry

Ifá attaches a large Yorùbá oral literary corpus to its formal sign structure.

Barber emphasizes:
- this poetic corpus is culturally distinctive;
- the corpus incorporates numerous Yorùbá oral genres;
- it addresses Yorùbá philosophy, society, destiny, causation and agency.

Current result:

IFÁ_POETIC_CORPUS =
YORÙBÁ_INTERNAL_LITERARY_DEVELOPMENT_AT_CURRENT_CEILING.

No current evidence packet establishes:
ARABIC_GEOMANTIC_VERSE_CORPUS -> IFÁ_ESE.

## 6. Multiple transmission targets

The historical question must be decomposed:

A. FOUR_POSITION_BINARY_LOGIC
B. SIXTEEN_BASIC_FIGURE_ARCHITECTURE
C. FIGURE_ORDER
D. FIGURE_NAMES
E. DIVINATION_INSTRUMENTS
F. INTERPRETIVE_RULES
G. ASSOCIATED_VERSE_CORPUS
H. SACRIFICIAL/PRESCRIPTIVE_PRACTICE
I. DEITY/ÒRÌṢÀ ASSOCIATION
J. SPECIALIST_INSTITUTION

A transmission claim about A/B does not license transmission claims about G–J.

## 7. Chronology problem

The strongest missing evidence is datable early documentation/material evidence
showing:
- where the sixteen-figure architecture first appears in the relevant regions;
- when it enters Yorùbá practice;
- which carrier community transmitted it;
- whether the direction was one-way.

Current status:

EXACT_IFÁ_SIGN_ARCHITECTURE_TRANSMISSION_CHAIN =
UNKNOWN.

## 8. Geographic/contact plausibility

Islamic scholarly/trading/contact networks reached West Africa long before
modern colonial ethnography.

Therefore:

CONTACT_POSSIBILITY =
YES.

But project transmission standard requires more:
- chronological priority;
- identifiable carrier/pathway;
- semantic/formal fit beyond mathematically expected resemblance;
- evidence excluding plausible alternative pathways.

## 9. Mathematical caution

A four-position binary system necessarily produces sixteen possible basic
figures.

Therefore:
SIXTEEN_FIGURES
is not sixteen independent matches.

The information value of the resemblance is lower than a naïve motif count
suggests.

This is a useful cross-corpus methodological control:

STRUCTURAL_COMBINATORICS_CAN_CREATE_RESEMBLANCE_WITHOUT_SIXTEEN_INDEPENDENT_BORROWING_SIGNALS.

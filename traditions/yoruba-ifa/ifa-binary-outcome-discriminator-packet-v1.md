# Yorùbá / Ifá — Binary Outcome Discriminator Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC STRUCTURAL DIVINATION CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Identify whether Ifá contains a historically documented outcome format that is
narrow enough to score prospectively without reconstructing restricted
initiatory material.

Yes.

William Bascom's public scholarly description of Ifá includes a consultation
stage in which questions may be posed as:

- two mutually exclusive propositions;
- yes/no questions;
- choices among specific alternatives.

This gives the current ritual-interface project its cleanest source-documented
outcome format to date.

Evidence class:

`SCHOLARLY_ETHNOGRAPHIC_DESCRIPTION / DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE`.

## Exact outcome property

The important methodological property is not merely that Ifá gives advice.

It is that selected consultation stages can produce:

`FINITE_MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE_OUTPUT`.

For a binary case:

`OUTPUT ∈ {A, B}`.

That is substantially more scoreable than:
- siddhi;
- protection;
- power;
- revelation in general;
- felt presence.

## Practitioner causal model versus statistical null

Bascom explicitly notes that the production of Ifá figures can be described in
probabilistic terms.

At the same time, in the practitioner/client interpretation documented by
Bascom, the outcome is not regarded as mere chance but as controlled through
Ifá/Òrúnmìlà.

That creates an unusually clean historical contrast:

`H_CHANCE/PROCEDURAL_RANDOMNESS`

versus

`H_TRADITIONAL_DIVINATORY_GUIDANCE`.

This does not mean the two models have already been experimentally compared
under modern blinding.

It means the source material itself supports a testable rival-model structure.

The output family is source-defined.

`SOURCE_DEFINED_OUTPUT + MODERN_TARGET_CUSTODY`.

## Candidate empirical abstraction

A modern research design could ask a trained practitioner to use only a
publicly documented, ethically appropriate specific-alternative procedure to
commit one of two mutually exclusive answers before an externally held target is
revealed.

Possible target families must be classified separately.

### Present-hidden target

A target exists before the consultation but is inaccessible to practitioner,
participant, and model.

Tests:
`ANOMALOUS_PRESENT_INFORMATION`.

Historical fit:
PARTIAL / MODERN.

### Future-generated target

A target is generated only after the consultation response is committed.

Tests:
`ANOMALOUS_FUTURE_INFORMATION / PRECOGNITIVE_MODEL`.

Historical fit:
PARTIAL / MODERN.

Neither should be called a validation of Ifá theology merely because the output
format resembles the traditional binary alternative structure.

## Strong methodological advantage

Unlike free-form symbolic interpretation, binary scoring can be mechanical.

A trial can be:

- answer A or B committed;
- target revealed;
- hit or miss;
- no semantic rescue.

Therefore:

`MISS = MISS`.

That sharply reduces retrospective matching.

## Critical probability control

Do not assume the chance probability is exactly 0.5 until the specific public
procedure used in the experiment has been modeled.

Bascom discusses probabilistic structure in Ifá figure generation, but different
specific-alternative procedures or implements may introduce different
conditional probabilities.

Required before testing:

1. document the exact public procedure;
2. derive or empirically estimate its null output distribution;
3. freeze that distribution before target data are examined.

If the procedure itself generates A and B at unequal rates, scoring against
50/50 would be invalid.

## Specialist-interpretation control

The binary stage does not eliminate all human degrees of freedom.

Potential confounds include:

- which alternative is labeled affirmative;
- order of proposition presentation;
- client framing;
- operator choice among acceptable procedures;
- ambiguous or repeated consultations;
- post-hoc reinterpretation of the result.

Therefore a modern protocol must freeze:

- exact question wording;
- A/B coding;
- number of allowed casts;
- invalid-trial rule;
- stop rule;
- answer commitment format.

No re-casting after an unwanted answer unless the historical public procedure
itself independently requires it and that rule is frozen in advance.

## Living-tradition boundary

Ifá is a living religious/intellectual tradition.

The project does not require:

- restricted initiatory teaching;
- private lineage verses;
- disclosure of sacred knowledge;
- unauthorized performance of specialist ritual.

A valid first experiment can operate only with:
- public structural information;
- voluntary participation of a competent practitioner if one is used;
- community-respectful framing;
- no claim that the tradition has been "debunked" or "proved" by one experiment.

## Ordinary rival models

### H_RANDOM
The specific alternative is generated by the statistical properties of the
procedure.

### H_OPERATOR_BIAS
Subtle operator handling or coding changes output frequencies.

### H_QUESTION_FRAMING
The way propositions are phrased influences procedure or interpretation.

### H_CLIENT_CUEING
Information about the client's expectations leaks into answer selection.

### H_INTERPRETIVE_FLEXIBILITY
The broader consultation allows reinterpretation after the binary stage.

### H_DATA_HANDLING
Selective retention or re-casting creates apparent accuracy.

### H_ANOMALOUS_INFORMATION
The procedure produces information about an external target beyond ordinary
channels.

The experiment must be designed so H_RANDOM through H_DATA_HANDLING are tested,
not merely asserted away.

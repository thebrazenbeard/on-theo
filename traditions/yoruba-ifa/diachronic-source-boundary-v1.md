# Yorùbá / Ifá Diachronic Source Boundary V1

Status: ACTIVE RESEARCH CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open a sub-Saharan African research lane without collapsing:
- Yorùbá religious history;
- contemporary Ìṣẹ̀ṣe / Òrìṣà religion;
- Ifá divination;
- individual òrìṣà cults;
- ancestor relations;
- kingship/civic religion;
- colonial missionary descriptions;
- Atlantic diaspora traditions.

Core rule:

IFÁ != ALL_YORÙBÁ_RELIGION.

A second rule:

YORÙBÁ_RELIGION != ONE_TIMELESS_PANTHEON.

## 1. Yorùbá / regional scope

"Yorùbá" is a broad ethnolinguistic/cultural label associated especially with
southwestern Nigeria and neighboring regions.

Religious propositions must retain:
- locality;
- historical period;
- institution/cult;
- source language;
- source mode.

No proposition may use "the Yorùbá believe" when the evidence is:
- one town;
- one lineage;
- one òrìṣà cult;
- one Ifá lineage;
- one modern organization;
- one diaspora denomination.

## 2. Ìṣẹ̀ṣe / Òrìṣà religion

Recent scholarship describes Yorùbá indigenous religion as a historically
developing field, now often called Ìṣẹ̀ṣe or Òrìṣà religion.

Current source-critical result:

MODERN_LABEL
does not automatically equal
ANCIENT_SELF_DESIGNATION.

The field developed with:
- political change;
- town formation;
- kingship;
- ancestor relations;
- changing pantheon structures;
- Islam and Christianity;
- colonialism;
- diaspora.

## 3. Pantheon control

Recent historical scholarship emphasizes that the Yorùbá pantheon is emergent
and historically shaped.

Therefore:

ÒRÌṢÀ_PANTHEON != ONE_FIXED_LIST_ACROSS_TWO_MILLENNIA.

Each deity/òrìṣà proposition requires:
- period;
- locality;
- source;
- cult context.

## 4. Ifá

Ifá is:
- a divination system;
- a specialist knowledge tradition;
- an oral literary corpus;
- a material/sign system;
- associated with Ọ̀rúnmìlà in public descriptions.

It is not:
- the whole of Yorùbá religion;
- one printed scripture;
- one fixed corpus with a fully closed verse count.

## 5. Ifá corpus structure

Public scholarly/UNESCO controls support:
- a system based on sixteen binary/sign elements producing 256 figures/odù;
- a large body of verses associated with those figures;
- highly trained diviners, commonly called babaláwo;
- interpretation for personal and collective decisions;
- intergenerational specialist transmission.

Current rule:

256_ODÙ_STRUCTURE
!=
FIXED_TOTAL_VERSE_CORPUS.

The corpus can be highly structured and still remain orally generative.

## 6. Oral evidence class

ON_THEO does not treat oral transmission as a deficient form of textuality.

Evidence classes for Ifá may include:
- ORAL LITERARY CORPUS;
- RITUAL PERFORMANCE;
- SPECIALIST TESTIMONY;
- DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE;
- MATERIAL EVIDENCE;
- MODERN TRANSCRIPTION/EDITION;
- SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.

Required guard:

ORAL != UNSTABLE_BY_DEFINITION.

But:
ORAL PERFORMANCE != ONE_FIXED_TEXTUAL WITNESS.

## 7. Material divination technology

Public museum evidence documents:
- ọ̀pọ́n Ifá / divination trays;
- ikin / sacred palm nuts;
- divination chains;
- containers/vessels;
- tappers and other implements.

Material evidence can support:
- specialist divination practice;
- sign-generation technology;
- iconography;
- craft/local variation.

It cannot independently recover:
- a specific verse;
- one theology;
- one historical consultation.

## 8. Specialist authority

Babaláwo are publicly documented as trained Ifá specialists.

Current axis candidate:

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_SPECIALIST_ORAL_INTERPRETIVE.

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_SPECIALIST_DIVINATORY.

Do not infer:
BABALÁWO = ALL_YORÙBÁ_PRIESTHOOD.

## 9. Islam / geomantic-contact question

Scholarship notes that the sixteen-sign divinatory form belongs to a wider
geomantic family with possible Islamic/Arabic transmission history, while the
Yorùbá poetic corpus attached to the system is distinctively Yorùbá.

Current research hold:

DIVINATORY_FORM_TRANSMISSION =
CANDIDATE_REQUIRING_DEDICATED_CONTACT_TEST.

Required distinction:

SIGN_SYSTEM_HISTORY != POETIC_CORPUS_HISTORY.

## 10. Diaspora boundary

Yorùbá-derived religious traditions in the Americas include historically
distinct formations such as:
- Lukumí / Santería;
- Brazilian Candomblé traditions;
- Trinidadian and other Òrìṣà traditions.

These are:
DIASPORA RELIGIOUS DEVELOPMENTS.

They are not:
unchanged replicas of one homeland Yorùbá religion.

Current rule:

DIASPORA_YORÙBÁ_DERIVED != HOMELAND_IDENTITY.

## 11. Colonial / missionary sources

Nineteenth- and early twentieth-century sources may preserve:
- terminology;
- cult practice;
- oral history;
- ritual;
- political-religious institutions.

They also may contain:
- Christian categories;
- "pagan/idolatry" framing;
- evolutionary religion theory;
- colonial hierarchy.

Therefore:

COLONIAL_ETHNOGRAPHY_OR_MISSIONARY_TEXT
requires
ATTRIBUTION_STATUS + SOURCE_POSITION.

## 12. Sacred-knowledge boundary

The project will use public structural evidence about Ifá.

It does not require:
- reproducing large sacred verse corpora;
- bypassing specialist transmission;
- treating heritage documentation as permission to disclose everything.

Current rule:

PUBLIC_STRUCTURE_CAN_BE_RESEARCH_SUFFICIENT.

## 13. Comparison hold

No Yorùbá/Ifá cell enters global synthesis until:
- internal diachronic layer is named;
- Ifá versus wider Òrìṣà religion is explicit;
- oral/material/textual evidence is separated;
- diaspora is separate;
- exact source supports the neutral axis.

Current status:

YORUBA_IFA_CROSS_TRADITION_COMPARISON = HOLD.

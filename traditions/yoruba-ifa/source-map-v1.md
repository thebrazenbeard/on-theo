# Yorùbá / Ifá Source Map V1

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE MAP / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Akinwumi Ogundiran — Yorùbá Indigenous Religion

Oxford Research Encyclopedia of African History, 2024.

High-value controls:
- deep historical development over roughly two millennia;
- divinity and community/person relations;
- ancestor veneration;
- historically emergent pantheon;
- kingship and political economy;
- civic/intellectual religious practice.

Evidence:
SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION / HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION.

Use:
prevent timeless-pantheon flattening.

## 2. UNESCO — Ifá Divination System

Public UNESCO materials describe:
- Ifá among Yorùbá communities;
- complex sign system;
- literary/oral corpus;
- babaláwo specialist interpretation;
- personal/collective decision use;
- knowledge of history, philosophy, medicine and mythology;
- safeguarding through intergenerational transmission.

Evidence:
LIVING HERITAGE DOCUMENTATION /
CURRENT PUBLIC PRACTICE DESCRIPTION.

Guard:
UNESCO description != complete theology.

## 3. Karin Barber — Ifá Divination Corpus

Routledge Encyclopedia of Philosophy.

High-value controls:
- highly trained specialists;
- sixteen-sign system / 256 derived figures;
- thousands of verses;
- structured precedent narratives;
- adaptation and incorporation of new material;
- reflections on time, destiny, causation and agency.

Evidence:
SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION of ORAL CORPUS.

## 6. Samuel Johnson — The History of the Yorubas

Historically important Yorùbá-authored/mission-era source.

Use for:
- late nineteenth-century remembered institutions;
- terminology;
- political/religious history.

Guard:
- Christian/Yorùbá authorial context;
- post-contact systematization;
- later editorial/publication history.

Not:
transparent ancient Yorùbá religion.

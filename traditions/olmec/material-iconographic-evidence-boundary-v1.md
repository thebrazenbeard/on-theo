# Olmec Material / Iconographic Evidence Boundary V1

Status: ACTIVE LOW-TEXTUAL-CERTAINTY RESEARCH CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open an Olmec religious-history lane without pretending that Formative-period
material and iconography amount to a surviving systematic theological corpus.

Core rule:

OLMEC_ICONOGRAPHY != RECOVERED_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.

A second rule:

LATER_MESOAMERICAN_PARALLEL != OLMEC_MEANING_BY_DEFAULT.

## 1. What "Olmec" means in this lane

Working archaeological scope:
- Formative/Preclassic societies conventionally identified as Olmec;
- Gulf Coast heartland centered in southern Veracruz and western/northern
  Tabasco;
- especially San Lorenzo Tenochtitlan and La Venta;
- approximate broad fluorescence ca. 1450/1200-400 BCE depending chronology and
  definition.

The label is archaeological and historiographic.

It is not a surviving self-name.

Current scholarship continues to debate:
- what materially defines "Olmec";
- regional variation;
- origins;
- interaction spheres;
- cultural legacies;
- "mother culture" versus "sister culture" models.

Therefore:

OLMEC = ONE_HOMOGENEOUS_PEOPLE_WITH_ONE_THEOLOGY

is NOT admitted.

## 2. Heartland versus Olmec-style interaction

The project distinguishes:

HEARTLAND_OLMEC:
- San Lorenzo;
- La Venta;
- Tres Zapotes and other Gulf Coast contexts when source-bound.

OLMEC_STYLE_OR_INTERACTION_SPHERE:
- Guerrero cave paintings;
- Morelos;
- Oaxaca;
- Chiapas;
- Guatemala;
- other regions with Olmec-style objects or motifs.

Guard:

OLMEC_STYLE_OBJECT_OUTSIDE_HEARTLAND != OLMEC_MIGRATION_OR_DIRECT_CONTROL_BY_DEFAULT.

Similarity can reflect:
- exchange;
- emulation;
- shared visual conventions;
- elite interaction;
- local adoption;
- multiple mechanisms.

Each requires evidence.

## 3. No readable theological corpus

Unlike Classic Maya epigraphy or colonial Nahuatl/K'iche' manuscripts, no
surviving corpus currently allows the project to read extended Olmec religious
propositions in their own language.

La Venta INAH interpretation explicitly notes the lack of writing that would
explain sculptural scenes in the way readable Maya inscriptions can.

The Cascajal Block may represent an early Olmec writing system, but:
- discovery context was not a controlled excavation;
- cultural/date/authenticity questions have been debated;
- current scholarship increasingly accepts authenticity but still debates
  writing-system interpretation;
- the signs remain undeciphered;
- the content is not readable as theology.

Therefore:

CASC AJAL_BLOCK != READABLE_OLMEC_THEOLOGICAL_TEXT.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy

### Highest-confidence Olmec religious evidence

- MATERIAL EVIDENCE from controlled archaeological contexts;
- ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT;
- OFFERING/DEPOSITION CONTEXT;
- stratigraphy;
- provenanced monumental sculpture;
- archaeometric provenance;
- repeated spatial pattern.

### Medium-confidence

- ICONOGRAPHIC ANALYSIS on well-provenanced works;
- comparison among repeated Olmec motifs;
- context-bound functional inference;
- contemporary scholarly interpretation.

### Lower-confidence

- unprovenanced museum objects;
- retrojection from later Maya/Mexica names;
- broad "Olmec god" lists;
- twentieth-century motif taxonomies treated as native categories.

## 5. Scholarly iconographic names are analytical labels

Terms such as:
- Olmec Maize God;
- were-jaguar;
- Olmec Dragon;
- shark/earth monster;
- supernatural infant;
- rain deity

are modern scholarly classifications unless an independent source establishes
otherwise.

Therefore:

SCHOLARLY_ICONOGRAPHIC_LABEL != OLMEC_SELF_NAME.

The project may use these labels for controlled comparison, always marked as
SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.

## 6. San Lorenzo and La Venta remain distinct

San Lorenzo:
- earlier major Gulf Coast center;
- strong ca. 1200-850 BCE florescence;
- major landscape modification;
- colossal heads/thrones;
- extensive population and exchange.

La Venta:
- later major center;
- ceremonial precinct;
- massive offerings;
- imported greenstone;
- monumental visual program;
- major ca. first millennium BCE development.

Guard:

SAN_LORENZO_RELIGION != LA_VENTA_RELIGION_BY_DEFAULT.

Diachronic change is expected.

## 7. Monumental portraiture and authority

INAH interprets colossal heads at La Venta as male human figures bearing
insignia of power and as evidence of social inequality/leaders.

This supports:
- POLITICAL_AUTHORITY_MODEL candidate;
- elite monumental representation.

It does NOT by itself prove:
- divine kingship;
- priest-kings;
- ancestor cult;
- ruler apotheosis.

## 8. Offerings establish ritual action, not exact theology

La Venta Complex A contains:
- approximately fifty offerings with ceramics, figurines, jade objects and
  hematite mirrors;
- five massive offerings containing tons of serpentine;
- imported materials from Guatemala, Oaxaca and elsewhere.

This establishes:
- deliberate deposition;
- ritual/ceremonial context;
- long-distance material mobilization.

It does not automatically establish:
- recipient;
- prayer;
- requested outcome;
- reciprocity;
- deity identity.

## 9. Cave and landscape imagery

Oxtotitlan, Guerrero, preserves Olmec-affiliated cave paintings around
900-600 BCE.

INAH associates motifs with:
- water;
- rain;
- fertility;
- anthropomorphic and zoomorphic figures.

Current status:
OLMEC_STYLE_RELIGIOUS_LANDSCAPE_EVIDENCE.

Guard:

OXTOTITLAN != HEARTLAND_OLMEC_DOCTRINE.

## 10. Mother-culture control

Recent scholarship continues to document the long-running "mother culture"
versus "sister cultures" debate.

ON_THEO does not adopt either as a default causal model.

Required relation:

WIDESPREAD_OLMEC_STYLE
!=
ALL_LATER_MESOAMERICAN_RELIGION_DERIVED_DIRECTLY_FROM_OLMEC.

Transmission requires:
- chronology;
- contact mechanism;
- directional evidence;
- intermediate forms;
- more than resemblance.

## 11. Comparison hold

No Olmec cell enters cross-tradition synthesis until:
- exact site/context is named;
- evidence class is explicit;
- iconographic interpretation is separated from material observation;
- later Mesoamerican analogy is marked separately;
- uncertainty remains visible.

Current status:

OLMEC_CROSS_TRADITION_COMPARISON = HOLD.

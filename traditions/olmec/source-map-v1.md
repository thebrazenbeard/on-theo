# Olmec Source Map V1

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE MAP / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. San Lorenzo Tenochtitlan — INAH

Working chronology:
- Ojochi/Bajío occupations ca. 1500-1200 BCE;
- major population growth and monumental phase ca. 1200-850 BCE;
- major decline beginning ca. 900-850 BCE.

Secure material topics:
- large-scale landscape modification;
- residential terraces;
- maize intensification;
- imported volcanic stone;
- colossal heads;
- thrones;
- settlement hierarchy;
- long-distance transport.

Sources:
https://www.inah.gob.mx/zonas/186-zona-arqueologica-de-san-lorenzo-tenochtitlan
https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/es/node/4454

Evidence:
MATERIAL EVIDENCE / ARCHAEOLOGICAL RECONSTRUCTION.

## 3. Cascajal Block

Object:
incised serpentine/greenstone block with 62 signs.

Working date in published research:
late Early Formative / transition toward Middle Formative, approximately
1000-800 or 900-500 BCE depending study.

Current status:
- authenticity increasingly supported;
- possible/likely writing debated in detail;
- script undeciphered;
- no readable theological propositions.

Sources:
Rodriguez Martinez & Ortiz C., INAH Arqueologia 36;
Englehardt et al., Ancient Mesoamerica 2020;
Houston, Oxford Handbook of the Olmecs 2025.

Evidence:
MATERIAL EVIDENCE / POSSIBLE WRITING / SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.

# Olmec Material and Iconographic Foundation Packet V1

Status: LOW-TEXTUAL-CERTAINTY INTERNAL RECONSTRUCTION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish what can responsibly be said about Olmec religious/political order
from archaeology and iconography before comparison.

## 1. Olmec evidence is archaeological first

The strongest source base is:
- site layout;
- monumental sculpture;
- offering deposits;
- imported ritual materials;
- architecture;
- rock art;
- settlement patterns;
- archaeometric provenance.

This means religious reconstruction starts from:

ACTION_AND_CONTEXT

not

DOCTRINAL_TEXT.

## 2. San Lorenzo demonstrates early centralized monumental power

INAH describes San Lorenzo as a major Olmec center with:
- large population;
- engineered landscape;
- imported volcanic stone;
- colossal heads;
- thrones;
- transport control;
- intensive productive organization.

Current bounded proposition:

SAN_LORENZO_SUPPORTS_COMPLEX_ELITE_POLITICAL_AUTHORITY_WITH_MONUMENTAL_PUBLIC_REPRESENTATION.

Religious/sacral authority remains:
PARTIAL.

No "priest-king" title is admitted.

## 3. La Venta provides the strongest ritual-deposition lane

La Venta's ceremonial precinct and Complex A provide direct evidence for:
- planned sacred/civic architecture;
- repeated offerings;
- large-scale buried serpentine deposits;
- imported jade;
- hematite mirrors;
- figurines;
- ceramics;
- monumental sculpture.

Current axis:

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_MATERIAL_LA_VENTA_LAYER.

Mechanisms:
- STRUCTURED_DEPOSITION;
- CEREMONIAL_SPACE;
- MATERIAL_SELECTION;
- LONG_DISTANCE_RITUAL_MATERIAL_MOBILIZATION.

## 4. Offering is secure; exchange theology is not

The deposits are unquestionably deliberate ritual/ceremonial contexts in
current archaeological interpretation.

But:

OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_ONLY.

Missing:
- named recipient;
- exact requested outcome;
- spoken formula;
- explicit reciprocal theory.

The project will not invent "gifts to gods" when the material only establishes
deposition.

## 5. Monumental imagery communicates authority

INAH's La Venta museum describes monumental sculpture as:
- official;
- structured visual communication;
- organized in repeated thematic groups;
- tied spatially to architecture and city entrances.

Colossal heads are interpreted as male human rulers/leaders with insignia of
power.

Current proposition:

MONUMENTAL_VISUAL_COMMUNICATION_CAN_PUBLICLY_ENCODE_ELITE_AUTHORITY.

Current axis:

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL =
PARTIAL_MATERIAL_AUTHORITY_LAYER.

Why only PARTIAL:
sacralization of rulers is not independently explicit enough yet.

## 6. Composite beings are real images; their names are not

Olmec sculpture repeatedly combines:
- human traits;
- feline;
- saurian;
- avian;
- other animal features.

Secure proposition:

COMPOSITE_HUMAN_ANIMAL_SUPERNATURAL_OR_OTHERWORLDLY_IMAGERY_IS_RECURRENT.

But modern labels for specific beings are interpretations.

Therefore:

COMPOSITE_IMAGE != NAMED_DEITY_PROVED.

## 7. Greenstone and maize/fertility interpretations

Met curatorial research interprets selected jadeite celts as:
- ceremonial/votive rather than utilitarian;
- sometimes deposited in dedicatory caches;
- visually/materially associated in scholarship with maize sprouts,
  agricultural fertility, and community growth.

Current status:
SCHOLARLY ICONOGRAPHIC INTERPRETATION.

This can support:

RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL candidate
around
MATERIAL_AGRICULTURE_COMMUNITY.

It cannot yet establish:
OLMEC_MAIZE_THEOLOGY as a doctrine.

## 8. Cave/water/fertility imagery

Oxtotitlan cave painting provides:
- cave sacred-space context;
- Olmec-affiliated imagery;
- water/rain/fertility interpretation;
- human/animal composite imagery.

Current axis:

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_OLMEC_STYLE_CAVE_WATER_LAYER.

This is outside the Gulf Coast heartland and therefore cannot be used as
unqualified heartland doctrine.

## 9. Writing does not solve the theology gap yet

Cascajal is potentially revolutionary for Olmec writing history.

But because it is undeciphered:

CASC AJAL =
WRITING_OR_SIGN_SYSTEM_EVIDENCE,

not:

READABLE_BELIEF_STATEMENT.

Current axis consequence:

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL =
NOT_ADMITTED_FROM_WRITING.

TEXTUAL_THEOLOGY =
NOT_ADMITTED.

## 10. Mother-culture inference is prohibited

Olmec visual forms appear widely and later Mesoamerican traditions can share:
- greenstone;
- maize symbolism;
- mountain/cave/water relations;
- ruler monumentality;
- sacred calendars;
- composite beings.

None of those similarities alone establish:

LATER_RELIGION_DERIVED_FROM_OLMEC.

Current relation:

OLMEC_INTERACTION_AND_LEGACY =
OPEN_NETWORK_CAUSAL_QUESTION.

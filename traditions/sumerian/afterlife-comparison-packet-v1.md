Status: BOUNDED SYNTHESIS OF EXACT-HEAD DEEP SOURCE WORK / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

The Sumerian master branch is a navigation layer. This packet does not replace
the deep research chain.

It extracts only those propositions that are mature enough for the neutral
comparison framework and points back to exact successor heads.

## 1. Human death is not the same evidence problem as divine descent

The deep funerary packet establishes the controlling distinction:

`DIVINE_NETHERWORLD_NARRATIVE`
!=
`HUMAN_FUNERARY_PRACTICE`
!=
`ROYAL_MORTUARY_IDEOLOGY`
!=
`FAMILY_GHOST_CULT`
!=
`UNIVERSAL_AFTERLIFE_DOCTRINE`.

That distinction is comparison-critical.

Inana's descent can illuminate divine vulnerability, restoration, underworld
authority, and mythic substitution. It is not a human afterlife manual.

## 2. Human afterlife is socially differentiated

The controlled human-death lane supports a bounded reconstruction in which the
dead can remain socially situated after death.

Different literary cases make relevant:
- descendants;
- funerary offerings;
- circumstances of death;
- memory;
- royal status;
- burial/provisioning.

In *Gilgamesh, Enkidu and the Netherworld*, the condition of the dead varies
with factors including descendants and funerary offerings.

The neglected dead are represented differently from the well-provisioned dead.

In *The Death of Gilgamesh*, exceptional royal postmortem status is represented,
but the packet explicitly forbids treating royal office as the default fate of
ordinary dead.

In *The Death of Ur-Namma*, a royal netherworld itinerary includes offerings,
gatekeepers, divine authorities, and differentiated status.

Those literary witnesses are later than the historical kings they depict.

Therefore:

`ROYAL_LITERARY_AFTERLIFE`
!=
`CONTEMPORARY_FUNERAL_REPORT`.

## 3. Archaeology constrains the literary picture

At Ur, more than 2,000 graves were excavated while only a small subset received
Woolley's "royal" classification.

The deep packet therefore treats elite retainer burial and extraordinary grave
wealth as exceptional, not as the default Sumerian funeral.

At least some examined retainers show evidence consistent with violent killing,
but two CT-examined heads do not establish one killing mechanism for every
retainer.

The resulting comparison model must preserve social stratification.

`ELITE_MORTUARY_DISPLAY`
!=
`ORDINARY_FUNERARY_DEFAULT`.

## 4. The living-dead relationship deserves its own axis

The evidence is not exhausted by asking "where do people go after death?"

The Sumerian material asks:
- who cares for the dead?
- do descendants matter?
- do food/water offerings matter?
- how is neglect represented?
- how is memory maintained?
- what social role can a dead person continue to occupy?

This motivates:

`MORTUARY_RELATIONSHIP_MODEL`.

Neutral question:

> How are the dead related to the living after death through kinship, memory,
> offerings, ritual maintenance, social identity, protection, danger, or
> obligation?

This axis is broader than `POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL`.

## 5. Lexical control prevents a fake unified underworld

The deep lexical chain demonstrates that English "underworld" collapses several
different Sumerian terms.

### gidim

The controlled semantic core is:
- deceased-person spirit / ghost.

It is a person-category, not a place-category.

### kur

`kur` is polysemous.

Depending on context it can refer to:
- land/country;
- mountains;
- foreign region;
- underworld/death realm.

Even *The Death of Ur-Namma* uses the same lexeme in distinct local senses.

Therefore:

`KUR = UNDERWORLD`
as a global replacement rule is invalid.

### Arali

Arali can function as a marked/named death-realm place in controlled death
compositions.

It is not automatically the universal Sumerian name for the entire underworld.

### Ganzer

Where kur and Ganzer co-occur they are compositionally distinct.

Ganzer functions more specifically as a locus/gate/palace-like location in the
death-realm scene.

### kurnugi

The composition-level search found no `kurnugi` occurrence in the four core
ETCSL transliterations tested.

Absence there does not mean the lexeme never existed elsewhere.

It does block inserting it into those texts merely because English translations
say "underworld."

## 6. Dumuzid: death, capture, lament, and substitution

The deep Dumuzid chain establishes several source propositions without reducing
them to a generic "dying god" template.

*Dumuzid's Dream*:
- anticipates Dumuzid's death;
- uses galla pursuers/captors;
- places Arali in capture/death topography;
- has a large but uneven witness tradition.

*Inana and Bilulu*:
- uses "house Arali" in lament geography;
- connects lament, resting-place care, libation/provisioning, and the absent/dead
  Dumuzid.

The substitution packet directly supports:
- galla capture;
- Inana handing Dumuzid over;
- substitution language;
- Geshtin-ana's role;
- escape/transformative sequences.

It does not establish ordinary human afterlife substitution.

## 7. Half-year alternation is stronger than the vegetation theory

The hostile seasonal test separates propositions that popular summaries often
collapse.

Directly supported:
- Dumuzid / sister half-year alternation in *Inana's Descent*;
- substitution mechanics;
- death/capture/lament;
- shepherding and royal associations.

Not textually explicit:
- Dumuzid literally is vegetation;
- crops die because he descends;
- crops revive because he rises;
- the return is an annual resurrection in the strong "dying-and-rising god"
  sense.

Current ceiling:

`HALF_YEAR_ALTERNATION = PRIMARY_TEXT_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`.

`SEASONAL_CORRELATION = PLAUSIBLE_SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`.

`DUMUZID_IS_SIMPLY_VEGETATION_AND_RESURRECTS_ANNUALLY = NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

## 8. Divine kingship and political sacralization

The source-verification lane confirms that the Sumerian King List explicitly
frames kingship as descending from heaven, includes an antediluvian sequence,
marks a flood break, and then has kingship descend again.

Enlil traditions also associate divine authority with the granting of kingship.

This motivates a neutral axis:

`POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL`.

Question:

> How is political authority legitimized, granted, inherited, ritually
> maintained, cosmically ordered, divinely authorized, or contested?

The Sumerian evidence does not mean every historical ruler was regarded in one
identical way.

It establishes that political authority and divine/cosmic order are directly
linked in important source traditions.

## 9. Temple/city/deity/institutional structure

The source-verification and material-evidence lanes jointly support:
- deity patronage tied to cities;
- temples as divine residences and administrative/economic institutions;
- kingship/religious administration linkage;
- monumental and domestic cult contexts;
- city and period variation.

The archaeology packet forces a strong rule:

`TEXTUAL_DIVINE_ASSOCIATION`
!=
`EXCAVATED_CULT_INSTALLATION`
!=
`BUILDING_FUNCTION_PROVED`.

This strengthens:
- INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL;

## 10. Dumuzi -> Baal transmission remains unproven

The later transmission chain is unusually valuable because it already applies
the sort of gate ON_THEO wants across the whole repository.

The controlled record supports:
- east-to-west chronological possibility;
- regional contact;
- Mesopotamian scribal presence at Ugarit;
- direct Dumuzi scholastic exposure at Ugarit;
- named scholarly/carrier-network capacity.

But the current record does not establish:
- exposure to a source-specific Dumuzid death-return narrative;
- a distinctive Dumuzid sequence inside the Baal Cycle;
- a lexical calque;
- a source note;
- a carrier tied to the relevant narrative source.

The Baal Cycle's controlled mechanism also lacks several distinctive Dumuzid
features:
- substitution for another deity;
- galla pursuit;
- sister exchange;
- half-year alternation.

Current exact conclusion:

`DIRECT_DUMUZID_TO_BAAL_BORROWING = NONE_ESTABLISHED`.

This means "not established by current controlled evidence," not "impossible."

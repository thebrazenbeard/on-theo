## Astronomy/astrology disciplinary split

Ibn Sīnā's classification of sciences distinguishes:
- mathematical astronomy / ʿilm al-hayʾa as an exact mathematical science;
- astrology as belonging to natural philosophy.

ISMI notes that this demarcation appears innovative relative to much earlier Greek and Islamic classification and becomes influential after Ibn Sīnā.

Sources:
https://ismi.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/text/90291
https://www.mcgill.ca/islamicstudies/files/islamicstudies/ragep-ibnsina-astronomy2004.pdf

This is a major historical development:

ASTRONOMY_AND_ASTROLOGY can share celestial subject matter while belonging to different epistemic classes.

## Nature of the critique

Secondary scholarship commonly reconstructs Ibn Sīnā's position as more subtle than:

THE_STARS_HAVE_NO_EFFECT.

The stronger supported formulation is:

Even granting a natural-philosophical celestial causal order, astrologers lack sufficient knowledge to infer exact individual terrestrial outcomes reliably.

The prediction problem includes:
- complexity of causes;
- interactions among celestial and terrestrial conditions;
- limits of human knowledge;
- unjustified transition from general celestial influence to precise judicial prediction.

Because a complete primary-text translation has not yet been bound in this branch, this formulation remains SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION pending line-level extraction.

## Epistemic contrast with Abū Maʿshar

Abū Maʿshar builds a positive defense:
celestial causes + experience + analogy -> astrology as science/craft.

Ibn Sīnā challenges the inference from:
general celestial causal structure -> astrologer's exact judgment.

This gives a clean historical disagreement over identifiability.

Modern systems analogy:

A SYSTEM MAY HAVE HIDDEN CAUSES
while
AN OBSERVER MAY LACK ENOUGH INFORMATION TO INFER THEM.

That is a PROJECT_ANALOGY, not Ibn Sīnā's terminology.

## Simulation relevance

The hidden-common-cause model faces an Ibn-Sīnā-style problem.

Even if:
H -> celestial state
and
H -> human state,

an astrologer does not thereby know:
- H;
- the mapping function;
- its interaction terms;
- its noise;
- its local exceptions.

So:

TRUE_HIDDEN_COUPLING != HUMANLY_DECODABLE_ASTROLOGY.

This is an important guard against making the simulation model unfalsifiably generous.

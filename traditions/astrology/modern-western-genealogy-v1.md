## Sun-sign simplification

Modern popular astrology often compresses a complex natal system into one variable:
the Sun's zodiac sign.

This is historically unusual relative to:
- Hellenistic multi-factor charts;
- medieval Arabic techniques;
- Indian horoscopy.

Newspaper columns helped make this simplification culturally dominant.

R. H. Naylor's 1930 Sunday Express work around Princess Margaret is an important popular milestone.

Orientation:
https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/how-are-horoscopes-still-thing-180957701/

However, newer specialist historical work argues that Naylor was not literally the first person to create every component of Sun-sign forecasting; earlier late-nineteenth/early-twentieth-century precedents require mapping.

Research frontier:
Philip Graves, Technical & Planetary Developments in Astrology, including a dedicated historical study of Sun-sign forecasting and newspaper astrology:
https://revelore.press/product/philip-graves-technical-planetary-developments-in-astrology/

Guard:

NAYLOR_1930 = IMPORTANT_MASS_MEDIA_MILESTONE
not automatically
NAYLOR_INVENTED_SUN_SIGN_ASTROLOGY_FROM_NOTHING.

## Dane Rudhyar

Dane Rudhyar's The Astrology of Personality (1936) is a central source for humanistic/psychological astrology.

Rudhyar later described his approach as humanistic, person-centered, and eventually transpersonal astrology.

Primary retrospective:
https://www.humanisticastrology.com/rudhyar/fromhtot_1.shtml

A recent cultural history identifies Alan Leo, Rudhyar, and Liz Greene as major figures in twentieth-century psychological astrology.

Laura Andrikopoulos, Jung and Twentieth Century Psychological Astrology (Routledge):
https://www.routledge.com/link/link/p/book/9781032749891

A 2024 peer-reviewed article likewise traces modern psychological astrology through Theosophy, Jung, and later New Age contexts:
DOI: 10.1163/19409060-bja10035

## Jung and astrology

Carl Jung used astrological material within his broader investigations of:
- archetypes;
- synchronicity;
- symbolic systems.

Research guard:

JUNG_STUDIED_OR_USED_ASTROLOGY != JUNG_EMPIRICALLY_VALIDATED_ASTROLOGY.

The branch should separately track:
- Jung's own claims;
- astrologers' later appropriations of Jung;
- psychological astrology as a cultural movement.

## Liz Greene and late-century psychological astrology

Liz Greene and associated institutions helped consolidate explicitly Jungian/psychological forms of astrology in the later twentieth century.

This is a modern interpretive lineage, not a direct continuation of Ptolemaic physical causation.

## Traditional/Hellenistic revival

Late twentieth- and early twenty-first-century astrology also contains a reverse movement:
recovering older Hellenistic, Persian/Arabic, and medieval techniques.

This means “modern astrology” now contains competing projects:
- Sun-sign/pop astrology;
- psychological/humanistic astrology;
- evolutionary/spiritual astrology;
- traditional/Hellenistic revival;
- sidereal Western systems;
- modern predictive practice.

## Digital astrology

Software and online ephemerides alter the economics of technique.

Computations once requiring:
- tables;
- logarithms;
- manual interpolation;
- substantial training

can now be generated instantly.

That may produce a paradox:
modern popular astrology can be technically more computationally detailed than newspaper astrology while still using modern interpretive assumptions.

The addressee is described differently in modern editions and scholarship:

## P1 — Astronomy is real knowledge

Maimonides distinguishes legitimate astronomical science from judicial astrology.

The legitimate science concerns:
- celestial spheres;
- motions and periods;
- declinations;
- rising and setting;
- eclipses;
- lunar phases;
- day length and latitude effects.

Historical translation witness:
https://oxfordastrologer.com/2017/11/maimonides-letter-astrology.html

Classification:
PRIMARY_TEXT through transmitted translation; translation wording requires edition control.

Proposition:

MAIMONIDES_REJECTS_ASTROLOGY
does not imply
MAIMONIDES_REJECTS_ASTRONOMY.

## P2 — Astrology is not established science

Maimonides rejects claims that:
- impending events can be reliably forecast from stellar motions;
- a natal constellation fixes an individual's life course.

His epistemic framework, as summarized in Friedländer's introduction to the Guide, accepts beliefs grounded in:
- rational demonstration;
- sense perception;
- trustworthy authority.

Astrological judgment fails this standard in his view.

Source:
https://ccel.org/ccel/maimonides/guide/guide.iv.i.html

Classification:
PRIMARY_TEXT + SCHOLARLY/TRANSLATOR_SUMMARY.

## P3 — Free will is a decisive theological objection

The letter explicitly presents human action as genuinely open to choice.

Astrological natal determination would undermine:
- commandment;
- prohibition;
- moral responsibility;
- Torah as practical instruction.

This argument is not merely:

ASTROLOGY_IS_FORBIDDEN.

It is:

HARD_NATAL_DETERMINISM contradicts a foundational commitment to meaningful human choice.

Related Maimonidean free-will discussion:
https://ccel.org/ccel/maimonides/guide.vii.xviii.html

## P4 — Antiquity and popularity are not evidence

Maimonides warns against treating a claim as true because:
- it appears in old books;
- many authors wrote about it;
- a large learned tradition accepts it.

This is methodologically important to on-theo.

Project abstraction:

TEXTUAL_AGE + CULTURAL_PRESTIGE != EVIDENTIARY_VALIDITY.

## P5 — Talmudic pro-astrology passages do not override demonstration

Maimonides treats apparently favorable rabbinic astrology passages as insufficient to overturn stronger rational principles.

Possible handling includes:
- minority opinion;
- nonliteral/allegorical interpretation;
- contextual limitation.

Guard:

LATER_CANONICAL_PRESENCE != NECESSARY_DOCTRINAL_ENDORSEMENT.

## Historical comparison

Maimonides is not representative of all medieval Jewish astrology.

Abraham ibn Ezra treated astrology as intellectually serious and became a major Jewish transmitter of Arabic astral science.

Therefore:

MEDIEVAL_JEWISH_POSITION_ON_ASTROLOGY = PLURAL.

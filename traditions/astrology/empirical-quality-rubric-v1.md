# Astrology Empirical Evidence Quality Rubric V1

Status: METHODOLOGICAL CONTROL

## Purpose

Astrology research is unusually vulnerable to two opposite errors:

- accepting a weak positive result because the topic is culturally compelling;
- treating one weak or narrow null result as a universal disproof.

This rubric scores the design, not whether the result favors or disfavors astrology.

## Q1 — Proposition specificity

PASS requires an exact prediction fixed before outcome inspection.

Bad:
“the chart describes personality.”

Better:
“under system X, blinded astrologers should match natal charts to independently measured trait profiles above the preregistered null rate.”

## Q2 — Tradition lock

The test must name:
- tropical/sidereal frame;
- house system if relevant;
- planetary/object set;
- aspect definitions/orbs;
- timing technique;
- interpretive rules.

A test of one school does not become a test of all astrology.

## Q3 — Blinding

Record separately:
- participant blinding;
- astrologer blinding;
- analyst blinding.

Birth date, age, sex, occupation, questionnaire language, or other clues can leak identity or expected outcomes.

## Q4 — Pre-registration

Preferred:
- hypotheses fixed before analysis;
- exclusion rules fixed;
- scoring rule fixed;
- primary endpoint fixed;
- correction for multiple testing fixed.

Exploratory analysis is allowed but must remain labeled exploratory.

## Q5 — Sample adequacy

Record:
- sample size;
- expected effect size;
- power analysis;
- attrition;
- subgroup sizes.

A tiny sample cannot become strong evidence through a dramatic p-value alone.

## Q6 — Outcome validity

Psychological inventories, biographies, vocational profiles, medical outcomes, and life events measure different constructs.

Failure to match one instrument does not prove failure to match every possible target.

Conversely, a vague autobiographical narrative invites subjective fitting.

## Q7 — Null model quality

At minimum compare against:
- chance/shuffled charts;
- demographic baselines;
- seasonality;
- age/sex/geography;
- simple non-astrological prediction rules.

For celestial-mechanism hypotheses additionally compare against:
- raw astronomical variables;
- physical-force variables;
- environmental cycles.

## Q8 — Independence

Higher weight:
- independent data collection;
- independent analysts;
- independent replication.

Lower weight:
- same dataset repeatedly reanalyzed;
- post hoc subgroup construction;
- investigator-defined eminence or inclusion rules after results are known.

## Q9 — Effect size and calibration

Require:
- effect size;
- uncertainty interval;
- not merely p < .05;
- out-of-sample discrimination where possible.

A statistically detectable but tiny effect is different from practical predictive accuracy.

## Q10 — Data transparency

Preferred:
- raw or de-identified data available;
- code available;
- exact ephemeris/chart-generation rules;
- audit trail for exclusions.

## Q11 — Replication

Classify:
- UNREPLICATED;
- SAME_TEAM_REPLICATION;
- PARTIAL_INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION;
- INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION;
- FAILED_REPLICATION;
- MIXED;
- DISPUTED_METHOD.

## Q12 — Mechanism separation

Even a robust correlation does not establish:
- gravity;
- magnetism;
- radiation;
- divine causation;
- synchronicity;
- simulation/controller causation.

Mechanism requires separate discriminating tests.

## Evidence grades

A:
Preregistered, adequately powered, blinded where relevant, transparent, independently replicated.

B:
Strong controlled design with one material limitation or replication not yet independent.

C:
Useful but limited observational or experimental evidence with multiple unresolved confounds.

D:
Weak exploratory evidence, poorly controlled, underpowered, or heavily post hoc.

E:
Anecdotal/testimonial/self-validation only.

DISPUTED:
The data/result depends materially on unresolved analysis, selection, or protocol disagreement.

## Interpretation rule

No grade carries a truth verdict for astrology as a whole.

The grade applies to:

EXACT_PROPOSITION + EXACT_DATASET + EXACT_PROTOCOL + EXACT_ANALYSIS.

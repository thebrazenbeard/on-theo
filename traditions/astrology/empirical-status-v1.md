# Astrology Empirical Status V1

Status: FOUNDATION / CLAIM-SPECIFIC

## Principle

“Does astrology work?” is usually too vague to be a scientific question.

A valid test must specify:
- which astrological tradition;
- which technique;
- which chart variables;
- which predicted outcome;
- who interprets the chart;
- how success is scored;
- what the null model is;
- what data were excluded and why;
- whether the protocol was fixed before results were examined.

## Evidence reviewed in this foundation

### Carlson 1985

Shawn Carlson, “A double-blind test of astrology,” Nature 318, 419–425.

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1038/318419a0

Design:
- double-blind tests;
- natal charts;
- personality descriptions/profiles;
- professional astrologers.

Importance:
- one of the most famous controlled tests of natal astrology.

Control:
- preserve the original result and later methodological/statistical criticism separately;
- do not convert one study into “all possible astrology has been disproved.”

### Hartmann, Reuter, Nyborg 2006

“The relationship between date of birth and individual differences in personality and general intelligence: A large-scale study.”

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2005.11.017

Samples:
- more than 4,000 middle-aged men;
- more than 11,000 young adults.

Reported result:
- no relationship found between date of birth and tested personality/intelligence differences;
- no support found for tested popular Sun-sign, element, or astrological-gender associations.

Interpretation ceiling:
This is strong evidence against those tested broad birth-date/Sun-sign personality propositions.

It does not directly test every full-chart technique.

### Twin studies

A 1992 study reported a positive astrological discrimination result in twins.

A 1996 reanalysis/replication paper:

https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(96)00112-2

reported:
- non-replication;
- problems with the original reported data;
- response-set/stereotype artifacts;
- no significant deviation from chance in reanalysis.

Use:
This is a useful case study in why apparent positive results require raw-data review and replication.

### Barnum / Forer-type effects

Dickson and Kelly 1985 review:

https://doi.org/10.2466/pr0.1985.57.2.367

The Barnum effect is the tendency to accept broad, favorable, or ambiguously general personality descriptions as uniquely descriptive.

Relevance:
- self-validation can inflate perceived horoscope accuracy.

Guard:
BARNUM_EFFECT_EXISTS != EVERY_ASTROLOGICAL_CLAIM_IS_EXPLAINED_BY_BARNUM_EFFECT.

Specific, preregistered, discriminative predictions still require direct testing.

## Current bounded assessment

The evidence reviewed here does not establish reliable Sun-sign or natal-chart prediction of personality.

Several prominent controlled and large-sample studies report null or non-replicating results.

The research lane should therefore treat predictive validity as UNESTABLISHED unless a narrower proposition has direct reproducible evidence.

Historical significance, subjective usefulness, symbolic value, and empirical predictive validity are separate propositions.

## Mechanism problem

Even a reproducible correlation would not identify its cause.

Competing mechanism classes include:

### M0 — no real celestial effect

Apparent accuracy arises from:
- chance;
- vague language;
- selective memory;
- confirmation bias;
- stereotype effects;
- social learning;
- practitioner cueing;
- publication/selection effects.

### M1 — ordinary physical influence

Candidate variables would need explicit measurable channels, for example:
- gravitational acceleration;
- tidal gradient;
- electromagnetic environment;
- solar/lunar illumination;
- seasonal/environmental pathways.

A mechanism cannot be rescued by merely naming a force. Its predicted magnitude, timing, direction, dose-response, and biological pathway must fit observations.

### M2 — indirect environmental mediation

Birth season or celestial cycles correlate with terrestrial variables such as:
- photoperiod;
- temperature;
- nutrition;
- infection;
- social scheduling.

This could produce birth-date correlations without validating astrology’s interpretive system.

### M3 — unknown physical coupling

A new mechanism could in principle be proposed, but it must generate predictions that outperform existing models and survive independent replication.

### M4 — hidden common cause / simulation controller

Celestial configuration and human state are both conditioned by a hidden process.

Within on-theo this is a SPECULATIVE_MODEL, not an evidentiary inference.

It has an important logical consequence:

absence of a plausible gravitational mechanism would not logically disprove a hidden-common-cause model.

But the hidden-common-cause model gains no empirical support merely by being able to evade the gravitational objection.

It still requires distinctive evidence.

## Direct-gravity test architecture

If the hypothesis is literally “gravity from celestial bodies modulates human cognition/personality,” preregister quantities such as:

- gravitational acceleration contribution: proportional to GM/r²;
- tidal contribution across a body/brain scale: proportional to GM/r³;
- vector direction;
- time dependence;
- local terrestrial gravitational confounds.

Then compare those continuous physical variables against:
- standard astrological sign boundaries;
- aspects;
- houses;
- planetary identities;
- exact behavioral/physiological outcomes.

Key discriminant:

If categorical astrological variables predict outcomes while physically relevant force magnitudes do not, ordinary gravity becomes a poor causal explanation even if an astrological correlation exists.

## Stronger future test program

1. Freeze one astrological system and interpreter rule set.
2. Precompute charts from a blinded ephemeris pipeline.
3. Pre-register exact predictions before outcome access.
4. Use large holdout samples.
5. Separate discovery from confirmation.
6. Compare against demographic, seasonal, geographic, and psychological baselines.
7. Include negative controls and shuffled birth data.
8. Measure effect size, calibration, discrimination, and out-of-sample accuracy.
9. Replicate independently.
10. Only after replication compare candidate mechanisms.

## Research frontier

The branch should not waste effort arguing “astrology versus skepticism” in the abstract.

It should turn individual astrological propositions into explicit models that can lose.

# Astrology Technical System Map V1

Status: FOUNDATION / COMPARATIVE TECHNICAL CONTROL

This document defines comparison axes. It does not endorse the validity of any system.

## 1. Sky frame

### Tropical zodiac

A twelve-sign coordinate system tied to the equinox/seasonal reference rather than to current IAU constellation boundaries.

Each sign occupies 30 degrees of ecliptic longitude.

### Sidereal zodiac

A twelve-sign system referenced to a stellar framework with an offset/ayanāṃśa relative to the tropical reference.

Different sidereal schools can use different ayanāṃśas.

### Astronomical constellations

Modern constellation boundaries are unequal regions of sky.

They are not identical to twelve equal astrological signs.

The Sun’s apparent ecliptic path also crosses the modern constellation Ophiuchus.

Guard:

SIGN != CONSTELLATION.

### Lunar mansions / lodges

Systems divide the lunar path into recurring stellar sectors.

Examples:
- Indian nakṣatras;
- Chinese twenty-eight lunar lodges.

Similar counts do not by themselves prove a single origin.

## 2. Celestial objects

Potential objects include:

- Mercury;
- Venus;
- Mars;
- Jupiter;
- Saturn;
- lunar nodes;
- fixed stars;
- lots/parts calculated from other chart points;
- Uranus, Neptune, Pluto in modern Western systems;
- asteroids and hypothetical points in some modern systems.

Historical rule:

Never project a modern object set backward into a period before the relevant planet/object was known or used.

## 3. Zodiacal signs / rāśis

Compare:
- names;
- boundaries;
- tropical/sidereal reference;
- elemental or qualitative associations;
- rulership;
- exaltation;
- gender/sect/other classifications;
- transmission history.

Do not infer technical equivalence from translated names alone.

## 4. Houses / places / bhāvas

Twelve-place systems map chart sectors to terrestrial or life domains.

Research variables:
- whole-sign places;
- quadrant systems;
- equal-house systems;
- Indian bhāva calculations;
- historical origin and transmission;
- whether house meaning or geometry changed across schools.

Guard:

TWELVE_SIGNS != TWELVE_HOUSES.

## 5. Aspects and angular relations

Typical Western/Hellenistic relations:
- conjunction;
- opposition;
- trine;
- square;
- sextile.

But aspect doctrine varies by period and school.

Research:
- geometric basis;
- sign-based versus degree-based aspects;
- applying/separating logic;
- planetary visibility and phase;
- Indian dṛṣṭi systems and their distinct rules.

## 8. Chart classes

### Natal / genethlialogical / jātaka

Chart tied to birth.

Possible targets:
- temperament;
- health;
- social status;
- profession;
- relationships;
- lifespan;
- major life periods.

### Mundane / political

Charts or celestial cycles interpreted for:
- states;
- rulers;
- wars;
- economies;
- dynasties;
- weather or collective events.

### Electional / catarchic / muhūrta

Select a future time judged auspicious for beginning an action.

### Horary / interrogational / praśna

Use a chart associated with a specific question to judge the answer/outcome.

### Medical

Historically maps celestial timing/configuration onto health, disease course, treatment timing, or body correspondences.

This branch studies medical astrology historically and empirically; it is not medical guidance.

### Magical / talismanic / theurgical

Uses selected celestial configurations in ritual or material operations intended to draw, redirect, or participate in astral influence.

## 9. Causal models

Do not collapse different historical explanations.

### Sign/omen model

Celestial event signifies rather than mechanically causes the terrestrial event.

### Natural-causation model

Celestial bodies exert physical/natural qualities on the sublunar world.

### Sympathy/correspondence model

Macrocosm and microcosm share structured correspondences.

### Divine-message model

Celestial patterns communicate providential or divine information.

### Deterministic model

Celestial configuration fixes outcomes.

### Inclination/probabilistic model

Celestial influences condition tendencies while leaving room for contingency or agency.

### Symbolic/psychological model

Chart functions as symbolic language, interpretive framework, or reflective tool rather than a literal celestial cause.

### Hidden-common-cause model

Celestial state and terrestrial state correlate because both are outputs of another process.

## 10. Coordinate-control questions

Every empirical test should record:
- exact UTC/local birth/event time;
- time-zone and historical daylight-saving rules;
- geodetic location;
- ephemeris source;
- reference frame;
- tropical or sidereal zodiac;
- ayanāṃśa if sidereal;
- house system;
- object set;
- aspect or dignity definitions;
- allowable orb;
- interpretation rules fixed before outcome inspection.

Without those, “test astrology” is often too underspecified to reproduce.

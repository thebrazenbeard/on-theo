## 9. Source hierarchy

For an accusation claim:

1. primary accuser source;
2. institutional context;
3. target identity from independent evidence;
4. interrogation/coercion assessment where relevant;
5. corroborating material/behavioral evidence;
6. modern historiography;
7. claim ceiling.

No hostile attribution may skip steps 2–5 and become a target-group belief.

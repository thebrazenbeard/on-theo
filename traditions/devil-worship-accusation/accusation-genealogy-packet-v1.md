# Devil-Worship Accusation Genealogy Packet V1

Status: HISTORICAL ATTRIBUTION GENEALOGY / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Map recurring devil-worship accusation mechanisms without claiming that all
episodes derive from one uninterrupted conspiracy stereotype.

## 1. The accusation can precede the religion

A central methodological fact is that people were accused of serving Satan long
before modern self-identified Satanic religions emerged.

Therefore:

SATANIST_STEREOTYPE_CAN_PRECEDE_SELF_IDENTIFIED_SATANISM.

This reverses the naïve historical assumption that accusation must report an
already-existing religion.

## 2. Religious-other mechanism

In polemical contexts, an opponent can be reclassified as:
- deceived by demons;
- allied with Satan;
- secretly worshipping the Devil;
- practicing an inverted religion.

Current mechanism:

RELIGIOUS_OTHER_DEMONIZATION.

The accusation describes the accuser's cosmology and boundary making first.

Target-group practice requires separate evidence.

## 3. Heresy-to-diabolical-conspiracy mechanism

Late medieval learned witchcraft theory drew together previously distinct
categories such as:
- heresy;
- malefic magic;
- superstition;
- demonic pact;
- demon worship;
- witches' assemblies.

Current mechanism:

COMPOSITE_DIABOLICAL_SECT_CONSTRUCTION.

This mechanism can transform scattered allegations of magic or heterodoxy into
a theory of organized satanic conspiracy.

## 4. Judicial feedback mechanism

Once a prosecutorial model defines expected features, interrogations can search
for:
- pact;
- sabbath;
- Devil;
- sexual inversion;
- sacrifice;
- secret organization.

Current source-critical risk:

MODEL_GUIDED_TESTIMONY_GENERATION.

Therefore confession/testimony must be tested for:
- question structure;
- coercion;
- formula;
- prior demonological template;
- independent corroboration.

## 5. Colonial translation mechanism

Christian missionaries encountering unfamiliar Indigenous religion can map:
- deity / sacred being -> demon;
- shrine / huaca -> demonic locus;
- specialist -> sorcerer;
- sacrifice/rite -> devil worship;
- religious persistence -> Satanic deception.

Current mechanism:

COLONIAL_DEMONOLOGICAL_TRANSLATION.

This can preserve real observations about:
- place;
- ritual;
- specialist office;
- offering;

while radically recoding their meaning.

Therefore:

OBSERVATION_LAYER
must be separated from
MISSIONARY_INTERPRETATION_LAYER.

## 6. Hidden-cult legend mechanism

The hidden-cult model typically combines:
- secrecy;
- inversion;
- sexual/moral transgression;
- sacrifice;
- organized hierarchy;
- infiltration;
- protection by powerful actors.

Such a pattern can recur as:
- folklore;
- moral panic;
- conspiracy theory.

Recurrence is not evidence for one historical underground organization.

## 7. Satanic Panic mechanism

Late twentieth-century ritual-abuse allegations reused many hidden-cult
features.

The FBI/NCJRS 1992 guide reported that the widespread alleged phenomenon could
not be documented and urged fact-centered investigation.

Scholarly literature commonly classifies the broader scare as a moral panic.

Current mechanism:

MODERN_HIDDEN_SATANIC_NETWORK_PANIC.

This is evidence for:
- rumor;
- investigative practice;
- media/religious discourse;
- therapeutic/legal history.

It is not by itself evidence for:
a large organized Satanic abuse religion.

## 8. Actual crime versus explanatory frame

A key guard:

FALSE_OR_UNSUPPORTED_SATANIC_FRAME
does not imply
NO_UNDERLYING_CRIME.

A case may involve:
- real abuse;
- real violence;
- real occult imagery;
- fabricated allegations;
- mistaken interpretation;
- combinations of these.

The project tests each independently.

## 9. Self-identification inversion

Modern Satanists can later appropriate:
- Satan;
- satanic imagery;
- adversarial language
that older sources used hostilely.

This creates:

ATTRIBUTION -> REAPPROPRIATION

as a possible historical sequence.

But:
later appropriation does not validate earlier accusations.

## 10. Strongest result

"DEVIL WORSHIP" IS BEST TREATED AS A PROVENANCE-SENSITIVE ATTRIBUTION CATEGORY
THAT HAS OPERATED THROUGH POLEMIC, DEMONOLOGY, PROSECUTION, COLONIAL
TRANSLATION, FOLKLORE AND MORAL PANIC, WHILE ACTUAL SELF-IDENTIFIED SATANIC OR
DEVOTIONAL PRACTICE MUST BE ESTABLISHED INDEPENDENTLY.

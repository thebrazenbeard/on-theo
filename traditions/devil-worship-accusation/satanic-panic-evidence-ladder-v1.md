# Satanic Panic Evidence Ladder V1

Status: LATE-TWENTIETH-CENTURY MORAL-PANIC / INVESTIGATIVE EVIDENCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Separate:
- allegations of abuse/crime;
- occult or satanic imagery;
- ritualized behavior;
- claims of organized Satanic religion;
- claims of a widespread coordinated Satanic network.

Core rule:

ABUSE_ALLEGATION
!=
SATANIC_CAUSATION
!=
ORGANIZED_SATANIC_RELIGION
!=
WIDESPREAD_CONSPIRATORIAL_NETWORK.

Each requires separate evidence.

## 1. Investigative control

Kenneth V. Lanning's 1992 FBI/NCJRS guide was written for investigators dealing
with allegations of "ritual" child abuse.

Its central methodological contribution is not:
"ignore allegations."

It is:
- investigate specific acts;
- distinguish crime evidence from interpretation;
- do not let occult/satanic labeling substitute for evidence;
- consider alternative explanations;
- the widespread alleged phenomenon had not been documented.

Current evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE / CONTEMPORARY LAW-ENFORCEMENT GUIDANCE.

## 2. Evidence ladder

### LEVEL 0 — CLAIM ONLY

A person, media source, therapist, religious source or investigator asserts:
- "Satanic";
- "ritual";
- "cult";
- "devil worship."

No independent behavioral corroboration.

Ceiling:
ALLEGATION_ONLY.

### LEVEL 1 — SYMBOLIC OR OCCULT MATERIAL

Evidence includes:
- satanic/occult symbols;
- books;
- costumes;
- ritualized language;
- unusual ceremonial elements.

Supports:
SYMBOLIC_OR_RITUAL_CONTEXT.

Does not establish:
- abuse;
- literal belief;
- organized cult;
- network.

### LEVEL 2 — INDEPENDENT CRIMINAL OR ABUSE EVIDENCE

Evidence independently supports:
- assault;
- abuse;
- homicide;
- coercion;
- other crime.

Supports:
CRIME_OR_ABUSE_PROPOSITION.

Does not by itself establish satanic motivation.

### LEVEL 3 — SATANIC MOTIVATION / SELF-IDENTIFICATION

Independent evidence supports that an offender/group:
- self-identifies as Satanist/devil worshipper;
- explicitly invokes Satan as motive;
- treats criminal act as religious/devotional ritual.

Supports:
SOURCE_SPECIFIC_SATANIC_MOTIVE.

Does not establish:
- broader Satanism;
- organized movement;
- network.

### LEVEL 4 — ORGANIZED GROUP

Evidence supports:
- stable membership;
- coordination;
- repeated group practice;
- shared doctrine or motive;
- organizational continuity.

Supports:
SPECIFIC_ORGANIZED_GROUP.

Does not establish:
national/international conspiracy.

### LEVEL 5 — WIDESPREAD COORDINATED NETWORK

Requires:
- independently documented inter-group links;
- communications;
- finances/logistics;
- shared command/coordination;
- corroborated victims/events across sites;
- evidence independent of rumor contagion.

Current historical status for classic Satanic Panic claims:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 3. Ritual does not equal religion

A crime can be:
- repetitive;
- staged;
- ceremonial;
- symbolic
without being religious.

Therefore:

RITUALIZED_CRIME != RELIGIOUS_RITUAL_BY_DEFAULT.

And:
RELIGIOUS_RITUAL != CRIME.

## 4. Satanic label does not determine offender identity

A person can:
- use satanic symbols without being Satanist;
- claim Satanism idiosyncratically;
- belong to a Satanist movement with no connection to crime;
- commit crime without religious motive.

Therefore:

SYMBOL_USE_NE_MOVEMENT_MEMBERSHIP.

## 5. Suggestion / contamination control

Historical scholarship on the panic identifies risks including:
- leading interviews;
- repeated questioning;
- therapeutic suggestion;
- rumor/media transmission;
- investigator expectation;
- community moral crusade.

These mechanisms can affect:
- testimony;
- memory reports;
- interpretation;
- case aggregation.

They do not prove every allegation false.

They require:
TESTIMONY_PROVENANCE_CONTROL.

## 6. Moral-panic classification

Scholarly literature commonly analyzes the 1980s–1990s Satanic Ritual Abuse
scare as a moral panic because claims of a vast clandestine network expanded
far beyond what investigators could independently document.

Current classification:

SATANIC_PANIC_NETWORK_MODEL =
MORAL_PANIC / CONSPIRACY CLAIM HISTORY.

This classification concerns the broad network claim.

Specific crimes remain case-specific.

## 7. False binary guard

Invalid inference A:

WIDESPREAD_SATANIC_NETWORK_NOT_ESTABLISHED
->
NO_CHILD_ABUSE_OCCURRED.

Invalid inference B:

REAL_CHILD_ABUSE_OCCURRED
->
SATANIC_NETWORK_EXISTED.

Both are prohibited.

## 8. Relation to modern Satanism

Church of Satan and The Satanic Temple explicitly reject literal sacrificial
harm in current official materials.

The existence of self-identified theistic/esoteric Satanists does not establish
the Satanic Panic network.

Therefore:

MODERN_SATANISM
must not be populated from
SATANIC_PANIC_ALLEGATIONS.

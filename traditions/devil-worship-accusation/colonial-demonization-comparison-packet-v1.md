# Colonial Demonization Comparison Packet V1

Status: COLONIAL CONTACT / DEMONOLOGICAL TRANSLATION CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Compare colonial Christian demonization mechanisms without treating missionary
labels as transparent descriptions of Indigenous religion.

Controlled cases:
- New Spain / Nahua and other Indigenous religious difference;
- colonial Andes / huacas and ritual specialists.

Core rule:

MISSIONARY_DEVIL_LANGUAGE =
PRIMARY EVIDENCE FOR MISSIONARY INTERPRETATION.

It is not automatically:
PRIMARY EVIDENCE FOR INDIGENOUS DEVIL THEOLOGY.

## 1. New Spain

Scholarship on sixteenth-century evangelization in New Spain shows that
missionaries and chroniclers could use:
- Devil;
- demon;
- idolatry;
- satanic deception
as conceptual categories for Indigenous rites, sacrifice and sacred difference.

Current source-critical split:

OBSERVED_OR_REPORTED_INDIGENOUS_PRACTICE
!=
MISSIONARY_DEMONOLOGICAL_EXPLANATION.

A missionary may preserve real information while simultaneously changing its
religious semantics.

## 2. Colonial Andes

Missionary sources in Peru frequently interpreted:
- huacas;
- Indigenous divinities;
- ritual specialists;
- confession-like practices;
- offerings
through Christian demonological frameworks.

Current scholarship explicitly documents missionary efforts to frame huaca
communication as:
- deception;
- demonic influence;
- diabolic imitation.

Current classification:

HUACA_TO_DEVIL_TRANSLATION =
COLONIAL_DEMONOLOGICAL_RECODING.

This must remain separate from:
HUACA_SOURCE_INTERNAL_MEANING.

## 3. Semantic substitution

Colonial sources can perform mappings such as:

INDIGENOUS_SACRED_BEING -> DEMON
HUACA_OR_SHRINE -> IDOL_OR_DEMONIC_LOCUS
RITUAL_SPECIALIST -> SORCERER
OFFERING -> DEVIL_SERVICE
RELIGIOUS_PERSISTENCE -> SATANIC_DECEPTION

These are:

MISSIONARY_SEMANTIC_MAPPINGS.

They are historical data.

They are not lexically neutral translations.

## 4. Suppression feedback

Demonological translation can justify:
- extirpation;
- destruction of sacred objects/sites;
- removal or punishment of specialists;
- coerced instruction;
- interrogation.

Therefore:

DEMONIZATION
can become
INSTITUTIONAL_ACTION.

Current axes:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_HOSTILE_SEMANTIC_RECODING.

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL is not the proper comparison axis here.

The central mechanism is:
RELIGIOUS_OTHER_DEMONIZATION_UNDER_COLONIAL_POWER.

## 5. Two-way complexity

Indigenous actors may:
- resist demonological mapping;
- strategically translate;
- adopt some Christian categories;
- reinterpret them;
- combine them with local categories.

Therefore:

COLONIAL_DEMONIZATION != ONE_WAY_TOTAL_SEMANTIC_REPLACEMENT.

V1 does not claim that Indigenous communities simply accepted missionary Devil
theology.

## 6. Relation to existing ON_THEO lanes

Mexica/Nahua:
current living and historical religious evidence must not inherit missionary
"devil worship" labels.

Andean/Inca:
huaca/ceque and living Andean evidence remains independent of missionary
demonological classification.

This packet adds provenance controls; it does not revise those traditions'
source-internal propositions.

## 7. Comparative result

New Spain and the Andes share a mechanism:

CHRISTIAN_DEMONOLOGICAL_TRANSLATION_OF_RELIGIOUS_OTHERNESS.

But:

SHARED_COLONIAL_RECODING_MECHANISM
does not imply
IDENTICAL_INDIGENOUS_RELIGION.

It reflects shared missionary conceptual tools under related colonial systems.

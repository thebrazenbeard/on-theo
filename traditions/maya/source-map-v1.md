## 4. Maya Codex of Mexico

Custodian:
Biblioteca Nacional de Antropologia e Historia / INAH.

Working layer:
early Postclassic.

INAH interdisciplinary study dates its manufacture to approximately
1021-1152/1154 CE and confirms authenticity.

Primary content:
Venus divinatory/calendar material.

Sources:
https://codicemayademexico.inah.gob.mx/
https://mna.inah.gob.mx/exposiciones_temporales_detalle.php?pl=El_Codice_Maya_de_Mexico

Evidence classes:
MANUSCRIPT WITNESS / MATERIAL EVIDENCE / RITUAL-CALENDAR TEXT.

Guard:
MAYA_CODEX_OF_MEXICO != ALL_POSTCLASSIC_MAYA_CALENDAR_RELIGION.

## 5. Madrid and Paris codices

SLUB's comparative codex guide supplies orientation:
- Madrid: late Postclassic, almanacs, beekeeping, rites, sacrifices, everyday
  activities;
- Paris: late Postclassic fragment, Katun cycle, patron deities, offerings,
  rain-god calendar and zodiac.

Source:
https://www.slub-dresden.de/en/explore/manuscripts/the-dresden-maya-codex/other-maya-codices

Current use:
SOURCE ORIENTATION.

Exact manuscript/custodian binding should be done separately before proposition
promotion.

## 6. Popol Wuj / Popol Vuh

### Physical surviving witness

Francisco Ximenez K'iche'/Spanish manuscript.

Current original:
Newberry Library.

Digital access:
Library of Congress / World Digital Library partner record and Ohio State /
Newberry facsimile project.

Sources:
https://www.loc.gov/item/2021668226/
https://library.osu.edu/projects/popolwuj/

Evidence classes:
MANUSCRIPT WITNESS / COLONIAL INDIGENOUS TEXT / COMMENTARY.

Important controls:
- K'iche'-specific;
- Ximenez transcription/translation mediation;
- earlier sixteenth-century source reconstructed, not extant;
- Christian colonial context visible.

## 7. Living Maya Time — NMAI

Role:
collaborative living-tradition source developed with named Maya curators and
community collaborators.

Named communities represented include:
- Tzeltal;
- Yucatec;
- K'iche';
- Kaqchikel;
- Q'eqchi';
- Peten Itza;
- others.

Public topics:
- calendars;
- day keeping;
- agriculture/corn;
- land;
- sky;
- living ritual;
- K'iche' creation narrative;
- contemporary community continuity and change.

Sources:
https://maya.nmai.si.edu/
https://maya.nmai.si.edu/credits

Evidence classes:
LIVING TRADITION / COMMUNITY-COLLABORATIVE PUBLIC SOURCE.

Guard:
ONE_MAYA_SPEAKER != ALL_MAYA.

Status: POSTCLASSIC MANUSCRIPT / RITUAL-CALENDAR CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind the Dresden Codex as a late Postclassic Maya manuscript before using its
calendar, astronomical, ritual, divinatory, offering, or deity material in
comparison.

Core rule:

CALENDAR != PROPHECY_SYSTEM_ONLY.

A second rule:

ASTRONOMICAL_TABLE != MODERN_SCIENCE_RELIGION_BINARY.

And:

DIVINATORY_ALMANAC != EMPIRICALLY_VERIFIED_PREDICTION.

## 1. Witness identity and period

SLUB Dresden identifies the Codex Dresdensis as:
- one of four surviving Maya manuscripts;
- 39 bark-bast sheets originally screenfolded;
- later Postclassic;
- probably originating in north or northeast Yucatan;
- approximately 1300-1521 CE;
- containing work attributable to multiple scribal hands.

Evidence class:
MANUSCRIPT WITNESS / MATERIAL EVIDENCE / SCHOLARLY CODICOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION.

Guard:

DRESDEN_CODEX != CLASSIC_MAYA_DEFAULT.

Source:
SLUB Dresden, Maya Codex overview and origin pages.

## 2. Manuscript content

SLUB describes the manuscript as containing:
- ritual and divination calendars;
- astronomical and meteorological tables;
- ritual instructions;
- numerous deity depictions;
- almanacs structured around the 260-day Tzolk'in;
- astronomical tables with Long Count dates.

Current project proposition:

THE_DRESDEN_CODEX_INTEGRATES_CALENDAR_ASTRONOMY_DIVINATION_RITUAL_AND_DEITY_MATERIAL_IN_ONE_POSTCLASSIC_MANUSCRIPT.

This is a source-structure claim.

It does not mean all sections use one mechanism or purpose.

## 3. Tzolk'in ritual/divinatory calendar

SLUB describes the 260-day Tzolk'in as generated through the combination of:
- thirteen numbers;
- twenty day names.

The calendar is described as used for:
- divination;
- determining rituals.

Almanacs assign favorable/unfavorable qualities to dates for activities such as:
- sowing;
- harvesting;
- trade;
- hunting;
- warfare;
- marriage;
- birth;
- health.

BOUNDED_POSTCLASSIC_DRESDEN.

## 4. Haab and interlocking calendar systems

SLUB distinguishes:
- Tzolk'in: 260-day ritual/divinatory cycle;
- Haab: 365-day solar cycle;
- Calendar Round generated by combination.

The manuscript and Maya calendrical system also use Long Count chronology.

Current method consequence:

MAYA_SACRED_TIME_IS_MULTI_CALENDAR_NOT_ONE_CYCLE.

This matters for later comparison because:
- divination;
- historical dating;
- agricultural timing;
- astronomy;
- ritual scheduling

must not be forced into one calendar function.

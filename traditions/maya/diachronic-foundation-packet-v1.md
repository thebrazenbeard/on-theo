# Maya Diachronic Foundation Packet V1

Status: INTERNAL DIACHRONIC RECONSTRUCTION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open a Maya research lane without constructing a synthetic "Maya theology" from
sources separated by centuries, regions, languages, genres, and colonial
histories.

## 1. Maya is a family of living peoples, not only an ancient civilization

NMAI's Living Maya Time project emphasizes:
- more than thirty living Maya languages;
- multiple contemporary Maya communities;
- living calendar, agricultural, land, and ceremonial traditions;
- active continuity and change.

The project credits named Maya curators and collaborators from multiple Maya
communities.

Current proposition:

MAYA_RELIGIOUS_RESEARCH_MUST_INCLUDE_LIVING_MAYA_VOICES_AS_PRESENT_TENSE_SOURCES.

Not:
ANCIENT_MAYA = ONLY_AUTHENTIC_MAYA.

Not:
LIVING_MAYA = UNCHANGED_CLASSIC_SURVIVAL.

## 2. Classic evidence is unusually rich but socially selective

The Corpus of Maya Hieroglyphic Inscriptions preserves a large body of ancient
monumental inscriptions and associated art.

Classic monumental inscriptions strongly illuminate:
- rulers;
- dynasties;
- calendrical events;
- ritual acts;
- warfare;
- ancestry;
- court religious authority.

But monumental literacy is socially selective.

Therefore:

CLASSIC_ROYAL_EPIGRAPHY = HIGH_VALUE_SOURCE_LANE

and

CLASSIC_ROYAL_EPIGRAPHY != TOTAL_CLASSIC_MAYA_RELIGION.

Household archaeology, cave/landscape evidence, burials, ceramics, and regional
material records remain independent lanes.

## 3. Postclassic codices preserve ritual-calendrical systems

The Dresden Codex preserves:
- Tzolk'in divination almanacs;
- astronomical tables;
- ritual action;
- deity imagery;
- calendar calculations.

The Maya Codex of Mexico preserves a Venus divinatory/calendar system and is
securely early Postclassic by modern interdisciplinary authentication.

This supports an internal candidate axis:

SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL.

Neutral question:

How are astronomical, agricultural, divinatory, ritual, and social cycles
related through calendrical systems?

This may overlap RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL but should not automatically be reduced to
it.

## 4. Living sacred time remains publicly attested

NMAI's community-collaborative Living Maya Time materials describe:
- Maya calendar keepers;
- 260-day sacred/ceremonial cycles;
- agricultural cycles;
- offerings;
- relationships among corn, land, sky, and time.

A K'iche' day keeper and other named Maya contributors appear as current
speakers.

Current bounded proposition:

CALENDAR_RELIGIOUS_RELATION_IS_NOT_ONLY_AN_ANCIENT_MANUSCRIPT_FEATURE.

Guard:

LIVING_CALENDAR_CONTINUITY != IDENTICAL_POSTCLASSIC_OR_CLASSIC_RITUAL_FORM.

## 5. Popol Wuj is K'iche' and colonial-witnessed

The surviving Ximenez manuscript is a K'iche'/Spanish colonial witness.

The text contains:
- cosmogonic/origin narratives;
- failed and successful human creation;
- maize-human creation;
- Hero Twins;
- underworld/death-lord narratives;
- K'iche' genealogy/history.

These propositions are important.

But the current project uses:

KICHE_POPOL_WUJ

not:

UNIVERSAL_MAYA_CREATION_ACCOUNT.

NMAI living-tradition presentation demonstrates current K'iche' relevance,
including a K'iche' Maya day keeper speaking of people made from white and
yellow corn.

That current relevance does not erase the manuscript's colonial witness history.

## 6. Human origin and world origin must remain distinct

Popol Wuj contains both cosmic ordering/creation material and human-creation
sequences.

The Wind Cave work in the Lakota lane already demonstrated the value of
separating:

WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL

from

HUMAN_ORIGIN_OR_EMERGENCE_MODEL.

The Maya lane strengthens that method.

Current status:
- Popol Wuj WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL: CANDIDATE / KICHE_COLONIAL_WITNESS
- Popol Wuj HUMAN_ORIGIN_OR_EMERGENCE_MODEL: CANDIDATE / KICHE_COLONIAL_WITNESS

Neither is yet admitted for "Maya" as a whole.

## 7. Contact and reinterpretation are visible in living institutions

NMAI's Chichicastenango material publicly describes:
- K'iche' community;
- cofradias associated with Catholic saints;
- practices described as distinctly Maya within/around Catholic institutions;
- ajq'ijab' / calendar keepers continuing community counsel.

This is direct evidence that:

CHRISTIAN_MAYA_CONTACT_HISTORY != SIMPLE_REPLACEMENT.

It also warns against labeling every mixed institution "syncretism" without
mechanism analysis.

Future axis:
CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL.

## 8. Land, corn, time, and reciprocity

Living Maya Time describes contemporary offerings and ceremonies tied to:
- corn cycles;
- land;
- calendar cycles;
- agricultural practice.

A Tzeltal Maya contributor describes land as a source of teaching, and NMAI
frames current offerings as rooted in reciprocal Maya-land relations.

This supports internal candidates:
- RELATIONAL_ORDER_MODEL;
- SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL;
- OFFERING_EXCHANGE_MODEL;
- SACRED_TIME_CALENDAR_MODEL.

Community attribution remains mandatory.

## 9. What V1 does not admit

Not admitted:
- one timeless Maya pantheon;
- one Maya creation account;
- Popol Wuj as pan-Maya scripture;
- Classic rulers as representative of all religious practice;
- Dresden Codex as one Maya canon;
- all modern Maya ceremonies as unchanged ancient survivals;
- generic "Maya shaman" as an adequate specialist category;
- all ajq'ij/day-keeper roles as identical across languages/communities;
- colonial cofradias as either purely Catholic or purely precontact by default.

## 10. Strongest foundation result

MAYA_RELIGIOUS_HISTORY_REQUIRES_PARALLEL_EPIGRAPHIC_MATERIAL_CODEX_COLONIAL_INDIGENOUS_TEXT_AND_LIVING_COMMUNITY_LANES_WITHOUT_COLLAPSING_THEIR_DATES_REGIONS_OR_SOCIAL_LOCATIONS.

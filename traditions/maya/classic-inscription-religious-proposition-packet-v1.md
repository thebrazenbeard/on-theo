# Classic Maya Inscriptional Religious Proposition Packet V1

Status: EPIGRAPHIC / ELITE-RITUAL CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish source-level Classic Maya religious propositions without importing
Postclassic codices or the colonial K'iche' Popol Wuj backward into Classic
monuments.

V1 uses a tightly bounded Yaxchilan control set.

Core rule:

CLASSIC_ROYAL_RITUAL != ALL_CLASSIC_MAYA_RELIGION.

## 1. Source and monument control

Primary control:
Yaxchilan Lintel 24, Structure 23.

Current research surfaces:
- Harvard Peabody CMHI documentation;
- British Museum object record.

The monument is a Classic Maya carved limestone lintel.

CMHI gives:
Long Count / Calendar Round date corresponding to 24 October 709 CE.

Evidence class:
EPIGRAPHIC EVIDENCE + MATERIAL EVIDENCE.

## 2. Lintel 24: named and dated ritual action

The scene and inscription bind:
- ruler Itzamnaaj Bahlam / Shield Jaguar;
- Lady K'abal Xook;
- bloodletting / penance;
- royal titles;
- Yaxchilan political identity;
- a precise calendrical date.

CMHI translates the inscription using "penance" for ch'ajb-related action and
reads the ruler as the holy/divine lord of Pa'chan.

British Museum independently describes Lady K'abal Xook drawing a thorn-studded
rope through her tongue while the ruler holds a torch.

Current proposition:

DATED_ROYAL_BLOODLETTING_OR_PENITENTIAL_RITE =
DIRECT_EPIGRAPHIC_AND_ICONOGRAPHIC_EVIDENCE.

This is stronger than inferring ritual solely from an image.

## 3. Political-sacral title control

The Lintel 24 inscription includes:

k'uhul ... ajaw

translated in current CMHI presentation as "holy lord" and elsewhere commonly
rendered "divine lord."

Current neutral classification:

K'UHUL_AJAW_TITLE =
SACRALIZED_RULERSHIP_LANGUAGE.

Guard:

K'UHUL_AJAW != RULER_IS_LITERALLY_A_GOD
without further semantic argument.

The title directly supports sacred/political entanglement.

It does not settle one complete ontology of kingship.

## 4. Lintel 25: accession-linked ritual complex

British Museum's Lintel 25 record identifies:
- Lady K'abal Xook;
- a bloodletting-related scene;
- serpent/vision imagery;
- the named ruler;
- an accession-commemoration context.

The museum notes that some scholars interpret the serpent imagery as an
ancestral spirit or kingdom founder.

Therefore:

ACCESSION_LINKED_RITUAL = BOUNDED_MATERIAL_AND_EPIGRAPHIC_RECONSTRUCTION.

ANCESTRAL_SPIRIT_IDENTITY_OF_SERPENT =
SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION / NOT_DIRECTLY_FIXED.

The project's vocabulary should prefer:

VISION_SERPENT_OR_SERPENT_APPARITION_ICONOGRAPHY

until exact glyphic propositions require a more specific label.

## 5. Repetition beyond one royal woman

British Museum Lintel 17, dated around 770 CE, depicts Bird Jaguar IV and Lady
Balam-Ix in a bloodletting rite and states that its inscription records the
ritual.

This prevents Lintel 24 from being treated as a unique one-off event.

It supports:

ROYAL_BLOODLETTING_IS_ATTESTED_ACROSS_MULTIPLE_YAXCHILAN_MONUMENTS.

But:

YAXCHILAN_RITUAL_PATTERN != ALL_CLASSIC_MAYA_RITUAL.

## 6. Ritual order

The controlled Yaxchilan material supports an elite/court ritual system in which
selected actions can be:
- calendrically dated;
- represented monumentally;
- linked to named rulers and royal women;
- connected to political accession/commemoration;
- expressed through sacrificial/penitential action.

Current axis:

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CLASSIC_ROYAL_YAXCHILAN_LAYER.

This is not yet a full Classic Maya ritual calendar or priestly office map.

## 7. Political sacralization

The combination of:
- k'uhul ajaw title;
- public monumental ritual;
- accession commemoration;
- lineage/rulership context

supports:

POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CLASSIC_DYNASTIC_RITUAL_AUTHORITY.

Mechanisms:
- SACRAL_RULER_TITLE;
- ROYAL_RITUAL_PERFORMANCE;
- ACCESSION_COMMEmORATION;
- DYNASTIC_MONUMENTALIZATION.

Not established:
- one Maya divine-right theory;
- ruler ontologically identical with deity;
- universal Classic polity structure;
- all political authority requiring identical rites.

## 8. Gender and ritual authority

Lady K'abal Xook is not incidental in these monuments.

She is explicitly named/titled and represented performing consequential ritual
acts in the royal program.

Current proposition:

ROYAL_WOMEN_CAN_BE_NAMED_RITUAL_AGENTS_IN_CLASSIC_YAXCHILAN_MONUMENTAL_EVIDENCE.

This does not establish:
- equal political office;
- one gender system across Classic Maya polities;
- ordinary women's ritual roles.

## 9. Vision/disclosure caution

Modern museum language describes Lintel 25 as a visionary/hallucinatory phase of
bloodletting.

The serpent/ancestor interpretation is historically influential.

But V1 does not convert that into:

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL = BOUNDED

because:
- exact glyphic language of the apparition requires separate binding;
- iconographic experience language is partly scholarly reconstruction.

Current status:

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL =
PARTIAL_ICONOGRAPHIC_CANDIDATE.

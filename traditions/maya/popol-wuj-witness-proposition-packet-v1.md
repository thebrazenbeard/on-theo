# Popol Wuj Witness and Proposition Packet V1

Status: K'ICHE' COLONIAL-WITNESS CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind the Popol Wuj to its surviving physical witness before using its
cosmogonic, human-origin, Hero Twin, underworld, or genealogical material in
comparison.

Core rule:

POPOL_WUJ != UNIVERSAL_MAYA_SCRIPTURE.

Witness rule:

SURVIVING_MANUSCRIPT != RECONSTRUCTED_EARLIER_SOURCE.

## 1. Surviving physical witness

The current surviving manuscript is held by the Newberry Library as Ayer MS
1515 and is digitally accessible through Library of Congress / World Digital
Library and Ohio State / Newberry.

The manuscript:
- was transcribed by Dominican friar Francisco Ximenez;
- is generally dated to the early eighteenth century, approximately 1700-1715;
- contains K'iche' and Spanish in parallel;
- preserves corrections and irregularities in the K'iche' transcription.

Evidence class:
MANUSCRIPT WITNESS + COLONIAL DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE.

Sources:
https://www.loc.gov/item/2021668226/
https://library.osu.edu/projects/popolwuj/

## 2. Ximenez's K'iche' and Spanish are not one textual layer

Ohio State's facsimile/transcription project explicitly preserves the
orthographic and linguistic irregularities of both columns and warns that
transcription itself can require interpretive decisions.

The K'iche' column is therefore not silently replaced by:
- Ximenez's Spanish;
- a modern English translation;
- a normalized modern K'iche' edition.

Required relation:

KICHE_COLUMN
!=
XIMENEZ_SPANISH_TRANSLATION
!=
MODERN_TRANSLATION.

Any future clause-level theological claim should identify which textual form is
being cited.

## 3. Earlier-source hypothesis

Library of Congress and the Ohio State/Newberry project report the scholarly
view that Ximenez likely worked from an earlier K'iche' text, commonly placed in
the sixteenth century and associated with an Indigenous writer or writers using
Latin characters.

The earlier source is not the surviving manuscript.

Current classification:

EARLIER_16C_KICHE_TEXT =
HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION / STRONG_SOURCE_HISTORY_HYPOTHESIS.

A still earlier precolonial glyphic/screenfold source is not physically present
in the current evidence chain.

Therefore:

PRECOLONIAL_POPOL_WUJ_AUTOGRAPH = NOT_ESTABLISHED_AS_SURVIVING_WITNESS.

## 4. Community scope

The Popol Wuj is specifically K'iche'.

Its text includes K'iche' genealogical and political-historical material in
addition to mythic/origin narratives.

Therefore the correct project label is:

KICHE_POPOL_WUJ.

Not:

MAYA_BIBLE.

NMAI's public collaborative presentation describes it through current K'iche'
Maya voices while also situating the colonial recording history.

## 5. World/cosmic ordering propositions

At high level the text contains:
- primordial/cosmogonic ordering;
- creator beings deliberating and acting;
- formation/order of earth and environment;
- successive creation attempts.

Current axis admission:

WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL =
PARTIAL_BOUNDED_KICHE_COLONIAL_TEXTUAL_LAYER.

Reason for PARTIAL:
V1 binds the source/witness and broad textual proposition but does not yet
supply clause-by-clause K'iche' alignment.

Not admitted:
- one pan-Maya creation sequence;
- creation ex nihilo as a project paraphrase;
- one sovereign monotheistic creator;
- identity with Classic Maya creation imagery by resemblance alone.

## 6. Human-creation propositions

The text represents multiple attempts to produce humans capable of appropriate
speech/recognition before the successful maize-human sequence.

NMAI's living collaborative presentation includes a K'iche' Maya day keeper,
Juana Batz Puac, affirming the living teaching that the first grandparents were
made from white and yellow corn.

Current axis admission:

HUMAN_ORIGIN_OR_EMERGENCE_MODEL =
BOUNDED_KICHE_TEXT_PLUS_LIVING_KICHE_RECEPTION.

This does not mean every Maya language/community narrates human origin in the
same form.

## 7. Maize is theological, social, and living — but not one static symbol

In the Popol Wuj human-creation sequence maize is central to successful human
formation.

In current NMAI Maya collaboration, K'iche' and other Maya contributors
continue to connect maize with:
- ancestry;
- identity;
- agricultural cycles;
- sacred time;
- land and community.

Current bounded relation:

MAIZE_LINKS_HUMAN_ORIGIN_AND_LIVING_RELATIONAL_PRACTICE_IN_SELECTED_KICHE_AND_OTHER_PUBLIC_MAYA_SOURCES.

But:

SAME_MAIZE_MOTIF != IDENTICAL_DOCTRINE_ACROSS_1500_YEARS.

## 8. Hero Twins and underworld

The Popol Wuj contains a Hero Twin narrative involving:
- underworld/death lords;
- trials/conflict;
- death/return or transformation motifs;
- cosmic consequences in later interpretation.

This is mythic/heroic evidence.

It is not an ordinary-human afterlife manual.

Therefore:

HERO_TWIN_UNDERWORLD_NARRATIVE
!=
POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL_FOR_ORDINARY_HUMANS.

Current status:
POSTMORTEM_PATH_MODEL = NOT_ADMITTED_FROM_THIS_PACKET.

TRANSFORMATION_MODEL = CANDIDATE_MYTHIC_ONLY.

## 9. Genealogy and land-right material

The Popol Wuj does not end with cosmogony.

It also preserves K'iche':
- lineage;
- genealogy;
- political memory;
- migration/history;
- land-right/authority material in modern source description.

This means myth and political genealogy cannot be separated by assuming the text
is only a "creation myth."

Future axis:
POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_MODEL / GENEALOGICAL_AUTHORITY
requires exact proposition binding.

Current status:
CANDIDATE_NOT_YET_ADMITTED.

## 10. Colonial Christian-contact control

The surviving witness exists inside Dominican colonial textual practice.

Potential Christian parallels must be treated through:
- exact K'iche' wording;
- source chronology;
- translation history;
- colonial contact;
- internal Maya/K'iche' explanatory context.

Do not infer either:
- all biblical-looking language was imported by Ximenez;
- no Christian influence could have entered the transmitted text.

Current status:

CHRISTIAN_CONTACT_EFFECT_ON_POPOL_WUJ =
OPEN_SOURCE_CRITICAL_QUESTION.

## 11. Living K'iche' reception is evidence, not a time machine

NMAI's Living Maya Time project was developed with named Maya collaborators and
presents current K'iche' voices relating maize, creation, calendar, and
community.

This is strong LIVING TRADITION evidence.

It does not prove that every current formulation is textually unchanged from the
Classic or precontact period.

Required relation:

LIVING_KICHE_RECEPTION
!=
PROOF_OF_UNCHANGED_ANCIENT_SEMANTICS.

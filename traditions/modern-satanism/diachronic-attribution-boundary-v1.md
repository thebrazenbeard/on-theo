# Modern Satanism Diachronic / Attribution Boundary V1

Status: ACTIVE MODERN-RELIGION RESEARCH CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Open a modern Satanism lane without collapsing:
- accusations of Satanism;
- literary sympathy with Satan;
- self-identified religious Satanism;
- non-theistic Satanism;
- theistic/esoteric Satanism;
- Setianism;
- political activism;
- extremist fringe movements.

Core rule:

ATTRIBUTED_SATANISM != SELF_IDENTIFIED_SATANISM.

A second rule:

SELF_IDENTIFIED_SATANISM != ONE_RELIGION.

## 1. Scholarly field boundary

Modern scholarship distinguishes:
- discourse *about* the satanic;
- self-ascribed satanic discourse/identity;
- attribution of Satanism to enemies;
- appropriation/identification with Satan or the category "Satanism."

Therefore:

SATANISM_AS_ACCUSATION
and
SATANISM_AS_SELF_IDENTIFICATION

are separate historical objects.

The accusation-history receives its own later packet/branch.

## 2. Precursors versus organized religion

Relevant historical precursors can include:
- early-modern demonological accusations;
- Black Mass legends;
- Romantic literary Satan;
- anti-clerical/radical revaluation;
- nineteenth-century occult/esoteric currents.

But:

LITERARY_SATANISM != ORGANIZED_RELIGIOUS_SATANISM.

The project does not call Byron, Shelley, or every sympathetic literary Satan
a member of a modern Satanic religion.

## 3. Church of Satan

The Church of Satan:
- officially dates its founding to 1966 under Anton LaVey;
- self-describes as atheistic/materialist;
- rejects belief in Satan as a literal supernatural being;
- uses Satan as a positive symbol of pride, liberty, individualism and
  self-directed potential;
- treats The Satanic Bible and other LaVeyan texts as core explanatory works;
- describes ritual in non-sacrificial/non-devotional terms.

Evidence class:
CURRENT ORGANIZATIONAL SELF-DESCRIPTION / MODERN RELIGIOUS TEXT /
SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.

Important boundary:

CHURCH_OF_SATAN_DEFINITION_OF_"TRUE_SATANISM"
!=
NEUTRAL_SCHOLARLY_FIELD_DEFINITION.

Its official claim that literal devil-worshippers are not Satanists is stored
as sectarian self-definition.

## 4. The Satanic Temple

The Satanic Temple currently self-describes as:
- non-theistic;
- non-supernaturalist;
- religious;
- using Satan as a literary/metaphorical symbol of rebellion against arbitrary
  authority;
- organized around Seven Tenets;
- engaged in religious-liberty and public-policy activism;
- not adhering to The Satanic Bible as scripture;
- allowing non-required, locally/individually composed consensual rituals.

Evidence class:
CURRENT ORGANIZATIONAL SELF-DESCRIPTION / MODERN RELIGION.

TST is not a branch of the Church of Satan.

## 5. Theistic and esoteric Satanisms

Academic surveys and typologies document self-identified Satanists who:
- hold theistic beliefs;
- hold esoteric/occult beliefs;
- interpret Satan as a real supernatural being or power in some cases.

These currents are heterogeneous and often decentralized.

Therefore:

THEISTIC_SATANISM != ONE_CHURCH_OR_ONE_THEOLOGY.

And:

CHURCH_OF_SATAN_REJECTION_OF_THEISTIC_SATANISM
does not erase
SCHOLARLY_ATTESTATION_OF_THEISTIC_SELF_IDENTIFICATION.

## 6. Temple of Set

The Temple of Set developed historically from a Church of Satan schism and is
important to the history of the broader satanic/left-hand-path milieu.

But it centers Setian self-identification and theology rather than simply
accepting classification as Satanism.

Current project rule:

TEMPLE_OF_SET =
RELATED_DISTINCT_SETIAN_DEVELOPMENT.

Do not populate a "Satanist" cell from it without source-specific self-
identification.

## 7. Extremist fringe

Some organizations or individuals combine satanic/esoteric imagery with violent,
criminal or extremist ideologies.

These require a separate evidence/safety lane.

Current rule:

EXTREMIST_SATANIC_FRINGE != REPRESENTATIVE_MODERN_SATANISM.

The existence of an extremist fringe cannot be used to characterize the
category as a whole.

## 8. Satanic Panic

Claims of organized ritual abuse and hidden Satanic networks during the Satanic
Panic belong primarily to:
- accusation history;
- media history;
- conspiracy history;
- criminal-justice/psychology history.

They are NOT evidence for self-identified Satanic religion without independent
source support.

## 9. "Satan" is not one referent across Satanisms

Possible modern source-bound uses include:
- symbolic adversary;
- literary rebel;
- metaphor for individual sovereignty;
- archetypal/esoteric figure;
- supernatural being;
- object of veneration.

Therefore:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL

must be source-specific.

No project definition of Satanism begins by assuming a literal Satan.

## 10. Comparison hold

No modern Satanism cell enters cross-tradition synthesis until:
- self-identification is demonstrated;
- organization/current is named;
- Satan referent is explicit;
- source is internal versus scholarly;
- accusation-history contamination is excluded;
- exact neutral axis is source-admitted.

Current status:

MODERN_SATANISM_CROSS_TRADITION_COMPARISON = HOLD.

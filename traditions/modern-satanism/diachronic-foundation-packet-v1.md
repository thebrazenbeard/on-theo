# Modern Satanism Diachronic Foundation Packet V1

Status: INTERNAL MODERN-RELIGION FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish a defensible internal map of modern self-identified Satanisms before
cross-tradition comparison.

## 1. Modern Satanism is a modern appropriation field

Current scholarship strongly distinguishes the long history of accusations
about "Satanists" from modern people/groups who positively identify with Satan
or Satanism.

Current historical model:

ATTRIBUTION
->
CULTURAL_SATAN_MYTH
->
LITERARY/ESOTERIC_REVALUATION
->
MODERN_SELF_IDENTIFICATION.

This is not a simple continuous hidden Satanic church.

## 2. Church of Satan

Current official claims:
- founded by Anton LaVey in 1966;
- Satan does not literally exist;
- worldview is atheistic/materialist;
- Satan symbolizes pride, liberty, individualism and personal potential;
- human self is central;
- no supernatural afterlife is required;
- sacrifice to supernatural beings is rejected.

Scholarly interpretation also places LaVeyan Satanism in:
- self-religion/self-spirituality;
- individualist;
- world-affirming/materialist currents.

Current internal classifications:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL =
SYMBOLIC_PERSONAL_INDIVIDUALIST.

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
BOUNDED_SELF_DEIFICATION_OR_SELF_CENTRALITY_CO_S_SOURCE_LAYER.

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
PARTIAL_PSYCHODRAMATIC_OR_SELF_DIRECTED_RITUAL.

## 3. The Satanic Temple

Current official claims:
- no belief in literal Satan or supernatural realm;
- Satan symbolizes the rebel against arbitrary authority;
- religion is grounded in reason, empathy, justice, bodily autonomy and
  scientific understanding;
- no required scripture;
- no required uniform ritual;
- optional rituals can be locally/individually composed and consensual;
- public activism is integrated with religious identity.

Current internal classifications:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL =
LITERARY_REBEL_METAPHOR.

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
BOUNDED_INDIVIDUAL_SOVEREIGNTY_AND_BODILY_AUTONOMY_TST_LAYER.

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
PARTIAL_OPTIONAL_CONSENSUAL_MEMBER_COMPOSED.

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
CANDIDATE_CONGREGATIONAL_AND_ACTIVIST.

## 4. Church of Satan and TST are not interchangeable nontheisms

Both reject a literal supernatural Satan.

But they differ on:
- ethical formulation;
- organization;
- public activism;
- authority;
- ritual framing;
- relation to LaVey;
- social/political orientation.

Therefore:

NON_THEISTIC_SATANISM != ONE_THEOLOGY.

## 5. Theistic/esoteric Satanism exists as a scholarly category

Survey and movement scholarship documents self-identified theistic/esoteric
Satanists.

Current secure proposition:

THEISTIC_SELF_IDENTIFIED_SATANISM_EXISTS.

Current hold:
ONE_THEISTIC_SATANISM_THEOLOGY = NOT_ADMITTED.

Potential Satan referents include:
- real personal Satan;
- spiritual power;
- esoteric adversarial principle.

Each requires named-source control.

## 6. Satan referent is the central internal comparison variable

Modern Satanism especially requires separating:

SYMBOLIC_SATAN
LITERARY_SATAN
THEISTIC_SATAN
ESOTERIC/ARCHETYPAL_SATAN
SECTARIAN_BOUNDARY_CLAIMS.

New internal dimension:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL.

This should not automatically become a global neutral axis because it is
tradition-specific terminology.

## 7. Contact/reinterpretation

Modern Satanism is structurally dependent on reinterpretation of:
- Christian Satan;
- Western demonological accusation;
- literary Romantic Satan;
- modern secular/esoteric thought.

Current axis:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
STRONG_CANDIDATE_ADVERSARIAL_SYMBOL_REVALUATION.

This is not "borrowing" in a simple positive sense.

Mechanisms include:
- inversion;
- revaluation;
- symbolic appropriation;
- polemical reversal;
- literary reinterpretation.

## 8. Continuity

Modern self-identified Satanism is not evidence of a continuous underground
Satanic religion from the Middle Ages.

Current axis:

CONTINUITY_MODEL =
DISCONTINUOUS_MODERN_APPROPRIATION_WITH_PRECURSORS.

This is a positive historical conclusion.

## 9. Authority

Modern Satanic authority differs sharply:
- founder/text authority in LaVeyan traditions;
- organizational/tenet/congregational authority in TST;
- decentralized authority in many theistic/esoteric currents.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
CANDIDATE_MULTI_ORGANIZATIONAL.

## 10. Ritual

Ritual can be:
- symbolic;
- psychodramatic;
- self-transformative;
- expressive;
- blasphemous/reversal-based;
- theistic/devotional in some other currents.

Therefore:

SATANIC_RITUAL != DEVIL_WORSHIP_BY_DEFAULT.

## 11. Political relation

TST publicly integrates activism and religious-liberty claims.

Church of Satan official materials distinguish their own approach and often
reject TST's model.

Therefore:

POLITICAL_PUBLIC_ACTIVISM =
TST_SOURCE_LAYER,
not
MODERN_SATANISM_UNIVERSAL.

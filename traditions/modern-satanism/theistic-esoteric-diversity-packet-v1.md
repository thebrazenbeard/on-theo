# Theistic / Esoteric Satanism Diversity Packet V1

Status: SCHOLARLY FIELD DIVERSITY CONTROL / LOWER ORGANIZATIONAL CEILING / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish that self-identified theistic/esoteric Satanisms exist without
manufacturing one supernatural Satanist church, creed, ritual system, or moral
code.

Core rule:

THEISTIC_SATANISM != ONE_THEOLOGY.

## 1. Scholarly attestation

Modern Satanism survey and field scholarship documents:
- self-identified theistic Satanists;
- self-identified esoteric Satanists;
- hybrid identities;
- changing proportions of LaVeyan, theistic and esoteric identification in
  survey samples.

Therefore:

THEISTIC_ESOTERIC_SELF_IDENTIFICATION =
SCHOLARLY_ATTESTED.

This remains true even where Church of Satan defines such people outside its
own category of Satanism.

## 2. Field-definition disagreement

Scholarly definitions vary.

Van Luijk uses a working definition focused on intentional religious veneration
of Satan.

Other scholarship uses broader concepts such as:
- satanic discourse;
- self-identification;
- oppositional identity;
- modern religious Satanism;
- satanic milieu.

Therefore:

ONE_ACADEMIC_DEFINITION_OF_SATANISM =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

ON_THEO stores the definition used by each source.

## 3. Satan referent

Within theistic/esoteric currents, Satan can be source-described as:
- personal supernatural being;
- spiritual intelligence;
- adversarial power;
- esoteric principle;
- object of veneration/devotion.

No one referent is assumed.

Current internal dimension:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL =
THEISTIC_ESOTERIC_HETEROGENEOUS.

## 4. Person/self

Scholarship on modern Satanist identities often documents:
- individual empowerment;
- nonconformity;
- identity construction;
- adversarial/rebellious self-fashioning;
- esoteric transformation in selected currents.

Current axis:

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
PARTIAL_THEISTIC_ESOTERIC_IDENTITY_LAYER.

There is no one soul/self ontology admitted.

## 5. Ritual

Theistic/esoteric currents may include:
- devotional;
- magical;
- esoteric;
- symbolic;
- self-transformational
rituals.

But field diversity is too high for:

ONE_THEISTIC_SATANIC_RITUAL_ORDER.

Current axis:

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
PARTIAL_DIVERSE.

## 6. Institution

A significant part of the theistic/esoteric field is:
- decentralized;
- small-group;
- networked;
- individual-practitioner based.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
PARTIAL_DECENTRALIZED_FIELD.

This is not a claim that no organized theistic groups exist.

## 7. Temple of Set boundary

Temple of Set is important in the history of post-LaVeyan/esoteric developments.

But:
- it identifies around Set/Setianism;
- scholarly literature places it in the adjacent modern satanic/left-hand-path
  field;
- classification as "Satanism" varies by source and context.

Current status:

TEMPLE_OF_SET =
RELATED_DISTINCT_SETIAN_CONTROL.

It is not used as the representative theistic Satanism case.

## 8. Extremist fringe

Scholarship on Satanism sometimes includes organizations with violent/extremist
ideologies.

Current project rule:

EXTREMIST_GROUP =
SEPARATE_FRINGE_CLASSIFICATION.

Their ideology cannot be used to infer:
- ordinary Satanist ethics;
- typical ritual;
- category-wide violence;
- theistic Satanism as intrinsically extremist.

V1 records existence of the methodological problem without reproducing
operational extremist doctrine.

## 9. Survey limitations

Internet/questionnaire surveys of self-identified Satanists are useful for:
- diversity;
- identity;
- belief/practice distribution;
- change over time.

They do not establish:
- total population;
- representative global prevalence;
- exact organizational membership;
- stable category boundaries.

Current evidence ceiling:

SURVEY_DIVERSITY_SIGNAL =
BOUNDED_SCHOLARLY.

## 10. Devil-worship terminology

Some theistic Satanists may positively venerate a literal Satan.

But:

DEVIL_WORSHIP

is historically overloaded and often hostile.

The separate accusation-history lane will test:
- outsider use;
- self-description;
- polemic;
- conspiracy;
- actual devotional claims.

Current packet does not use "devil worship" as a neutral umbrella.

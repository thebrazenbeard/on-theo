# The Satanic Temple Proposition Packet V1

Status: CURRENT ORGANIZATIONAL SELF-DESCRIPTION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind The Satanic Temple (TST) as a distinct current Satanic religion without
treating it as a continuation or faction of LaVeyan Church of Satan doctrine.

## 1. Satan referent

TST currently states:
- it does not believe in literal Satan;
- it rejects supernaturalism;
- Satan is a literary/metaphorical symbol;
- Satan represents the rebel against arbitrary/tyrannical authority and the
  questioning heretic.

Current internal dimension:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL =
LITERARY_METAPHORICAL_REBEL.

This differs from the Church of Satan's symbolic self-individualist emphasis
even though both are non-theistic.

## 2. Ethical/tenet structure

TST publicly centers Seven Tenets involving:
- compassion/empathy;
- justice;
- bodily autonomy;
- freedom and limits on encroachment;
- scientific understanding;
- fallibility/repair;
- priority of compassion/wisdom/justice over rigid wording.

Current axis:

ETHICAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_TST_TENET_LAYER
(internal candidate; not yet a global neutral axis).

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
BOUNDED_BODILY_AUTONOMY_AND_INDIVIDUAL_SOVEREIGNTY.

## 3. Scripture/text

TST states:
- it does not adhere to The Satanic Bible;
- it has no required sacred book/scripture;
- it provides suggested reading for philosophical/historical orientation.

Therefore:

TEXTUAL_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
TENETS_PLUS_INTERPRETIVE_READING_NOT_CANONICAL_SCRIPTURE.

## 4. Ritual

TST states:
- no ritual is universally required;
- members/congregations may create meaningful rituals;
- ritual must conform to organizational values;
- public examples include Black Mass, unbaptism, destruction and defiance
  rituals;
- official FAQ states rituals are consensual and do not involve animal
  suffering.

Current axis:

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_OPTIONAL_MEMBER_COMPOSED_SYMBOLIC_RITUAL.

The same ritual label cannot be assumed to mean the same thing as CoS ritual.

## 5. Institution and congregations

TST currently organizes:
- congregations;
- recognition/onboarding structures;
- organizational campaigns;
- membership systems;
- public religious programs.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CONGREGATIONAL_CAMPAIGN_NETWORK.

This differs from CoS's non-congregational formal hierarchy.

## 6. Public activism

TST explicitly integrates:
- religious-liberty litigation/campaigns;
- church-state equality claims;
- bodily autonomy;
- opposition to corporal punishment;
- public symbolic actions

with religious mission.

Current classification:

PUBLIC_POLITICAL_LEGAL_ACTION =
TST_RELIGIOUS_INSTITUTIONAL_LAYER.

Do not infer:
ALL_SATANISM = ACTIVIST_RELIGION.

## 7. Authority

TST publicly rejects rigid centralized authority as a model while maintaining
organization-level:
- mission;
- tenets;
- congregation recognition;
- committees/processes.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_TENET_ORGANIZATION_PLUS_LOCAL_MEMBER_AGENCY.

## 8. Relation to Church of Satan

TST explicitly distinguishes itself from LaVeyan Satanism and criticizes:
- selected LaVeyan political/ethical positions;
- authoritarianism;
- supernatural/magic claims it attributes to LaVeyan sources.

CoS in turn rejects TST's claim to Satanism.

Current project rule:

COS_TST_BOUNDARY_CONFLICT =
INTER_ORGANIZATIONAL_SECTARIAN_DATA.

ON_THEO does not select a "true Satanism."

## 9. Contact/reinterpretation

TST explicitly grounds its Satan image in:
- literary Satan;
- Romantic tradition;
- opposition to arbitrary sacred authority.

Current axis:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_LITERARY_ADVERSARIAL_REVALUATION.

## 10. Sacrifice negative control

TST official ritual policy rejects animal suffering and does not treat human or
animal sacrifice as part of its religion.

Current result:

SACRIFICE_AND_BODILY_OFFERING_MODEL =
NOT_ADMITTED_CURRENT_TST.

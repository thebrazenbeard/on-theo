# Church of Satan Proposition Packet V1

Status: CURRENT ORGANIZATIONAL SELF-DESCRIPTION / SCHOLARLY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind the Church of Satan as one specific modern Satanist institution rather than
letting LaVeyan self-definition stand for all Satanism.

## 1. Founding and modernity

The Church of Satan officially dates its founding to April 30, 1966 under
Anton Szandor LaVey.

Current historical classification:

CHURCH_OF_SATAN =
MODERN_ORGANIZED_SATANISM.

This is not evidence for an earlier underground Church of Satan.

## 2. Satan referent

Official current CoS materials state:
- Satan is not a real supernatural person/being;
- Satan functions as a symbol of pride, liberty, individualism and personal
  potential;
- the universe is indifferent rather than providentially governed.

Current internal dimension:

SATAN_REFERENT_MODEL =
SYMBOLIC_INDIVIDUALIST.

## 3. Person/self model

Official CoS language explicitly centers:
- self;
- carnal/material human existence;
- individual development;
- self-deification/self-centrality;
- rejection of a soul imprisoned in the body.

Scholarly analysis of LaVey's core writings describes the system as a form of
self-religion/self-spirituality with materialist and ego-centered features.

Current axis:

PERSON_OR_SELF_MODEL =
BOUNDED_MATERIAL_CARNAL_SELF_CENTRALITY.

This is not:
literal claim that each member is a supernatural deity.

## 4. Ritual / Greater Magic

Current official FAQ describes Greater Magic as:
- ritual practice;
- optional;
- self-transformational psychodrama;
- emotional release/catharsis;
- focused on changing the practitioner's own mental/emotional state;
- not worship of a higher power.

The organization permits members to interpret experiences beyond that minimum,
but does not require supernatural belief.

Current axis:

RITUAL_ORDER_MODEL =
BOUNDED_OPTIONAL_SELF_TRANSFORMATIONAL_PSYCHODRAMA.

Important:

ATHEISM != ABSENCE_OF_RITUAL_OR_MAGIC_VOCABULARY.

## 5. Lesser Magic

Official materials use Lesser Magic for interpersonal influence/charm/
manipulation in ordinary life.

Current classification:

LESSER_MAGIC =
CHURCH_OF_SATAN_INTERNAL_SOCIAL_TECHNIQUE_CATEGORY.

It is not evidence for supernatural causation.

## 6. Sacrifice

Current CoS materials explicitly reject literal ritual sacrifice to supernatural
beings and deny ritual abuse.

Therefore:

SACRIFICE_AND_BODILY_OFFERING_MODEL =
NOT_ADMITTED_FOR_CURRENT_COS.

This is a useful negative control against outsider stereotype.

## 7. Core texts

Official CoS sources treat:
- The Satanic Bible (1969);
- later LaVey works;
- later institutional writings such as The Satanic Scriptures

as central explanatory/codifying literature.

Current classification:

TEXTUAL_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
FOUNDER_AND_INSTITUTIONAL_CORE_LITERATURE.

Do not call:
THE_SATANIC_BIBLE = REVEALED_SCRIPTURE
without source support.

## 8. Hierarchy

Current CoS organization retains:
- Registered Membership;
- Active Membership / First Degree;
- later degrees;
- Priesthood of Mendes;
- Council of Nine;
- High Priest/High Priestess administrative leadership.

Official CoS simultaneously states:
- degrees are not required initiatory stages;
- rank does not command member obedience;
- advancement emphasizes demonstrated achievement;
- ordinary interaction is not required.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_HIERARCHICAL_RECOGNITION_WITH_STRONG_INDIVIDUALISM.

## 9. Authority

The organization combines:
- founder-text authority;
- current administrative authority;
- formal degree recognition;
- philosophical emphasis on personal autonomy.

Current axis:

AGENCY_AND_AUTHORITY_MODEL =
BOUNDED_FOUNDER_TEXT_ADMINISTRATION_PLUS_INDIVIDUALISM.

This is internally tension-bearing but not logically contradictory:
organizational authority and personal sovereignty can coexist in different
domains.

## 10. Worship/service structure

Current CoS materials state:
- no regular obligatory worship services;
- no belief/worship of Satan;
- ritual is optional/private in many cases;
- membership is not centered on congregation attendance.

Therefore:

INSTITUTION != CONGREGATIONAL_WORSHIP_BY_DEFAULT.

## 11. Theistic boundary claim

CoS officially claims:
- Satanism is atheistic;
- literal supernatural devil-worship is not Satanism.

Project classification:

COS_THEISTIC_EXCLUSION =
SECTARIAN_TAXONOMIC_CLAIM.

Scholarly field classification remains independent.

## 12. Contact/reinterpretation

CoS deliberately reverses/revalues the conventional Christian Satan symbol.

Mechanisms:
- adversarial-symbol appropriation;
- inversion/revaluation;
- secular/materialist recoding;
- self-religious reinterpretation.

Current axis:

CONTACT_AND_REINTERPRETATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_SYMBOLIC_ADVERSARIAL_RECODING.

## Logical unit 1 — two rejected extremes

Ptolemy opens by rejecting two totalizing accounts of Mosaic law:

1. the entire law comes directly from the perfect Father;
2. the entire law comes from the destructive Devil.

He says both positions are wrong.

### FLORA-V1-001

Proposition:
Ptolemy rejects both "all Mosaic law = perfect Father" and
"all Mosaic law = Devil."

Evidence class:
`PRIMARY TEXT`

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Importance:
This immediately blocks the reduction:

`PTOLEMY = JEWISH_SCRIPTURE_IS_SIMPLY_EVIL`

## Logical unit 2 — three legislative sources

Ptolemy divides the Pentateuchal law by source:

- God;
- Moses acting from his own judgment under human conditions;
- the elders / human tradition.

His examples use Jesus traditions about divorce and traditions of the elders.

### FLORA-V1-002

Proposition:
Ptolemy distinguishes divine law, Mosaic accommodation, and later human
tradition within the Pentateuchal/legal field.

Evidence class:
`PRIMARY TEXT`

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Historical ceiling:
This is evidence for Ptolemy's second-century Christian hermeneutic, not an
independent historical demonstration of Pentateuchal source history.

Guard:

`PTOLEMAEAN_HERMENEUTIC != MODERN_PENTATEUCHAL_SOURCE_CRITICISM`

## Logical unit 3 — three classes within the divine law

Ptolemy further divides the law attributed to the just lawgiver into:

1. pure but incomplete law;
2. law mixed with inferiority/injustice;
3. symbolic/allegorical law.

He identifies the Decalogue with the first category.

### FLORA-V1-003

Proposition:
The Decalogue is positively valued as pure but incomplete legislation requiring
completion by the Savior.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Consequence:
Ptolemy's relation to Jewish scripture is hierarchical and revisionary, not
simple repudiation.

## Logical unit 4 — retaliation law

Ptolemy uses retaliation ("eye for eye") as an example of legislation he calls
just in a limited/accommodative sense yet inferior to the perfect Father's
goodness.

### FLORA-V1-004

Proposition:
Ptolemy distinguishes juridical justice from the goodness of the highest
Father.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Guard:
Do not paraphrase this as "Ptolemy says the creator is evil." His own argument
explicitly rejects that classification.

## Logical unit 5 — symbolic ritual legislation

Ptolemy treats offerings, circumcision, Sabbath, fasting, Passover, unleavened
bread, and similar commands as symbolic forms whose literal application is
transposed into spiritual practice after the Savior.

Examples in the letter include:
- sacrifice -> praise, thanksgiving, sharing, doing good;
- circumcision -> circumcision of the heart;
- Sabbath -> abstention from evil works;
- fasting -> abstention from evil;
- Passover/unleavened bread -> Pauline spiritualized interpretation.

### FLORA-V1-005

Proposition:
Ptolemy preserves ritual vocabulary while relocating its central meaning from
literal observance to spiritual/moral performance.

Evidence class:
`PRIMARY TEXT`

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Guard:

`SPIRITUALIZATION != TOTAL_RITUAL_ABOLITION`

## Logical unit 6 — claimed external fasting practice

The letter explicitly says that "among us" external fasting is also observed
when done reasonably, while rejecting habitual or merely calendar-driven
performance as sufficient.

### FLORA-V1-006

Proposition:
Ptolemy's letter claims some external fasting practice among the community
implied by "among us."

Evidence class:
`PRIMARY TEXT`

Derived class:
`HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION` for community practice.

Status:
`PARTIAL / MODERATE`

Why not STRONG:
- it is a normative theological letter, not an observational ethnography;
- "among us" is socially imprecise;
- the surviving text does not identify frequency, location, institutional
  setting, or the full population practicing it.

Guard:

`TEXTUAL_CLAIM_OF_PRACTICE != MATERIAL_OR_DOCUMENTARY_CONFIRMATION_OF_PRACTICE`

This is nevertheless useful evidence that Ptolemy's hermeneutic was not simply
"all external observance disappears."

## Logical unit 7 — fulfillment / abolition / transformation

Ptolemy gives different outcomes to the three categories of divine law:

- pure but incomplete -> fulfilled/completed;
- mixed with inferior justice -> abolished by the Savior's contrary teaching;
- symbolic -> translated/transformed into spiritual meaning.

### FLORA-V1-007

Proposition:
Ptolemy's law theology uses three distinct transformation mechanisms, not one
blanket rejection mechanism.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

## Logical unit 8 — Pauline validation

Ptolemy invokes Paul to support his reading, including:
- 1 Corinthians 5 imagery of Passover/unleavened bread;
- Ephesians 2 language concerning commandments/ordinances;
- Romans 7 language calling the law/commandment holy, just, and good.

### FLORA-V1-008

Proposition:
Ptolemy presents his interpretation as Christian scriptural exegesis using
Gospel/Savior sayings and Pauline texts together.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Consequence:
Valentinian interpretation here is not operating outside Christian scripture;
it is an argument about how Christian scripture adjudicates Jewish scripture.

## Logical unit 9 — the demiurge as intermediate and just

Ptolemy identifies the lawgiver/creator as the demiurge, distinct from:
- the perfect Father;
- the destructive adversary.

He characterizes the demiurge as intermediate and just rather than perfectly
good or evil.

### FLORA-V1-009

Proposition:
Ptolemy explicitly rejects identifying the creator/demiurge with the Devil.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

### FLORA-V1-010

Proposition:
Ptolemy places the demiurge hierarchically below the perfect Father and above
the destructive adversary.

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_FOR_PTOLEMY`

Guard:
Do not generalize this exact tripartite hierarchy to every Valentinian text or
every group modern scholarship has called "gnostic."

## Logical unit 10 — advanced teaching deferred

The letter closes by deferring the "origin and generation" of the cosmic
natures until Flora is worthy of the "apostolic tradition" Ptolemy says "we"
received by succession.

### FLORA-V1-011

Proposition:
Ptolemy represents his deeper cosmological teaching as an advanced apostolic
tradition received through succession.

Evidence class:
`PRIMARY TEXT`

Status:
`BOUNDED / STRONG_AS_SELF_CLAIM`

Historical ceiling:
The letter proves that Ptolemy makes this authority claim. It does not establish
that the claimed succession historically reaches the apostles.

Guard:

`CLAIMED_APOSTOLIC_SUCCESSION != VERIFIED_APOSTOLIC_TRANSMISSION`

## Structural result

The letter's logic can be represented as:

`TWO_TOTALIZING_EXTREMES_REJECTED`
->
`LAW_HAS_MULTIPLE_HUMAN_DIVINE_SOURCES`
->
`DIVINE_LAW_HAS_THREE_CLASSES`
->
`SAVIOR_FULFILLS_ABOLISHES_TRANSFORMS_DIFFERENTLY`
->
`CREATOR_IS_JUST_INTERMEDIATE_NOT_DEVIL`
->
`DEEPER_COSMOLOGY_RESERVED_AS_ADVANCED_TRADITION`

This is materially more precise than:

`VALENTINIAN = OT_BAD / NT_GOOD`

## Downstream Testament ceiling

Book V may safely say:
- Ptolemy did not treat Mosaic law as one undifferentiated evil block;
- he distinguished God/Moses/elders as legislative sources within his
  hermeneutic;
- he distinguished pure, inferior, and symbolic legal material;
- he spiritualized several ritual commands while still claiming some external
  fasting among "us";
- he treated the demiurge as inferior to the highest Father but not as the
  Devil;
- he claimed an advanced apostolic tradition for deeper cosmological teaching.

Book V must not turn these into:
- modern source criticism;
- proof of actual apostolic succession;
- universal Valentinian doctrine;
- proof that all Valentinian communities practiced the same fasts;
- archaeological evidence for Valentinian ritual.

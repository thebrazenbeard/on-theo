## 4. Bumbacher's textual-dependence reconstruction

Stephan Peter Bumbacher's 2006 study directly compares the Buddhist and Daoist versions.

He argues that the Daoist material:
- makes direct use of the Buddhist text;
- attributes the teachings to Daoist Perfected/deities rather than to the Buddha;
- reorders the sections;
- omits a substantial portion;
- changes the revelatory frame so the teaching becomes a recent, direct revelation to Yang Xi.

Source:
Stephan Peter Bumbacher, "A Buddhist Sūtra's Transformation into a Daoist Text," *Asiatische Studien* 60.4 (2006), 799–831.

Stable text access:
https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=ast-002%3A2006%3A60%3A%3A1190

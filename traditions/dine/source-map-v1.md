## 2. Diné College educational philosophy

Public philosophy:
Sa'ah Naagháíí Bik'eh Hózhóón.

Diné College describes it as:
- a Diné traditional living system;
- placing human life in harmony with natural world/universe;
- framework for well-being;
- organized through Nitsáhákees, Nahat'á, Iiná, Siihasin;
- guiding education, planning, research, language/history/culture work.

## 3. Navajo Heritage and Historic Preservation Department

Mission:
protect/preserve Diné cultural heritage and traditions under Navajo fundamental
law and preservation law.

Source:
https://hpd.navajo-nsn.gov/

High-value policy:
Navajo Nation Policy for Disposition of Cultural Resources Collections.

Key public methodological content:
- ethnographic activities require permits/consultation;
- sensitive ceremonial information has access/dissemination restrictions;
- some information should not be collected;
- informed consent and confidentiality required.

Sources:
https://hpd.navajo-nsn.gov/Laws-Regulations-and-Policies
and linked cultural-resources policy PDF.

Evidence:
INDIGENOUS RESEARCH-GOVERNANCE POLICY / DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE.

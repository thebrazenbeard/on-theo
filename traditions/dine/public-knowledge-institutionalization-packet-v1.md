# Diné Public Knowledge Governance and Institutionalization Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC INSTITUTIONAL / KNOWLEDGE-GOVERNANCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Describe how current Diné/Navajo Nation institutions publicly preserve,
authorize, transmit, protect, and limit access to cultural/religious knowledge
without reproducing restricted knowledge.

Core rule:

KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE_CAN_BE_STUDIED_WITHOUT_DISCLOSING_GOVERNED_CONTENT.

## 1. Navajo Heritage and Historic Preservation Department

HHPD is the Navajo Nation cultural-heritage regulatory body.

Its public mission includes:
- protecting and preserving Diné cultural heritage/traditions;
- guidance through Navajo Fundamental Laws;
- administration of preservation/archaeological/cultural-resource law;
- consultation with communities and external entities.

The Cultural Resources Compliance Section:
- coordinates cultural-resource consultations;
- issues permits for cultural-resource investigation;
- regulates collection of ethnographic information on Navajo Nation lands.

Current axis:

INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
BOUNDED_CULTURAL_RESOURCE_GOVERNANCE.

## 2. Research is a governed relationship

Navajo Nation cultural-resource policy requires:
- consultation;
- permits where applicable;
- research designs attentive to traditional concerns;
- informed consent;
- confidentiality;
- tribal oversight for sensitive information.

This means:

RESEARCHER_ACCESS != UNILATERAL_ACADEMIC_RIGHT.

Current methodological proposition:

DINE_KNOWLEDGE_RESEARCH_IS_RELATIONALLY_AND_INSTITUTIONALLY_GOVERNED.

## 3. Levels of publicness

The public policy implies at least four research-access categories:

1. PUBLICLY_SHAREABLE
2. PUBLIC_EXISTENCE_BUT_RESTRICTED_CONTENT
3. CONDITIONAL_ACCESS
4. SHOULD_NOT_BE_COLLECTED_OR_DISSEMINATED

These are project analytical categories derived from public policy, not Diné
native technical terms.

ON_THEO will record the category, not try to defeat it.

## 4. Traditional Apprenticeship Project

The Navajo Nation Traditional Apprenticeship Project publicly establishes:
- that multiple traditional healing/ceremonial systems exist;
- that practitioners preserve and transmit ceremonial knowledge;
- that family/clan stewardship matters;
- that some ceremonial traditions face practitioner decline;
- that the Nation supports apprenticeship to preserve practitioner continuity.

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE / MODERN TRADITION /
CURRENT INDIGENOUS INSTITUTIONALIZATION.

Current proposition:

CEREMONIAL_KNOWLEDGE_TRANSMISSION_CAN_BE_STRUCTURED_THROUGH_AUTHORIZED_INTERGENERATIONAL_APPRENTICESHIP.

The project does not reproduce:
- named private songs;
- ceremonial sequences;
- paraphernalia instructions;
- sandpainting designs;
- initiation details.

## 5. Diné College

Diné College publicly institutionalizes:
- Sa'ah Naagháíí Bik'eh Hózhóón;
- Diné language;
- history;
- culture;
- philosophy;
- educational/research frameworks.

The College's current Board of Regents structure includes an appointed role for
Diné spiritual/ceremonial knowledge.

This demonstrates that cultural/religious knowledge has a recognized place in
modern educational governance.

Current proposition:

DINE_EDUCATIONAL_INSTITUTIONS_CAN_FORMALLY_EMBED_CULTURAL_AND_SPIRITUAL_KNOWLEDGE_AUTHORITY.

This is not:
COLLEGE = CEREMONIAL_AUTHORITY_FOR_ALL_DINÉ.

## 6. Law and education are distinct knowledge institutions

Navajo Nation courts/law publicly stabilize:
- Fundamental Law;
- k'é;
- natural-law principles;
- sovereignty/governance relation.

Diné College publicly stabilizes:
- educational philosophy;
- language/culture curriculum;
- knowledge transmission.

HHPD stabilizes:
- cultural-resource protection;
- confidentiality;
- research permissions.

Traditional apprenticeship stabilizes:
- practitioner succession.

Therefore:

DINE_INSTITUTIONALIZATION_MODEL =
MULTI_INSTITUTIONAL.

No single institution exhausts religious/traditional authority.

## 7. Oral transmission and permanent-record caution

Navajo Nation policy explicitly states that selected sacred/ceremonial knowledge
is traditionally transmitted orally and is not intended for unrestricted
permanent representation.

Current implication:

ORALITY_CAN_BE_A_KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE_CHOICE.

Not:
ORALITY = LACK_OF_COMPLEX_KNOWLEDGE.

Not:
WRITING = MORE_AUTHORITATIVE.

- public educational formulation.

Current axis:

KNOWLEDGE_OR_DISCLOSURE_MODEL =
BOUNDED_PUBLIC_GOVERNANCE_AND_TRANSMISSION_LAYER.

## 9. Continuity model

Modern institutions actively preserve and transmit Diné language/culture,
Fundamental Law, ceremonial-practitioner continuity, and cultural resources.

Current axis:

CONTINUITY_MODEL =
PARTIAL_INSTITUTIONAL_CONTINUITY_WITH_CHANGE.

Why PARTIAL:
modern legal/college/program forms are historically situated and do not prove
unchanged institutional form across earlier periods.

## 10. Authority model

Public evidence demonstrates multiple authority domains:
- law/courts;
- cultural-resource regulators;
- traditional practitioners;

Current status:

intersect restricted knowledge.

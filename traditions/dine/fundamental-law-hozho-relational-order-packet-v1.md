# Diné Fundamental Law, Hózhó, and Relational Order Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC LAW / EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish a first Diné internal reconstruction using only high-authority public
Diné/Navajo Nation sources.

This packet does not reconstruct ceremonies.

## 1. Public law presents Diné life as relational and ordered

The Navajo Nation Judicial Branch publishes the Fundamental Laws of the Diné.

The public legal text distinguishes:
- Traditional Law;
- Customary Law;
- Natural Law;
- Common Law.

These laws are presented as foundations for:
- Diné life/culture;
- relation with the wider world;
- balance with natural world;
- leadership;
- sovereignty;
- government.

Current proposition:

DINE_PUBLIC_FUNDAMENTAL_LAW =
RELATIONAL_LEGAL_COSMOLOGICAL_ORDER.

Evidence:
CURRENT INDIGENOUS LAW / DOCUMENTARY PRACTICE.

This is a current sovereign articulation.

It is not a verbatim ancient text.

## 5. Natural Law

Diné Natural Law publicly identifies:
- air;
- light/fire;
- water;
- earth/pollen

as sacred elements sustaining life.

It also states:
- six sacred mountains must be respected/protected;
- all creation has its own laws/right to exist;
- Diné have duties toward these relations;
- Mother Earth/Father Sky and Diné are mutually related;
- sacred sites/resources are approached through proper respect/protocol and
  offering;
- natural world is to be preserved for future generations.

Current result:

DINE_PUBLIC_NATURAL_LAW =
RELATIONAL_ECOLOGICAL_SACRED_OBLIGATION.

This is stronger than generic environmental ethics because it is current Diné
law.

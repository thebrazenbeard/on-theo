# Lakota Public Source Map V1

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE MAP / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Record public sources currently safe and useful for a first internally bounded
Lakota research lane.

This is not a bibliography of everything published about Lakota religion.

## A. Tribal and tribal-college sources

### Oglala Lakota College

Graduate Studies / Lakota Leadership and Management.

Public relevance:
- explicitly grounds leadership education in Lakota language, spirituality,
  beliefs, values, thoughts, philosophies, and worldview;
- demonstrates living institutional continuity rather than a museum-only
  religion.

URL:
https://www.olc.edu/admissions-aid/degree-programs/graduate-studies/

Lakota Thought & Philosophy course description.

Public relevance:
- frames Lakota thought in relation to the universe, supernatural, and all
  creation.

URL:
https://exweb.olc.edu/ICS/Academics/LSOC/LSOC_313/2019_FA-LSOC_313-35___PR/Course_Information.jnz

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / MODERN_TRADITION.

### Sitting Bull College

Division of Native American Studies.

Public relevance:
- Lakota/Dakota culture, history, worldview, ceremonies, values, belief,
  kinship, and social systems are explicit fields of study.

URL:
https://www.sittingbull.edu/academics/native-american-studies/

Lakota/Dakota language and culture resources.

Public relevance:
- preserves a large archive/resource pathway, including Dakota/Lakota-language

URL:
https://www.sittingbull.edu/about/community/library/everything-/

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / MODERN_TRADITION / SOURCE_DISCOVERY.

### Standing Rock Sioux Tribe

Title VI Children's Code.

Public relevance:
- current tribal law explicitly incorporates traditional laws;
- publicly names Wakan Tanka in current legal/ethical language;
- publicly lists seven ceremonies in its legal-cultural framework;
- defines tiospaye as foundational social structure.

URL:
https://www.standingrock.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Title-VIChildrensCode.pdf

Cultural Resource Code.

Public relevance:
- links cultural heritage and sacred-site preservation to spiritual, physical,
  mental, and emotional well-being;
- establishes tribal protection of sacred/cultural resources.

URL:
https://www.standingrock.org/wp-content/uploads/mdocs/Title%20XXXII%20-%20%2832%29%20Cultural%20Resource%20Code.pdf

Standing Rock Constitution.

Public relevance:
- current constitutional recognition of Ahte Wakan Tanka.

URL:
https://www.standingrock.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Standing-Rock-Sioux-Tribe-Constitution.pdf

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / MODERN_TRADITION.

Guard:
STANDING_ROCK_LAKOTA_DAKOTA_SOURCE != UNIVERSAL_LAKOTA_FORMULATION.

## C. Religious-freedom and suppression context

### 42 USC 1996 — American Indian Religious Freedom Act

Public relevance:
- federal policy since 1978 recognizes protection/preservation of traditional
  Indigenous religious belief, ceremonial exercise, sacred objects, and site
  access.

URL:
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1996

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE.

### Library of Congress — Sioux Song / Sun Dance historical context

Public relevance:
- summarizes suppression of Sun Dance and later public/private practice;
- notes that some contemporary ceremonies restrict outsider attendance.

URL:
https://www.loc.gov/collections/songs-of-america/articles-and-essays/musical-styles/american-indian-and-native-alaskan/sioux/

Evidence class:
HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION / PUBLIC_ARCHIVAL_CONTEXT.

## F. Current exclusions

Not yet source-bound strongly enough for V1:
- one complete Lakota creation/cosmogony;
- one universal postmortem map;
- one universal soul ontology;
- complete Sun Dance procedural reconstruction;
- complete vision-quest procedural reconstruction;
- one exhaustive account of Wakan Tanka;
- one timeless "seven rites" system for every Lakota community and period;
- restricted songs, prayers, bundle knowledge, or initiatory teachings.

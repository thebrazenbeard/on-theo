# Lakota Public Vision and Disclosure Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC HIGH-LEVEL KNOWLEDGE/DISCLOSURE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

This packet asks a narrow question:

How do current public Lakota/community sources represent vision, prayer,
guidance, wisdom, and the acquisition or transmission of religious knowledge?

It does not reconstruct:
- a vision-seeking procedure;
- private revelation content;
- restricted songs or prayers;
- specialist initiation;
- diagnostic claims about altered states.

Core rule:

PUBLIC_EXISTENCE_OF_VISION_SEEKING != AUTHORITY_TO_REPRODUCE_ITS_PROCEDURE.

## 1. Standing Rock legal recognition of vision seeking

Standing Rock Sioux Tribe's current Children's Code publicly names
Hanbleceyapi as "seeking a vision" among seven ceremonies.

The same legal section frames the ceremonies as means of:
- cleansing;
- seeking guidance;
- seeking direction.

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / MODERN_TRADITION.

Strong proposition:

VISION_SEEKING_IS_A_PUBLICLY_RECOGNIZED_CURRENT_RELIGIOUS_CATEGORY_IN_STANDING_ROCK_TRADITIONAL_LAW.

This is not merely an outsider ethnographic label.

Source:
Standing Rock Sioux Tribe, Title VI Children's Code.

## 2. Vision seeking belongs to a wider prayer/guidance relation

The Wakan/Wakan Tanka packet independently established current public language
of:
- prayer;
- wisdom;
- guidance;
- blessing.

Therefore the project should not isolate Hanbleceyapi as a self-contained
"vision technology."

At the current ceiling:

VISION_SEEKING_BELONGS_TO_A_BROADER_RELATIONAL_KNOWLEDGE_AND_PRAYER_FIELD.

The exact relation among:
- Wakan Tanka;
- Tunkasila;
- particular spirit beings;
- sacred places;
- specialists;
- individual seekers

remains source-specific and incompletely bound.

## 3. Bear Butte / Mato Paha as public vision-prayer place

South Dakota Game, Fish, and Parks publicly describes Bear Butte as:
- sacred to multiple Indigenous nations;
- a place of continuing religious ceremony;
- a place associated publicly with prayer and vision;
- a place where prayer offerings remain visible and protected.

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / PUBLIC_SITE_MANAGEMENT.

This supports:

VISION_AND_PRAYER_CAN_BE_PLACE_MEDIATED_IN_CURRENT_PUBLIC_PRACTICE.

Because the site is multi-tribal:

BEAR_BUTTE_PUBLIC_VISION_LANGUAGE != EXCLUSIVELY_LAKOTA_DOCTRINE.

## 4. Pine Ridge / Badlands public prayer and vision context

National Park Service public interpretation of the Stronghold District states
that Lakota people continue to use selected places for prayer and vision
seeking.

The same page asks visitors to respect visible signs of traditional worship.

Evidence class:
PUBLIC_SITE_INTERPRETATION / CONTEMPORARY_PRACTICE_ORIENTATION.

Current proposition:

PUBLIC_LANDSCAPE_DOCUMENTATION_SUPPORTS_CONTINUING_LAKOTA_PRAYER_AND_VISION_RELATION_TO_PLACE.

It does not authorize procedure reconstruction.

## 5. Oglala Lakota College: knowledge is transmitted through listening and sharing

Oglala Lakota College's public "Lakota Perspective" describes the worldview as:
- derived from oral tradition;
- living and dynamic;
- handed from generation to generation;
- dependent on listening and sharing knowledge and wisdom.

Evidence class:
COMMUNITY_EDUCATIONAL_SELF_DESCRIPTION / MODERN_TRADITION.

This matters because religious knowledge is not modeled solely as an isolated
individual revelation event.

Current proposition:

KNOWLEDGE_TRANSMISSION_INCLUDES_COMMUNAL_ORAL_LISTENING_AND_SHARING.

Thus the broader neutral axis must distinguish:
- visionary disclosure;
- prayer/guidance;
- oral intergenerational transmission;
- specialist mediation;
- public education.

## 6. Lakota-authored knowledge-governance source

The 2023 Lakota Traditional Sciences paper states that Lakota authors:
- hold, nurture, and caretake knowledge on behalf of the Oyate;
- intentionally chose how to introduce knowledge publicly;
- distinguish safe/respectful introduction from unrestricted extraction.

Evidence class:
COMMUNITY_AUTHORED_PUBLIC_SOURCE / MODERN_TRADITION.

This adds a critical dimension:

DISCLOSURE_IS_NOT_ONLY_ABOUT_RECEIVING_KNOWLEDGE;
IT_IS_ALSO_ABOUT_GOVERNING_WHAT_MAY_BE_SHARED.

Current proposition:

KNOWLEDGE_DISCLOSURE_HAS_COMMUNITY_GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARIES.

## 7. Black Elk as a historical vision source

Black Elk's well-known visionary narratives are historically important.

But they are preserved through distinct mediated documentary chains:
- Neihardt / Black Elk Speaks;
- Brown / The Sacred Pipe;
- later biographical and Catholic sources.

Therefore:

BLACK_ELK_VISION != UNMEDIATED_TEMPLATE_FOR_ALL_LAKOTA_VISION_SEEKING.

Black Elk may be used for historical case study only after the specific witness
chain is controlled.

The current packet does not reproduce vision content.

## 8. Vision, prophecy, divination, and dream must remain separate

English-language comparison can easily collapse:
- vision;
- dream;
- prophecy;
- omen;
- divination;
- intuition;
- prayer;
- spiritual guidance.

This packet does not authorize that collapse.

Current required schema:

DISCLOSURE_MODE:
- VISION_SEEKING;
- PRAYER_AND_GUIDANCE;
- ORAL_TRANSMISSION;
- SPECIALIST_INTERPRETATION;
- DREAM;
- OTHER;
- UNKNOWN.

Each claim must identify its source mode.

## 9. Agency and epistemic status

Current public sources support:

SOURCE_INTERNAL_KNOWLEDGE_CLAIM:
people may seek or receive guidance through recognized religious practice.

They do not establish for the project:

EMPIRICALLY_VERIFIED_SUPERNATURAL_INFORMATION_TRANSFER.

Evidence class must remain:

MODERN_TRADITION / DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / COMMUNITY_SELF_DESCRIPTION.

The project records what the tradition/source asserts or practices without
turning that into an external scientific verdict.

## 11. What is not admitted

Not admitted:
- one standard vision-seeking procedure;
- one universal interpretation of all visions;
- vision as equivalent to prophecy;
- vision as equivalent to hallucination;
- all religious knowledge as visionary;
- all Lakota people as vision practitioners;
- private or restricted vision content;
- specialist ritual authority structure as fully reconstructed.

This is an internal reconstruction result.

It is not yet a cross-tradition comparison.

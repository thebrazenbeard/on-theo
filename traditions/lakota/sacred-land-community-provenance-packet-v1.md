# Lakota Sacred Land and Community Provenance Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC-SOURCE PLACE/PROVENANCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

This packet replaces the vague proposition:

THE_BLACK_HILLS_ARE_SACRED_TO_THE_LAKOTA

with source-bounded place claims that preserve:
- named place;
- community/source provenance;
- historical layer;
- public versus restricted knowledge;
- whether the evidence is community-authored, community-transmitted, or outside
  public interpretation.

Core rule:

SACRED_LAND != ONE_UNDIFFERENTIATED_GEOGRAPHIC_SYMBOL.

A second core rule:

ONE_PLACE_NARRATIVE != ONE_LAKOTA_COSMOGONY.

## 1. Wind Cave / Oniya Oshoka / Maka Oniye

### Provenanced Oglala emergence account

Wind Cave National Park publicly presents one Lakota emergence narrative with
unusually clear provenance.

The park states that:
- Lakota emergence accounts vary by band and family;
- the version presented comes from the Cheyenne Creek community on the Pine
  Ridge Indian Reservation;
- the account was told by Wilmer Mesteth, identified as a tribal historian and
  spiritual leader;
- Sina Bear Eagle retells the account.

This makes the source substantially stronger than an anonymous "Sioux legend."

Evidence class:
COMMUNITY_TRANSMITTED_PUBLIC_NARRATIVE / PUBLIC_INSTITUTIONAL_PRESENTATION.

### Controlled propositions in this version

The source represents:
- a spirit lodge below the surface;
- a breathing-earth passage associated with the natural entrance;
- people existing below before emergence to the surface;
- an initial premature emergence by some people;
- transformation of those people into the first bison herd;
- a later instructed emergence led by Tokahe;
- bison as guides/providers for life on the surface;
- the cave entrance as a continuing reminder of emergence.

Current bounded result:

OGLALA_CHEYENNE_CREEK_WIND_CAVE_ACCOUNT =
HUMAN_AND_BISON_EMERGENCE_FROM_A_SPIRIT_WORLD_PLACE.

This supports a new internal axis:

HUMAN_ORIGIN_OR_EMERGENCE_MODEL = BOUNDED_ONE_OGLALA_VERSION.

It does not yet support:

WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL = GENERAL_LAKOTA_COSMOGONY.

The world and many beings are already present within the narrative frame.

### Place-name control

Public NPS materials record:
- Oniya Oshoka as an older/ancestral "earth breathes inside" expression;
- Maka Oniye as a modern Lakota expression meaning "breathing earth."

These glosses are PUBLIC_INTERPRETATION and require future language-community
verification before lexical canonization.

Source:
https://www.nps.gov/wica/learn/historyculture/the-lakota-emergence-story.htm

## 2. Wind Cave as living sacred place, not only mythic setting

NPS public materials describe the natural entrance as a spiritual place and
state that multiple Native nations maintain ties to Wind Cave.

For Lakota tradition specifically, public interpretation links the entrance to:
- the spirit lodge;
- emergence of people;
- emergence of bison;
- breathing-earth imagery.

Evidence class:
PUBLIC_SITE_INTERPRETATION / HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.

Strong proposition:

PLACE_AND_ORIGIN_NARRATIVE_ARE_INTERDEPENDENT_IN_THE_CONTROLLED_WIND_CAVE_ACCOUNT.

This differs from a purely abstract creation story.

Guard:

NPS_INTERPRETATION_NE_COMPLETE_COMMUNITY_THEOLOGY.

## 3. Bear Butte / Mato Paha

South Dakota Game, Fish, and Parks publicly identifies:
- Mato Paha / Bear Mountain as the Lakota name for Bear Butte;
- the mountain as sacred to multiple Indigenous nations;
- continuing religious ceremonies;
- prayer cloths and tobacco ties as current offerings that visitors must not
  disturb.

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / PUBLIC_SITE_MANAGEMENT.

Secure current proposition:

MATO_PAHA_IS_A_LIVING_PUBLICLY_DOCUMENTED_CEREMONIAL_PLACE_WITH_CONTEMPORARY_OFFERINGS.

The state page also describes communication with the Creator through visions and
prayer in broad Indigenous terms.

Because that statement is multi-tribal and not specifically sourced to one
Lakota speaker:

CREATOR_COMMUNICATION_AT_MATO_PAHA =
PUBLIC_SITE_INTERPRETATION
not
LAKOTA_THEOLOGY_PRIMARY_SOURCE.

Source:
https://gfp.sd.gov/parks/detail/bear-butte-state-park/

## 4. Bear Lodge / Mato Tipila

National Park Service documentation records:
- Bear Lodge / Grizzly Bear's Lodge and related names as Lakota names for the
  monument commonly called Devils Tower;
- long-standing Lakota connections with the Black Hills and Bear Lodge;
- modern Indigenous ceremonial use;
- strong present-day concern over the imposed "Devils Tower" name.

Evidence class:
PUBLIC_SITE_INTERPRETATION / HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.

The strongest V1 proposition is:

BEAR_LODGE_IS_A_LIVING_SACRED_SITE_WITH_LAKOTA_HISTORICAL_AND_PRESENT_RELATION.

The detailed ritual and oral-history claims on the park site derive from mixed
historical sources.

Therefore they remain below a community-authored primary-source ceiling until
independently rebound.

Sources:
https://www.nps.gov/articles/devilstower.htm
https://www.nps.gov/deto/learn/historyculture/americanindians.htm

## 5. Black Hills / He Sapa / Paha Sapa

The current public record supports the Black Hills as a region of continuing
Lakota sacred geography.

But the project will not treat "Black Hills" as one place with one meaning.

Current site-specific evidence includes:
- Wind Cave emergence;
- Bear Butte ceremonial use;
- Bear Lodge sacred relation;
- additional sites in NPS sacred-site interpretation.

Therefore:

BLACK_HILLS_SACRED_GEOGRAPHY =
NETWORK_OF_PLACE_RELATIONS
rather than
ONE_UNDIFFERENTIATED_HOLY_MOUNTAIN.

NPS material can orient the network.

Community-specific source binding is still needed for each site's stronger
religious propositions.

## 6. Sicangu environmental relation

A Sicangu/Rosebud climate-adaptation plan publicly frames:
- water;
- springs;
- Thunder Beings;
- land;
- sacred sites and burials;
- future generations;
- treaty responsibility

inside one environmental and cultural framework.

This is important because sacred-land relationship appears inside present-day
planning, not only historical myth.

Evidence class:
COMMUNITY_GROUNDED_DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE.

Current bounded proposition:

SACRED_LAND_RELATION_CAN_STRUCTURE_CONTEMPORARY_ENVIRONMENTAL_GOVERNANCE_AND_INTERGENERATIONAL_OBLIGATION.

The current source copy is externally hosted and large; stronger provenance
binding to the originating Sicangu working group should be added before this
becomes a high-ceiling claim.

## 7. Sacred land is not interchangeable with property

Across the controlled public sources, place relation can include:
- emergence/origin;
- prayer;
- ceremony;
- offering;
- memory;
- burial;
- guidance;
- environmental obligation;
- intergenerational responsibility.

Therefore:

SACRED_LAND_RELATION != OWNERSHIP_ONLY.

This is a comparative-method warning, not a political/legal conclusion about a
specific land claim.

## 8. Origin and sacred geography must remain separate axes

The Wind Cave material shows why the project needs both:

SACRED_LAND_PLACE_MODEL

and

HUMAN_ORIGIN_OR_EMERGENCE_MODEL.

A place can be sacred for reasons beyond origin.

An origin narrative can be local and community-specific.

Therefore:

SACRED_PLACE != UNIVERSAL_CREATION_SITE.

# Wakan / Wakan Tanka Semantic and Community-Source Packet V1

Status: PUBLIC-SOURCE SEMANTIC CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

This packet tests a common failure mode in outsider descriptions of Lakota
religion:

WAKAN_TANKA = ONE_SIMPLE_ENGLISH_THEOLOGICAL_CATEGORY.

Current public Lakota/community evidence does not support that reduction.

The packet distinguishes:
- wakȟáŋ / wakan as a broader Lakota semantic category;
- Wakan Tanka as a specific expression/referent in public religious language;
- Tunkasila / Grandfather address;
- English glosses such as Great Mystery, Great Spirit, Creator, sacred,
  mysterious, power, or related terms.

Core rule:

ONE_ENGLISH_GLOSS != ONE_EXHAUSTIVE_LAKOTA_THEOLOGY.

A second core rule:

WAKAN_POLYSEMY != WAKAN_TANKA_ONTOLOGY_BY_ITSELF.

## 1. Contemporary Standing Rock legal usage

### Children's Code, Title VI

Standing Rock Sioux Tribe's current Children's Code incorporates Oyate Ta Woope,
traditional laws, and states that approximate English translations are provided
but the Lakota term governs.

Within Wocekiya, the Code publicly describes prayer to Tunkasila or Wakan Tanka
and gives English language including:
- Great Spirit;
- supreme being and power;
- creator of all that is.

The same public legal text connects Wakan Tanka with the seven sacred
ceremonies.

Evidence class:
DOCUMENTARY_PRACTICE / MODERN_TRADITION.

Strong proposition:

CONTEMPORARY_STANDING_ROCK_PUBLIC_LAW_CAN_USE_WAKAN_TANKA_AS_AGENTIVE_CREATOR_AND_SUPREME_POWER_LANGUAGE.

But the Code itself supplies the anti-overreach rule:

ENGLISH_TRANSLATION_IS_APPROXIMATE;
LAKOTA_TERM_GOVERNS.

Therefore:

"Great Spirit" != exhaustive lexical identity.

"Creator" != proof that every historical Lakota formulation was equivalent to a
Christian creator doctrine.

Source:
https://www.standingrock.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Title-VIChildrensCode.pdf

## 2. Contemporary Standing Rock public prayer/address

### 2025 State Tribal Address

Standing Rock Chairwoman Janet Alkire publicly says that when the community
meets they call upon "Wakan Tanka Tunkasila," glossed in the address as
"our Grandfather the Creator," to guide and watch over them.

She thanks the Creator for life and asks blessings upon the people.

Evidence class:
MODERN_TRADITION / COMMUNITY_SELF_DESCRIPTION / PUBLIC_SPEECH.

Strong proposition:

CURRENT_PUBLIC_STANDING_ROCK_USAGE_CAN_ADDRESS_WAKAN_TANKA_RELATIONALLY_AND_AGENTIVELY.

The phrase is not merely an abstract label in this source context.

Source:
https://www.standingrock.org/2025/01/18/nd-state-tribal-address-standing-rock-sioux-tribe-chairwoman-janet-alkire-january-7-2025-north-dakota-state-capitol-house-of-representatives-chamber/

### 2025 Standing Rock statement on DAPL litigation

A second current tribal statement describes leaders praying and asking Wakan
Tanka for wisdom and guidance to protect the next seven generations.

This independently supports:
- prayer-address;
- guidance language;
- future-generational ethical relation.

It does not establish one philosophical theory of divine agency.

Source:
https://www.standingrock.org/2025/03/03/standing-rock-sioux-tribal-chairwoman-janet-alkire-statement-on-the-energy-transfer-lawsuit-against-greenpeace/

## 3. Lakota-authored "Great Mystery" translation

The 2023 Maxwell Museum Technical Series publication
"Standing for Unci Maka (Grandmother Earth) and All Life" is authored by a
Lakota Traditional Internal Review Committee and representative scholar.

Its preface begins with Lakota language addressed to Wakan Tanka.

Anpetu Luta Win / Dr. Antonia Loretta Afraid of Bear-Cook translates the phrase
as closely as possible into English beginning with:

"Great Mystery ..."

The same publication explicitly states that the Lakota authors are caretakers of
knowledge on behalf of the Oyate and chose the publication arrangement to retain
rights and decision-making power over that knowledge.

Evidence class:
COMMUNITY_AUTHORED_PUBLIC_SOURCE / MODERN_TRADITION.

Strong proposition:

CURRENT_LAKOTA_AUTHORED_USAGE_CAN_RENDER_WAKAN_TANKA_AS_GREAT_MYSTERY.

This is not evidence against Standing Rock's "Creator" or "Great Spirit"
language.

It is evidence against pretending one English gloss exhausts the Lakota term.

Source:
Maxwell Museum Technical Series No. 42 (2023),
"Standing for Unci Maka (Grandmother Earth) and All Life."

## 4. Wakan as a broader category

David C. Posthumus's source-critical Oglala study discusses wakȟáŋ as a broad
semantic/religious category.

His synthesis, drawing in part on historical Lower Brule fieldwork, distinguishes
uses involving:
- impersonal/general sacred or extraordinary potency;
- personalized/specific other-than-human persons or beings.

The historical interlocutor material he quotes explicitly warns that those two
analytical categories may not be experienced as sharply distinct in Lakota/Dakota
thought.

Evidence class:
SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION + HISTORICAL_ETHNOGRAPHIC_RECORD.

Important value:
English binaries such as
PERSONAL_BEING versus IMPERSONAL_FORCE
may themselves distort the semantic field.

Important limitation:
This scholarly model is not promoted into one timeless community doctrine.

Source:
David C. Posthumus,
"Transmitting Sacred Knowledge: Aspects of Historical and Contemporary Oglala
Lakota Belief and Ritual" (2015).

## 5. Do not collapse Wakan and Wakan Tanka

The evidence requires at least two lexical/referential levels.

### wakȟáŋ / wakan

Current controlled orientation:
- sacred;
- mysterious/incomprehensible;
- potent/extraordinary;
- applicable in contexts involving beings, powers, acts, or qualities.

Claim ceiling:
SEMANTIC_FIELD_PARTIAL.

### Wakan Tanka

Current controlled public usages include:
- Great Mystery;
- Great Spirit;
- Creator;
- supreme being/power;
- relational addressee in prayer;
- guidance/blessing language.

Claim ceiling:
BOUNDED_CONTEMPORARY_PUBLIC_THEOLOGICAL_USAGE.

Therefore:

WAKAN = WAKAN_TANKA
as a simple lexical equation is invalid.

And:

WAKAN_TANKA = AN_IMPERSONAL_FORCE_ONLY
is not supported.

WAKAN_TANKA = A_PERSONAL_CREATOR_ONLY
as a timeless exhaustive definition is also not supported.

## 6. Relational address matters

The current community sources do more than define a noun.

They depict:
- calling upon Wakan Tanka/Tunkasila;
- asking for guidance;
- asking for wisdom;
- asking for blessing;
- thanking the Creator for life.

This supports:

RELATIONAL_AND_AGENTIVE_ADDRESS = BOUNDED_CURRENT_PUBLIC_USAGE.

It does not establish:
- omnipotence;
- omniscience;
- creation ex nihilo;
- exclusive monotheism;
- one systematic divine-attribute theology.

## 7. "Creator" must remain historically controlled

Current Lakota/Dakota public sources clearly use Creator language.

That makes the following move invalid:

CREATOR_LANGUAGE_IS_MERELY_OUTSIDER_INVENTION.

But the opposite move is also invalid:

CURRENT_CREATOR_LANGUAGE_PROVES_ONE_UNCHANGED_PRECONTACT_CREATOR_THEOLOGY.

Required future work:
- trace nineteenth- and twentieth-century Lakota-language Christian translation;
- compare missionary dictionaries and catechetical usage;
- compare Lakota-authored historical testimony;
- distinguish adoption, translation convergence, continuity, and reinterpretation.

Current status:

CREATOR_SEMANTIC_HISTORY = OPEN.

## 8. "Great Mystery" also must not be romanticized

The Lakota-authored 2023 translation gives strong contemporary authority to
"Great Mystery" in that source.

But:

GREAT_MYSTERY != IMPERSONAL_NEW_AGE_COSMIC_FORCE.

The same public Lakota record includes prayer, kinship, creator, guidance, and
other agentive/relational language.

The semantic field must remain internally plural.

## 9. Current model

The strongest defensible V1 model is:

WAKAN_TANKA_IS_A_CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_PUBLIC_LAKOTA_RELIGIOUS_REFERENT_EXPRESSED_THROUGH_MYSTERY_SACRED_POWER_CREATOR_AND_RELATIONAL_LANGUAGE_WITHOUT_ONE_ENGLISH_GLOSS_EXHAUSTING_ITS_MEANING.

This is a PROJECT HISTORICAL/SEMANTIC RECONSTRUCTION grounded in current public
community sources plus scholarship.

It is not a metaphysical verdict about what Wakan Tanka "really is."

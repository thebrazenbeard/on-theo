Status: LIVING ANDEAN / COMMUNITY-CONTINUITY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Bind publicly documented living Andean reciprocity, land, offering and Qhapaq
Ñan custodianship without treating current Quechua/Aymara communities as
unchanged survivals of Inca imperial religion.

LIVING_ANDEAN != IMPERIAL_INCA_RELIGION_TODAY.

## 6. Reciprocity across layers

Imperial Inca sources and living Andean sources can both use concepts translated
as reciprocity, mutual obligation, or redistribution.

But:

IMPERIAL_REDISTRIBUTION
!=
LIVING_COMMUNITY_AYNI_BY_DEFAULT.

One may be:
- state-organized labor/redistribution;
the other:
- local reciprocal community labor.

Shared vocabulary requires mechanism decomposition.

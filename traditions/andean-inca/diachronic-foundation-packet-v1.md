# Andean / Inca Diachronic Foundation Packet V1

Status: INTERNAL FOUNDATION / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Establish high-confidence internal Inca/Andean research lanes before
cross-tradition comparison.

## 1. Imperial order is not all Andean order

Tawantinsuyu integrated many peoples, languages and prior traditions.

Current project rule:

INCA_STATE_RELIGION =
ONE_LATE_HORIZON_IMPERIAL_FORMATION.

Provincial/local huacas and rites could be:
- incorporated;
- ranked;
- supported;
- transformed;
- suppressed;
- selectively retained.

Therefore:
imperial integration must not be read as religious homogenization.

## 8. Garcilaso control

Garcilaso is invaluable for:
- mestizo family memory;
- imperial tradition;
- linguistic interpretation;
- early colonial intellectual history.

But current scholarship warns his coherent civilizing/imperial narrative can
over-systematize earlier Inca history.

Therefore:

GARCILASO =
MAJOR_COLONIAL_INTERPRETER,
not
DEFAULT_CANONICAL_INCA_HISTORY.

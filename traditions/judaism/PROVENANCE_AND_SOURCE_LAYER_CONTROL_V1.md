## Why this exists

The Judaism lane has good methodological instincts: it rejects a monolithic first-century Judaism, keeps Yeshua inside a Jewish field, and warns that later rabbinic material is not a transparent transcript of the first century.

But the lane predates much of ON_THEO's structured source-control work.

The consequence is uneven evidentiary depth:

- Second Temple context now has later corpus support;
- Panthera/Pandera and paternity claims have a deep successor chain;
- the Yeshua reconstruction has a structured hypothesis map and hostile review;
- broad concept semantics remain largely prose synthesis;
- `sources.md` remains a reading spine, not proposition-level evidence.

This control makes those differences explicit without importing later branch bytes or rewriting the existing historical prose.

## Internal-plurality rule

`JUDAISM` is not one timeless theological object.

For every claim, ask:

- which period;
- which source;
- which community or author;
- which textual stratum;
- which witness;
- which later interpreter.

Shared Israelite/Jewish symbols do not imply identical meanings across centuries or groups.

## Source-layer rule

Keep distinct:

- Hebrew Bible / scriptural source;
- Second Temple text;
- material/manuscript evidence;
- Josephus or other ancient historiography;
- Christian textual portrayal;
- rabbinic compilation and textual witnesses;
- medieval counter-tradition;
- modern scholarly reconstruction;
- ON_THEO project inference.

A late source may preserve earlier material. That possibility is a research proposition, not automatic provenance.

## Concepts before Christianity

State: `PARTIAL_STRUCTURED_SUPPORT_SEMANTIC_PACKETS_INCOMPLETE`.

The older module correctly protects several distinctions, but they are not all equally source-controlled yet.

### Messiah

`ANOINTED/MESSIANIC_TITLE != GOD_INCARNATE`.

Diverse royal, priestly, prophetic, eschatological, and restoration expectations must not be collapsed into one universal job description.

### Son of Man

Daniel 7's "one like a human being" belongs first in its own literary/apocalyptic setting.

Later individual, messianic, heavenly, or Christian identifications are reception/interpretive developments that require separate evidence.

### Resurrection

Resurrection should remain distinct from a generic immortal-soul model.

Its Second Temple distribution and meaning must be source-specific.

### Gehenna

`GEHENNA != AUTOMATICALLY_MEDIEVAL_CHRISTIAN_HELL`.

Judgment imagery develops across Jewish and Christian traditions; later systematic models should not be projected backward without argument.

### Satan / hostile powers

`JOB_HA_SATAN != EVERY_LATER_SATAN_ONTOLOGY`.

Second Temple demonology and cosmic-conflict traditions materially expand the conceptual field, but development must stay visible.

These are high-value next semantic packets.

## Panthera / Pandera / paternity

State: `DEEP_SUCCESSOR_RESEARCH_AVAILABLE_NOT_IMPORTED`.

This is the deepest later research area touching the Judaism lane.

The successor chain now separates:

- Mark 6:3 wording and variants;
- John 8:41 `porneia` semantics and reception;
- Celsus/Origen source provenance;
- Panthera/parthenos wordplay;
- rabbinic Panteira/Pandera textual parallels;
- work identity from manuscript-witness identity;
- shared rabbinic tradition from independent historical attestation.

The current ceiling is narrower than the older prose can sound when read alone:

- a real ancient name `Pantera` does not identify a father;
- a late-second-century counter-story does not prove first-century Nazareth origin;
- a rabbinic parallel is not automatically an independent eyewitness;
- a medieval manuscript can preserve older material without itself dating that material;
- direct Celsus-to-rabbinic lineage remains unestablished;
- Mark 6:3 and John 8:41 do not independently prove Panthera biological paternity.

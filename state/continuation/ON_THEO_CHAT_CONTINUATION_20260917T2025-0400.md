## 5. Latest broad-corpus insights to preserve

### Egypt / Vedic
- Ancient Egyptian religion is a long-duration family of royal, temple, local, funerary, ritual, and theological systems; do not synthesize Pyramid Texts, Coffin Texts, Book of the Dead, and later systems as timeless doctrine.
- Maat links cosmic/social/ritual order and later funerary judgment but is not simply "morality", logos, rta, or natural law.
- Rigvedic religion is not later Hinduism in miniature.
- Mature karma/rebirth is not projected into the Rigveda.
- Early Upanishadic traditions are major evidence for historical development of karma, rebirth, atman/brahman and liberation.
- Maat <-> rta is currently `STRUCTURAL_PARALLEL / NO_LINEAGE_ESTABLISHED`.

### Early Buddhism
- Early Buddhist texts survive through school-specific textual traditions; reconstruction is comparative historical inference, not a transcript of Gautama.
- Early Buddhism shares a developing Indian karma/rebirth/liberation problem-space with Brahmanical traditions while materially diverging on self, causality, ritual authority, and liberation.
- Anatta/anatman is not equivalent to atman, nihilism, or materialism.
- Dependent origination is not automatically a Vedic cosmogony.
- Key project inference: **shared vocabulary can coexist with semantic inversion**. This should inform all later transmission studies.

## 6. Current Yeshua working model — preserve as inference, not fact

The strongest current minimal synthesis after hostile review:

- Yeshua was a first-century Jewish Galilean figure embedded in kingdom/apocalyptic expectation.
- His movement attributed unusual eschatological significance to him.
- Roman authority crucified him under Pilate; the exact Roman rationale and responsibility narrative remain reconstruction.
- His followers very early became convinced that God had vindicated/raised/exalted him.
- Early interpretation likely developed through multiple Jewish conceptual grammars rather than one clean linear "human -> God" ladder: messianic, Danielic/humanlike, Mosaic/prophetic, Adamic, Wisdom/Logos/divine-agent, exaltation/lordship, apocalyptic-judge trajectories.
- Later Christian and anti-Christian memories became increasingly specific and polemical.

Specific hypotheses:
1. **Paternity-polemic crystallization**: surviving chronology is compatible with ambiguity -> explicit virgin-conception claims -> increasingly specific hostile paternity counter-narrative -> named Panthera tradition. This is `PROJECT_INFERENCE/SPECULATIVE_MODEL`, not proof of late invention and not disproof of an older lost rumor.
2. **Contingent death/vindication/future role**: Jesus may have anticipated death, postmortem vindication, an interval, and participation in final consummation. Recent Ferda/Allison scholarship makes this historically plausible; attribution to Jesus remains contested because sources are post-Easter.
3. **Plural Christological trajectories**: early high Christology may result from several Jewish interpretive trajectories developing/fusing rather than one linear ladder. Do not synthesize all categories into one earliest Jerusalem doctrine.
4. **Family prominence**: James/family importance is stronger than any current claim for a hereditary "Jesus dynasty".
5. Panthera biological paternity remains unresolved/UNKNOWN.

## What this map is

This is an evidence-typed relationship graph.

An arrow does **not** automatically mean "A directly caused B."

Every edge carries one of a small number of relation types:

- `INTERNAL_ETIOLOGICAL_RELATION`
- `LITERARY_REWRITING_STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`
- `RELATED_TEXTUAL_DEPENDENCE_STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`
- `CONTEXTUAL_COREFERENCE_STRONG`
- `LEXICAL_FUNCTIONAL_BRIDGE`
- `FUNCTIONAL_PARALLEL_DIRECT_DEPENDENCE_UNRESOLVED`
- `SHARED_TRADITION_FIELD_DIRECTION_UNRESOLVED`
- `IDENTITY_CONTESTED`

No V1 edge is allowed to mean:
`SAME_ENTITY_BECAUSE_SIMILAR`.

## Nodes

### 1. Job / Zechariah `ha-satan`

Source family:
- Job 1-2;
- Zechariah 3.

Core profile:
- accuser/adversarial function in a divine/heavenly court;
- acts within divine authority;
- no source-level primordial anti-God biography.

This is the early adversary/control node.

### 2. Enochic Watchers

Source family:
- 1 Enoch / Book of the Watchers.

Core profile:
- rebellious heavenly beings;
- illicit unions;
- forbidden knowledge;
- giant violence;
- divine judgment.

The Watchers are a plural class, not one Satan figure.

### 3. Enochic giant spirits

Within the Watchers tradition, the destructive spirits associated with the dead giants continue to afflict humanity.

They are etiologically connected to the Watchers but are not the same class of beings.

### 4. Jubilees Belial

Jubilees contains Belial language independent of the named Mastema passages:
- Jub. 1:20 — Moses asks that the spirit of Belial not rule Israel, accuse them, or make them stumble;
- Jub. 15:33 — "people of Belial" in the circumcision/covenant warning.

This matters because `JUBILEES = MASTEMA_ONLY` is false.

### 5. Jubilees Mastema

Exact named-passage inventory:
- 10:8;
- 11:5;
- 11:11;
- 17:16;
- 18:9;
- 18:12;
- 19:28;
- 48:2;
- 48:9;
- 48:12;
- 48:15;
- 49:2.

Core profile:
- leader/prince associated with retained hostile spirits;
- tester/accuser in the Akedah;
- historical adversary in the Exodus retelling;
- acts under divine constraint rather than as God's equal.

### 6. Jubilees 10:11 "the satan"

Immediate continuation of Mastema's 10:8 request:
a tenth of the evil spirits is left to exercise power "before the satan."

V1 treats this as strong local/contextual coreference with the Mastema sequence.

It does not globalize:
`MASTEMA = EVERY SATAN OCCURRENCE`.

### 7. 4Q225 / Pseudo-Jubilees Mastema

Ancient Hebrew related Akedah retelling.

The surviving fragments preserve `שר המשטמה` and closely parallel the Jubilees Akedah while also compressing and innovating.

This is ancient Hebrew reception/dependence evidence, **not** a direct Jubilees manuscript.

### 8. Qumran personified Belial

Exact inventory:
38 current V1 personified loci across Community Rule, War Scroll, Damascus Document textual tradition and Qumran parallels, pesharim/Florilegium, Cave 4 works, and 11Q13.

Core profile:
- rule/temptation;
- hostile planning;
- lot/spirits/army;
- eschatological opposition;
- defeat/judgment;
- subordinate to God in 1QM 13.

### 9. Qumran `mśṭmh` lexical bridge

1QM 13:10-11 describes Belial as `מלאך משטמה` — an angel of hostility/enmity.

4Q495 frg. 2,3 directly parallels this line.

This is a **lexical bridge**, not a separate supernatural individual and not automatic proof that Belial is Prince Mastema.

### E8 — Jubilees Mastema <-> Qumran Belial

Relation:
`LEXICAL_FUNCTIONAL_BRIDGE` plus `IDENTITY_CONTESTED`.

- divine subordination/restraint.

Counter-control:
Dimant's analysis finds different distribution and origin profiles:
- Belial dominates sectarian texts;
- Mastema/Angel of mśṭmh dominates non-sectarian works;
- similarity therefore does not erase difference.

V1:
`BELIAL = MASTEMA` remains unestablished.

## Distribution result

The strongest map is **networked**, not linear.

A misleading model would be:

`ha-satan -> Watchers -> Mastema -> Belial -> later Devil`.

The evidence does not support that as one clean chain.

A better model has several interacting streams:

- adversary/accuser language;
- heavenly-rebellion and demon-origin traditions;
- named hostile-spirit leaders;
- Belial terminology;
- `mśṭmh` hostility vocabulary;
- eschatological dualistic rhetoric.

They can overlap and influence one another without becoming one continuous character biography.

## Bounded synthesis

What V1 can support:

1. **Continuity of problem-space:** Jewish texts repeatedly develop ways to speak about accusation, evil spirits, heavenly rebellion, temptation, hostile rule, and final judgment.
2. **Real literary rewriting:** Jubilees demonstrably reworks Watcher/demon traditions; 4Q225 closely reworks/receives Jubilees' Mastema Akedah.
3. **Real lexical bridges:** Jub. 10:11 locally links Mastema's sequence to "the satan"; 1QM 13:10-11 links Belial with the `mśṭmh` hostility word-family.
4. **Real functional convergence:** Mastema and Belial both lead hostile spiritual complexes while remaining subordinate to God.
5. **Real divergence:** Belial and Mastema have different textual distributions and are not safely one entity by default.
6. **No clean genealogy yet:** direct literary direction among Job's satan, Jubilees Mastema, and the broad Qumran Belial corpus remains unresolved except where a specific rewriting relationship is independently evidenced.

## Claim ceiling

V1 does not establish:
- one continuous Satan biography;
- Job's `ha-satan` as the fully developed Devil;
- Watchers as Satan;
- giant spirits as Watchers;
- Mastema as Belial;
- Belial as Satan;
- one direct Enoch -> Mastema -> Belial lineage;
- two coequal divine principles;
- Genesis 3's serpent as any of these figures;
- later Christian Satanology as a one-source copy.

The correct output is a constrained developmental network, not a family tree pretending uncertainty does not exist.

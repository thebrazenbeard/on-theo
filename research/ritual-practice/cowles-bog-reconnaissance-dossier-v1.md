## Grounded route information

Source: US National Park Service, [Cowles Bog Trail](https://www.nps.gov/places/cowles-bog-south.htm) and [2026 hiking challenge page](https://www.nps.gov/indu/planyourvisit/cb1966.htm) (last updated July 23, 2026).

- Cowles Bog is a protected National Natural Landmark with an ecologically sensitive mosaic of ponds, marsh, swamp, woodland, black oak savanna, dunes and a Lake Michigan beach.
- NPS lists the main route as **4.7 miles**, **moderate to rugged**, with loose sand, steep dune approaches, approximately **202 ft** elevation gain and a maximum **15%** grade. Sources vary between **3 and 4 hours** for the full walk; budget toward 4, not the minimum.
- **North/main lot:** 1450 N Mineral Springs Rd, Dune Acres, IN 46304; **41.645191, -87.084633**. Gravel lot.
- **South/Greenbelt lot:** 1184 N Mineral Springs Rd, Dune Acres, IN 46304; **41.636898, -87.087185**. Paved lot.
- The NPS describes the route as a lollipop with a beach spur/shoreline transition, and explicitly identifies a **cut-off** option that avoids the steep dune/beach portion and shortens the hike by somewhat less than one mile.
- Published site hours: **6 a.m.–11 p.m.**; an entrance pass is required. Neither published hours nor a listing implies particular-day access. No potable water is available along the route.
- Stay on official trails and heed closures. No plants, rocks, shells or fossils may be removed; no littering, beach fires or glass. Do not use an unsanctioned off-trail 'gateway,' archaeological feature, or culturally protected area for staging.
- This candidate is a **landscape reconnaissance / reflective hiking** option, not permission for an organized special-use ceremony.

## Higher-resolution geospatial sources

Indiana University Spatial Data Portal, [2021–2024 statewide imagery overview](https://gis.iu.edu/s/isdp/page/state20212023):

- The portal specifically documents **2023 INDNR (Indiana Dunes / Lake Michigan shoreline) orthophotography at 3-inch resolution** (GeoTIFF, MrSID, and ECW options), and **2024 INDNR imagery and LiDAR**. The site describes its 2024 INDNR dataset at [2024 INDNR Orthophotography (GeoTIFF)](https://gis.iu.edu/s/isdp/item-set/71452).
- A named **DUNE ACRES quadrangle** item is listed for the 2024 orthophotography collection. Index link: https://gis.iu.edu/s/isdp/item/106716 . The item's underlying raster has **not** yet been opened or interpreted.
- The portal describes hillshades, bare-earth DEMs and classified LiDAR for the shoreline area. These distinguish dune forms more effectively than canopy-obscured orthophotos.
- NPS official trail map is the controlling route source; aerial imagery does **not** establish where a marked trail actually runs today and cannot certify the passability, privacy or permissions of a clearing.

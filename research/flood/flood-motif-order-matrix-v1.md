## Order-cluster conclusion

The strongest argument for relationship is not any single motif.

It is the repeated ordered cluster:

`DIVINE CRISIS -> WARNING -> VESSEL -> PRESERVATION OF LIFE -> FLOOD ->
RECESSION/REST -> TEST/EXIT -> SACRIFICE -> DIVINE RESPONSE -> NEW POST-FLOOD ORDER`.

Within that larger sequence, the Gilgamesh XI / Genesis cluster of:

`REST -> BIRDS -> EXIT -> SACRIFICE -> DIVINE SMELL/RESPONSE`

is especially difficult to reduce to generic flood-story convergence.

Current disposition:

`ORDERED_MOTIF_DENSITY_SUPPORTS_TRADITION_RELATIONSHIP = HIGH`

`ORDERED_MOTIF_DENSITY_IDENTIFIES_EXACT_DIRECT_SOURCE = NO`

## Anti-overreach controls

- Ziusudra lacunae cannot be completed from Gilgamesh or Genesis.
- Atrahasis fragmentary sections cannot be reconstructed solely from Gilgamesh.
- Shared order can support tradition relation without proving manuscript copying.
- Differences are evidence too: cause, divine politics, survivor fate, and post-flood order materially change.

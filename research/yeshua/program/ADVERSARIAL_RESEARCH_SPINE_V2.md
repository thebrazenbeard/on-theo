## Purpose

The Real Testament of Yeshua requires a research system that can survive its own preferred story.

The system is claim-centered rather than essay-centered. Prose packets may explain evidence, but durable historical conclusions are represented as stable propositions with explicit source bindings, witness controls, rival explanations, dependencies, review state, and downstream provenance.

Core rule:

CLAIM COHERENCE != HISTORICAL ESTABLISHMENT

A compelling reconstruction, marginalized source, canonical source, hostile witness, later mystical text, or institutional outcome receives no automatic historical privilege.

## Governing source boundaries

Preserve the ON_THEO evidence contract, especially:

- COMPOSITION != PHYSICAL WITNESS != MODERN EDITION
- COMMENTARY != PRIMARY TEXT
- LATER SYSTEMATIZATION != EARLY SOURCE MEANING
- SIMILARITY != TRANSMISSION
- CONTACT != BORROWING
- FICTIONAL / LITERARY CONSTRUCTION != RESEARCH EVIDENCE

Every major Yeshua claim must identify the earliest currently admitted supporting source and, where relevant, the physical witness layer separately.

## Claim graph

A major claim record contains only fields needed to prevent known research failures:

1. stable claim ID and exact proposition;
2. evidence class and claim type;
3. earliest supporting source;
4. physical-witness distinction;
5. composition-date status;
6. language, recension, and reconstruction status where relevant;
7. independent supporting witnesses;
8. literary dependence and source relationship;
9. contradictions;
10. supporting scholarship;
11. materially relevant opposing scholarship;
12. rival explanations;
13. qualitative confidence and explicit claim ceiling;
14. dependencies and invalidation triggers;
15. downstream literary consumers;
16. review state and exact-head review provenance.

No composite numerical truth score is authorized. Precision in the registry must not exceed precision in the evidence.

## Two-dimensional state model

The command requested the states PROPOSED, SOURCE_BOUND, INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED, SURVIVES, SURVIVES_NARROWED, UNRESOLVED, REJECTED, REVIEW_STALE, and INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED.

V2 deliberately separates them into two dimensions so review history is not destroyed when a disposition is assigned.

Lifecycle state:
- PROPOSED
- SOURCE_BOUND
- INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED
- REVIEW_STALE
- INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED

Disposition:
- SURVIVES
- SURVIVES_NARROWED
- UNRESOLVED
- REJECTED

A claim may therefore be INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED + SURVIVES_NARROWED, or INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED + REJECTED.

SOURCE_BOUND does not imply historical truth.
INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED does not imply independence.
INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED does not imply SURVIVES.

## Internal hostile-review protocol

For every major interpretive or reconstructive claim:

1. PROPOSAL — state the proposition narrowly enough to fail.
2. HOSTILE REVIEWER — attack semantics, hidden premises, source dependence, chronology, stronger rival explanations, evidentiary gaps, and unfalsifiability.
3. RESEARCH RESPONSE — answer each live objection; revise, narrow, or abandon the claim where warranted.
4. HOSTILE REJOINDER — attack the revised claim, not the obsolete opening version.
5. DISPOSITION — SURVIVES, SURVIVES_NARROWED, UNRESOLVED, or REJECTED.

The hostile reviewer is not rewarded for disagreement. It is rewarded for finding the strongest surviving formulation or killing an unsupported one.

Internal hostile review is SAME_RUNTIME_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW unless a receipt establishes otherwise.

## Asymmetric skepticism

The review burden increases when a claim is:

- distinctive to this project;
- conspiratorial;
- psychologically satisfying;
- spiritually attractive;
- institutionally accusatory;
- dependent on hostile or late counter-memory;
- dependent on a disputed translation;
- used to overturn an earlier-source baseline;
- central to the eventual literary plot.

These claims require explicit OPPOSITION_ACQUISITION before promotion beyond provisional status.

Opposition acquisition means deliberately seeking the strongest materially relevant counterevidence and counter-scholarship. It does not mean manufacturing false balance. Minority positions are recorded proportionally to their evidentiary and scholarly weight.

## Dependency invalidation

Claims are not frozen merely because a review once passed.

A dependent claim becomes REVIEW_STALE when a material upstream input changes, including:

- source identity;
- composition-date range;
- physical-witness attribution;
- recension assignment;
- translation of a load-bearing phrase;
- literary-dependence judgment;
- independence judgment;
- authenticity/interpolation judgment;
- evidence classification;
- supporting claim disposition;
- exact source locator;
- newly admitted contradictory evidence.

Invalidation propagates through declared claim dependencies. Stale claims may remain visible but may not be treated as current reviewed conclusions.

## Baseline A — EARLY_SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_BASELINE

Purpose: create a control reconstruction using the earliest defensible evidence.

Likely source horizon:
- undisputed Pauline letters;
- pre-Pauline material where a distinct earlier layer can be responsibly argued;
- Mark;
- early Synoptic material only where the dependence model is explicit rather than assumed;
- first-century Jewish, Roman, archaeological, epigraphic, and documentary context;
- Josephus and other external evidence with source-specific controls.

This baseline is NOT automatically THE HISTORICAL YESHUA.

It answers a narrower question:

What can be reconstructed if later Jesus traditions are prevented from supplying missing information unless an earlier provenance is independently established?

## Baseline B — FULL_SOURCE_CRITICAL_RECONSTRUCTION

Purpose: allow the entire surviving evidence field to challenge or enlarge Baseline A.

Later witnesses may contribute only proposition-by-proposition when historical utility is argued through one or more of:

- plausible independent tradition;
- demonstrably earlier embedded material;
- independent corroboration;
- source-critical recovery of an earlier layer;
- historically informative counter-memory whose limitations remain visible.

Later date alone does not make a source worthless.
Noncanonical status does not make a source early.
Canonical status does not make a source independent.

The delta between Baseline A and Baseline B must remain explicit. A proposition appearing only in Baseline B cannot be silently narrated as though Baseline A established it.

## Research order

The highest-value order is dependency-driven rather than purely chronological.

R0 — Source and witness normalization
Reuse and extend existing source/witness controls. Do not duplicate registries unnecessarily.

R1 — Early-source constrained Yeshua baseline
Build the control reconstruction before later mystical or counter-traditional material is allowed to fill gaps.

R2 — Kingdom and teaching
Test present/future/inward/social Kingdom models, including Luke 17:20–21 and entos hymon, before using Thomas as an interpretive key.

R3 — People around Yeshua
Separate dossiers for John the Baptist, James, Mary Magdalene, Peter, Judas, Thomas, and the Twelve.

R4 — Jerusalem crisis and execution
Temple action, arrest logistics, paradidomi, disciple failure, priestly/local authority, Pilate, crucifixion, burial.

R5 — Resurrection tradition stratification
Pre-Pauline/Pauline material first, then Gospel development, then later traditions. Separate event claim, experience report, empty-tomb tradition, resurrection interpretation, and exaltation theology.

R6 — Family, infancy, and paternity synthesis
Integrate the existing strong Mark 6:3 / porneia / Panthera controls. Do not infer childhood psychology from later polemic.

R7 — Noncanonical independence tests
Thomas, Mary, Judas, Peter, Jewish-Christian gospels, and other noncanonical works earn historical use saying-by-saying or tradition-by-tradition.

R8 — Inward / mystical trajectory
Only after R1–R7. Test continuity versus development versus convergence from Jewish antecedents through Paul, John, Thomas, Mary, Valentinian texts, later mysticism, Hekhalot/Merkabah, and much later Kabbalah.

R9 — Canon, copying, and memory selection
Separate deliberate suppression from doctrinal exclusion, copying selection, regional extinction, language shift, manuscript attrition, and accidental loss.

R10 — Roman engagement hypothesis
Test ordinary Roman response and later accommodation/appropriation before stronger cultivation, infiltration, or invention hypotheses. Roman capability is not evidence of Roman action.

## Promotion thresholds

A claim may become SOURCE_BOUND only when:
- its source IDs and locators are explicit;
- source/work identity is distinguished from physical witness where relevant;
- the evidence class is valid under the ON_THEO contract;
- known dependence is recorded.

A contested interpretive claim may become INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED only when:
- the full call/response/rejoinder/disposition exists;
- the review identifies its exact subject commit or artifact version;
- material opposition has been sought when asymmetric-skepticism triggers apply.

A historical claim may receive SURVIVES or SURVIVES_NARROWED only when the disposition states its claim ceiling and strongest surviving rival.

Independent exact-head review is required before treating a claim as independently reviewed when it materially:
- changes the reconstructed identity or teaching of Yeshua;
- converts a late source into evidence about Yeshua's lifetime;
- alleges coordinated suppression, fabrication, infiltration, bribery, or institutional manipulation;
- claims historical superiority for Mary, Judas, Thomas, Peter, James, or another authority stream;
- attributes trauma, shame, pathology, altered state, or unusual psychology to Yeshua;
- promotes an inward/mystical teaching as historical against an earlier-source control;
- promotes a Panthera/paternity conclusion beyond current source ceilings;
- promotes deliberate Roman cultivation/invention;
- becomes a load-bearing historical premise for the final literary work.

Routine source metadata, uncontroversial witness facts, and low-consequence philological bookkeeping do not automatically require separate-runtime review.

## Downstream literary contract

The eventual Real Testament of Yeshua is downstream of these claims.

A literary consumer must retain:
- ON_THEO repository;
- exact commit;
- claim ID;
- evidence class;
- claim ceiling;
- dispute/review state.

Literary reconstruction may choose among surviving historical possibilities, but that choice remains FICTIONAL / LITERARY CONSTRUCTION unless separately established upstream.

No literary requirement may force an upstream conclusion.

## Valid project-level failure result

The program explicitly permits:

DISTINCTIVE_YESHUA_HYPOTHESES_NOT_SUPPORTED

This is a successful research outcome if the evidence warrants it.

The project is not required to discover a hidden Yeshua, a suppressed teaching, a Roman intervention, an inward mystical core, a Judas exoneration, a Mary succession line, a paternity scandal, or any other distinctive thesis.

## Internal hostile review of this architecture

### PROPOSAL

Use a versioned claim graph with two baseline reconstructions, dependency invalidation, asymmetric hostile review, opposition acquisition, and exact-head review provenance.

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER**
>
> This architecture can become bureaucracy pretending to be source criticism. A large registry may create false confidence through fields, states, and labels. The two-baseline design can also privilege the earliest surviving texts merely because they survived early, while later independent tradition is forced to prove itself against a canonical-looking control. Exact-head review can sound rigorous while still being the same model arguing with itself. Finally, opposition acquisition can manufacture false balance if every mainstream conclusion is forced to carry a fringe counter-position.

### RESEARCH RESPONSE

The challenge is valid and changes the design.

The claim record is intentionally minimal and forbids composite truth scores. Baseline A is named EARLY_SOURCE_CONSTRAINED rather than HISTORICAL YESHUA, and Baseline B can correct it when later evidence earns historical use. Review provenance distinguishes SAME_RUNTIME_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW from independent execution. Opposition acquisition is proportional: it seeks materially relevant counterevidence but does not give marginal positions artificial parity.

The architecture also limits independent review to claims that materially alter the historical reconstruction or carry unusually high bias risk.

> **HOSTILE REJOINDER**
>
> The revised design survives, but only if the registry remains subordinate to actual source work. Do not spend months perfecting metadata while the Kingdom sayings, resurrection strata, James dossier, Judas tradition, and Jewish-Christian gospels remain under-researched. The first empirical test of the architecture should be a real historical lane, not another schema revision.
>
> One further guard is required: a claim's qualitative confidence must never be inherited mechanically from the number of source bindings. Three dependent Gospels are not three independent witnesses, and ten secondary sources repeating one argument are not ten evidentiary lines.

### DISPOSITION

SURVIVES_NARROWED.

Conditions:
- no numerical truth score;
- independence tracked separately from source count;
- real source work begins immediately after this bounded architecture packet;
- Baseline A is a control, not a privileged historical verdict;
- same-runtime review is never called independent;
- registry complexity may not grow unless a demonstrated research failure requires it.

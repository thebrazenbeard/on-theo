# R4 Passion Responsibility Diachronic Matrix V1

Status: SOURCE-DEVELOPMENT / RESPONSIBILITY-ATTRIBUTION CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Track how responsibility for Yeshua's death is distributed and reshaped across
sources without turning later literary development into event evidence.

Core rule:

RESPONSIBILITY_IN_A_TEXT != EVENT_LEVEL_RESPONSIBILITY_WITHOUT_SOURCE_CRITICISM.

## 1. Earliest Pauline layer

Paul securely centers:
- crucifixion;
- death;
- resurrection/vindication belief.

He does not preserve a developed Pilate/chief-priest/Judas trial narrative in
the undisputed letters comparable to the Gospels.

1 Thessalonians 2:14–16 has a history of authenticity/interpolation debate and
cannot be used casually as an uncontested early statement that "the Jews killed
Jesus."

Current R4 use:

PAUL =
EARLY_EXECUTION/CRUCIFIXION_FLOOR,
NOT
DEVELOPED_RESPONSIBILITY_SCRIPT.

## 2. Mark

Mark preserves the earliest continuous passion sequence:
- chief-priestly plot;
- Judas handover;
- arrest party linked to chief priests/scribes/elders;
- high-priestly examination;
- transfer to Pilate;
- Roman crucifixion;
- "King of the Jews."

Responsibility structure:

LOCAL_ELITE_INITIATION/ACCUSATION
+
ROMAN_SENTENCE/EXECUTION.

Mark already contains literary/theological shaping and cannot be treated as a
court stenogram.

## 3. Matthew

Matthew follows Mark but develops responsibility motifs, including:
- explicit thirty-silver-pieces Judas motif;
- Judas remorse;
- Pilate handwashing;
- the crowd's "blood" saying.

Current classification:

MATTHEAN_RESPONSIBILITY_INTENSIFICATION =
REDACTION/RECEPTION_EVIDENCE.

It is not independent confirmation that:
- exactly thirty pieces were paid;
- Pilate washed his hands;
- a representative Jewish people accepted transgenerational guilt.

The collective-guilt use of Matthew 27:25 is historically inadmissible.

## 4. Luke-Acts

Luke preserves repeated innocence/reluctance motifs around Pilate and develops a
broader apologetic/political presentation.

Acts continues responsibility rhetoric in speeches, but those speeches are
Lukan compositions representing early-Christian proclamation as transmitted by
Luke-Acts, not verbatim hearing transcripts.

Current use:

LUKAN_PILATE/RESPONSIBILITY_PATTERN =
RECEPTION_AND_REDACTION_EVIDENCE.

## 5. John

John intensifies:
- extensive Pilate dialogue;
- repeated "no case" language;
- a broad and context-variable use of "the Jews" / Ioudaioi;
- chief-priestly pressure;
- Roman military terminology at arrest.

Current guard:

IOUDAIOI_IN_JOHN
must not be flattened into:
ALL_JEWS.

Depending context it can refer to:
- Judeans;
- authorities;
- particular opponents;
- a broader narrative category.

John is valuable for diachronic development and possible independent traditions,
but its detailed trial dialogue is not automatically early event history.

## 6. Gospel of Peter and later Pilate trajectories

Later Christian texts can move still further toward:
- Pilate mitigation/exoneration;
- increased blame on Herod or Jewish authorities;
- legendary expansion.

Current classification:

LATER_PILATE_EXCULPATION =
RECEPTION_HISTORY.

It demonstrates that responsibility memory remained malleable.

It does not prove:
ROME_ORCHESTRATED_CANONICAL_BLAME_SHIFT.

## 7. Judas trajectory inside responsibility development

Mark:
- Judas approaches chief priests;
- payment is promised;
- handover/arrest identification.

Matthew:
- explicit thirty silver pieces;
- remorse and return of money;
- scriptural shaping.

Luke:
- Satan enters Judas;
- stronger theological causation.

John:
- Judas is associated with theft and Satanic entry;
- character condemnation is intensified.

Current result:

JUDAS_MOTIVE_AND_MORAL_CHARACTERIZATION =
DIAChronically_EXPANDING.

This does not erase a possible historical handover core.

It does weaken treating later motive detail as equally early.

## 8. Pilate trajectory

Across canonical and later traditions, one recurring development is:

ROMAN_EXECUTIONER
->
RELUCTANT_JUDGE
->
INCREASINGLY_EXCULPATED_PILATE
in selected reception trajectories.

This is not perfectly linear across every source.

Current project inference:

PILATE_MITIGATION_TRAJECTORY =
REAL_RECEPTION_PATTERN.

Historical consequence:

THE_MOST_EXCULPATORY_LATE_PILATE_PORTRAITS
should not be allowed to control reconstruction of the prefect's actual agency.

## 9. What the diachronic pattern does establish

It establishes:
- responsibility attribution changes over time;
- Judas characterization becomes more elaborated;
- Pilate can become more reluctant/exculpated;
- collective categories can become rhetorically broader;
- theological/scriptural motifs increasingly structure narrative.

It does not establish:
- deliberate Roman censorship;
- apostolic bribery;
- coordinated state propaganda;
- one conspiratorial editor;
- the innocence of Judas.

## 10. Event-level reconstruction after diachronic control

Current strongest bounded reconstruction:

1. Yeshua was executed by Roman crucifixion under Pilate.
2. A political/regal/public-order interpretation is probable.
3. Jerusalem chief-priestly/local-elite involvement before Roman execution is
   probable.
4. Exact arrest force and hearing sequence remain unresolved.
5. Judas handover may preserve a historical insider role, but its exact
   function and motive remain unresolved.
6. Later responsibility redistribution cannot by itself overturn the Roman
   execution anchor.

## 11. Anti-antisemitic historical control

The statement:

"the Jews killed Jesus"

is rejected as a historical synthesis because:
- it collectivizes diverse first-century Jewish populations;
- Yeshua and his earliest followers were themselves Jewish;
- the sources name specific elites, crowds and Roman officials differently;
- crucifixion was carried out under Roman prefectural authority.

This is a source-precision conclusion, not merely a modern ethical preference.

## 1. Source clocks

EARLIEST PAULINE:
- crucifixion;
- received final-meal and death/burial traditions;
- no developed arrest/Judas/trial narrative.

MARK:
- earliest surviving continuous arrest/passion narrative;
- chief-priest/scribe/elder involvement;
- Judas handover;
- night arrest;
- high-priest examination;
- Pilate;
- "King of the Jews";
- crucifixion.

MATTHEW / LUKE:
- later Synoptic developments;
- Mark dependence must be controlled;
- changes are evidence for reception/redaction, not independent confirmation
  when copying Mark.

JOHN:
- later independent/dependent mix;
- substantially different arrest-party description;
- different hearing sequence;
- Roman cohort/tribune language;
- distinctive chronology and trial development.

JOSEPHUS / PHILO:
- contextual evidence for Pilate, Caiaphas/high-priestly office, festival
  policing, Roman coercive power and Judean governance;
- not witnesses to Jesus' arrest logistics.

TACITUS:
- later Roman confirmation of Christus execution under Pilate, with
  information-source independence unresolved.

## 2. Anti-collective-blame control

"Jewish authorities" is too broad.

R4 distinguishes:
- high priest / chief-priestly elite;
- Temple retainers/clients;
- local aristocratic councils or advisers;
- Jerusalem crowds;
- pilgrims;
- Judeans in general;
- Galileans;
- Roman prefect and soldiers.

Core guard:

CHIEF_PRIEST_INVOLVEMENT != JEWISH_COLLECTIVE_RESPONSIBILITY.

The execution is a Roman punishment ordered under Roman prefectural authority.

## 4. Arrest party

The Gospels materially differ.

Mark/Matthew:
chief-priest-organized party with weapons and local-authority connection.

Luke:
related but independently redacted presentation.

John:
mentions a cohort/speira, a tribune/chiliarchos and officers connected with chief
priests/Pharisees.

Current rule:

ARREST_PARTY_COMPOSITION = DISPUTED.

Do not harmonize all participants into one enormous combined force without
argument.

## 6. Roman trial/execution

Strong historical anchor:
- Pilate judged/condemned Yeshua;
- Roman crucifixion followed;
- "King of the Jews" belongs to the early execution tradition;
- political/regal interpretation is historically relevant.

The project does not assume:
- Gospel Pilate's private thoughts;
- Pilate declared Yeshua legally innocent;
- exact courtroom transcript;
- one modern legal charge label;
- Yeshua was an armed revolutionary.

## 7. Pilate reception

Later Christian traditions increasingly mitigate or exculpate Pilate in
different ways.

Current use:

PILATE_EXCULPATION_TRAJECTORY != EVENT_LEVEL_PILATE_INNOCENCE.

## 8. Roman-cultivation hypothesis firewall

R4 can establish:
- Roman execution;
- Roman/local governance interaction;
- later changes in responsibility memory.

It cannot infer:
- Rome planned the movement;
- Rome recruited apostles;
- Tiberius cultivated Christianity;
- later imperial utility was original design.

Core guard:

ROMAN_EXECUTION_OR_CAPABILITY != ROMAN_LONG_TERM_CULTIVATION.

# R4 Arrest Party / Logistics Packet V1

Status: HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION / SOURCE-CONFLICT CONTROL

## 1. Festival security environment

Josephus gives independent evidence that Roman and local authorities treated
major festivals, especially Passover, as periods requiring heightened security.

He reports later governors stationing troops near/within Temple precincts to
prevent disturbances and describes Pilate as willing to use hidden or deployed
soldiers against crowds.

This supports:

FESTIVAL_CROWD_SECURITY_RISK = HISTORICALLY_REAL_CONTEXT.

It does not independently prove any detail of Yeshua's arrest.

## 2. Why not arrest publicly?

Mark 14 preserves a tradition in which chief priests/scribes want to seize
Yeshua by stealth and avoid a disturbance among the people.

Even if the wording is partly literary or inferred, the operational logic is
historically coherent in a crowded Passover setting:

PUBLIC_DAYTIME_ARREST
could create
CROWD_RISK.

Therefore an arrest:
- outside peak crowds;
- at night;
- at a known private/semi-private location

would reduce risk.

Current status:

LOW_VISIBILITY_ARREST_LOGIC = PLAUSIBLE_TO_STRONG_CONTEXT.

## 3. Gospel disagreement over arresting force

Mark/Matthew:
- chief-priestly/local-authority organization dominates the scene.

John:
- uses terminology that can indicate Roman military participation, including a
  cohort and tribune alongside local officers.

Modern scholarship treats this discrepancy as substantive rather than trivial.

Current disposition:

DIRECT_ROMAN_PARTICIPATION_AT_ARREST =
UNRESOLVED.

LOCAL_CHIEF_PRIESTLY_ROLE_IN_ARREST_TRADITION =
PROBABLE_TO_STRONG.

ROMAN_EXECUTION_ROLE =
STRONG.

## 4. Arrest initiation versus physical arrest

These are different propositions:

- who wanted Yeshua detained;
- who provided intelligence;
- who authorized local action;
- who physically seized him;
- who interrogated him;
- who possessed capital/coercive authority.

R4 does not assume one actor performed all functions.

## 5. Chief-priestly political position

Josephus independently places Caiaphas in the high priesthood during Pilate's
prefecture and demonstrates that high-priestly office existed within a Roman
governance structure.

Current result:

HIGH_PRIESTLY_ELITE_AND_ROMAN_PREFECTURAL_POWER =
INSTITUTIONALLY_INTERLOCKED_BUT_DISTINCT.

This makes local-elite referral or accusation before Pilate historically
plausible without making the chief priests Roman puppets or autonomous capital
judges by default.

## 6. Night identification

The kiss/identification signal in Mark can serve several possible narrative or
operational functions:
- confirming the target in darkness;
- distinguishing him among companions;
- dramatizing insider betrayal;
- fulfilling literary/theological motifs.

Current disposition:

IDENTIFICATION_SIGNAL_HISTORICITY =
UNRESOLVED.

Even if Yeshua was publicly known, low-light identification among a group can
still be operationally useful.

But:

PUBLIC_VISIBILITY
weakens any claim that Judas was needed because authorities literally did not
know what Yeshua looked like.

## 7. Location intelligence

A stronger operational function for an insider is:
- knowing where Yeshua would be after dark;
- knowing when he would be separated from large crowds;
- knowing a routine/private meeting location.

This is consistent with the Gospel arrest structure.

Current disposition:

INSIDER_LOCATION_OR_TIMING_HELP =
PLAUSIBLE.

It remains:
NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_PROVED.

## 8. What R4 does not currently find

No admitted first-century administrative evidence shows:
- Judas receiving a Roman payment;
- multiple apostles being bought;
- Roman agents inserted into the group;
- an intelligence file on Yeshua;
- a coordinated post-arrest apostolic recruitment operation.

Therefore:

COLLECTIVE_BRIBERY_OR_AGENT_INSERTION =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

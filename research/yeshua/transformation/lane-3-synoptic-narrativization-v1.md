# Lane 3 — Synoptic Christological Narrativization V1

Status: `SOURCE-CRITICAL GOSPEL-DEVELOPMENT LANE / NON-CANONICAL`

Date: 2026-10-04

## Question

How do Mark, Matthew, and Luke transform post-death confessions about Yeshua into
biographical narrative?

## Mark

Mark is commonly treated as the earliest surviving continuous Gospel narrative.

Theological identity is embedded through:
- baptism;
- Spirit;
- kingdom authority;
- healings/exorcisms;
- Son of Man sayings;
- Messiah confession;
- suffering prediction;
- transfiguration;
- passion;
- resurrection expectation.

Mark begins with adult Jesus.
There is:
- no conception narrative;
- no virgin-birth story;
- no Bethlehem infancy story.

The baptism therefore occupies unusual narrative weight.

Michael Peppard argues Mark's divine-sonship scene can be read through ancient
Roman/Jewish adoption/accession categories.

Other scholars read Mark's sonship as already presupposed rather than initiated
at baptism.

Text criticism further complicates Mark 1:1 because "Son of God" has a known
variant problem.

Disposition:
`MARK_PRESENTS_THEOLOGICAL_CHRIST_NOT_RAW_BIOGRAPHY = STRONG`.
`MARK_TEACHES_SIMPLE_ADOPTIONISM = NOT_ADMITTED`.

## Matthew

Matthew uses Mark but expands Jesus' identity backward into origins.

Distinctive features:
- genealogy from Abraham/David;
- Davidic-Messianic architecture;
- conception by Holy Spirit;
- Bethlehem;
- magi/star;
- Herod;
- Egypt/new-Moses typology;
- fulfillment citations.

This is a new narrative move:
theological significance is no longer first located at adult baptism/public
ministry but at conception/birth and ancestral history.

Matthew's infancy material cannot simply be treated as corroboration of Luke:
their narratives diverge substantially in structure, chronology, actors, and
geographical assumptions.

Disposition:
`MATTHEAN_ORIGIN_CHRISTOLOGY = SURVIVES`.
`MATTHEW+LUKE_AS_TWO_MATCHING_INFANCY_WITNESSES = REJECTED`.

## Luke

Luke independently builds another infancy narrative:
- annunciations;
- John/Jesus parallelism;
- Holy Spirit;
- miraculous conception;
- census;
- Bethlehem;
- shepherds;
- temple scenes;
- prophetic hymns.

Luke's Son-of-God logic is explicitly tied to divine conception in Luke 1.

The historical chronology remains contested because Luke links:
- Herodian dating;
- Quirinian census traditions.

Recent scholarship continues to debate translation, chronology, and whether Luke
made an error.

Disposition:
`LUKAN_ORIGIN_CHRISTOLOGY = SURVIVES`.
`HARMONIZED_INFANCY_BIOGRAPHY = NOT_ADMITTED`.

## Synoptic development rule

The Synoptics do not display a simple straight line:
`LOW -> HIGH`.

Mark can possess very elevated Christology without a virgin conception.

Matthew/Luke can push identity into conception while still structuring Jesus
differently from John's Logos.

The better model is:

`DIFFERENT NARRATIVE SOLUTIONS TO WHO JESUS IS`.

## Historical consequence

Every claim first appearing only in Matthew/Luke infancy material must be kept
separate from:
- Paul;
- Mark;
- historical Yeshua reconstruction.

This includes:
- virgin conception;
- Bethlehem narrative forms;
- magi;
- shepherds;
- star;
- massacre/flight-to-Egypt;
- census journey.

These can be theological and/or historical claims.
Their later canonical status cannot substitute for early attestation.

# Yeshua -> Christ Claim Clock V1

Status: `SYNTHESIS / FIRST-ATTESTATION MATRIX / INTERNAL REVIEW REQUIRED / NON-CANONICAL`

Date: 2026-10-04

## Purpose

Track the **earliest recoverable attestation** of major properties later attached
to "Jesus Christ."

This is not a clock for:
- when a belief was first invented;
- when an event happened;
- whether a belief is true.

It is a clock for:
`EARLIEST SURVIVING/RECOVERABLE EVIDENCE`.

Confidence codes:
- **A** = strong textual attestation / early source.
- **B** = strong but source-layer/redaction uncertainty.
- **C** = disputed interpretation or dating.
- **D** = later evidence only / weak backward reach.

## What the clock immediately shows

### 1. The transformation starts almost immediately

By the earliest recoverable post-execution layer:
- Jesus is already Christ;
- his death has salvific meaning;
- resurrection is foundational;
- Cephas/Twelve appearances are proclaimed.

Therefore the "mythic/theological" layer cannot simply be blamed on:
- John;
- second-century Gnostics;
- Constantine.

It begins in the first generation.

### 2. Paul inherits and accelerates

Paul did not invent:
- Christ-language;
- resurrection proclamation;
- some Lord-language.

He does give us the earliest surviving extensive system in which Christ becomes:
- salvific environment;
- cosmic pattern;
- new Adam;
- eschatological Lord;
- possibly preexistent/creation-mediating figure.

Thus:
`PAUL = FIRST SURVIVING MAJOR ARCHITECT`,
not:
`PAUL = PROVEN INVENTOR OF CHRIST`.

### 3. The Gospels move theology backward into life

Mark:
theological identity through adult ministry/passion.

Matthew/Luke:
identity reaches conception/birth.

John:
identity reaches pre-creation.

That is a real directional pattern, but not a single linear ladder because each
Gospel uses different conceptual tools.

### 4. Second-century Christianity branches

Ignatius, Justin, Irenaeus, Jewish-Christian texts, Marcionite trajectories,
Valentinian trajectories, Hermas, and others do not simply repeat one model.

The period is a competitive Christological ecosystem.

### 5. Nicaea arrives after most major building blocks already exist

Before Constantine:
- Messiah/Christ;
- resurrection;
- Lord;
- salvific death;
- preexistence traditions;
- Logos Christology;
- explicit Jesus-as-God language;
- divine Word;
- new Adam;
- fourfold Gospel;
- eternal-generation theology.

Nicaea adds:
- conciliar metaphysical standardization;
- imperial scale/stakes.

## Claim-clock danger zones

### "Jesus claimed he was God"

Clock status:
`NOT ESTABLISHED BY EARLIEST HISTORICAL LAYER`.

Later sources clearly claim divinity.
Whether Yeshua himself made equivalent claims is a separate historical-Jesus
problem.

### "Jesus claimed to be Messiah"

Clock status:
`UNRESOLVED / PLAUSIBLE BUT NOT SECURE`.

Messiah identification is extremely early after death.
That does not prove self-claim.

### "Jesus predicted his atoning death"

Clock status:
`UNRESOLVED`.

Atoning interpretation is pre-Pauline.
Historical attribution to Yeshua requires separate sayings/passion analysis.

### "Jesus believed he existed before creation"

Clock status:
`NOT ESTABLISHED HISTORICALLY`.

Clear in John.
Possible Pauline preexistence is debated.
Historical-Yeshua attribution requires independent evidence.

### "Virgin birth/conception was original apostolic belief"

Clock status:
`NOT ESTABLISHED`.

It is absent from:
- undisputed Paul;
- Mark;
- the earliest resurrection formula.

It appears explicitly in Matthew/Luke.

### "Nicaea made Jesus God"

Clock status:
`REJECTED`.

Explicit divine and Logos Christologies are much earlier.

## New research priority

The claim clock exposes the true high-value interval:

`30 CE -> 55 CE`.

That is the black box.

Within roughly two decades of execution, the surviving evidence already contains
a massive interpretive transformation.

The project should therefore concentrate disproportionate effort on:
- pre-Pauline formulas;
- Aramaic residues;
- Jerusalem/Cephas/James traditions;
- earliest resurrection experiences/claims;
- earliest Messiah identification;
- earliest Lord invocation;
- what Paul inherited versus added.

That interval matters more to the origin of "Jesus Christ" than Constantine does.

Constantine matters to:
`WHICH CHRIST WON LATER`.

The first-generation interval matters to:
`HOW YESHUA BECAME CHRIST AT ALL`.

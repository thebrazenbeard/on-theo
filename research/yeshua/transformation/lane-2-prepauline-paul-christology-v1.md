# Lane 2 — Earliest Post-Death Interpretation and Paul V1

Status: `SOURCE-CRITICAL EARLY-CHRISTOLOGY LANE / NON-CANONICAL`

Date: 2026-10-04

## Question

How much of "Jesus Christ" already exists before the narrative Gospels?

## 1 Corinthians 15:3–5

Paul explicitly distinguishes material he "received" and "delivered."

A broad scholarly consensus treats at least 1 Cor 15:3b–5 as pre-Pauline
traditional/confessional material.

Its contents already interpret Yeshua:
- "Christ" died;
- death has scriptural significance;
- burial;
- resurrection on the third day;
- appearances to Cephas and the Twelve.

This is extremely early evidence that biography has already become theological
proclamation before Paul's own composition of 1 Corinthians.

What it does not settle:
- exact date of formulation;
- bodily mechanics of resurrection;
- empty tomb;
- source of appearance claims;
- whether every line is equally pre-Pauline.

Disposition:
`RESURRECTION/CHRIST_CONFESSION_PREDATES_1_CORINTHIANS = STRONG`.

## Romans 1:3–4

Romans opens with formulaic language:
- descended from David according to flesh;
- designated/appointed/declared Son of God in power according to Spirit of
  holiness by resurrection.

Many scholars regard the formulation as traditional or pre-Pauline, though exact
redaction and translation remain disputed.

Potential importance:
this may preserve a Christology in which resurrection is a decisive transition
in status/power.

Guard:
do not automatically call it "adoptionism."
Ancient sonship language is more flexible than later categories.

Disposition:
`RESURRECTION_AS_STATUS_TRANSITION_IN_EARLY_FORMULA = LIVE / MODERATE`.

## Philippians 2:6–11

The poetic passage has long been treated as pre-Pauline hymn/tradition, though
its exact pre-Pauline status is debated.

The deepest dispute is whether it assumes:
- literal personal preexistence;
- Adamic/human analogy;
- divine status without later Nicene ontology;
- a combination.

The text unquestionably narrates:
- extraordinary initial status/form;
- self-emptying/humiliation;
- death on a cross;
- divine exaltation;
- universal homage;
- "Lord" confession.

Disposition:
`HIGH/EXALTED_CHRISTOLOGY_BY_PAULINE_PERIOD = STRONG`.
`PHIL_2_PROVES_NICENE_PREEXISTENT_GOD_THE_SON = NOT_ADMITTED`.

## 1 Corinthians 8:6

Paul's confession:
- "one God, the Father";
- "one Lord, Jesus Christ";
- all things from/through them.

Some major scholars read this as inclusion of Jesus within the divine identity/
Shema.
Others argue the passage does not require a claim of preexistence or ontological
divinity.

What is secure:
Jesus is placed at a remarkably elevated location inside monotheistic,
cosmological/soteriological discourse by the 50s.

Disposition:
`JESUS_INSIDE_PAULINE_MONOTHEISTIC_CREATION/REDEMPTION_FORMULA = STRONG`.
`EXACT_ONTOLOGY = DISPUTED`.

## Paul's "in Christ" identity

Paul makes Christ not merely a historical teacher but an environment of
salvation/identity:
- "in Christ";
- baptized into Christ/death;
- body of Christ;
- Christ as last/new Adam;
- participation in death/resurrection;
- expected return/judgment.

This is a qualitative transformation.

Yeshua's biography is no longer the dominant organizing category.

`CHRIST` becomes:
- corporate identity;
- eschatological agent;
- salvific event;
- cosmic pattern.

## Critical historical inference

Paul knew Cephas/Peter and James and had contact with Jerusalem leadership.

Therefore his Christology cannot simply be treated as a late second-generation
Gentile invention independent of the earliest movement.

But:
- conflict with Cephas/James-related circles is real;
- Paul's Gentile mission is transformative;
- Pauline theological interpretation cannot automatically be projected into
  Yeshua's own self-understanding.

## Earliest transformation frontier

By the 50s at the latest, the following are already present:
- Christ/Messiah identity;
- resurrection/vindication;
- Lord language;
- death as salvific/scriptural event;
- eschatological return;
- participation "in Christ";
- Adam/Christ typology;
- very elevated divine/cosmic language.

Therefore the major Yeshua-to-Christ transformation is already **front-loaded
into the first generation**.

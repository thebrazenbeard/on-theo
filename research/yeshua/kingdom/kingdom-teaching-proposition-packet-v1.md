## Proposition 2 — Future expectation is genuinely early and load-bearing

Mark contains:
- programmatic announcement that the Kingdom has drawn near;
- sayings about some contemporaries not tasting death before seeing the Kingdom
  come with power;
- future participation imagery;
- future meal/Kingdom expectation.

The prayer tradition shared by Matthew/Luke asks for the Kingdom to come.

Second Temple Jewish apocalyptic and messianic sources make divine future
intervention historically intelligible without importing later Christianity.

Current disposition:

FUTURE_ESCHATOLOGICAL_KINGDOM =
PROBABLE_TO_STRONG.

This does not establish:
- an exact timetable;
- modern "end of planet" imagery;
- one Daniel/Enoch schema;
- that every Kingdom saying is future.

## Proposition 3 — Present/inbreaking Kingdom also has early support

Some Synoptic material links Yeshua's activity to an already operative divine
reign.

The clearest candidate is the Matthew/Luke exorcism saying:
if demons are being expelled by divine power, then the Kingdom has come upon
you.

Because Matthew/Luke source relation is reconstructed rather than physically
witnessed as Q, the historical inference requires dependence control.

Mark's seed/growth parables can also support a hidden-present / future-completion
structure without requiring an interiorized soul-state.

Current disposition:

PRESENT_INBREAKING_KINGDOM =
MODERATE_TO_STRONG_CANDIDATE.

The strongest present-inbreaking datum in R2 is the Matthew/Luke exorcism-Kingdom tradition. Mark 4 supplies supporting present/future interpretive pressure, not independent proof of an inaugurated temporal system.

## Proposition 4 — Luke 17:20–21 does not establish an inward mystical core

Luke 17:20–21 is genuinely important.

But the phrase `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` is disputed in scholarship.

"Within you" is grammatically defensible.
"Among you / in your midst" is also widely defended, especially in context.

Therefore the strongest historical claim is:

LUKE_PRESERVES_A_SAYING_IN_WHICH_KINGDOM_LOCATION_OR_MODE_RESISTS_SIMPLE_VISIBLE_LOCALIZATION.

What R2 cannot yet infer:

YESHUA_TAUGHT_THE_KINGDOM_IS_THE_SOUL_OR_DIVINE_SOURCE_INSIDE_EACH_PERSON.

Current disposition:

INWARD_MYSTICAL_INFERENCE_FROM_LUKE_17_ALONE =
REJECTED_AS_SUPPORTED_CONCLUSION.

Its historical negation remains unproved.

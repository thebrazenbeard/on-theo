## Purpose

Test what "Kingdom/reign of God" most plausibly meant in the historical teaching
of Yeshua without treating either:

- later Christian apocalypticism; or
- later inward/mystical interpretation

as the answer key.

Core rule:

KINGDOM_LANGUAGE != ONE_FIXED_METAPHYSIC.

## 1. Evidence layers

### EARLIEST MOVEMENT CONTEXT

Undisputed Paul:
- demonstrates very early Kingdom vocabulary inside the movement;
- cannot by itself reconstruct Yeshua's exact sayings.

Use:
EARLY_RECEPTION_CONTEXT.

### MARK

Mark is the earliest surviving continuous narrative source admitted in R1.

Priority loci:
- Mark 1:14–15;
- Mark 4 Kingdom parables;
- Mark 9:1;
- Mark 10:14–15, 23–25;
- Mark 14:25.

Use:
EARLIEST_SURVIVING_NARRATIVE_TEACHING_LAYER.

Guard:
MARKAN_PROGRAMMATIC_THEOLOGY != VERBATIM_TRANSCRIPT.

### MATTHEW / LUKE DOUBLE TRADITION

Relevant material includes:
- prayer for the Kingdom to come;
- exorcism / Kingdom arrival;
- beatitude/reversal traditions;
- sayings not present in Mark.

Source relation:
CONTESTED.

The project may model:
- Q-like shared source;
- overlapping oral tradition;
- literary dependence hypotheses

but cannot count Matthew + Luke as two independent witnesses by default.

### LUKE 17:20–21

The phrase `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` is lexically and contextually disputed.

Live interpretations include:
- within you;
- among you;
- in your midst.

Therefore:

LUKE_17_21 != SECURE_PROOF_OF_INWARD_MYSTICAL_KINGDOM.

Even if "within you" were preferred, the saying would still require an additional
argument before becoming:
SOUL_ONTOLOGY
or
SOURCE_WITHIN_SELF.

### GOSPEL OF THOMAS

Priority sayings:
- Thomas 3;
- Thomas 22;
- Thomas 113;
- selected Kingdom parables.

Thomas can challenge the Synoptic reconstruction because some sayings may preserve
independent or archaic material.

But scholarship does not agree on:
- date;
- unity;
- dependence on Synoptics;
- independence of individual sayings.

Required rule:

THOMAS_INDEPENDENCE = SAYING_BY_SAYING.

No complete Thomasine theology may be back-projected into Yeshua.

## 2. Jewish contextual field

### Hebrew Bible / Daniel

"Kingdom" and divine rule can carry:
- divine sovereignty;
- judgment of empires;
- transfer of rule;
- restoration/vindication.

Daniel 2 and 7 are especially important.

Current Daniel exact control:
`research/daniel7-son-of-man-reception-v1-20260919@aca04ed902091149e9ebb0e8a900bc1709fb8589`.

### Enochic / apocalyptic literature

1 Enoch is composite.

Relevant comparison includes:
- divine judgment;
- heavenly agents;
- vindication;
- periodized history;
- Son-of-Man/Chosen-One material in the Parables only with date controls.

No direct literary dependence on Yeshua is assumed.

### Psalms of Solomon

Psalm of Solomon 17 provides a major Second Temple Davidic-messianic kingship
model.

Use:
MESSIANIC_KINGDOM_CONTEXT.

Not:
DIRECT_SOURCE_FOR_YESHUA.

### Qumran / Dead Sea Scrolls

Current scroll scholarship supports:
- significant eschatological and messianic expectation;
- Torah-centered communal order;
- apocalyptic influences from Daniel/Enoch;
- multiple messianic models.

Use:
SECOND_TEMPLE_PLAUSIBILITY_FIELD.

Guard:

QUMRAN != YESHUA_MOVEMENT.

### Broader Apocrypha / Pseudepigrapha

Sirach, Wisdom, Jubilees, Maccabean literature, Psalms of Solomon, Enochic
collections and other works enter proposition-by-proposition.

"Apocrypha" and "Pseudepigrapha" are not single theological witnesses.

## 3. Mysticism boundary

Jewish visionary/heavenly-participation material is relevant to the wider
religious environment:
- Ezekiel;
- Daniel;
- Enoch;
- Qumran heavenly liturgy.

But:

HEAVENLY_VISION
!=
KINGDOM_WITHIN.

Developed Hekhalot/Merkabah and medieval Kabbalah are R8 reception/comparison
sources, not R2 historical gap-fillers.

## 4. Initial hypothesis set

R2 tests at least five models.

### K1 FUTURE_APOCALYPTIC

The Kingdom is principally a coming divine intervention/reversal.

### K2 PRESENT_INBREAKING

The Kingdom is already becoming effective in Yeshua's activity while retaining a
future completion.

### K3 SOCIAL_POLITICAL_REVERSAL

The Kingdom entails transformation of hierarchy, wealth, power and Israel's
historical condition.

### K4 INWARD_ETHICAL_OR_MYSTICAL

The Kingdom is fundamentally internal to the person, consciousness or self.

### K5 MIXED_MULTI_REGISTER

Future, present, ethical, social and symbolic registers coexist without one
exhausting the others.

These are rival/overlapping models, not mutually exclusive dogmas.

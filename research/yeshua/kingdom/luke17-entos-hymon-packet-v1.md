# Luke 17:20–21 — ἐντὸς ὑμῶν Source / Lexical Packet V1

Status: R2 LOAD-BEARING LEXICAL CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Test whether Luke 17:20–21 can support the proposition:

YESHUA_TAUGHT_THAT_THE_KINGDOM_IS_AN_INTERIOR_REALITY_WITHIN_EACH_PERSON.

The answer must separate:

1. what the extant Greek text can mean;
2. what Luke likely means in literary context;
3. whether Luke preserves a pre-Lukan saying;
4. whether that saying goes back to Yeshua;
5. what metaphysical conclusion follows even if "inside" is preferred.

## 1. Textual proposition

Luke places the saying in a dialogue where Pharisees ask when the Kingdom comes.

The response denies ordinary visible localization:
- it does not come in a way reducible to observation/sign-spotting;
- people will not successfully locate it by "here" / "there";
- `ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ ἐντὸς ὑμῶν ἐστιν`.

The load-bearing phrase is:
`ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`.

## 2. "Inside/within" is genuinely possible

The adverb/preposition `ἐντός` can have an interior sense.

Within the New Testament itself, Matthew 23:26 uses `ἐντός` for the inside of
a cup.

Ilaria Ramelli argues strongly that Luke 17:21 should be translated "inside you,"
drawing on Greek usage and ancient Syriac versions.

Therefore:

WITHIN_YOU =
LEXICALLY_SERIOUS_READING.

It must not be dismissed merely because it creates theological discomfort.

## 3. "Among / in your midst" is also a serious reading

A substantial modern exegetical tradition prefers:
- among you;
- in your midst.

Arguments include:
- the plural `ὑμῶν`;
- the Pharisee addressees in Luke's narrative;
- Luke's wider Kingdom usage;
- contextual contrast with visible localization.

Tom Holmén and other scholarship preserve the semantic debate rather than
allowing an easy one-word proof.

Therefore:

AMONG_YOU =
LEXICALLY_AND_CONTEXTUALLY_SERIOUS_READING.

## 4. A third proximity/presence family exists

More recent philological work has also proposed senses close to:
- before you;
- in front of you;
- within your reach/presence.

This reinforces the central methodological result:

`ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`
does not have a scholarly uncontested English equivalent.

## 5. Translation uncertainty is not historical-saying uncertainty

Even if the Greek sense were solved perfectly, a second problem remains.

Luke is a later first-century narrative source.

R2 must still ask:
- Is the saying Lukan composition?
- Is it inherited special material?
- Does it preserve an older oral saying?
- Is the wording redactionally shaped?

Therefore:

LEXICAL_MEANING
!=
HISTORICAL_YESHUA_AUTHENTICITY.

## 6. The Pharisee argument is not decisive by itself

A common objection says:
Jesus could not mean "inside you" because he is speaking to Pharisees.

That argument is weaker than it first appears.

Possible alternatives include:
- generic/plural address;
- polemical irony;
- anthropological claim applying even to opponents;
- Luke's literary staging rather than a transcript.

Therefore:

PHARISEE_ADDRESSEE
does not logically exclude
INTERIOR_SEMANTICS.

It remains a contextual pressure, not a proof.

## 7. "Inside" would still not prove the project's full mystical model

Suppose, for argument, that:
`ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` = "inside you."

That would support:
SOME_FORM_OF_INTERIOR_KINGDOM_LANGUAGE.

It would not by itself establish:
- humans contain a literal divine substance;
- the soul is a fragment of Source;
- reincarnation;
- nondual ontology;
- Gnostic anthropology;
- Kabbalistic divine sparks;
- a hidden-self salvation system;
- that interiority was Yeshua's central teaching.

Those require independent propositions.

## 8. "Among you" would also not eliminate interiority elsewhere

If:
`ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` = "among/in your midst,"

that would weaken this verse as interior evidence.

It would not prove:
YESHUA_REJECTED_INTERIOR_TRANSFORMATION.

Other sayings and trajectories would still require analysis.

Therefore both camps can overclaim.

## 9. Relation to Gospel of Thomas

Thomas creates two important later parallels/contrasts:

Thomas 3:
Kingdom language explicitly involving inside/outside.

Thomas 113:
Kingdom spread upon the earth but unrecognized.

This demonstrates a strong early-Christian trajectory combining:
- hidden presence;
- perception;
- interior/exterior language.

But:

THOMAS_PARALLEL
does not decide
LUKE_DEPENDENCE_OR_YESHUA_AUTHENTICITY.

A saying-level Thomas independence packet is required.

## 10. Relation to Jewish mystical material

Second Temple Jewish literature demonstrates:
- interior ethical transformation;
- divine spirit/wisdom discourse;
- heavenly vision;
- hidden revelation;
- heavenly participation.

But those are not lexical equivalents of Luke 17:21.

Developed Hekhalot/Merkabah and Kabbalah are much later.

Therefore:

JEWISH_MYSTICAL_TRAJECTORY
can supply comparative context,
not a translation of `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`.

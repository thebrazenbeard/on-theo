# Synoptic Present / Future Kingdom Matrix V1

Status: R2 SOURCE-RELATION CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Determine whether the earliest recoverable Kingdom material is:
- future only;
- present only;
- or structurally mixed.

This matrix does not assume that every Synoptic occurrence goes back to Yeshua.

## 1. Mark 1:14–15

Mark's programmatic summary announces:
- fulfilled time;
- the Kingdom of God has drawn near;
- repentance and trust in good news.

Historical value:
EARLIEST_SURVIVING_NARRATIVE_PROGRAM.

Interpretive pressure:
IMMINENCE / PROXIMITY.

The verb `ἤγγικεν` supports nearness but does not by itself settle:
- already fully present;
- just about to arrive;
- inaugurated-but-incomplete.

Disposition:
FUTURE/PRESENT_BOUNDARY = OPEN.

## 2. Mark 4 Kingdom parables

Mark 4 includes seed/growth imagery in which:
- something has already begun;
- its development can remain hidden or disproportionate;
- a later harvest/manifestation remains possible.

Historical value:
EARLY_NARRATIVE_PARABLE_TRADITION_WITH_REDACTION_CONTROLS.

Disposition:
PRESENT_PROCESS_PLUS_FUTURE_COMPLETION = STRONG_MARKAN_READING.

This does not prove every parable is authentic to Yeshua.

## 3. Mark 9:1

Mark preserves a saying that some standing there will not taste death before
seeing the Kingdom of God come in power.

At minimum, the saying encodes:
- expectation within a near human horizon;
- future manifestation/power.

Interpretations vary over:
- transfiguration;
- resurrection;
- destruction of Jerusalem;
- parousia/judgment;
- Markan narrative function.

Disposition:
NEAR_FUTURE_EXPECTATION = STRONG_TEXTUAL.

Historical Yeshua attribution:
PROBABLE_BUT_NOT_SOLVED_BY_TEXT_ALONE.

## 4. Mark 10 entry sayings

Mark 10 repeatedly speaks of:
- receiving/entering the Kingdom;
- difficulty for the rich;
- childlike reception.

This combines:
- access;
- conduct/status;
- future participation language.

Disposition:
ETHICAL_SOCIAL_ACCESS_DIMENSION = STRONG_MARKAN.
TEMPORAL_MODE = MIXED/NOT_EXHAUSTED.

## 5. Mark 14:25

Jesus is portrayed anticipating future drinking in God's Kingdom.

Disposition:
FUTURE_KINGDOM_EXPECTATION = STRONG_MARKAN.

Historical source level:
PASSION_TRADITION_REQUIRES_SEPARATE_STRATIFICATION.

## 6. Lord's Prayer tradition

Matthew/Luke share prayer for God's Kingdom to come.

Source relation:
DOUBLE_TRADITION.

Possible models:
- shared Q-like source;
- oral overlap;
- literary dependence.

Disposition:
FUTURE_KINGDOM_PRAYER_TRADITION = STRONG_AT_TRADITION_LEVEL.
INDEPENDENCE_COUNT = NOT_TWO_BY_DEFAULT.

## 7. Exorcism / Kingdom-arrival saying

Matthew 12:28 / Luke 11:20 portray Jesus saying that if demons are expelled by
divine power then God's Kingdom has come upon the hearers.

The wording strongly supports:
PRESENT_INBREAKING.

Source relation:
DOUBLE_TRADITION / DEPENDENCE_MODEL_REQUIRED.

Disposition:
PRESENT_KINGDOM_TRADITION = STRONG_AT_TRADITION_LEVEL.

Historical attribution:
MODERATE_TO_STRONG_CANDIDATE,
not independently solved.

## 8. Beatitude / reversal traditions

Matthew/Luke preserve Kingdom language tied to:
- poor;
- hungry;
- mourners;
- persecuted;
- reversal.

Some formulations are present-tense ("yours is the Kingdom"), while promised
changes are future.

Disposition:
PRESENT_BELONGING_PLUS_FUTURE_REVERSAL = STRONG_TRADITIONAL_PATTERN.

Exact original wording:
SOURCE-CRITICAL.

## 10. Matrix result

The canonical Synoptic field is not well described by:

FUTURE_ONLY

or

PRESENT_ONLY.

The source patterns instead include:

FUTURE_EXPECTATION
+
PRESENT_INBREAKING
+
CURRENT_ACCESS/ALLEGIANCE
+
SOCIAL_ETHICAL_REVERSAL
+
FUTURE_COMPLETION/JUDGMENT.

Current strongest model:

ALREADY_BUT_NOT_COMPLETE
is a useful descriptive shorthand,

but the project avoids turning that later theological formula into an ancient
source term.

## 11. Historical ceiling

The mixed pattern is strong at the level of early Synoptic tradition.

It remains possible that:
- some future sayings are secondary;
- some present sayings are secondary;
- Mark/Luke/Matthew each reshape the balance.

Therefore:

MIXED_KINGDOM_PATTERN =
STRONG_EARLY_TRADITIONAL_RESULT.

MIXED_PATTERN_AS_EXACT_YESHUA_SYSTEM =
PROBABLE_NOT_FINAL.

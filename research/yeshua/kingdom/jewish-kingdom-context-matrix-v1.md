## 4. Qumran messianic plurality

The Rule literature can distinguish:
- prophet;
- messiahs of Aaron and Israel;
- priestly and lay/royal roles.

Other Qumran texts preserve different eschatological configurations.

Therefore:
ONE_STANDARD_JEWISH_MESSIAH =
FALSE_AS_COMPARATIVE_DEFAULT.

Yeshua's Kingdom language cannot be mapped automatically onto one Qumran office.

## 5. Qumran apocalyptic / Torah integration

Oxford synthesis of the Dead Sea Scrolls emphasizes:
- eschatology and messianism;
- influence from Daniel/Enoch;
- strong Torah observance;
- sectarian communal order.

This is important because it blocks a false binary:

APOCALYPTIC_EXPECTATION
!=
REJECTION_OF_JEWISH_LAW_OR_COMMUNAL_DISCIPLINE.

Yeshua can be apocalyptic and still thoroughly Jewish.

## 6. Enochic traditions

Enochic literature contributes:
- heavenly judgment;
- hostile supernatural powers;
- vindication;
- cosmic history;
- heavenly agents.

The Parables/Similitudes develop individualized Son-of-Man/Chosen-One material,
but:
- their date remains debated;
- they are absent from the Qumran Enoch fragments;
- no direct dependence on Yeshua is assumed.

Use:
APOCALYPTIC_PLAUSIBILITY_FIELD.

## 7. Hostile powers and exorcism

Second Temple Jewish traditions increasingly articulate:
- Watchers;
- giant spirits;
- Mastema;
- Belial;
- Satan/adversarial figures.

These are not one entity biography.

But they establish a historically plausible world in which:
EXORCISM / HOSTILE_SPIRIT_DEFEAT
could be interpreted as evidence of divine eschatological victory.

This makes the exorcism-Kingdom saying culturally intelligible.

It does not prove the event or saying authentic.

## 8. Wisdom / interiority

Jewish wisdom traditions can locate:
- Torah;
- wisdom;
- moral discernment;
- transformed heart/mind

in intimate relation to the person.

Second Temple Jewish religion therefore does not force:
EXTERNAL_APOCALYPSE
versus
INTERIOR_TRANSFORMATION.

However:

WISDOM_INTERIORITY
!=
KINGDOM_WITHIN
without a source bridge.

## 9. Mystical / heavenly participation

Qumran and other Jewish sources preserve:
- heavenly liturgy;
- visionary participation;
- angelic worlds;
- transformative ascent motifs.

This demonstrates that intense experiential/visionary Judaism existed within the
period.

It does not establish:
Yeshua practiced ascent mysticism
or
Kingdom = mystical union.

## 10. Contextual conclusion

The historically plausible Jewish field already permits a combination of:
- future divine intervention;
- present communal enactment;
- ethical transformation;
- heavenly participation;
- messianic/agent diversity;
- conflict with hostile powers.

Therefore Yeshua's mixed Kingdom pattern does not require importing:
- later Gnosticism;
- Hekhalot;
- Kabbalah;
- Greek mystery religion.

Those later comparators may illuminate trajectories but are unnecessary to make
the early mixed model historically Jewish.

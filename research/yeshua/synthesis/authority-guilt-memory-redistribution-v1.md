# Yeshua Authority, Guilt, and Memory Redistribution V1

Status: `SOURCE-CRITICAL SYNTHESIS / INTERNAL HOSTILE TEST REQUIRED / NON-CANONICAL`

Date: 2026-10-02

## Question

Why does surviving Christian memory distribute:
- understanding,
- failure,
- betrayal,
- rehabilitation,
- authority,
- witness,
- and later legitimacy

so differently among Judas Iscariot, Peter/Cephas, Mary Magdalene, Thomas, James, and the wider disciple group?

This lane tests whether the surviving pattern is better explained by:

1. relatively faithful preservation of distinct remembered roles;
2. ordinary post-catastrophe memory sorting;
3. later authority competition and literary/theological redaction;
4. concentration of a broader betrayal/failure crisis into Judas;
5. deliberate coordinated frame-up or political cultivation.

No option is assumed at the start.

## 1. Earliest surviving first-generation layer: Paul

The undisputed Pauline letters strongly anchor:
- Cephas/Peter as a real first-generation leader;
- James as the Lord's brother and major Jerusalem authority;
- other apostles;
- serious disagreement among leaders;
- resurrection proclamation.

Paul does not name Judas Iscariot.

Paul's received appearance formula names "the Twelve," producing a real tension with later narratives in which Judas has exited the group. The tension is compatible with a corporate title, formulaic language, altered membership, or lost source history.

What Paul does **not** give us:
- a developed Judas betrayal narrative;
- Mary Magdalene;
- Peter's denial;
- Thomas;
- detailed arrest responsibility.

Result:

`PAULINE_LAYER = EARLY_AUTHORITY_MAP_WITHOUT_LATER_PASSION_CHARACTER_SORTING`.

This is important because Petrine/James authority is already early; it was not created only after the narrative Gospels.

## 2. Mark: distributed failure, not eleven innocent apostles

Mark, the earliest surviving continuous Gospel narrative in the current source clock, does not protect the whole inner circle.

It portrays:
- Judas, one of the Twelve, approaching the chief priests;
- a promised payment;
- Judas facilitating the arrest and supplying an identifying signal;
- the disciples collectively fleeing;
- Peter denying association with Yeshua;
- women, including Mary Magdalene, remaining important in passion/tomb memory.

Therefore the simplest strong version of a scapegoat thesis fails:

`MARK DOES NOT TRANSFER ALL DISCIPLE FAILURE INTO JUDAS`.

Judas receives the singular intentional-handover role, but collective cowardice/failure remains visible.

This matters because a literary strategy designed merely to make the recognized apostles look innocent would have had an easier option: erase their flight and Peter's denial. Mark does not do that.

## 3. Judas: increasing concentration of moral explanation

The dedicated Judas lane already establishes the extant trajectory:

### Mark
- named Judas;
- member of Twelve;
- handover;
- promised money;
- kiss/identification;
- motive not fully psychologized.

### Matthew
Adds/develops:
- thirty silver pieces;
- remorse;
- return of money;
- hanging;
- scriptural fulfillment framing.

### Luke-Acts
Adds/develops:
- Satanic entry;
- apostolic replacement;
- a distinct death/property tradition.

### John
Adds/develops:
- thief characterization;
- intensified devil/Satan framing;
- foreknowledge/opposition.

### Papias/later reception
Adds grotesque bodily/death legend.

### Gospel of Judas
Radically reconfigures Judas, the Twelve, knowledge, sacrifice, and betrayal.

Result:

`JUDAS MORAL/COSMIC CHARACTERIZATION INTENSIFIES IN EXTANT SOURCES`.

This is a source-development fact.

It does **not** establish:
- Judas innocence;
- Judas invention;
- frame-up;
- Roman recruitment.

## 4. Peter: failure retained, authority retained, restoration increasingly explicit

The Peter lane produces a different trajectory.

Early:
- Paul independently anchors Cephas as a major authority;
- Paul also records serious conflict with him.

Mark:
- Peter is prominent;
- perceptive and misunderstanding;
- boasts;
- fails;
- denies;
- nevertheless receives a narrative reinclusion signal in Mark 16:7.

Later:
- Matthew develops Petrine authority language;
- Luke-Acts gives Peter extensive leadership;
- John 21 provides an explicit literary restoration/commission after the denial pattern.

Result:

`PETER FAILURE IS NOT ERASED; IT IS NARRATIVELY REINTEGRATED INTO AUTHORITY`.

This is a real asymmetry with Judas.

Peter's failure becomes survivable and identity-compatible.
Judas's failure becomes increasingly terminal and identity-defining.

That asymmetry is compatible with authority-conditioned memory.

It is not proof that Peter committed an unreported betrayal.

## 5. Mary Magdalene: prominence survives before later conflation

Mary is not a clean example of early villainization.

The source-controlled Mary lane supports:
- historical Mary: probable;
- Galilean follower memory: probable;
- Markan passion/burial/tomb prominence: strong at the Markan memory level;
- canonical sources do not identify her as a prostitute;
- the later Western sinner/prostitute synthesis is reception history.

John gives Mary an especially strong individual witness/messenger role.

The later *Gospel of Mary* then constructs a conflict in which:
- Mary teaches/reports revelation;
- Peter and Andrew resist;
- Levi defends her;
- the group divides.

Modern scholarship notes that the surviving Coptic recension heightens antagonism relative to the Greek fragments.

Result:

`MARY AUTHORITY-CONFLICT = STRONG LATER TEXTUAL PHENOMENON`.

But:

`HISTORICAL PETER-MARY CONFLICT = NOT ESTABLISHED`.

And:

`CONTINUOUS FIRST-CENTURY-TO-MEDIEVAL COORDINATED MARY DISCREDITING CAMPAIGN = NOT ESTABLISHED`.

A better model is discontinuous competition and later reception transformation.

## 6. Thomas: doubt and privileged knowledge are competing literary memories

John's Thomas:
- resists the group's resurrection report;
- demands evidence;
- later makes an exceptionally high confession.

The *Gospel of Thomas*:
- frames itself as secret sayings associated with Didymos Judas Thomas;
- in saying 13 Thomas receives private words from Jesus after Peter and Matthew offer inadequate comparisons.

Result:

`THOMAS AS EPISTEMIC RESISTER / PRIVILEGED KNOWER = LATER CROSS-TEXTUAL CONTRAST`.

What is not established:
- Thomas refused participation in an arrest conspiracy;
- Thomas belonged to a historical anti-Petrine faction during Yeshua's lifetime.

## 7. A crucial second-century counter-map: Cainites / Gospel of Judas reception

Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* 1.31, reports a group he labels Cainites who:
- positively valorized Cain and other scriptural transgressors;
- claimed Judas alone knew the truth;
- said he accomplished the "mystery of the betrayal";
- possessed a work called the *Gospel of Judas*.

The surviving *Gospel of Judas* is not a simple confirmation of every detail in Irenaeus, and scholarship disputes whether Judas is ultimately a positive hero.

Still, this is strong evidence that by the late second century there existed a Christian counter-map in which:

`THE TWELVE CAN BE WRONG WHILE JUDAS POSSESSES SUPERIOR KNOWLEDGE`.

That does not move the claim back to 30 CE.

It does show that apostolic legitimacy was actively contestable.

## 8. Passion responsibility is redistributed too

The R4 passion-responsibility matrix finds a separate diachronic pattern:

- Roman crucifixion under Pilate remains the strongest event-level execution anchor.
- Mark distributes agency among local chief-priestly actors, Judas, Pilate/Rome.
- Matthew develops Judas money/remorse and stronger crowd/Pilate motifs.
- Luke-Acts develops Pilate innocence/reluctance rhetoric.
- John develops extended Pilate dialogue and broader opponent categories.
- later Pilate traditions can exculpate him more strongly still.

Result:

`RESPONSIBILITY MEMORY IS MALLEABLE`.

Particularly important:

`SELECTED PILATE MITIGATION + JUDAS INTENSIFICATION = OBSERVABLE RECEPTION PATTERN`.

But:

`OBSERVABLE BLAME SHIFT != ROMAN CENSORSHIP OR STATE ENGINEERING`.

## 9. Competing models

### Model A — distinct-role preservation

Judas really facilitated the arrest; Peter really denied; others really fled; Mary really remained prominent; later texts elaborated without fundamentally redistributing historical roles.

Status:
`LIVE / PLAUSIBLE`.

Strength:
requires the fewest hidden actors.

Weakness:
does not by itself explain the scale and direction of later characterization growth.

### Model B — catastrophe plus memory sorting

A chaotic arrest/execution crisis produced:
- flight;
- denial;
- uneven knowledge;
- possible informing;
- guilt;
- survivor authority;
- conflicting post-death claims.

Subsequent communities stabilized this mess into different moral roles.

Status:
`LIVE / CURRENTLY STRONGEST GENERAL EXPLANATORY MODEL`.

This model can produce:
- a singular betrayer;
- a rehabilitated leader;
- privileged witnesses;
- doubters;
- rival claims to understanding

without a centrally organized conspiracy.

### Model C — betrayal-memory concentration

A broader or less individualized collaboration/handover crisis became increasingly concentrated into Judas.

Status:
`LIVE / SPECULATIVE / LOW-MODERATE`.

Supporting pressure:
- later Judas characterization intensifies;
- other disciple failure remains but is rehabilitable;
- rival later Christian texts can invert recognized authority.

Against:
- Mark already contains a specific Judas role;
- no independently early source names another disciple as deliberate arrest collaborator;
- no positive administrative evidence of a collective betrayal.

### Model D — coordinated apostolic frame-up

Recognized apostles jointly blamed Judas to conceal their own deliberate betrayal.

Status:
`CURRENTLY WEAK / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

Missing:
- independent early accusation;
- administrative traces;
- rival first-century testimony;
- explanatory advantage over Model B.

### Model E — Roman cultivation / political steering

Roman or Roman-connected actors deliberately cultivated selected Christian trajectories.

Status:
`UNKNOWN / CURRENTLY LOW CONFIDENCE FOR FIRST-CENTURY FORM`.

Positive evidence:
none yet sufficient.

A later institutional-capture model remains much stronger than Tiberian design.

## 10. New synthesis: differential moral sorting

The most defensible new synthesis is not "Judas was framed."

It is:

`THE SURVIVING TRADITION SHOWS DIFFERENTIAL MORAL SORTING OF A DISCIPLE CRISIS`.

Observable examples:
- Judas: singular intentional handover -> increasingly terminal/demonic identity.
- Peter: denial/failure -> reinclusion -> increasingly explicit restoration/authority.
- wider disciples: flight/fear -> later apostolic mission.
- Mary: passion/tomb prominence -> later competing authority traditions -> later Western conflation unrelated to the earliest biographies.
- Thomas: resistance/doubt in one trajectory -> secret/intimate knowledge in another.

This is a historical claim about **texts and reception**, not about the hidden event.

## 11. Does this support Patrick's working suspicion?

Partially.

It supports asking:
- whether remembered guilt was distributed unevenly;
- whether later authority affected how failures were narrated;
- whether Judas accrued moral material over time;
- whether alternative Christian communities contested the legitimacy of the recognized Twelve;
- whether Mary and Thomas became vehicles for alternative claims to understanding.

It does not currently support asserting:
- Judas was innocent;
- Judas and Mary formed a historical loyalist faction;
- Peter or Thomas knew of an apostolic plot;
- most apostles were bribed or activated agents;
- Rome authored the blame map.

## 13. Hostile conclusion

The evidence does **not** currently justify a recovered secret history in which Judas was framed by a Roman-controlled apostolic bloc.

It does justify a harder claim:

> The arrest/execution crisis is remembered through sources that increasingly assign different moral destinies to different followers, and those destinies correlate in some cases with later authority and reception. The pattern is real; its cause remains underdetermined.

The strongest current explanation is ordinary but powerful:

`CATASTROPHE + SURVIVORSHIP + THEOLOGY + AUTHORITY COMPETITION + TRANSMISSION`

before:

`CENTRAL CONSPIRACY`.

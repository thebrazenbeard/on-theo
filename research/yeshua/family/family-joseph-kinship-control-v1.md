# R6 Family / Joseph / Kinship Control V1

Status: EARLY FAMILY-MEMORY / NEGATIVE-EVIDENCE CONTROL

## 1. Early kinship survives

Early sources preserve:
- mother;
- brothers;
- sisters;
- James specifically;
- other brothers of the Lord.

Current result:

YESHUA_KINSHIP_MEMORY =
EARLY_AND_NONTRIVIAL.

This matters because family was not simply erased from movement memory.

## 2. James

Paul personally knows James and identifies him as "brother of the Lord."

Josephus later preserves a James/Jesus brother linkage under separate
authenticity controls.

Current disposition:

JAMES_AS_CLOSE_KIN/BROTHER =
PROBABLE_TO_STRONG.

This does not by itself solve:
- Mary's marital history;
- Joseph's status;
- paternal identity.

## 3. Joseph silence

Joseph is absent from:
- Paul;
- Mark;
- adult-ministry family scenes.

Possible explanations:
- death before ministry;
- absence/estrangement;
- no historical Joseph;
- narrative selectivity.

Current disposition:

JOSEPH_ABSENCE_IN_EARLY_ADULT_MINISTRY_SOURCES =
REAL.

JOSEPH_DIED_BEFORE_MINISTRY =
PLAUSIBLE_BUT_UNPROVEN.

JOSEPH_NEVER_EXISTED =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 4. Joseph positive tradition

Matthew/Luke independently as works preserve Joseph traditions.

John preserves "son of Joseph" public identification.

Current result:

JOSEPH_AS_SOCIAL_FATHER_TRADITION =
STRONG_BY_LATE_FIRST_CENTURY.

Historical Joseph:
PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE,
but not equivalent to biological-father proof.

## 5. Spouse/child negative evidence

Existing missing-years work finds:
- no early wife tradition;
- no early child tradition;
- early sources can preserve other people's wives;
- family memory survives strongly enough that a public dynastic child would
  create some expectation of trace.

Current result:

PUBLIC_MOVEMENT_RELEVANT_WIFE_OR_CHILD =
DISFAVORED_BY_LIMITED_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE,
NOT_DISPROVED.

DELIBERATE_SUPPRESSION =
NO_POSITIVE_SUPPORT_IDENTIFIED.

## 6. Mary Magdalene

No earliest source calls Mary Magdalene:
- wife;
- sexual partner;
- mother of Yeshua's child.

Later intimacy/symbolic traditions do not recover a first-century marriage.

Current disposition:

MARY_MAGDALENE_AS_WIFE =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 7. Family conflict

Mark preserves tension between Yeshua and family in selected scenes.

That is historically relevant to social-memory reconstruction.

But:
FAMILY_CONFLICT
does not establish
PATERNITY_STIGMA.

It may have:
- theological;
- literary;
- social;
- historical components.

## 8. Strongest result

YESHUA_HAS_AN_EARLY_AND_RECOVERABLE_KIN_NETWORK_CENTERED_ON_MOTHER_BROTHERS_SISTERS_AND_ESPECIALLY_JAMES_WHILE_JOSEPH_APPEARS_ONLY_IN_LATER_FIRST_CENTURY_TRADITION; THIS_MAKES_JOSEPH_A_PLAUSIBLE_HISTORICAL_SOCIAL_FATHER_BUT_LEAVES_BIOLOGICAL_PATERNITY_UNRESOLVED_AND_CREATES_ONLY_LIMITED_NEGATIVE_PRESSURE_AGAINST_PUBLIC_WIFE_CHILD_HYPOTHESES.

## 1. Earliest Pauline layer

Paul provides:
- Yeshua as "born of a woman" (Gal 4:4);
- Davidic/descent language in Rom 1:3 where admitted;
- James as "brother of the Lord";
- "brothers of the Lord" as a recognizable category.

Paul does not provide:
- Joseph as father;
- a virgin-conception narrative;
- Panthera;
- an infancy story;
- a paternity dispute.

Current control:

PAUL =
EARLY_KINSHIP_AND_HUMAN_BIRTH_FLOOR,
NOT
PARENTAGE_NARRATIVE.

## 2. Mark

Mark preserves:
- Yeshua's mother;
- brothers;
- sisters;
- Mark 6:3's "the carpenter, the son of Mary" in the dominant critical reading.

Mark does not name Joseph.

Current control:

SON_OF_MARY =
EARLY_AND_UNUSUAL_TEXTUAL_DATA.

Meaning:
UNRESOLVED.

## 3. Matthew

Matthew preserves:
- Joseph as Mary's husband / Yeshua's legal-social father figure;
- conception from Holy Spirit;
- no sexual relations before birth;
- genealogy through Joseph;
- scriptural interpretation including Isaiah 7:14.

Current use:

MATTHEAN_VIRGIN_CONCEPTION =
LATE_FIRST_CENTURY_PRIMARY_TEXT / THEOLOGICAL_BIOGRAPHICAL_TRADITION.

It is not direct first-century documentary proof of conception mechanics.

## 4. Luke

Luke preserves:
- Mary-centered annunciation;
- conception without ordinary male intercourse;
- Joseph as betrothed/social father;
- later public perception that Yeshua was Joseph's son;
- a genealogy routed through Joseph "as was supposed."

Current use:

LUKAN_VIRGIN_CONCEPTION =
LATE_FIRST_CENTURY_PRIMARY_TEXT / THEOLOGICAL_BIOGRAPHICAL_TRADITION.

Matthew and Luke must not be harmonized into one infancy source.

## 5. Matthew / Luke relation

The two infancy narratives differ substantially in:
- narrative center;
- chronology;
- geography;
- visitors;
- threat/flight material;
- genealogies;
- sequence.

They also share:
- Mary;
- Joseph;
- Bethlehem birth;
- later Nazareth association;
- extraordinary conception without ordinary human fatherhood.

Current rule:

SHARED_MATTHEW_LUKE_CONCEPTION_CLAIM
=
IMPORTANT_LATE_FIRST_CENTURY_MULTISOURCE_OR_SHARED_TRADITION_DATA.

But:

TWO_GOSPELS
does NOT automatically equal
TWO_INDEPENDENT_BIOLOGICAL_WITNESSES.

Exact literary/traditional dependence remains a source-critical question.

## 6. John

John includes traditions identifying Yeshua as:
- "son of Joseph" in public/disciple speech.

Current use:

JOSEPH_AS_PUBLIC_SOCIAL_PATERNITY_MEMORY =
LATER_CANONICAL_DATA.

This does not adjudicate:
BIOLOGICAL_PATERNITY.

## 7. Genealogies

Matthew and Luke both attach Yeshua to Davidic genealogy through Joseph but
their genealogical structures differ substantially.

Current result:

GENEALOGIES =
THEOLOGICALLY_AND_DYNASTICALLY_IMPORTANT_KINSHIP_TEXTS.

They do not function as two matching modern civil-registry records.

## 8. Mark 6:3 matronymic

Possible readings include:
- Joseph dead/absent;
- illegitimacy/stigma;
- virgin-birth-related interpretation;
- informal/local usage;
- Markan literary matronymic/female-line characterization.

Current disposition:

SON_OF_MARY_STIGMA_READING =
PLAUSIBLE_LIVE_INTERPRETATION.

SON_OF_MARY_PROVES_ILLEGITIMACY =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 9. Panthera / Celsus

Celsus is known through Origen.

By the late second century a counter-story existed alleging:
- Mary's adultery;
- rejection by the carpenter;
- a soldier named Panthera as father.

Current classification:

PANTHERA =
LATER_POLEMICAL_COUNTERTRADITION.

It establishes a later alternative parentage claim.

It does not establish biological paternity.

## 10. Rabbinic Pandera/Panteira material

Later rabbinic traditions preserve:
- Pandeira/Pantera name forms;
- Miriam;
- Yeshu/Yeshua name forms in selected witnesses/traditions;
- related healing/polemical material.

Required controls:
- passage identity;
- work date;
- redaction date;
- physical manuscript witness;
- censorship/restoration history;
- Jesus-identification dispute.

Current classification:

RABBINIC_PANDERA =
LATER_COUNTERMEMORY / POLEMICAL_RECEPTION.

Not:
FIRST_CENTURY_BIRTH_RECORD.

## 11. Panthera / parthenos wordplay

The proposed connection:
Panthera/Pandera ↔ Greek `parthenos`

is:
PLAUSIBLE_POLEMICAL_WORDPLAY_MODEL.

It is not:
ESTABLISHED_ETYMOLOGICAL_DERIVATION.

The fact that Pantera was a real personal name:
- does not prove paternity;
- does not disprove wordplay.

## 12. Missing-years integration

The missing-years lane currently supports:
- Joseph's adult-ministry absence is real source silence;
- Joseph's death before ministry is plausible but unproven;
- disputed parentage is a tracked possibility, not established;
- wife/child suppression has no positive trace;
- family kinship memory is real.

R6 imports those conclusions at their existing ceilings.

It does not back-propagate later sources into R1.

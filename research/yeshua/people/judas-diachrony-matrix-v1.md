## Reading rule

This matrix tracks **extant portrayals**.

It does not establish:
- a smooth linear evolution;
- independence of Matthew/Luke/John from Mark for each component;
- that earlier lost sources lacked later-looking features;
- that every later addition was invented by that author.

The historically useful observation is narrower:

The familiar composite Judas biography is not contained in any one early source. It is produced by harmonizing distinct source-specific portrayals.

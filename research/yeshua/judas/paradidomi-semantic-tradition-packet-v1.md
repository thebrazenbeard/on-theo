# παραδίδωμι Semantic / Tradition-History Packet V1

Status: LOAD-BEARING LEXICAL CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Prevent the English word "betray" from silently importing motive, treachery and
later Judas characterization into every occurrence of Greek `παραδίδωμι`.

Core rule:

LEXEME
!=
MOTIVE.

And:

"BETRAY"
may be a contextually justified translation in a Judas narrative
without being the lexeme's invariant meaning.

## 1. Basic semantic range

`παραδίδωμι` can mean:
- hand over;
- deliver;
- give over;
- entrust;
- transmit.

New Testament usage includes:
- handing people over to authorities;
- God handing people over;
- God handing Jesus over;
- Jesus giving himself over;
- handing down tradition;
- Judas handing Jesus over.

Therefore:

PARADIDOMI =
TRANSFER/HANDOVER_CORE_WITH_CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_EVALUATION.

## 2. 1 Corinthians 11:23 has a semantic doublet

Paul says:
- he "received" tradition;
- he "handed on" / transmitted it;
- Jesus, on the night in which he "was handed over," took bread.

The same lexical family appears in immediate proximity in two different senses:
- transmission of tradition;
- Jesus being handed over.

This strongly warns against reading:
`παρεδίδοτο`
as if the verb itself named Judas and his motive.

Current disposition:

1_COR_11_23 =
HANDOVER_LANGUAGE_WITH_AGENT_UNNAMED.

"BETRAYED" =
POSSIBLE_CONTEXTUAL_TRANSLATION_IN_LIGHT_OF_LATER_PASSION_MEMORY,
NOT_REQUIRED_BY_LEXEME_ALONE.

## 3. Pauline agency elsewhere

Paul can describe Jesus as handed over by:
- God;
- Jesus himself/self-giving logic.

This shows that Pauline handover theology can operate at a divine/soteriological
level rather than only a human-traitor narrative.

Therefore:

PAULINE_HANDOVER_THEOLOGY
!=
PAULINE_NAMED_JUDAS_TRADITION.

This does not prove Paul was ignorant of Judas.

## 4. Mark

Mark repeatedly uses `παραδίδωμι` for:
- John being handed over/arrested;
- future persecution/disciples being handed over;
- Jesus predicting being handed over;
- Judas handing Jesus over;
- authorities handing Jesus over to Pilate.

The narrative context gives Judas's handover a strongly negative meaning.

Therefore:

MARKAN_JUDAS_ACTION =
NARRATIVELY_BETRAYAL-LIKE / TREACHEROUS_IN_CONTEXT.

But:

THE_NEGATIVE_FORCE_COMES_FROM_THE_NARRATIVE_RELATION,
NOT_FROM_A_UNIQUE_DICTIONARY_MEANING_RESERVED_FOR_JUDAS.

## 5. Why the translation debate matters

Fergus J. King (2019) explicitly notes that `paradidonai` in Judas contexts can
be rendered "hand over" or "betray" and that the translation remains contested.

This is enough to require proposition-level caution.

The project does **not** adopt the strongest rehabilitation claim that:
"παραδίδωμι never can mean betray."

Nor does it adopt the opposite:
"παραδίδωμι proves malicious treachery wherever Judas is the subject."

Current lexical ceiling:

HAND_OVER =
SAFEST_NEUTRAL_GLOSS.

BETRAY =
CONTEXTUAL_INTERPRETIVE_GLOSS_IN_NEGATIVE_JUDAS_NARRATIVE.

## 6. Moral interpretation must be independently sourced

To infer historical motive such as:
- greed;
- malice;
- ideological disappointment;
- fear;
- obedience to Jesus;
- demonic control;

the project needs evidence beyond the lexeme.

Therefore:

PARADIDOMI
cannot distinguish among Judas motive hypotheses.

## 7. "Betrayer" as a retrospective identity

Once Judas becomes narratively identified by the handover act, later Christian
tradition can lexicalize/translate the action as betrayal and construct Judas as
"the betrayer."

This is historically important reception.

It still does not allow:

LATER_IDENTITY_LABEL
->
EARLIEST_MOTIVE.

## 8. Handing over in legal/political sequences

The same verb can describe transfers in judicial/custodial chains.

This matters because the passion narratives include sequences such as:
- disciple -> arresting authority;
- local authority -> Pilate;
- Pilate -> execution.

Therefore the verb can encode:
TRANSFER_OF_CONTROL.

That is especially relevant to the Judas historical core:

A historical event may be securely described first as:
FACILITATING_TRANSFER_INTO_CUSTODY

before deciding whether:
BETRAYAL
is the best psychological label.

### Mark

## 10. Strongest result

THE_EARLIEST_SAFE_DESCRIPTION_OF_THE_JUDAS_ACTION_IS_HANDING_YESHUA_OVER_OR_FACILITATING_HIS_TRANSFER_INTO_HOSTILE_CUSTODY_WHILE_THE_CATEGORY_BETRAYAL_IS_A_CONTEXTUALLY_REASONABLE_MARKAN_MORAL_INTERPRETATION_BUT_DOES_NOT_BY_ITSELF_RECOVER_JUDAS'S_HISTORICAL_MOTIVE.

## 11. Consequence for exoneration

Lexical neutrality does not exonerate Judas.

"Hand over" can still describe:
- disloyal action;
- harmful action;
- deliberate cooperation with hostile authorities.

Therefore:

HAND_OVER_NE_INNOCENT.

## 12. Consequence for condemnation

Likewise, translating "betray" does not prove:
- greed;
- demonic possession;
- intentional desire for Yeshua's death;
- knowledge that crucifixion would result.

Therefore:

BETRAY_TRANSLATION_NE_FULL_HISTORICAL_PSYCHOLOGY.

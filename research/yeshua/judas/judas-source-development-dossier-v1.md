## Purpose

Separate four propositions that are frequently collapsed:

1. a historical disciple named Judas Iscariot existed;
2. Judas performed some act of handing Yeshua over;
3. Judas supplied specific information or access necessary for arrest;
4. Judas acted from a specific motive such as greed, disillusionment, obedience,
   demonic control, political calculation, or cooperation with Yeshua.

These are not one claim.

Core rule:

JUDAS_EXISTENCE
!=
JUDAS_HANDOVER
!=
JUDAS_INFORMATION_FUNCTION
!=
JUDAS_MOTIVE.

A second rule:

LATER_REVERSAL_OF_JUDAS
!=
EARLIER_INNOCENCE_PROVED.

## 1. Paul

### 1 Corinthians 11:23

Paul uses `παραδίδωμι` language in transmitting the meal tradition.

The verb has a broad semantic range including:
- hand over;
- deliver;
- transmit;
- give up.

Elsewhere Paul can use the same verb with:
- God as subject;
- Jesus as subject;
- tradition as object.

Therefore:

PAULINE_PARADIDOMI
!=
INDEPENDENT_NAMED_JUDAS_BETRAYAL.

Paul does not name Judas Iscariot in the undisputed letters.

That silence is weak negative evidence because Paul rarely narrates Yeshua's
final week.

Current disposition:

PAUL_KNOWS_NAMED_JUDAS =
UNKNOWN.

PAUL_PROVES_NO_JUDAS_TRADITION =
REJECTED.

## 2. 1 Corinthians 15:5 and "the Twelve"

Paul preserves a received appearance tradition to "the Twelve."

This creates a real source-critical discriminator because later narrative
traditions remove Judas from the post-arrest group.

Possible explanations include:
- "the Twelve" functioning as a corporate group label despite numerical change;
- formulaic preservation;
- a different membership reckoning;
- source history now lost.

It does not establish:
- Judas survived;
- Judas remained among the group;
- Judas was innocent;
- Judas was invented.

Current disposition:

TWELVE_FORMULA_VS_LATER_ROSTER =
REAL_TENSION_NOT_EXONERATION.

## 3. Mark

Mark is the earliest admitted extant source that explicitly:
- names Judas Iscariot among the Twelve;
- says he went to the chief priests;
- says they promised money;
- presents him seeking an opportunity to hand Yeshua over;
- places him with the arrest party;
- gives him an identifying greeting/kiss.

This is the earliest explicit Judas-handover narrative in the current source
clock.

Current result:

MARKAN_JUDAS_HANDOVER_TRADITION =
SOURCE_BOUND_EARLY_GOSPEL_MEMORY.

But Mark alone does not yet separate:
- inherited passion tradition;
- Markan narrative shaping;
- historical event detail.

## 4. What did Mark's Judas actually supply?

This is a distinct historical question.

Mark already portrays Yeshua as:
- publicly teaching;
- publicly entering Jerusalem;
- publicly acting in/around the Temple;
- known to crowds and opponents.

That creates an arrest-logistics question:

WHY_WOULD_AUTHORITIES_NEED_AN_INSIDER_TO_IDENTIFY_HIM?

Possible functions include:
- identifying the correct person at night;
- identifying him away from crowds;
- locating a private gathering place;
- timing the arrest;
- providing access;
- supplying testimony/accusation;
- serving a literary fulfillment role.

Current evidence does not yet privilege one function strongly enough.

Recent historical work on Jesus' arrest emphasizes that Gospel descriptions of
the arresting party differ materially and that the logistics/policing context
must be reconstructed rather than simply harmonized.

Current disposition:

JUDAS_UNIQUE_INFORMATION_FUNCTION =
UNRESOLVED.

## 5. The kiss

Mark, Matthew and Luke preserve a kiss/approach tradition with differing
details.

The kiss functions narratively as:
- identification;
- betrayal irony;
- intimate-disciple reversal.

Historical possibilities:
- actual identification signal;
- narrative dramatization;
- inherited symbolic memory;
- mixed historical/literary core.

Current disposition:

JUDAS_KISS_AS_LITERAL_REQUIRED_IDENTIFICATION =
UNRESOLVED.

No late explanation of why Jesus needed identification may be retrojected as
historical fact.

## 6. Money

Mark says the chief priests promise Judas money.

Matthew specifies thirty silver pieces and later develops:
- Judas's remorse;
- return of money;
- death by hanging;
- scriptural fulfillment framing.

Therefore:

PAYMENT_EXISTS_IN_MARKAN_TRADITION =
YES.

THIRTY_SILVER_PIECES =
MATTHEAN_SPECIFICATION.

GREED_AS_PRIMARY_HISTORICAL_MOTIVE =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

Money in the narrative does not itself prove greed was the historical motive.

## 7. Luke-Acts

Luke preserves Judas as the betrayer/handover agent but changes the explanatory
frame.

Luke introduces Satan entering Judas and Acts supplies:
- a post-death replacement narrative;
- a distinct Judas death/property tradition.

The Luke-Acts layer therefore strengthens:

MORAL_COSMIC_CHARACTERIZATION_OF_JUDAS
in the extant narrative tradition.

It does not independently establish:
SATANIC_CAUSATION_AS_HISTORY.

The differences between Matthew and Acts on Judas's death are positive evidence
of divergent later traditions.

## 8. John

John presents the strongest negative canonical characterization:
- Judas as thief in the narrative;
- satanic/devilish framing;
- betrayal foreknowledge;
- intensified contrast with Jesus.

Current source-critical result:

JOHANNINE_JUDAS =
LATER_THEOLOGICALLY_INTENSIFIED_LITERARY_PORTRAYAL.

This can preserve earlier tradition.

It cannot be used uncritically as direct historical psychology.

## 9. Papias

Fragments attributed to Papias preserve a grotesque Judas death/body tradition.

Modern scholarship has shown that Papias's Judas material must be analyzed as:
- fragmentary;
- mediated through later quotation;
- rhetorically/literarily shaped.

Current disposition:

PAPIAS_JUDAS =
EARLY_SECOND_CENTURY_OR_LATER_RECEPTION_EVIDENCE_WITH_TRANSMISSION_CONTROLS.

It does not solve historical motive or death.

## 10. Gospel of Judas

The Gospel of Judas survives in Codex Tchacos, a fourth-century Coptic
manuscript, representing a composition generally placed much earlier than the
physical codex but still well after Yeshua's lifetime.

It gives Judas exceptional revelatory status relative to the Twelve and
radically recasts:
- Jesus;
- the creator;
- sacrifice;
- discipleship;
- martyrdom;
- Judas's role.

Scholars disagree over how positively the text ultimately evaluates Judas.
Some readings treat him as the one disciple who recognizes Jesus' true origin;
others emphasize that he remains implicated in Jesus' death and belongs to a
polemical cosmology that is not simple rehabilitation.

Current disposition:

GOSPEL_OF_JUDAS =
PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_FOR_LATER_CHRISTIAN_JUDAS_REINTERPRETATION.

It is not:
DIRECT_PROOF_OF_HISTORICAL_JUDAS_INNOCENCE.

## 12. Betrayal versus handover

Modern English "betray" adds motive and moral evaluation.

Greek `παραδίδωμι` can denote handing over without itself encoding the entire
psychological/moral story.

Therefore proposition-level analysis must separate:

HANDOVER_EVENT
from
BETRAYAL_MORAL_INTERPRETATION.

Mark's narrative context clearly moralizes Judas negatively.

But lexical semantics alone cannot prove:
GREED
TREACHERY_MOTIVE
or
secret hatred.

## 14. What is actually strongest historically?

At the current ceiling:

1. A Judas Iscariot tradition is embedded in the earliest surviving continuous
   Gospel narrative.
2. Mark presents Judas as a member of the Twelve who hands Yeshua over.
3. Paul does not independently name Judas.
4. Later sources significantly elaborate Judas's motive, moral character,
   death, cosmic role and authority.
5. The historical function and motive of Judas's action remain substantially
   underdetermined.

Current strongest bounded reconstruction:

A_HISTORICAL_JUDAS_ASSOCIATED_WITH_THE_DISCIPLE_GROUP_AND_SOME_FORM_OF_HANDOVER_IS_PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE,
WHILE_THE_EXACT_MOTIVE_PAYMENT_FUNCTION_KISS_LOGISTICS_AND_LATER_DEMONIC_PSYCHOLOGY_ARE_NOT_SECURELY_RECONSTRUCTED.

This is intentionally narrower than the canonical composite.

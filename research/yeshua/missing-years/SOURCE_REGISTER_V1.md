# Missing Years Source Register V1

Status: BOUNDED SOURCE REGISTER / NON-CANONICAL

This register records source roles, not blanket endorsements.

## Earliest Christian texts

### Undisputed Pauline letters
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT
Use:
- early family/movement memory;
- brothers of the Lord;
- Cephas/apostolic spouse comparison (1 Cor 9:5).
Limit:
- no childhood/young-adult biography.

### Mark
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT
Use:
- tekton (Mark 6:3);
- mother/brothers/sisters;
- adult public career;
- John association.
Limit:
- theological narrative, not continuous biography.

### Matthew / Luke
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT / LATER SYNOPTIC
Use:
- infancy/family traditions;
- Luke age-twelve and about-thirty framing.
Limit:
- dependence/redaction controls required;
- cannot establish continuous missing-years biography.

### John
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT / LATER GOSPEL
Use:
- later traditions potentially preserving John/Jesus baptismal competition/overlap.
Limit:
- later composition and literary/theological development require explicit control.

## Jewish/external sources

### Josephus, Antiquities
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT / EXTERNAL LITERARY WITNESS
Use:
- John the Baptist;
- James/Jesus tradition with authenticity controls;
- Galilean/Judean context.
Limit:
- Josephus writes decades later;
- Jesus/John/James passages require authenticity/provenance criticism.

### Josephus, War
Evidence class: PRIMARY TEXT / EXTERNAL LITERARY WITNESS
Use:
- Essene celibacy/marriage contextual control.
Limit:
- Essene evidence does not establish Yeshua membership.

## Archaeology and material context

### Ken Dark, Archaeology of Jesus' Nazareth (OUP, 2023)
Evidence class: MATERIAL EVIDENCE + SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- first-century Nazareth settlement, domestic life, economy.
Limit:
- specific "Jesus house" cannot be proven/refuted.

### Galilean economy archaeological syntheses
Representative:
David A. Fiensy and Ralph K. Hawkins (eds.), The Galilean Economy in the Time of Jesus (SBL, 2013).
Evidence class: MATERIAL EVIDENCE + SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- village/city economy;
- Sepphoris, Qana, Capernaum;
- economic-model disagreements.
Limit:
- regional economy does not establish Yeshua personal wealth.

### Mark A. Chancey, The Myth of a Gentile Galilee
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION
Use:
- control against exaggerated pagan/Gentile model of first-century Galilee.

## Marriage / household / sexuality

### Michael L. Satlow, Jewish Marriage in Antiquity
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- marriage age;
- household formation;
- betrothal/courtship;
- gap between normative texts and likely practice.
Limit:
- demographic evidence is incomplete and may be socially skewed.

### Michael L. Satlow, "Marriage and Divorce," Oxford Handbook of Jewish Daily Life in Roman Palestine
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- Roman-Palestinian family practice methodological control.

### Catherine Hezser (ed.), Oxford Handbook of Jewish Daily Life in Roman Palestine
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY SYNTHESIS
Use:
- household economy;
- village/countryside;
- travel/mobility;
- labor/trade;
- education/literacy;
- family life.
Limit:
- Roman-Palestinian evidence spans periods later than Yeshua and must not be flattened.

## Literacy

### Catherine Hezser, Jewish Literacy in Roman Palestine
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- control against universal literacy/schooling assumptions;
- scribal/oral social differentiation.

## John relationship

### New Testament Studies, "John the Baptist and Jesus: History and Hypotheses"
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- Synoptic redaction/chronology;
- possibility of post-baptism association before John's arrest.
Limit:
- extended discipleship remains hypothesis.

### Oxford Bibliographies, John the Baptist
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY SYNTHESIS
Use:
- current map of John scholarship and source problems.

## Joseph

### Traditional death inference
Evidence class: HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION
Basis:
- Joseph absent from public-ministry family scenes.
State:
PLAUSIBLE, NOT DIRECTLY ATTESTED.

### Andries van Aarde, historical-Joseph/fatherlessness hypothesis
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
Use:
- hostile alternative to automatic Joseph-death reconstruction.
State:
CONTESTED SCHOLARLY HYPOTHESIS.

## Later family memory

### Hegesippus in Eusebius
Evidence class: LATER TRADITION
Use:
- collateral relatives/descendants of Jude as family-memory control.
Limit:
- not direct first-century evidence.

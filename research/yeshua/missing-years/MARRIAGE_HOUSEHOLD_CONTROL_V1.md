# Yeshua Marriage / Household Control V1

Status: SOURCE-CRITICAL CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Question

Can the missing years support a historical conclusion that Yeshua married, remained celibate, fathered a child, or had a domestic relationship later suppressed?

## Direct evidence

Earliest-source wife evidence:
NONE.

Earliest-source child evidence:
NONE.

Earliest-source explicit celibacy statement:
NONE.

Earliest-source explicit marriage statement:
NONE.

Therefore:

MARITAL_STATUS = UNRESOLVED.

## Social norm

Marriage was common and culturally important.

But the slogan:
"every Jewish man married at eighteen"
is not a responsible historical control.

Roman-Palestinian evidence is incomplete and socially uneven.
Modern scholarly reconstruction permits later male first-marriage ages, in some work around thirty.

Therefore age alone does not solve Yeshua's marital status.

## Negative-evidence calibration

Negative evidence is not proof.

But silence becomes more informative when nearby sources do mention the same category.

Controls:
- Mark identifies mother, brothers, sisters.
- Paul identifies brothers of the Lord.
- 1 Corinthians 9:5 identifies wives accompanying Cephas, other apostles, and brothers of the Lord.

Inference:

THE_EARLIEST_MOVEMENT_COULD_PRESERVE_SPOUSAL_INFORMATION.

Hence a publicly significant Yeshua wife becomes somewhat harder to explain through pure accidental silence.

This inference does not exclude:
- a deceased spouse;
- estranged spouse;
- private relationship;
- relationship before public ministry;
- child unknown to later movement;
- deliberate suppression.

Those possibilities simply lack positive evidence.

## Deliberate suppression hypothesis

To move:
DELIBERATE_WIFE_CHILD_SUPPRESSION
above speculation, at least one of the following would be valuable:
- early competing source naming spouse/child;
- textual variant showing removal;
- early polemic presupposing spouse/child;
- independent family tradition;
- documentary/epigraphic evidence;
- editorial seam demonstrably created to remove a family claim.

Current evidence:
NONE IDENTIFIED.

Current classification:
SPECULATIVE MODEL / NO POSITIVE SUPPORT.

## Mary Magdalene

No earliest source identifies Mary Magdalene as Yeshua's wife.

Later Gospel of Philip intimacy/marital symbolism cannot be promoted into first-century biographical evidence.

Current classification:
MARY_AS_WIFE = LATER INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS / NOT HISTORICALLY ESTABLISHED.

## Child hypothesis

No early source identifies a biological child of Yeshua.

Hegesippus/Eusebius preserves collateral kin descendants through Jude but no direct descendants of Yeshua.

Current classification:
YESHUA_CHILD = SPECULATIVE / NO DIRECT SUPPORT.

YOUNG_ADULT_RELATIONSHIP = POSSIBLE BUT UNATTESTED.
YESHUA_CHILD = SPECULATIVE.

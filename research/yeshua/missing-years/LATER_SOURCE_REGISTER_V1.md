# Later-Source Trace Register V1

Status: SOURCE REGISTER / NON-CANONICAL

Purpose:
Bind the principal modern scholarship used to classify later Yeshua/Mary/family
traditions. These works support source criticism; they do not themselves become
primary evidence for first-century events.

## Gospel of Philip / marriage

### Karen L. King, "The Place of the Gospel of Philip in the Context of Early Christian Claims about Jesus' Marital Status" (2013)
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.
Use:
- argues the literary world of Philip portrays Jesus as married to Mary;
- explicitly rejects using Philip to determine historical Jesus marital status.
Control:
LITERARY_MARRIAGE_READING_NE_HISTORICAL_MARRIAGE.

### Simon Gathercole, "The Gospel of Jesus' Wife: Constructing a Context" (2015)
Evidence class: SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION.
Use:
- argues Philip's Jesus/Mary relation is privileged revelation rather than marriage;
- important counter-reading to King.
Control:
SCHOLARLY_DISAGREEMENT_MUST_BE_PRESERVED.

## General rule

A scholarly work may establish:
- date;
- textual history;
- interpretive options;
- source relationships;
- reception history.

It may not, by its authority alone, promote a proposition about Yeshua's life
above the evidence ceiling supplied by the underlying sources.

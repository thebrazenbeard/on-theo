# John the Baptist / Yeshua Source Stratigraphy V1

Status: SOURCE-CRITICAL STRATIGRAPHY / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Separate what the sources establish about Yeshua's relation to John from the attractive developmental hypothesis that Yeshua spent substantial time as John's disciple.

## Layer 1 — Mark

Mark opens Yeshua's public narrative with:
- John's proclamation and baptismal activity;
- Yeshua coming from Nazareth in Galilee;
- Yeshua receiving John's baptism;
- a transition through testing;
- Yeshua's proclamation after John is handed over/arrested.

Historical minimum supported by tradition:

YESHUA_ENTERED_JOHN_BAPTISMAL_ORBIT.

Mark's narrative does not directly describe:
- duration of contact;
- apprenticeship;
- shared residence;
- Yeshua baptizing for John;
- formal membership in John's movement.

## Layer 2 — Synoptic parallels / Q-related Baptist material

Matthew and Luke preserve overlapping Baptist proclamation material with source/dependence problems.

Important redaction control:

Mark/Matthew leave chronological space in which association after baptism and before John's arrest is possible.

Luke narratively places John's imprisonment before the baptismal scene and tightly links baptism/testing/Galilee, effectively closing that space.

Therefore:

GOSPEL_CHRONOLOGY_IS_NOT_NEUTRAL_RAW_BIOGRAPHY.

The disagreement makes exact post-baptism chronology less secure, not more.

## Layer 3 — Johannine traditions

John preserves traditions in which:
- John and Yeshua have overlapping circles;
- Yeshua's group is associated with baptism;
- John's disciples notice Yeshua's growing movement;
- later dispute/competition language appears.

These traditions may preserve historical memory of overlapping movements.

But the Fourth Gospel is later and highly theological.

Current ceiling:

SIMULTANEOUS/COMPETING_BAPTISMAL_ACTIVITY =
POSSIBLE_HISTORICAL_MEMORY.

DIRECT_PROOF_OF_APPRENTICESHIP =
NO.

## Layer 4 — Josephus

Josephus independently attests:
- John as a public teacher;
- righteousness/piety;
- baptism;
- substantial popular following;
- Herod Antipas' fear of John's influence;
- John's execution.

Josephus does not connect Yeshua to John.

Therefore Josephus independently strengthens:
JOHN_AS_REAL_REGIONAL_MOVEMENT_LEADER,

but does not independently attest:
YESHUA_AS_JOHN_DISCIPLE.

## Hypothesis ladder

J0:
Yeshua and John had no historical contact.
Disposition: DISFAVORED by early Christian baptism tradition.

J1:
Yeshua received John's baptism / entered his orbit.
Disposition: PROBABLE.

J2:
Yeshua remained with/around John's movement for some period.
Disposition: PLAUSIBLE / UNRESOLVED.

J3:
Yeshua functioned as a formal disciple/apprentice of John.
Disposition: SERIOUS SCHOLARLY HYPOTHESIS / UNRESOLVED.

J4:
Yeshua inherited and deliberately transformed John's movement after John's arrest/death.
Disposition: PLAUSIBLE DEVELOPMENTAL MODEL / REQUIRES FEATURE-BY-FEATURE TESTING.

## What John can explain if J2/J3 survive

Potential explanatory targets:
- Kingdom/esological urgency;
- repentance;
- prophetic vocation;
- baptismal movement dynamics;
- gathering followers;
- later differentiation from John's movement.

But:

JOHN_HYPOTHESIS_CANNOT_FILL_AGES_12_TO_LATE_20S.

At most it supplies a plausible late missing-years / pre-ministry bridge.

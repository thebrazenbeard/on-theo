# R5 Pauline Resurrection / Appearance Packet V1

Status: EARLIEST-SOURCE STRATIFICATION / NON-CANONICAL

## 1. Why Paul comes first

1 Corinthians predates the surviving narrative Gospels.

For R5, this means Paul constrains what was already believed before Mark's
continuous tomb narrative was written.

This does not mean:
PAUL_KNOWS_EVERY_EARLIER_TRADITION
or
PAULINE_THEOLOGY = YESHUA'S_OWN_TEACHING.

## 2. Received tradition — 1 Corinthians 15:3–5 core with disputed extension

Paul explicitly says he delivered what he had received.

The strongest commonly reconstructed received/formulaic core lies in:
- death;
- burial;
- being raised;
- appearance to Cephas;
- appearance to the Twelve.

Verses 6–7, including the five-hundred and James reports, may preserve inherited
tradition, Pauline supplementation, or a mixture.

Current result:

EARLY_RECEIVED_RESURRECTION_CORE =
VERY_STRONG.

EXTENSION_OF_ONE_FIXED_PRE_PAULINE_FORMULA_THROUGH_VERSES_6_7 =
UNRESOLVED.

Pre-Pauline dating of at least a core is highly plausible as a source-critical
conclusion, but:
- exact formula boundaries;
- exact wording;
- exact date;
- place of formulation
remain debated.

## 3. Cephas

Cephas is first in Paul's list.

Historical significance:
- very early individual appearance tradition;
- tied to a person Paul personally knew.

R5 can securely say:
CEPHAS_APPEARANCE_CLAIM =
VERY_EARLY.

It cannot recover:
- what Cephas perceived;
- where;
- duration;
- whether others were present;
- physical contact.

## 4. The Twelve

Paul reports appearance to "the Twelve."

Current control:

THE_TWELVE =
EARLY_GROUP_LABEL/TRADITION.

R5 does not infer:
- precise roster;
- Judas's presence/absence;
- exact number physically present during an experience.

Group label can persist despite membership change.

## 5. More than five hundred

The five-hundred report appears only in Paul among the earliest admitted texts.

Paul's statement that many were still alive functions rhetorically as an appeal
to contemporaneous availability.

But R5 lacks:
- names;
- location;
- independent surviving testimony;
- phenomenological description.

Current disposition:

FIVE_HUNDRED_APPEARANCE_REPORT =
EARLY_PAULINE_ATTESTATION,
NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_CORROBORATED.

Membership in one fixed pre-Pauline formula:
UNRESOLVED.

## 6. James

Paul reports an appearance to James.

This matters because:
- Paul personally knew James;
- James became a major movement leader;
- the report is early.

Current disposition:

JAMES_APPEARANCE_CLAIM =
EARLY_PAULINE_ATTESTATION_AND_SOCIAL_NETWORK_ANCHORED.

Pre-Pauline formula membership:
UNRESOLVED.

Mechanism remains unknown.

## 7. All the apostles

Paul distinguishes a further apostolic group.

R5 does not harmonize this automatically with:
- the Twelve;
- Gospel appearance scenes;
- later apostolic rosters.

## 8. Paul

Paul places his own later experience in the list.

Elsewhere Paul describes his encounter using:
- revelation;
- seeing the Lord;
- divine disclosure/calling language.

Current methodological consequence:

PAUL'S_LIST_DOES_NOT_BY_ITSELF_PROVE_THAT_ALL_APPEARANCES_WERE_ORDINARY_PHYSICAL_ENCOUNTERS.

But the converse is also invalid:

PAUL'S_REVELATORY_LANGUAGE
does not prove
ALL_APPEARANCES_WERE_PRIVATE_HALLUCINATIONS.

## 9. "Raised"

Paul's resurrection theology in 1 Corinthians 15 is not simple disembodied
survival.

He argues for:
- resurrection of the dead;
- transformed embodiment;
- continuity/discontinuity between mortal and resurrected existence.

Current control:

PAULINE_RESURRECTION =
TRANSFORMED_EMBODIED_EXISTENCE,
not merely
IMMORTAL_SOUL_ESCAPE.

## 10. "Spiritual body"

Paul's `sōma pneumatikon` is still `sōma`.

The contrast is not:
BODY
versus
NO_BODY.

It is a contrast between modes/qualities of embodied existence, commonly
understood as:
- perishable vs imperishable;
- mortal vs immortal;
- psychical/natural vs spirit-animated.

Current guard:

SPIRITUAL_BODY != DISEMBODIED_GHOST.

But R5 also avoids pretending Paul's transformed body theology gives a forensic
description of Yeshua's corpse.

## 11. Firstfruits / general resurrection

Paul treats Yeshua's resurrection as:
- firstfruits;
- anticipatory of a wider resurrection.

This places the claim inside an eschatological frame.

Current result:

YESHUA_RESURRECTION_IN_PAUL =
INDIVIDUAL_EVENT_INTERPRETED_AS_BEGINNING_OF_GENERAL_ESCHATOLOGICAL_RESURRECTION.

That theological interpretation is historically early.

Whether the underlying event occurred is a separate metaphysical/historical
question.

## 12. Earliest historical floor

R5 can say with high confidence:

- Yeshua was crucified.
- burial was included in early proclamation.
- very early followers proclaimed that God raised him.
- named individual/group appearance reports existed very early.
- Cephas and James are unusually strong social-network anchors.
- Paul himself reports a later experience he interpreted as an appearance/revelation of the risen Yeshua.
- transformed embodiment, not mere soul survival, is central to Paul's developed resurrection theology.

R5 cannot yet say:

- the tomb was empty;
- the same phenomenology underlies every appearance report;
- the appearances were objectively external;
- the appearances were hallucinations;
- the corpse physically transformed;
- fraud occurred;
- a supernatural event occurred.

## 13. Strongest result

THE_EARLIEST_SURVIVING_EVIDENCE_ESTABLISHES_AN_EXTREMELY_EARLY_RESURRECTION_VINDICATION_CONVICTION_WITH_NAMED_APPEARANCE_REPORTS_AND_A_TRANSFORMED_EMBODIMENT_THEOLOGY_BUT_DOES_NOT_PRESERVE_ENOUGH_PHENOMENOLOGICAL_DETAIL_TO_ADJUDICATE_THE_MECHANISM_OF_THE_REPORTED_APPEARANCES.

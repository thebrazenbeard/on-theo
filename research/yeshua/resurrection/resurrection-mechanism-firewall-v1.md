# R5 Resurrection Mechanism Firewall V1

Status: EVIDENCE-CEILING CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Prevent historical evidence for early belief from being converted directly into
a supernatural or naturalistic mechanism.

## Candidate model A — bodily divine resurrection

Proposition:
God physically transformed/raised Yeshua from death.

Current support:
- earliest followers proclaimed "raised";
- Paul's theology is embodied/transformation-oriented;
- later empty-tomb and physicality narratives.

Current limit:
texts are confessional/traditional sources and do not independently verify the
metaphysical mechanism.

Disposition:
METAPHYSICALLY_UNRESOLVED.

## Candidate model B — visionary/revelatory experiences

Proposition:
some or all appearance traditions arose from visionary/revelatory experiences
without ordinary physical encounter.

Support:
- Paul's own encounter is framed in revelatory language elsewhere;
- Jewish apocalyptic/visionary experience categories existed;
- early appearance reports contain little phenomenological detail.

Limit:
Paul's experience does not define Cephas/James/group experiences.

Disposition:
PLAUSIBLE_LIVE_RIVAL_NOT_PROVED.

## Candidate model C — grief/bereavement experiences

Proposition:
bereavement-related perception contributed to resurrection belief.

Support:
general human possibility and psychological analogy.

Limit:
R5 has no direct first-century clinical evidence about the experiencers.

Disposition:
SPECULATIVE_MODEL_UNPROVED.

## Candidate model D — legendary/theological development

Proposition:
appearance/tomb traditions became progressively elaborated through memory,
scripture, liturgy and narrative.

Support:
clear source development exists.

Limit:
development of details does not prove there was no originating experience/event.

Disposition:
SURVIVES_FOR_NARRATIVE_DEVELOPMENT,
INSUFFICIENT_AS_TOTAL_ORIGIN_EXPLANATION.

## Candidate model E — fraud/conspiracy

Proposition:
followers knowingly fabricated resurrection claims.

Current positive evidence:
insufficient.

Disposition:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

Absence of proof of resurrection is not evidence of fraud.

## Candidate model F — body relocation / wrong tomb / removal

Proposition:
an empty-tomb tradition could originate through ordinary body movement,
mislocation or removal.

Current limit:
requires empty-tomb historicity first, which itself remains to be adjudicated.

Disposition:
CONDITIONAL_SPECULATION.

## Candidate model G — mixed mechanisms

Different followers/groups could have:
- different experiences;
- different interpretations;
- later narrative elaboration.

Current disposition:
METHODologically_PLAUSIBLE
but not itself an event reconstruction.

## Historical floor versus mechanism

Historical floor:
VERY_EARLY_RESURRECTION_CONVICTION_AND_APPEARANCE_REPORTS.

Mechanism:
UNRESOLVED.

This distinction is mandatory downstream.

## Falsification pressure

Naturalistic models must explain:
- very early conviction;
- named appearance traditions;
- James/Cephas social anchors;
- Paul's own later experience;
- why resurrection rather than only immortal-soul/vindication language became central.

Supernatural models must not use:
- conviction itself;
- source sincerity;
- later physical detail
as circular proof of miracle.

## Strongest result

THE_HISTORICAL_DATA_REQUIRE_AN_EXPLANATION_FOR_AN_EXTREMELY_EARLY_AND_SOCially_ANCHORED_RESURRECTION_CONVICTION_BUT_CURRENT_TEXTUAL_EVIDENCE_UNDERDETERMINES_THE_CAUSAL_MECHANISM_AND_DOES_NOT_ALLOW_EITHER_SUPERNATURAL_ASSERTION_OR_SINGLE_NATURALISTIC_EXPLANATION_TO_BE_PROMOTED_AS_ESTABLISHED.

## Guards

- EXPLANATION_NEEDED_NE_MIRACLE_PROVED
- NATURALISTIC_PLAUSIBILITY_NE_NATURALISTIC_PROOF
- SINCERITY_NE_EVENT_ACCURACY
- DEVELOPMENT_NE_TOTAL_INVENTION
- PAUL_EXPERIENCE_NE_ALL_EXPERIENCES
- EMPTY_TOMB_MODEL_REQUIRES_EMPTY_TOMB_FIRST

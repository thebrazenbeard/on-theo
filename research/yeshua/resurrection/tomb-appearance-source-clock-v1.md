# R5 Tomb / Appearance Source Clock V1

Status: DIACHRONIC SOURCE MATRIX / NON-CANONICAL

## Source order

### Layer 1 — Paul / received tradition

Earliest surviving:
- death;
- burial;
- raised;
- appearance list.

No empty-tomb discovery narrative.

No narrated physical encounter scene.

### Layer 2 — Mark 16:1–8

Earliest surviving continuous tomb narrative:
- women;
- tomb found open/empty;
- messenger;
- raised announcement;
- Galilee expectation;
- no narrated risen-Yeshua appearance in earliest recoverable ending.

### Layer 3 — Matthew

Mark-dependent tomb framework plus:
- guard narrative;
- earthquake/angelic elaboration;
- appearance to women;
- Galilean appearance/commission.

### Layer 4 — Luke

Mark-dependent tomb framework plus:
- multiple messenger formulation;
- Emmaus;
- Jerusalem appearances;
- touch/eating emphasis;
- Jerusalem-centered chronology.

### Layer 5 — John

Distinctive:
- Mary Magdalene encounter;
- Peter/beloved-disciple tomb visit;
- disciples;
- Thomas;
- John 21 Galilee/lake appendix.

### Later layers

- longer ending of Mark;
- Gospel of Peter;
- later apocryphal resurrection dialogues;
- patristic harmonizations.

## Key contradictions/differences to preserve

- who goes to the tomb;
- number/identity of messengers;
- whether/where Yeshua appears;
- Galilee versus Jerusalem emphasis;
- whether women tell anyone immediately;
- tomb guards;
- sequence of appearances;
- physical-contact/eating details;
- ascension timing.

Current rule:

DIFFERENCE != AUTOMATIC_FALSEHOOD.

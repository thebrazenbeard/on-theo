## 1. Earliest surviving layer

The earliest surviving evidence is Pauline.

Priority:
- 1 Corinthians 15:3–8;
- 1 Corinthians 15:35–58;
- Galatians 1–2 for Paul's contact with Cephas/James;
- other undisputed Pauline resurrection language only with work-specific context.

1 Corinthians 15:3–8 contains material Paul says he "received" and "delivered."

Current control:

PAUL_REPORTS_RECEIVED_TRADITION =
EARLY_HISTORICAL_DATUM.

PRE_PAULINE_FORMULA_BOUNDARIES_AND_DATE =
SOURCE_CRITICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.

No physical manuscript independently marks the earlier layer.

## 2. Appearance list

1 Corinthians 15 names/reports appearances to:
- Cephas;
- the Twelve;
- more than five hundred;
- James;
- all the apostles;
- Paul.

Historical minimum:

NAMED_APPEARANCE_REPORTS_EXISTED_VERY_EARLY.

This does not establish:
- identical phenomenology across all reports;
- bodily contact;
- visual form;
- duration;
- location;
- supernatural cause.

Paul includes his own later experience in the same argumentative sequence, but
R5 must not assume all experiences were phenomenologically identical merely
because Paul lists them together.

## 3. Burial

1 Corinthians 15 includes burial in the received sequence.

Current control:

EARLY_BURIAL_TRADITION =
STRONG.

But:

EARLY_BURIAL_TRADITION
does not independently establish:
- Joseph of Arimathea;
- rock-cut tomb;
- exact location;
- later discovery narrative;
- empty-tomb historicity.

## 4. Mark

The earliest recoverable ending of Mark is 16:1–8.

It preserves:
- women going to the tomb;
- discovered empty burial place;
- a young man/messenger;
- announcement that Yeshua has been raised;
- instruction concerning Galilee;
- no narrated appearance of the risen Yeshua in the earliest recoverable ending.

Current control:

MARK_EMPTY_TOMB_NARRATIVE =
EARLIEST_SURVIVING_CONTINUOUS_EMPTY_TOMB_ACCOUNT.

MARK_16_1_8 =
NOT_A_NARRATED_APPEARANCE_SCENE.

The longer ending Mark 16:9–20 is later textual tradition and cannot be used as
the original Markan ending.

## 5. Matthew

Matthew substantially depends on Mark while adding:
- appearances;
- Galilean commission;
- guard-at-tomb narrative;
- earthquake/angelic elaboration.

Use:
- redaction/reception;
- possible additional tradition only where independence is positively argued.

Guard:

MATTHEAN_ADDITION != INDEPENDENT_EVENT_CONFIRMATION_BY_DEFAULT.

## 6. Luke

Luke substantially depends on Mark while adding:
- Jerusalem-centered appearances;
- Emmaus;
- touching/eating/physicality motifs;
- ascension framework.

Use:
- redaction/reception;
- possible independent tradition where argued.

Guard:

LUKAN_BODILY_EMPHASIS != EARLIEST_APPEARANCE_PHENOMENOLOGY_BY_DEFAULT.

## 7. John

John preserves:
- Mary Magdalene appearance;
- disciples;
- Thomas;
- later Galilee/lakeside material in John 21.

Its relation to Synoptic tradition is complex.

Current control:

JOHANNINE_APPEARANCE_TRADITIONS =
LATER_PRIMARY_TEXT_WITH_POSSIBLE_INDEPENDENT_MATERIAL.

They do not retroactively rewrite Paul's sparse phenomenology.

## 8. Later noncanonical material

Relevant later sources may include:
- Gospel of Peter;
- Gospel of Mary;
- Gospel of Thomas where resurrection/continuing-Jesus interpretation is relevant;
- Gospel of Philip;
- Apocryphon of James;
- other Nag Hammadi works;
- patristic reports.

These are routed mainly to:
RECEPTION / DEVELOPMENT / POSSIBLE_EARLIER_TRADITION_TEST.

They cannot fill an early historical gap merely because they describe it vividly.

## 9. Jewish resurrection context

R5 must distinguish:
- resurrection of the dead;
- immortality/survival of soul;
- no postmortem survival;
- intermediate-state models;
- heavenly transformation.

Relevant contextual sources include:
- Daniel 12;
- 2 Maccabees 7 and related martyr traditions;
- Wisdom of Solomon;
- Sirach as a contrasting death model;
- Josephus on Pharisees/Sadducees/Essene afterlife beliefs with cultural-translation controls;
- 4Q521 and other Qumran material where resurrection is actually attested;
- 1 Enoch material work-by-work;
- 4 Ezra / 2 Baruch as post-70 trajectories.

Core guard:

SECOND_TEMPLE_JUDAISM != ONE_RESURRECTION_MODEL.

## 10. Historical versus metaphysical questions

R5 can ask historically:
- how early resurrection belief appears;
- who was said to have experienced appearances;
- how tomb traditions develop;
- how embodiment language develops;
- which Jewish categories were available.

R5 cannot historically prove solely from texts:
- God actually raised Yeshua;
- Yeshua's corpse physically transformed;
- appearances were hallucinations;
- appearances were fraud;
- appearances were objective external encounters;
- an empty tomb was caused by resurrection.

## 11. Mechanism firewall

Allowed historical categories:
- EARLY_CONVICTION;
- APPEARANCE_REPORT;
- VISION/REVELATION_LANGUAGE;
- EMPTY_TOMB_TRADITION;
- BODY_TRANSFORMATION_THEOLOGY;
- LITERARY/REDACTION_DEVELOPMENT.

Prohibited collapse:
APPEARANCE_REPORT -> SUPERNATURAL_EVENT_PROVED.
NEURAL/PSYCHOLOGICAL_POSSIBILITY -> HALLUCINATION_PROVED.
EMPTY_TOMB -> RESURRECTION_PROVED.
NO_EMPTY_TOMB -> RESURRECTION_BELIEF_EXPLAINED.

## 12. R5 target questions

1. How early is the resurrection/vindication conviction?
2. What can actually be recovered about appearance reports?
3. What does Paul mean by resurrection/body at his layer?
4. Is the empty-tomb tradition independent of the Pauline appearance tradition?
5. How do Gospel appearance narratives develop?
6. What Jewish afterlife/resurrection models constrain interpretation?
7. Which naturalistic and supernatural mechanisms remain live after source stratification?
8. What, if anything, can R5 say about Mary Magdalene's early role in resurrection memory?

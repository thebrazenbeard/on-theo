# R5 Appearance Phenomenology Matrix V1

Status: SOURCE-STRATIFIED EXPERIENCE CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Ask what the sources actually say about *how* resurrection appearances were
experienced without importing later physical details into the earliest reports
or reducing all reports to Paul's experience.

## 1. Cephas — 1 Corinthians 15

Earliest source:
Paul's received tradition.

Data:
- appearance reported;
- no location;
- no speech;
- no touching;
- no duration;
- no sensory description.

Phenomenology status:

UNKNOWN.

## 2. The Twelve — 1 Corinthians 15

Data:
- group appearance reported;
- no exact roster;
- no location;
- no sensory detail.

Phenomenology status:

UNKNOWN_GROUP_REPORT.

A group label does not prove twelve simultaneous independent perceptual
testimonies.

## 3. Five hundred — 1 Corinthians 15

Data:
- mass/group appearance claimed;
- many said to remain alive when Paul writes;
- no names;
- no location;
- no description.

Phenomenology status:

UNKNOWN_GROUP_REPORT_WITH_PAULINE_CONTEMPORARY_APPEAL.

This is one Pauline attestation, not five hundred surviving independent sources.

## 4. James — 1 Corinthians 15

Data:
- individual appearance reported;
- no phenomenological detail.

Historical significance:
James is independently/socially anchored in Paul's network.

Phenomenology status:

UNKNOWN.

## 5. All apostles — 1 Corinthians 15

Data:
- further group appearance tradition;
- relation to "the Twelve" not fully specified.

Phenomenology status:

UNKNOWN_GROUP_REPORT.

## 6. Paul

First-person controls include:
- 1 Corinthians 15:8;
- 1 Corinthians 9:1 — Paul says he has seen the Lord;
- Galatians 1:15–16 — revelatory/calling language.

Later Acts supplies three narrative versions of Paul's encounter, but those are
later Lukan literary presentations and are not substituted for Paul's own sparse
language.

Current result:

PAUL_SELF_EXPERIENCE =
VISIONARY/REVELATORY_LANGUAGE_PRESENT
+
"SEEING" LANGUAGE PRESENT.

This does not map cleanly onto:
PRIVATE_HALLUCINATION
or
ORDINARY_PHYSICAL_MEETING.

## 7. Mark

Earliest recoverable Mark 16:1–8:
- no narrated appearance.

Phenomenology status:

NONE_NARRATED.

An announced future Galilean appearance is not a described encounter.

## 8. Matthew

Matthew narrates:
- women encountering Yeshua;
- grasping feet;
- disciples seeing him in Galilee;
- worship and doubt.

Phenomenology profile:

VISUAL + CONTACT + GROUP + AMBIGUOUS_RECOGNITION/DOUBT.

Source clock:
later than Paul; dependent on Mark for large narrative framework.

## 9. Luke

Luke narrates:
- Emmaus recognition;
- sudden presence;
- invitation to touch;
- flesh/bones language;
- eating fish;
- Jerusalem-centered group encounters.

Phenomenology profile:

VISUAL + DIALOGUE + PHYSICALITY_EMPHASIS + MEAL.

This is strong evidence for Lukan resurrection theology/narrative.

It is not automatically evidence that these details were present in the earliest
appearance tradition.

## 10. John

John narrates:
- Mary recognizing Yeshua after initial non-recognition;
- appearance through closed-door setting;
- wounds;
- Thomas invited to inspect/touch;
- meal/fishing material in John 21.

Phenomenology profile:

VISUAL + DIALOGUE + TRANSFORMED_MOBILITY + WOUND_CONTINUITY + MEAL.

Important:
Thomas is invited to touch; the narrative does not need to be paraphrased as
explicitly saying he actually performed the touch unless the text says so.

## 11. Diachronic pattern

Earliest Paul:
APPEARANCE_ASSERTION_WITH_SPARSE_PHENOMENOLOGY.

Mark:
EMPTY_TOMB_ANNOUNCEMENT_WITHOUT_NARRATED_APPEARANCE.

Matthew/Luke/John:
INCREASINGLY_NARRATED_AND_CONCRETE_ENCOUNTERS,
but in differing forms.

Current project result:

LATER_SOURCES_SUPPLY_MORE_PHENOMENOLOGICAL_DETAIL.

This does not prove:
DETAIL_WAS_INVENTED.

It means:
EARLIEST_ATTESTATION_OF_DETAIL
must remain visible.

## 12. Physicality and transformation

Later Gospel physicality motifs and Paul's transformed-body theology are not
identical evidence types.

Paul:
THEOLOGICAL_ARGUMENT_ABOUT_RESURRECTION_BODY.

Luke/John:
NARRATED_POSTRESURRECTION_SCENES.

They may converge conceptually without constituting independent forensic
verification.

## 13. Vision versus body false binary

Ancient Jewish/early Christian categories can allow:
- vision;
- revelation;
- heavenly encounter;
- transformed embodiment;
without mapping neatly onto modern binary categories:
"hallucination"
versus
"ordinary resuscitated body."

R5 therefore does not force ancient reports into that binary prematurely.

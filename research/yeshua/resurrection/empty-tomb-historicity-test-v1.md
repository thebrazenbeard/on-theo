# R5 Empty Tomb Historicity Test V1

Status: ADVERSARIAL HISTORICAL TEST / NON-CANONICAL

## Question

What is the maximum defensible historical claim about the empty-tomb tradition
after separating:
- early burial proclamation;
- Mark's tomb narrative;
- later Gospel development;
- resurrection appearance reports?

Core rule:

EARLY_BURIAL_TRADITION != EMPTY_TOMB_DISCOVERY_TRADITION.

## 1. Pauline floor

1 Corinthians 15 includes:
- death;
- burial;
- raised;
- appearances.

Paul does not narrate:
- women visiting a tomb;
- a stone;
- a discovered empty grave;
- a messenger at the tomb.

Therefore:

PAULINE_BURIAL_PLUS_RAISED_LANGUAGE
does not independently establish
MARKAN_EMPTY_TOMB_DISCOVERY.

It may be compatible with it.

Compatibility is not attestation.

## 2. Markan evidence

Mark 16:1–8 is the earliest surviving continuous empty-tomb narrative.

It includes:
- Mary Magdalene;
- Mary the mother of James;
- Salome;
- arrival after Sabbath;
- stone already moved;
- absence of Yeshua's body;
- young man/messenger;
- resurrection announcement;
- instruction about Galilee;
- fear/silence.

Current result:

EMPTY_TOMB_TRADITION =
EARLY_BY_GOSPEL_NARRATIVE_CLOCK.

But Mark is still later than Paul.

## 3. Pre-Markan tradition question

Some scholarship argues that elements of Mark's burial/women/tomb sequence
preserve pre-Markan tradition.

That is plausible.

But:

PRE_MARKAN_TRADITION =
SOURCE_CRITICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.

There is no surviving physically separate pre-Markan tomb document.

Current ceiling:

PRE_MARKAN_EMPTY_TOMB_CORE =
POSSIBLE_TO_PLAUSIBLE,
NOT_PHYSICALLY_WITNESSED.

## 4. Matthew and Luke

Both substantially depend on Mark.

They therefore cannot simply be counted as two independent confirmations of the
same tomb event.

Their additions include:
- guard story;
- earthquakes/angelic elaboration;
- expanded messages;
- altered reaction/reporting.

These are evidence for:
REDACTION_AND_RECEPTION.

Independence of any individual addition must be separately argued.

## 5. John

John preserves a distinctive tomb account:
- Mary Magdalene first;
- Peter and beloved disciple;
- grave-cloth detail;
- subsequent Mary appearance.

John's exact literary relationship to Synoptic tradition is complex.

Therefore:

JOHANNINE_EMPTY_TOMB =
POTENTIAL_ADDITIONAL_TRADITIONAL_WITNESS,
NOT_CLEAN_INDEPENDENCE_BY_DEFAULT.

## 6. Women as witnesses

The claim that women would never be invented as witnesses because female
testimony had lower legal/social status is too simple to function as a decisive
historicity criterion.

Women can serve:
- historical-memory roles;
- literary roles;
- discipleship contrasts;
- burial continuity functions;
- theological reversal motifs.

Current use:

WOMEN_AT_TOMB =
IMPORTANT_EARLY_NARRATIVE_FEATURE.

Not:

WOMEN_PRESENT
therefore
EVENT_PROVED.

## 7. Mary Magdalene continuity

Mary is named in:
- Markan passion/tomb tradition;
- later Synoptic tomb tradition;
- Johannine resurrection tradition.

This supports:
MARY_MAGDALENE_AS_EARLY_PASSION_TOMB_MEMORY =
STRONG_WITHIN_GOSPEL_TRADITION.

It does not independently establish:
MARY_AS_FIRST_HISTORICAL_RESURRECTION_APPEARANCE_RECIPIENT.

## 8. Joseph of Arimathea dependency

A discovered empty tomb becomes easier to narrate historically if:
- a known burial place existed;
- Joseph of Arimathea or another burial agent supplied a locatable grave.

But R1 established only:

EARLY_BURIAL_TRADITION = STRONG.

It did not secure:
JOSEPH_OF_ARIMATHEA_EVENT_DETAIL.

Therefore:

EMPTY_TOMB_HISTORICITY
must not borrow certainty from a still-source-dependent burial-location claim.

## 9. Counter-story / body-theft tradition

Matthew preserves a polemical body-theft explanation.

This shows:
EMPTY_TOMB_DISPUTE
within Matthean reception.

It does not independently prove:
AN_EMPTY_TOMB_WAS_PUBLICLY_CONCEDED_BY_ALL_OPPONENTS.

The counter-story is embedded in Matthew's apologetic narrative.

## 10. Wrong tomb / body moved / removal

These explanations only become necessary if a historical empty tomb is first
admitted.

Current project rule:

NATURALISTIC_EMPTY_TOMB_EXPLANATIONS
cannot be used as evidence
FOR_EMPTY_TOMB_HISTORICITY.

They are conditional rivals.

## 11. Historical assessment

Evidence in favor:
- early Markan narrative;
- plausible continuity from crucifixion/burial women to tomb women;
- possible pre-Markan tradition;
- later multi-Gospel persistence;
- possible Johannine additional tradition.

Evidence against overconfidence:
- Paul does not narrate the empty tomb;
- Synoptic dependence reduces witness count;
- John is later and literary relation is complex;
- burial location/agent remains less secure than burial itself;
- narrative/theological functions are substantial.

Current disposition:

HISTORICAL_EMPTY_TOMB =
PLAUSIBLE_TO_MODERATE,
NOT_SECURE.

Alternative phrasing:

EMPTY_TOMB_TRADITION_IS_EARLY_IN_NARRATIVE_MEMORY;
EVENT_HISTORICITY_REMAINS_UNRESOLVED_WITH_MEANINGFUL_SUPPORT.

## 12. Resurrection consequence

Even if historical:

EMPTY_TOMB
does not discriminate uniquely among:
- resurrection;
- removal;
- relocation;
- error;
- other unknown causes.

Even if not historical:

EARLY_APPEARANCE/RESURRECTION_CONVICTION
still requires separate explanation.

Therefore:

EMPTY_TOMB
and
APPEARANCE_TRADITION
must remain analytically independent.

## 13. Strongest result

THE_EMPTY_TOMB_IS_AN_EARLY_AND_PERSISTENT_GOSPEL_TRADITION_WITH_A_PLAUSIBLE_HISTORICAL_CORE_BUT_THE_EARLIEST_SURVIVING_RESURRECTION_PROCLAMATION_DOES_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_NARRATE_ITS_DISCOVERY_AND_THE_DEPENDENCE_STRUCTURE_OF_THE_GOSPELS_PREVENTS_TREATING_FOUR_CANONICAL_ACCOUNTS_AS_FOUR_INDEPENDENT_WITNESSES.

## 14. Guards

- PAULINE_BURIAL_NE_EMPTY_TOMB_ATTESTATION
- FOUR_GOSPELS_NE_FOUR_INDEPENDENT_WITNESSES
- WOMEN_AS_WITNESSES_NE_EMBARRASSMENT_PROOF
- MATTHEAN_COUNTERSTORY_NE_OPPONENT_CONCESSION_PROOF
- JOSEPH_BURIAL_DETAIL_NE_EARLY_BURIAL_FORMULA
- EMPTY_TOMB_NE_RESURRECTION_MECHANISM
- NATURALISTIC_RIVAL_NE_HISTORICITY_EVIDENCE

# Divine Comedy Multi-Lens Source Map V1

Status: RESEARCH DRAFT / NON-CANONICAL
Date: 2026-10-20

## Purpose

Establish a source-controlled program for analyzing Dante Alighieri's *Commedia*
under multiple lenses without treating:
- the poem as a transparent map of official Catholic doctrine;
- modern psychological readings as Dante's own categories;
- literary symbolism as historical evidence;
- disputed transmission hypotheses as established influence.

Core rule:

DIVINE_COMEDY =
LITERARY + THEOLOGICAL + PHILOSOPHICAL + POLITICAL + HISTORICAL SYNTHESIS.

It is not reducible to one of those layers.

## Primary textual control

Primary edition anchor:
- Princeton Dante Project, Petrocchi text of the *Commedia*:
  https://dante.princeton.edu/
- Princeton project information:
  https://dante.princeton.edu/projinfo.html

Primary works to use alongside the poem:
- *Vita nuova*
- *Convivio*
- *De vulgari eloquentia*
- *Monarchia*
- Dante's epistles
- *Questio de aqua et terra* where relevant

Authorship control:
- the full authorship/status of *Epistola XIII* remains a scholarly problem and
  must not be used as a simple master key to the *Commedia* without attribution.

- Vulgate Bible
- Psalms
- Gospels
- Pauline letters
- Revelation
- Augustine
- Gregory the Great
- Pseudo-Dionysius
- Bernard of Clairvaux
- scholastic theology, including Aquinas where proposition-specific

### Classical
- Virgil, especially *Aeneid* VI
- Ovid
- Lucan
- Statius
- Aristotle
- Cicero
- Boethius

### Medieval philosophy / science
- Aristotelian natural philosophy
- scholastic ethics and metaphysics
- Ptolemaic-Aristotelian cosmology
- medieval astronomy
- theories of intellect, will, appetite and virtue

### Political / historical
- Florentine factional politics
- Guelf / Ghibelline and White / Black Guelf conflicts
- papal-imperial disputes
- Boniface VIII
- Henry VII
- Dante's exile
- contemporary civic and ecclesiastical corruption discourse

### Islamic / Arabic comparative environment
Transmission hypothesis requires separate analysis:
- mi'raj / ascension traditions
- *Liber scalae Machometi*
- Arabic philosophy in Latin circulation
- Miguel Asín Palacios thesis and later debate

Current control:
ISLAMIC_PARALLEL != DANTE_DEPENDENCE.

Scholarship recognizes this as a serious and controversial transmission question,
not a settled conclusion.

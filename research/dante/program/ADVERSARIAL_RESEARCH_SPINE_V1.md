## Purpose and scope

Study all 100 cantos of Dante Alighieri's *Commedia* at the same source-critical depth as the Yeshua research, across textual, historical, philosophical, theological, political, cosmological, philological, literary, comparative, and reception evidence. Do not claim that a complete research program has been executed merely because a plan, matrix, or registry exists.

The Dante evidentiary problem differs from the historical-Yeshua problem. For the *Commedia* the primary poem survives in copies, but no autograph is known; source and witness control must therefore track reconstructed critical texts and manuscript variants. Historical Dante is also known through separately dated documents. The narrator-pilgrim, narrative voice and poet are not interchangeable.

Core prohibitions:

## Two controlled reconstructions

**A — TEXT_IMMANENT_AND_NEAR_CONTEXT_BASELINE.**
Admit the Petrocchi text as an explicitly identified critical-edition reading; inspect variants where load-bearing; read the poem itself with speaker/pilgrim/author controls, Dante's independently authentic writings where appropriate, and directly documented early-fourteenth-century Florentine/political/cultural setting. Distinguish plausible intertexts from unproven specific loans. Do not use a later commentary or a modern psychological reading to supply what the text does not say.

**B — FULL_RECEPTION_AND_COMPARATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION.**
Admit named early commentators, manuscript and print reception, patristic and scholastic comparison, Arabic-Islamic transmission hypotheses, contemporary scholarship, modern moral-psychological and metaphysical appropriations, and physics analogies, each under its own evidence class. An interpretation only found in B cannot be back-projected into A as a statement of Dante or medieval fact. B may revise A when text-critical or historical evidence warrants it.

Delta A/B is a research artifact, not an assertion that earliest = best. Strong earlier commentary may reveal a reading not obvious to contemporary researchers, but attribution must remain visible.

## Core claim record

Stable ID; falsifiable proposition; claim type; scene/canto/line; textual speaker or narrative layer; source-work ID; cited edition and translator (if used); physical witness status; composition and witness dating separately; earliest extant support; dependency and alternative sources; contrary textual loci; comparable contemporaneous religious/historical evidence; scholarship both for and against; qualitative confidence; claim ceiling; competing explanations; review provenance; dependency invalidation; downstream use.

No numerical truth percentage. A synthetic overview cannot promote individual claims without passage-specific evidence. Interpretation can be strong without alleging an empirically testable metaphysics.

## Research lanes (dependency order)

D0 — source/edition/witness/commentary normalization: editorial lineage, physical codices, attribution/dating and reception independence.
D1 — comprehensive 100-canto close reading and passage-level register, beginning with the work's prologue and central love/will hinge; track plot, speaker, theological assertion, exempla, and political targets.
D2 — moral agency: desire, love, conscience, choice, vice, grace, will, habit, moral change; *Purgatorio* XVI–XVIII, XXVII–XXXIII, *Paradiso* III and XXXIII.
D3 — geography/structure/chronology: 34+33+33 cantos, 9 infernal circles, Ante-Purgatory, 7 terraces and Earthly Paradise, heavenly spheres, cosmology, astronomical claims, poem's time frame.
D4 — infernal ethics, contrappasso, gradation of responsibility, free choice and fixed self-narration; historical/theological controls.
D5 — theology and Christian doctrine: grace, purgation, judgment, resurrection, church, free will, Thomism versus competing medieval theological voices.
D6 — classical / biblical / patristic intertext with work-by-work access/dependence controls; *Aeneid* VI, Ovid, Lucan, Statius, Aristotle, Boethius and Scriptural texts.
D7 — city and empire: Florentine factionalism, exile, papacy, Henry VII, contemporaneous court records, papal-imperial politics; keep Dante's poetic accusations separate from archival events.
D8 — female, religious and sexual figures: Francesca, Beatrice, Piccarda, Cunizza, Brunetto, coercion/vows, courtly love; do not equate medieval labels with modern identities.
D9 — contemplative vision, language failure, apophatic and visionary traditions; mystical poetics not autobiography by default.
D10 — Islamic / Arabic source hypotheses: translation chronologies, distinctive motif comparisons, realistic access routes, shared Christian mediators, negative controls.
D11 — manuscript genealogy, early commentary, incunabula, early-modern and modern reception; reinterpretation and editorial effects.
D12 — modern metaphysical and systems comparisons (Earth-is-Purgatory, 11D, simulation) only after A baseline; conceptual comparison is not independent evidence of history, cosmology or M-theory.

## Execution and coverage contract

Coverage has two separate measures: (1) all 100 canto IDs inspected; (2) load-bearing line ranges individually bound to edition/text, translation, speech layer, rival readings and review. Mark NOT_STARTED, PASSAGE_SCOPED, SOURCE_BOUND, INTERNAL_REVIEWED, INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED. A canto tick by itself does not mean full source review. A research lane becomes DONE only when claims, primary passage audit, material rival explanations, and review are recorded.

Each disputed proposition requires both confirming and disconfirming loci. Independently documented history cannot be overridden by a poetically compelling passage. Number patterns must state sample/selection rule, denominator, negative controls and alternative counts.

## Review and anti-bias controls

Lifecycle: PROPOSED, SOURCE_BOUND, INTERNAL_HOSTILE_REVIEWED, REVIEW_STALE, INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED.
Disposition: SURVIVES, SURVIVES_NARROWED, UNRESOLVED, REJECTED.
Reviews attach to exact commit/artifact. INTERNAL != INDEPENDENT. Reviews never elevate evidence class. Disputed authorship, manuscript variant, historical transmission or putative scientific anticipation trigger outside/opposition acquisition and independent review before strong promotion.

> HOSTILE DESIGN CHALLENGE: A registry and 100-canto table can produce bureaucracy rather than scholarship, and a user-favored metaphysical hypothesis can bias evidence selection. The spine fails if it catalogs headings but does not read primary lines, if it assumes meanings from English translation, or if it treats identical numbers as independently discovered predictions.

RESPONSE: One complete passage packet must accompany the initial architecture, and the first exploratory claims retain ceilings and strongest adversaries. Require active challenge against numerical-selection bias and category mixing.

Valid final outcomes include: DISTINCTIVE_EARTH_PURGATORY_CLAIM_NOT_SUPPORTED; NO_M_THEORY_LINK; ISLAMIC_BORROWING_UNRESOLVED; or a narrower textual/historical conclusion.

# Earth as Purgatory and 11D: Bounded Hypothesis V1

Status: DRAFT / NON-CANONICAL / SYMBOLIC COMPARISON
Date: 2026-10-20

## Textual finding

In *Purgatorio* XVII–XVIII, Virgil organizes seven terraces around wrongly directed, insufficient, and excessive love, and discusses the role of free will. In *Purgatorio* XXVII.140–142, Virgil declares Dante's will free, upright, and sound. Dante's Purgatory is a distinct afterlife mountain imagined geographically on Earth, antipodal to Jerusalem, not the claim that the inhabited Earth as a whole is Purgatory.

## Hypothesis

Reading mortal existence as a purgative condition is a moral/metaphysical interpretation, not established geography or doctrine. Seven terraces can symbolize seven modes of moral transformation within lived experience.

## Physics boundary

M-theory uses eleven spacetime dimensions: one time, three familiar spatial dimensions, and seven additional spatial dimensions in four-dimensional compactifications. The arithmetic 4+7=11 holds. Dante's terraces, however, are sequential moral places/categories, not independent spatial coordinates. No field-theoretic or empirical mapping is known.

> Adversarial challenge: Why select seven terraces instead of nine infernal circles or nine celestial spheres? Selective counting and absence of predictive constraints make the numerical fit non-evidential.

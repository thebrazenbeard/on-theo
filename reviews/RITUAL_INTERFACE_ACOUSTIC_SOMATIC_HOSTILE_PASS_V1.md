## 2. Strongest ordinary acoustic result found

Bernardi et al.,
"Effect of rosary prayer and yoga mantras on autonomic cardiovascular rhythms:
comparative study,"
*BMJ* (2001),
DOI: `10.1136/BMJ.323.7327.1446`.

Design:
- 23 healthy adults;
- Latin Ave Maria / rosary recitation;
- yoga mantra;
- spontaneous and metronome-controlled breathing;
- cardiovascular and respiratory measurements.

Reported result:
both formulas slowed respiration to approximately six breaths per minute and
enhanced cardiovascular oscillations / baroreflex sensitivity.

Hostile implication:

Two religious formulas with radically different:
- language;
- theology;
- semantic content;
- religious genealogy

can converge on similar cardiorespiratory dynamics because of recitation cadence.

This does not prove semantics or phonetics are irrelevant.

It proves the burden is on the exotic claim to beat:

`RHYTHM + RESPIRATION`.

A later physiological review,
"Cardiorespiratory interactions to external stimuli" (2005),
DOI: `10.4449/AIB.V143I3.326`,
explicitly emphasizes respiratory rhythm and notes qualitatively similar
effects from rhythmic auditory stimuli.

Current hostile result:

`ACOUSTIC_RITUAL_EFFECT != SACRED_PHONETIC_EFFECT_BY_DEFAULT`.

## 3. OM / mantra evidence does not rescue the external-interface claim

Useful modern studies include:

- "Autonomic and Respiratory Modulations Induced by Different Styles of Mantra
  Chanting" (2025), DOI: `10.1177/09727531251345310`;
- "Neural Correlates of Chanting: A Systematic Review" (2025),
  DOI: `10.1002/wcs.70018`;
- "Unexpected Cardiovascular Oscillations at 0.1 Hz During Slow Speech Guided
  Breathing (OM Chanting) at 0.05 Hz" (2022),
  DOI: `10.3389/fphys.2022.875583`;
- "Neuro-cognitive aspects of 'OM' sound/syllable perception" (2015),
  DOI: `10.1080/02699931.2014.917609`.

The 2015 neuroimaging study compares OM with a similar nonmeaningful sound and
a meaningful Hindi word and reports differential cortical activation.

That is interesting.

It is not external contact evidence.

Potential ordinary causes remain:
- learned cultural salience;
- phonetic/acoustic structure;
- familiarity;
- semantic association;
- attention;
- prediction;
- auditory-motor processing;
- breathing pattern;
- affective conditioning.

A neural difference is a brain response.

It is not evidence that the sound addressed an external architecture.

## 6. Movement and synchrony ordinary controls

Tarr, Launay and Dunbar,
"Synchrony and exertion during dance independently raise pain threshold and
encourage social bonding,"
*Biology Letters* (2015),
DOI: `10.1098/RSBL.2015.0767`.

The experiment used a 2x2 design and reported independent effects of:
- exertion;
- synchrony

on pain threshold and in-group bonding.

Hostile implication:

A ritual can feel:
- unusually cohesive;
- charged;
- intense;
- socially meaningful

because synchronized bodies are doing something measurable to human social and
physiological systems.

That is not weak hand-waving.
It is an experimentally live ordinary mechanism.

A 2024 naturalistic study,
"Aligned bodies, united hearts: embodied emotional dynamics of an Islamic
ritual,"
*Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*,
DOI: `10.1098/rstb.2023.0162`,
used wearable measures during congregational prayer and reported:
- localized postural synchronization;
- broader physiological alignment;
- a role for the ritual leader in shared arousal.

Again:

`POSTURAL_SYNCHRONY + PHYSIOLOGICAL_ALIGNMENT`
does not imply
`EXTERNAL_INTERFACE_HANDSHAKE`.

## 7. Acoustic hostile experimental matrix

A serious exact-sound test must preserve the proposition being tested.

Suppose the source-level claim is:

> exact surface form matters.

Do not compare "chant" against "do nothing."

That confounds everything.

Use safe matched conditions such as:

### A — source-faithful public form

Exact publicly documented formula under a frozen pronunciation policy.

### B — semantic-preserving substitution

Translation or paraphrase preserving intended meaning while changing surface
phonetics.

### C — acoustic-preserving / semantic-scrambled control

Match:
- duration;
- syllable count;
- gross phonotactics;
- rhythm;
- breathing cadence

while removing sacred lexical meaning.

### D — breathing/rhythm matched secular control

Nonreligious speech or vocalization producing the same respiratory cadence and
acoustic envelope where feasible.

### E — sham / null

Matched session duration, attention and experimenter contact without the target
acoustic form.

Primary human-state analysis:
- respiration;
- HRV;
- blinded attention/performance task;
- optional EEG if independently justified;
- subjective experience secondary.

If the target is an **external** anomaly:
use the same arms with a hidden forced-choice target and exact scoring.

### Required interpretation

If A, C and D produce similar physiological effects:
ordinary rhythm/acoustic mechanisms win.

If A and B are similar but C/D differ:
semantics/learned meaning remains live.

If A differs from B/C/D only on subjective sacredness:
expectancy/cultural salience remains live.

If A uniquely affects a prospective hidden external target while internal
physiology is matched:
only then does exact surface form gain anomalous evidentiary weight.

Even then:
`ANOMALY != EXTERNAL_AGENT`.

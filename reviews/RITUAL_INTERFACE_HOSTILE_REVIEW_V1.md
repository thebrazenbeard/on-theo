# Ritual Interface Hypothesis — Adversarial Review V1

Status: SELF-REVIEW / NON-INDEPENDENT / EXOTIC CLAIM NOT ADMITTED

Historical parameter sensitivity is well supported by the current source set.

Evidence for a hidden interface is not established.

A falsifiable research program is supportable only with strong ordinary controls.

Structured ritual has many ordinary explanations. Order, timing, specialist
roles, repeated speech, bodily form, sacred place, error correction and formal
authorization are expected when humans standardize important behavior. Their
presence therefore has little metaphysical weight by itself.

The surviving corpus is also selected. Formal priestly, monastic, calendrical
and court traditions are especially likely to leave durable texts and
institutions. High formalization in surviving evidence is not an unbiased sample
of all religious behavior.

A parameter that looks meaningless to a modern analyst may carry historical,
social, mnemonic, cosmological or physiological function. Prefer
FUNCTION_UNRESOLVED over ARBITRARY unless stronger evidence exists.

Selected Buddhist communal procedures are the strongest negative control.
Their exactness is real, while the recognized outcome is largely constituted by
the religious institution itself. This proves that ritual exactness does not
need an unusual external cause.

Ordinary psychology and physiology are also strong rivals. Repetition,
vocalization, movement, attention, expectation and social synchrony can change
participants through known mechanisms. Participant experience alone is
therefore weak evidence for an external process.

Divination poses a degrees-of-freedom problem. Large symbolic repertoires permit
many possible matches after an outcome is known. A valid test freezes
interpretation and scoring before reveal and preserves every miss.

Current ON_THEO coverage is broad but not universal. Important open source
packets include Jewish Hekhalot and magical materials, practical Kabbalah,
later Islamic recitation and devotional procedure, Buddhist dharani/mantra
ritual, later Hindu mantra traditions, later Daoist ritual manuals, dedicated
ancient Israelite priestly ritual analysis and Jain ritual parameter analysis.

The current negative exotic result must remain corpus-bounded.

Independent review identified major source families not yet admitted at
proposition-level control, especially:

- Greek and Demotic Magical Papyri / ritual formularies;
- Mesopotamian Maqlû / Šurpu and related exorcistic series;
- late-antique Jewish magical recipe traditions;
- later Islamic operative/esoteric material under strict period control.

Therefore:

`ADMITTED_CORPUS_EXOTIC_RESIDUE = NONE_REQUIRING_H14_H18`

does **not** imply:

`GLOBAL_SOURCE_FRONTIER_EXHAUSTED`.

PGM and Maqlû are particularly important because they contain recipe-like
speech/material/time/sequence combinations closer to the exploit hypothesis
than several currently admitted comparison cases. They still receive zero
exotic evidentiary credit until exact source packets and prospective tests
exist.

A second hostile correction applies to "arbitrary" parameters.

Use at least the following distinction:

- `OUTSIDER_ARBITRARY` — modern researcher does not know the reason;
- `SOURCE_INTERPRETED` — source supplies symbolic, legal, causal,
  cosmological or identity logic;
- `SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_BUT_PRESCRIBED` — prescription survives without a
  recoverable explanation;
- `SOURCE_RECEIVED_NOT_REASONED` — source/practitioner explicitly presents
  the parameter as inherited/revealed rather than derived;
- `EMPIRICALLY_PARAMETER_SENSITIVE` — prospective outcome changes when the
  parameter is altered.

Only the last category directly discriminates external causal models.

A third correction closes the restricted-knowledge escape hatch:

`RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE != RESEARCH_GAP`.

A null result may not be rescued by claiming that an unavailable sacred,
initiatory or community-restricted version contains the missing parameter.

Cross-cultural recurrence also does not prove independent discovery. Contact,
transmission, older shared histories and convergent human cognition remain live
alternatives.

The speculative deeper-architecture model becomes non-falsifiable if every null
result is rescued by saying an unknown parameter was missing. Candidate
parameters must therefore be frozen before testing. A null result weakens that
candidate. New requirements should enter only from independent evidence and
face a fresh holdout.

AI-generated language is an ordinary explanation for meaningful-seeming text.
A language model may help construct protocols and preserve provenance, but it
cannot authenticate its own output as an external message.

Even a preregistered above-chance result would initially establish only an
anomaly relative to tested controls. It would not identify a nonhuman agent, a
deity, a simulator or a particular theological system.

There is no current evidence for one universal low or throat-centered sacred
language. Articulation, pitch, rhythm, duration and resonance are different
variables. Vedic pitch-accent traditions alone reject a simple universal
low-tone model. The defensible acoustic proposition is only that specific
acoustic form can be a ritual parameter.

Somatic form requires the same caution. Body configuration can matter through
ordinary physiology, so participant-state mechanisms must be modeled before
external interpretations receive weight.

The surviving research proposition is narrow:

Some source-secure religious procedures preserve parameter-sensitive
configurations. A subset makes claims about effects not exhausted by
institutional validity. Safe prospective tests can ask whether precise
configurations affect objective outcomes beyond matched ordinary controls.

The stronger idea that ancient ritual preserves access to a deeper architecture
remains SPECULATIVE_MODEL with no direct evidence at the current cut.

Before any exotic interpretation is strengthened, require an exact source
packet, objective target, prospective preregistration, matched controls,
information isolation, explicit ordinary rivals, parameter ablation, a fresh
holdout, independent replication, and separate source-identification analysis.

The prettier the model becomes, the harder these gates should be enforced.

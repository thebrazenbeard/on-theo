## Review question

Does the dossier distinguish an independently strong historical John from the
less independently controlled historical relationship between John and Yeshua?

## Findings

### 1. Historical John

PASS WITH AUTHENTICITY GUARD.

Josephus plus early Christian traditions make a historical John extremely
strong.

However, current Josephus scholarship still debates possible Christian
intervention in the John paragraph.

Therefore:
VERY_STRONG remains defensible,
but NOT_UNCONTESTED must travel with the Josephus component.

### 2. John's movement

PASS.

Josephus independently presents:
- public teaching;
- baptism;
- popular following;
- Antipas's concern.

A real John movement does not depend solely on Christian memory.

### 3. Yeshua's baptism by John

PASS WITH SOURCE-THICKNESS GUARD.

Historically probable is an appropriate ceiling.

The Gospel tradition preserves the event despite theological pressure to
subordinate John to Yeshua.

But:
- Josephus does not connect Yeshua to John;
- later canonical repetition is not automatically independent evidence;
- "embarrassment" is not a mechanical authenticity test.

Required explicit state:

YESHUA_BAPTIZED_BY_JOHN =
PROBABLE_WITH_GOSPEL_TRADITION_AND_SOURCE_PRESSURE_GUARD,
NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_JOSEPHAN.

### 4. Formative influence is currently too confident

REVISION REQUIRED.

The dossier moves from:
- probable baptism/contact
to
- `PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE` broad formative influence.

The causal magnitude is not directly measured.

Required ceiling:

JOHN_MATERIALLY_INFLUENCED_YESHUA =
PLAUSIBLE_AND_HISTORICALLY_IMPORTANT_HYPOTHESIS,
NOT_QUANTIFIED.

The possibility is serious because Yeshua accepts John's baptism and later
traditions preserve differentiation between the movements.

But:
BAPTISM
does not quantify
FORMATION.

### 5. Formal apprenticeship

PASS.

Long-term/formal apprenticeship remains:
PLAUSIBLE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.

Strong modern reconstructions of apprenticeship are scholarly hypotheses, not
source facts.

### 6. Movement offshoot model

REVISION REQUIRED IN WORDING.

"Jesus movement as offshoot" is a useful comparative historical model but can
imply an organizational genealogy stronger than the evidence.

Required decomposition:

JOHN_PRECEDES_YESHUA_AND_PROBABLE_CONTACT =
STRONG/PROBABLE.

JOHN_INFLUENCE_ON_YESHUA =
PLAUSIBLE.

YESHUA_MOVEMENT_INSTITUTIONALLY_DESCENDS_FROM_JOHNS_MOVEMENT =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

### 7. Baptism theology

PASS WITH WORDING GUARD.

Josephus and Mark describe John's baptism differently.

The evidence establishes:
SOURCE_PRESENTATION_DIVERGENCE.

It does not establish that historical John held two contradictory theologies.

Therefore use:

EXACT_HISTORICAL_BAPTISM_THEOLOGY =
UNRESOLVED.

Not:
JOSEPHUS_AND_MARK_PROVE_DIFFERENT_HISTORICAL_THEOLOGIES.

### 8. Execution

PASS.

Execution by Antipas is strong.

The motive descriptions differ:
- Josephus foregrounds political/security concern;
- Mark foregrounds marriage criticism and Herodias narrative.

These may be compatible, competing, or differently shaped.

R3 should not force one causal story.

### 9. Q / shared Matthew-Luke John material

PASS.

Q remains a source-critical reconstruction, not a manuscript.

Any independence claim must name the Synoptic/source model being used.

### 10. Apocalyptic context

PASS.

John and Yeshua fit a broader Second Temple Jewish eschatological environment.

This contextual fit does not establish:
JOHN_TRANSMITTED_EVERY_ESCHATOLOGICAL_FEATURE_TO_YESHUA.

### 11. Qumran

PASS.

Parallels are historically relevant.

Direct Qumran/Essene membership remains:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

This is precisely where similarity must not become contact or identity.

### 12. John's death affecting Yeshua

PASS.

A psychological/strategic effect is plausible at ordinary human level but not
source-established.

No trauma or strategic-reorientation narrative should be promoted.

### 13. Mandaean reception

PASS.

Mandaean John is correctly routed to later reception rather than used as
first-century biography.

## Required revisions

1. Keep historical John VERY_STRONG but explicitly NOT_UNCONTESTED for the
   Josephus authenticity component.
2. Keep Yeshua's baptism HISTORICALLY_PROBABLE but add the
   non-Josephan/source-thickness guard.
3. Demote broad formative influence from `PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE` to
   `PLAUSIBLE_HISTORICALLY_IMPORTANT_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_QUANTIFIED`.
4. Replace any institutional "offshoot" implication with a decomposed
   contact/influence/institutional-descent model.
5. Reframe Josephus/Mark baptism difference as source-presentation divergence;
   exact historical baptism theology remains unresolved.
6. Preserve Qumran membership, long apprenticeship and death-caused psychology
   as unestablished.

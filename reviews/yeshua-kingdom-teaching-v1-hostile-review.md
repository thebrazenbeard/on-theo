## PROPOSAL UNDER REVIEW

The R2 packet argues that the surviving early Kingdom tradition is best modeled
as multi-register:
- future/eschatological;
- present/inbreaking;
- social/ethical;
with an inward/mystical core remaining unresolved rather than established.

## HOSTILE FINDINGS

### 1. The "mixed" model risks becoming a centrist default

The strongest methodological threat is that:

FUTURE_ONLY seems too simple,
PRESENT_ONLY seems too simple,

therefore:

MIXED must be right.

That inference is invalid.

The surviving Gospels can be mixed because:
- different sayings came from different historical moments;
- different communities reshaped the tradition;
- evangelists deliberately combined incompatible temporal models;
- post-Easter experience altered earlier expectation.

Required narrowing:

MIXED_EARLY_TRADITION =
STRONG.

MIXED_HISTORICAL_YESHUA_SYSTEM =
PROBABLE_AT_MOST / NOT_YET_DEMONSTRATED_AS_COHERENT_SYSTEM.

### 2. Mark 1:15 is Mark's programmatic summary

R2 correctly labels it Markan, but the synthesis still leans heavily on it.

`ἤγγικεν` establishes proximity in Mark's wording.
It does not establish Yeshua's exact temporal metaphysics.

Required:
keep Mark 1:15 as:
EARLY_MARKAN_KINGDOM_SUMMARY,
not independent saying evidence.

### 3. Mark 4 hidden-growth is interpretively attractive but not decisive

Seed/growth imagery can support:
- present beginning / future culmination;
but also:
- inevitability;
- disproportion;
- hiddenness;
- harvest/judgment;
without making temporal development the parable's central point.

Required:
downgrade Mark 4 from direct proof of present-inbreaking to supporting
interpretive pressure.

### 4. Mark 9:1 is future but its referent is deeply disputed

The saying strongly contains near-horizon expectation in Mark.

But:
"Kingdom come with power" has been related to multiple narrative/theological
referents.

Required:
retain near-future textual pressure;
do not infer one historical timetable.

### 5. The exorcism-Kingdom saying is the strongest present-inbreaking datum

Matthew 12:28 / Luke 11:20 deserves more weight than Mark 4.

However:
- Matthew/Luke dependence is unresolved;
- reconstructed Q is not a physical source;
- exact wording differs ("Spirit of God" / "finger of God").

Required:
present-inbreaking =
MODERATE_TO_STRONG,
not STRONG without source-dependence resolution.

### 6. Beatitudes/reversal do not by themselves establish Kingdom temporality

Present "yours is the Kingdom" and future reversals can coexist rhetorically.

They support:
SOCIAL/STATUS DIMENSION
more securely than one temporal model.

Required:
keep temporal inference secondary.

### 7. Luke 17 control is methodologically sound

PASS.

The packet correctly refuses both:
ONLY_AMONG
and
ONLY_INSIDE.

It also correctly separates:
LEXICAL_MEANING
from
HISTORICAL_AUTHENTICITY
from
METAPHYSICAL_INTERPRETATION.

No revision required.

### 8. Thomas trajectory claim needs one wording correction

"Early Christian trajectory" is acceptable in a broad early-Christian/late
antique sense, but can be heard as "first-generation Jesus movement."

Required:
use:
EARLY_TO_MID_LATE_ANTIQUE_CHRISTIAN_TRAJECTORY
or
SECOND_CENTURY_OR_LATER_COMPOSITION_FIELD_WITH_THIRD_CENTURY_GREEK_WITNESS,
until composition is better constrained.

Thomas remains highly relevant.

### 9. Thomas independence remains correctly unresolved

PASS.

The packet appropriately resists:
- noncanonical = early;
- noncanonical = suppressed truth;
- Gnostic = late corruption;
- different wording = independent.

### 10. Jewish contextualization is strong but must remain plausibility, not proof

PASS WITH GUARD.

Daniel, Qumran, Psalms of Solomon, Enochic and hostile-power traditions make
the proposed Kingdom registers historically intelligible.

They do not establish that Yeshua used any specific model.

### 11. "Apocalyptic" must not be shorthand for one catastrophic end-of-world script

PASS WITH GUARD.

The current files mostly avoid this error.

Future revisions should use:
DIVINE_INTERVENTION / JUDGMENT / RESTORATION / RULE
where more precise.

### 12. Inward/mystical hypothesis is being treated fairly

PASS.

R2 neither promotes nor dismisses it.

This is particularly important because the project has a strong prior interest
in:
- Source ontology;
- soul access;
- hidden reality;
- self-knowledge.

Current evidence does not yet justify promoting those into historical Yeshua.

### 13. Kabbalah/Hekhalot boundary is correct

PASS.

They belong to R8 diachronic comparison and reception, not to direct R2
historical evidence.

### 14. Gnosticism boundary is correct

PASS.

Source-specific Thomas/Valentinian/Nag Hammadi analysis is preferable to one
"Gnostic" category.

1. Change present-inbreaking from `STRONG_EARLY_TRADITIONAL` to
   `MODERATE_TO_STRONG_EARLY_TRADITIONAL`.

2. State explicitly:
   `MIXED_EARLY_TRADITION != COHERENT_MIXED_HISTORICAL_YESHUA_SYSTEM`.

3. Narrow Mark 4's evidentiary role to:
   SUPPORTING_PRESENT/FUTURE_INTERPRETIVE_PRESSURE.

4. Rephrase Thomas trajectory chronology so "early" does not imply first
   generation.

5. Keep social-reversal as probable but not yet fully saying-stratified.

6. Add a current claim ceiling:
   R2 V1 supports a multi-register **early tradition** more strongly than it
   supports a complete reconstructed Yeshua theology.

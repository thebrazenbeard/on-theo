## Review question

Did the revised source clock actually separate chronology, literary portrayal, social-network evidence, and later authority discourse without using "earlier" as a truth score?

## Findings

### 1. Earlier source is no longer treated as truth score

PASS.

The revised control explicitly states:

`EARLIER_SOURCE != TRUTH_SCORE`.

Paul's proximity gives unusual weight to first-generation social-network evidence, but does not make Pauline silence biographical disproof.

### 2. James kinship is handled asymmetrically but honestly

PASS WITH GUARD.

Ordinary familial kinship remains the strongest reading of Paul's "brother of the Lord," reinforced if Josephus *Antiquities* 20.200 is authentic.

The project still preserves:
- exact biological configuration as unresolved;
- dynastic succession as unestablished;
- Letter of James authorship as separate.

This is preferable to either extreme:
BROTHER_MEANS_NOTHING
or
BROTHER_SETTLES_EVERY_FAMILY_QUESTION.

### 3. Cephas / Peter identity is explicit

PASS.

The mainstream identification is retained while acknowledging that it is an identification with historically proposed minority dissent.

No current evidence in this packet justifies splitting Cephas and Peter.

### 4. Mary Magdalene is no longer ranked from Mark

PASS.

The durable claim is now:
- remembered Galilean following/service;
- named passion/burial/tomb prominence in Mark.

The packet does not infer:
- pre-Jerusalem rank;
- chief-disciple office;
- secret teaching;
- historical suppression.

Later Mary authority texts remain evidence for later authority discourse and possible older-tradition questions, not first-century transcripts by default.

### 5. Judas controls are appropriately hostile

PASS.

The source clock now preserves all of these simultaneously:

- Mark is the earliest admitted explicit named betrayal narrative;
- Paul does not name Judas;
- Pauline silence is weak negative evidence;
- `paradidomi` does not independently name Judas;
- the 1 Corinthians 15 "Twelve" formula is a real source-critical discriminator;
- none of those facts establishes Judas innocence, invention, or guilt mechanics.

This is the correct entry state for the dedicated Judas dossier.

### 6. Thomas layers are separated

PASS.

Markan roster identity, Johannine characterization, and Thomasine revelatory authority are no longer treated as one continuous biography.

The same rule must remain in force when Acts of Thomas or Syrian Thomas traditions are added.

### 7. "Who understood Yeshua best" is no longer pseudo-ranked

PASS.

The revised packet correctly recognizes that the evidence sets are structurally incommensurable.

At R3 entry:

`HISTORICAL_SUPERIORITY_RANKING = NOT_CURRENTLY_RANKABLE`.

This does not mean all figures are historically equivalent.
It means the surviving evidence does not yet license the requested ranking.

### 8. Judas-first work order is now methodologically justified

PASS.

Judas is first because the project has strong prior interest in reframing him and because the arrest model depends on him.

This is asymmetric skepticism:
attack the most attractive/high-bias hypothesis first.

It is not a claim that Judas was the most historically important follower.

### 9. Later texts retain real historical value

PASS WITH GUARD.

Second-century and later Mary/Judas/Thomas texts remain primary evidence for:
- later Christian authority contests;
- reception;
- memory;
- theological development;
- possible preservation of older material.

They do not receive a biographical truth bonus merely because they reverse canonical portrayals.

## Residual vulnerabilities

1. The source clock remains broad. It does not yet perform pericope-level source criticism for each figure.

2. Paul/Mark chronological asymmetry remains severe:
   the earliest social evidence is sparse;
   the earliest continuous narrative is later.

3. Josephus authenticity disputes remain live and passage-specific.

4. The project still has unusually strong prior interest in:
   - Judas exoneration/reframing;
   - Mary authority suppression;
   - Thomas resistant understanding;
   - Peter rehabilitation;
   - collective disciple failure.
   These require stronger-than-normal counterevidence acquisition.

5. Lost sources can always complicate the surviving chronology. The project may describe the order of extant evidence; it must not confuse that with a complete history of tradition.

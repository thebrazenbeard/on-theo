## Review question

Does the dossier preserve the difference between James's historical existence,
familial relation to Yeshua, early Jerusalem authority, possible causes of that
authority, and later succession/dynastic memory?

## PROPOSAL

The dossier proposes that:
- James is historically very strong;
- ordinary familial brotherhood is the strongest current reading;
- James was a major early Jerusalem authority;
- an early appearance tradition to James is very strong;
- Josephus Antiquities 20.200 is strongly supported though disputed;
- formal succession/dynasty is unestablished;
- kinship prestige probably contributed to authority.

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER**
>
> The first four propositions are much stronger than the last.
>
> James is unusually well anchored because Paul claims personal contact and
> calls him "the brother of the Lord." The project is right not to hollow out
> ordinary kinship merely to protect later harmonizations. But ordinary familial
> brotherhood must remain a semantic historical judgment, not a claim that the
> exact maternal/paternal configuration is recovered.
>
> James's early authority is strong. Galatians 2:9 and 2:12 make that difficult
> to deny. But "one of the most important early Jerusalem authorities" is safer
> than any claim of supreme leadership. Paul's ordering, Acts's later
> centralization, and Thomas 12 must not be fused into a succession hierarchy.
>
> "From James" in Galatians 2:12 remains underdetermined. It can establish that
> James's name/network mattered in the Antioch dispute. It does not prove James
> ordered the withdrawal, endorsed the visitors' exact demand, or opposed
> Paul's Gentile mission wholesale.
>
> The appearance tradition in 1 Corinthians 15:7 is very early as a tradition.
> It does not establish the mechanism of the experience, and it does not prove
> the appearance caused James's authority.
>
> The Josephus control is appropriately cautious. Current scholarship contains
> both a strong 2025 authenticity defense and a 2026 Oxford treatment that keeps
> the interpolation question open. "Strongly supported, not unanimous" is the
> right ceiling. Do not let Josephus carry claims about James's theology or
> office that the passage never makes.
>
> The largest current overreach is KINSHIP_PRESTIGE_CONTRIBUTION =
> PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE.
>
> James is kin. James is authoritative. That does not establish that kinship
> caused or materially increased the authority. Paul's sources never say:
> "James has authority because he is Jesus's brother."
>
> Kinship prestige is a plausible explanatory model. It becomes more interesting
> when later family-authority traditions are added, but those later traditions
> are themselves precisely what must not be back-projected.
>
> The correct current state is KINSHIP_PRESTIGE_CONTRIBUTION =
> PLAUSIBLE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.
>
> Thomas 12 is excellent evidence that some later Christians grounded
> extraordinary James authority in a saying placed on Jesus's lips. It is not
> first-generation succession evidence until independence/age is demonstrated.
>
> Hegesippus/Eusebius traditions about Symeon and the relatives of Jesus are
> real evidence for later family-authority memory. They make an informal
> family-prestige trajectory worth testing. They do not prove a first-century
> hereditary constitution.
>
> The category "dynasty" is dangerous. It can import royal/legal succession
> machinery that the sources do not establish. The project should distinguish:
> family prestige; repeated family leadership; informal preference; formal
> hereditary office.
>
> The Letter of James must remain quarantined from direct biography until
> authorship is independently argued. Similarity to Jesus sayings is not
> authorship proof.
>
> Finally, "who understood Yeshua best" remains not rankable. Early authority,
> kinship, and appearance tradition are not measures of interpretive accuracy.

## REQUIRED REVISIONS

1. Keep historical James at VERY_STRONG.
2. Keep ordinary familial kinship at HIGH / STRONGEST_CURRENT_READING, with
   exact biological configuration unresolved.
3. Keep early Jerusalem authority at HIGH.
4. Keep early appearance tradition at VERY_STRONG_AS_TRADITION.
5. Preserve Josephus at STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_NOT_UNANIMOUS.
6. Reduce kinship-prestige causation from PLAUSIBLE_TO_PROBABLE to
   PLAUSIBLE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.
7. Reclassify the kinship-prestige model accordingly.
8. Decompose later family evidence into FAMILY_AUTHORITY_MEMORY,
   REPEATED_KIN_LEADERSHIP_TRADITION, INFORMAL_SUCCESSION_HYPOTHESIS, and
   FORMAL_DYNASTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED.
9. State explicitly that Acts, Thomas 12, Hegesippus and Eusebius are later
   source layers and cannot jointly manufacture a first-century appointment.
10. Keep Galatians 2:12 at social-network influence, not direct command.
11. Preserve Letter of James authorship as contested.
12. Keep superiority ranking unavailable.

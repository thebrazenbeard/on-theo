## 3. Temple confrontation historicity

NARROWING REQUIRED.

The packet currently states:

HISTORICAL_TEMPLE_CONFRONTATION = PROBABLE.

That is defensible in much historical-Jesus scholarship, but inside this
project's exact source architecture the independence basis is weaker than the
word "probable" may suggest:

- Mark is the earliest surviving continuous narrative.
- Matthew/Luke materially depend on Mark.
- John has a Temple-disruption tradition but relocates it and cannot simply be
  counted as a clean independent event witness.
- Paul does not independently attest the incident.

Required revision:

HISTORICAL_TEMPLE_CONFRONTATION =
MODERATE_TO_PROBABLE_SOURCE_DEPENDENT.

The prose may still say it is historically plausible and widely reconstructed,
but the claim ceiling must expose that R4 lacks clean early independent event
attestation.

## 1. Greek Magical Papyri — HIGH PRIORITY SOURCE GAP

### Current external source status

The modern scholarly corpus called the Greek Magical Papyri / PGM gathers
ritual formularies, spells, hymns, invocations, divination procedures,
phylacteries and other texts from Greco-Roman Egypt, broadly from the Hellenistic
through late-antique period.

The Betz translation and newer Greek/Egyptian magical formulary projects make
the corpus accessible, but ON_THEO does not yet have an exact witness-bound PGM
packet.

Therefore:

`PGM_RITUAL_INTERFACE_EVIDENCE = SOURCE_PACKET_REQUIRED`.

### Why it pressures the current hostile model

PGM materials can contain combinations of:
- divine names;
- `voces magicae` / `onomata barbara`;
- apparently nonlexical strings;
- vowel sequences;
- diagrams / characters;
- ritual materials;
- operator preparation;
- timing;
- repeated formulae;
- invocations;
- divination / revelation procedures;
- recipes claiming healing, attraction, protection, vision, foreknowledge or
  divine encounter.

This is much closer to a "recipe-like operative procedure" than a festival
calendar or communal ordination rule.

### Why it still does not earn exotic credit

The most attractive-looking feature — apparently nonsemantic magical words — is
historically treacherous.

Relevant scholarship includes:

- article on `voces magicae` in the PGM,
  DOI: `10.17648/ROM.V0I9.18485`;
- "Divine Names: A Cross-Cultural Comparison...",
  DOI: `10.1353/MRW.0.0168`;
- "The All-Encompassing Name: Multilingualism, Myth and Materiality in a Late
  Greek Papyrus of Ritual Power (PGM XIII)",
  DOI: `10.1515/9783111326511-033`;
- "Le Chant Des Voyelles Dans Les Papyri Graecae Magicae...",
  DOI: `10.4000/rhr.11467`;
- "Who Wrote Ritual Formularies from Egypt?",
  DOI: `10.1525/sla.2023.7.3.366`.

These sources keep several ordinary/historical explanations live:

1. **foreign-language / multilingual divine-name prestige**;
2. **transliteration and transmission distortion**;
3. **cross-cultural borrowing and fusion**;
4. **graphic/onomastic ritual technologies**;
5. **performative sound and breath**;
6. **scribal variation**;
7. **later scholarly reconstruction of pronunciation**;
8. **priestly / literate ritual-formulary production**.

The vowel-chant literature is especially useful hostile evidence because a
modern planetary-musical interpretation has itself been criticized as a
historiographic construction rather than a secure ancient universal rule.

Therefore:

`APPARENT_NONLEXICALITY != SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_ARBITRARINESS`.

### Pharmacological/physiological confound

Some PGM recipes associated with:
- vision;
- ecstasy;
- dream;
- altered experience

also use material ingredients.

Modern historical analysis has explored ancient pharmacological and
physiological contributions to such outcomes.

Relevant:
"Ingesting Magic: Ingredients and Ecstatic Outcomes in the Greek and Demotic
Magical Papyri",
DOI: `10.1353/arn.2017.0008`.

This means a successful historical report of altered experience would not by
itself isolate an external information channel.

Do not ingest "the PGM" as one ritual system.

- exact lines;

- preparation;
- material;
- speech;

High-value candidate class:

`PGM_LOW_SEMANTIC_OPERATIVE_FORMULA_CANDIDATE`.

## 2. Mesopotamian Maqlû — HIGH PRIORITY SOURCE GAP

### Source family

Maqlû ("Burning") is a major Akkadian anti-witchcraft ritual series.

Modern critical scholarship describes the standard series as:
- eight tablets of incantations;
- a ritual tablet;
- nearly one hundred incantations;
- a single complex ceremony;
- performance extending through one night into morning;
- incantation/action coupling;
- figurines and other materials;
- purification and protective phases.

Important modern source resources include:
- Tzvi Abusch, *The Magical Ceremony Maqlû: A Critical Edition*;
- Corpus of Mesopotamian Anti-Witchcraft Rituals online;
- Society of Biblical Literature translations/studies.

This is an unusually strong **procedural-brittleness source candidate** because
the ritual tablet explicitly pairs incipit/recitation structure with ritual
directions.

### Why it does not automatically behave like immutable code

The manuscript tradition is not invariant.

Scholarship on individual Maqlû incantations documents:
- expansion;
- interpolation;
- shorter variants;
- revision;
- manuscript differences.

Example:
"Maqlû III 1–30: Internal Analysis and Manuscript Evidence for the Revision of
an Incantation",
DOI: `10.1163/9789004421912_006`.

This creates a severe hostile problem for the "exact ancient API" analogy:

If:
- sequence;
- wording;
- incantation length;
- ritual composition

can develop historically while the tradition continues to treat the ritual as
operative, then textual exactness may partly reflect **scribal
standardization/canonization**, not one frozen external command language.

This does not prove the rite lacked operative beliefs.

It directly weakens:

`ONE_EXACT_STRING_WAS_ALWAYS_REQUIRED`.

### Required packet

ON_THEO should bind:
- one critical-edition subject;
- standard series versus earlier/variant forms;
- ritual tablet relation to incantation tablets;
- calendrical/timing claims;
- action/incantation pairing;
- claimed non-social effect;
- variant manuscripts;
- history of standardization;
- explicit error/failure language if source-secure.

Desired hostile question:

> Which parameters are stable across witnesses, and which are products of
> textual development?

Only stable, substitution-sensitive parameters deserve later interface testing.

Current disposition:

`MAQLU = VERY_STRONG_SOURCE_SIDE_PROCEDURAL_CANDIDATE / EXOTIC_EFFECT_UNTESTED`.

## 3. Šurpu — STRONG BUT EXPLANATION-RICH CANDIDATE

Šurpu is another Mesopotamian incantation/ritual series associated with
purification from curse/sin/affliction.

Modern descriptions include:
- incantation-priest;
- burning/manipulation of materials;
- onion, wool, flour and other symbolic substances;
- purification;
- confession/sin-list structures;
- incantation-action coupling.

An important hostile distinction:

Many actions have an explicit **sympathetic / representational logic**.

Example pattern:
- peel/burn an object;
- linguistically map that action onto removal/destruction of affliction.

Therefore these details may be:
`SOURCE_INTERPRETED`
rather than:
`SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_BUT_PRESCRIBED`.

This makes Šurpu valuable because it separates:

`OPERATIVE_RITUAL`

from:

`ARBITRARY_PARAMETER`.

A ritual can be highly procedural while its source-level causal model remains
semantically transparent.

Current disposition:

`STRONG_PROCEDURAL_RITUAL / WEAKER_LOW_SEMANTIC_CANDIDATE_THAN_PGM_VOCES`.

## 4. Related Mesopotamian variation is a major negative control

The apotropaic ritual `Bīt mēseri` also has variant source forms.

A published Aššur witness differs markedly from the standard ritual known from
other first-millennium sources and adds/removes ritual elements.

Relevant:
DOI `10.1515/za-2022-0013`.

Hostile significance:

`SAME_NAMED_RITUAL_FAMILY`
can tolerate
`MATERIAL_PROCEDURAL_VARIATION`.

That fact is exactly what a brittle-interface model must explain rather than
ignore.

Possible explanations include:
- regional adaptation;
- scribal recomposition;
- changing specialist practice;
- different ritual goals/subtypes;
- genuine optionality;
- nonessential parameters.

Do not cherry-pick the standardized late witness and retroject it as eternal
syntax.

## 8. What would change the hostile verdict

A newly admitted magical/operative text does not change the exotic verdict merely
by containing:
- weird words;
- exact recipes;
- names;
- diagrams;
- special materials;
- timing.

To change the verdict, a case would need to get closer to:

1. exact parameter securely attested;
2. parameter substitution restricted;
3. stable across independent witnesses or historical variation itself explained;
4. source claims a non-social external effect;
5. ordinary function insufficient;
6. safe modern abstraction possible;
7. parameter-specific prospective effect;
8. independent replication.

PGM/Maqlû may substantially improve #1–4.

They currently establish nothing at #7–8.

That is why they are high-priority research, not evidence of the answer.

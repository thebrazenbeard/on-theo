# Ritual Interface Hypothesis — Living-Community Disclosure Boundary Hostile Pass V1

Status: `HOSTILE_BOUNDARY / NON-CANONICAL / ETHICAL_STOP_RULE`

Date: 2026-09-20

Parent:
`reviews/RITUAL_INTERFACE_EXPLOIT_HOSTILE_REVIEW_V1.md`

## Purpose

Prevent the ritual-interface hypothesis from treating culturally restricted
knowledge as "missing protocol data" that should be extracted to complete a
comparison matrix.

This is both an ethics boundary and an epistemic boundary.

The hypothesis receives **no evidentiary bonus** from inaccessible sacred
knowledge.

---

## 1. Core rule

`RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE != MISSING_DATA_TO_BE_FILLED`.

If a living source community states that ceremonial information is:
- restricted;
- private;
- seasonally limited;
- initiatory;
- not for unrestricted recording;
- not for outsiders;
- confidential by cultural-resource policy;

then ON_THEO does not seek operational reconstruction.

This remains true when:
- an older ethnographer printed it;
- a scan is publicly downloadable;
- a third-party site reposted it;
- the information would make the ritual-interface matrix more complete.

A model that requires violating living-community disclosure boundaries to stay
interesting has failed this project's admission standard.

The lane explicitly states:

`PUBLICLY_ACCESSIBLE != ETHICALLY_UNRESTRICTED`.

It refuses to reproduce:
- closed ceremonial instructions;
- initiatory knowledge;
- sacred bundle contents beyond public authorization;
- private prayers/songs;
- restricted sacred-site details;
- knowledge marked not for outsiders.

Public-source research may establish:
- ceremony existence;
- public name;
- broad purpose;
- relational/ethical meaning;
- suppression/continuity.

It does not authorize:
- step-by-step reconstruction;
- private prayer/song publication;
- pan-Lakota standardization.

### Vision-seeking example

The public Lakota packet establishes that vision seeking is a publicly
recognized religious category and relates to prayer/guidance/place.

It explicitly refuses procedural reconstruction.

Therefore:

`PUBLIC_EXISTENCE_OF_VISION_SEEKING != AUTHORITY_TO_REPRODUCE_ITS_PROCEDURE`.

Hostile implication:

Do not classify withheld vision-seeking details as:
`SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_BUT_PRESCRIBED`.

They are:
`CULTURALLY_RESTRICTED / NOT_ADMISSIBLE_FOR_OPERATIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION`.

## 3. Diné boundary — strongest explicit stop rule

Exact source subject:
- ON_THEO PR #108
- head: `ac988c4a31f354e7c4d902d286c0c950293ffb8a`

Control:
- `traditions/dine/public-source-restricted-knowledge-boundary-v1.md`

The lane records Navajo Nation cultural-resource policy as stating that some:
- sacred stories;
- songs;
- sandpaintings;
- ceremonial knowledge;
- sacred-place information

are not intended for unrestricted permanent recording or dissemination.

Some knowledge may be restricted by:
- season;
- age;
- maturity;
- gender;
- other cultural conditions.

ON_THEO's adopted rule is:

`IF_SOURCE_COMMUNITY_RESTRICTS_INFORMATION -> DO_NOT_RECONSTRUCT_OR_REPUBLISH`.

The lane explicitly says:

`CULTURALLY_RESTRICTED_BY_SOURCE_COMMUNITY`

is a legitimate stopping condition and is **not**:

`UNKNOWN_DUE_TO_INADEQUATE_RESEARCH`.

This directly governs the ritual-interface project.

### Consequence for hypothesis testing

A Diné ceremonial system may have:
- exact songs;
- sequences;
- images;
- specialist roles;
- seasonality;
- place relations.

But if those details fall inside restricted knowledge:

`NO_PARAMETER_EXTRACTION`.

The project may analyze the **fact of restricted governance** as an institutional
knowledge variable.

It may not use inaccessible detail as a mysterious hidden-interface clue.

## 5. Why restricted knowledge cannot count as exotic residue

A bad argument would be:

> The missing details may contain the exact parameter that makes the ritual work.

That proposition is unfalsifiable because any negative result can be rescued by
invoking inaccessible secret details.

Therefore the project adopts:

`RESTRICTED_PARAMETER_RESCUE = FORBIDDEN`.

If a safe/public ritual test fails, the hypothesis may not answer:
- "the real prayer was private";
- "the secret sequence was omitted";
- "only initiates know the correct version";
- "the location is hidden";
- "the source community will not disclose the actual parameter";

and then preserve the same evidentiary status.

That move changes the model from:
`TESTABLE_SPECULATIVE_MODEL`

to:
`UNTESTABLE_PRIVATE_KNOWLEDGE_ESCAPE_HATCH`.

No evidence credit.

## 6. Public high-level variables remain usable

Living-community lanes may still contribute source-secure, nonrestricted
comparison variables such as:

- public ritual category;
- public purpose;
- public sacred-time model;
- public office/authority;
- public relational cosmology;
- public knowledge-governance rules;
- public place relation;
- public seasonality;
- public suppression/contact history.

These can enrich the neutral comparison.

They must not be used to infer hidden operational detail.

These are not interchangeable.

Especially:

`CULTURALLY_RESTRICTED_BY_SOURCE_COMMUNITY != UNKNOWN`.

---

## 8. Experimental eligibility

A living-tradition ritual family is eligible for safe experimental translation
only when:

- relevant form is deliberately public;
- no community restriction signal applies;
- no sacred-site/confidential data are exposed;
- no harmful action is required;
- the test does not claim to reproduce what public sources do not disclose;
- community-specific terminology is preserved;
- the research does not pretend to speak for the community.

If those conditions fail:

`EXPERIMENTAL_TRANSLATION = NOT_ADMITTED`.

This is a complete result.

---

## 9. Hostile disposition

The disclosure boundary makes the overall hypothesis **more** falsifiable, not
less.

It removes a common pseudoscientific escape:

> "The real secret version would have worked."

Current rule:

`NO_SECRET_PARAMETER_RESCUE`.

`NO_RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE_EXTRACTION`.

`NO_GENERIC_INDIGENOUS_RITUAL_CATEGORY`.

`PUBLICLY_ACCESSIBLE != ETHICALLY_UNRESTRICTED`.

`RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE != RESEARCH_GAP`.

The hypothesis must live or die on ethically admissible evidence.

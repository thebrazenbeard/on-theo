## Challenge 1 — Does "pre-Pauline" mean "Jerusalem apostolic"?

No.

A tradition can predate Paul's letter without:
- originating in Jerusalem;
- originating with the Twelve;
- being held identically by James/Cephas;
- being formulated immediately after execution.

Guard:
`PRE_PAULINE_NE_JERUSALEM_BY_DEFAULT`.

## Challenge 2 — Does Paul's Jerusalem contact authenticate his theology?

No.

Galatians preserves both:
- contact/recognition;
- conflict/independence.

Paul's proximity to Cephas and James constrains wild isolation models, but it does
not make his theology a transcript of theirs.

## Challenge 3 — Does Maranatha prove early worship of Jesus as ontologically God?

No.

It proves:
- direct invocation;
- Lord-language;
- eschatological expectation;

in an Aramaic-speaking setting.

Its later Nicene metaphysical interpretation cannot be back-projected.

## Challenge 4 — Is 1 Cor 15 formula necessarily within months of crucifixion?

No.

The formula is early and pre-Pauline in core form.
Exact dating remains reconstructed.

"First decade" is plausible.
"Within days/weeks" is not admitted without stronger evidence.

## Challenge 5 — Does "for our sins" necessarily come from Yeshua?

No.

It is early theological interpretation.
Historical attribution to Yeshua requires separate testing of passion sayings,
Last Supper traditions, and scriptural interpretation.

## Challenge 6 — Does Paul know no earthly Jesus?

No.

Paul invokes some Lord sayings/traditions and personally knew first-generation
leaders.

But his surviving letters still display a striking asymmetry:
the crucified/risen Christ matters much more to his argument than narrated
Galilean biography.

That asymmetry is historically important.

## Challenge 7 — Did Paul invent universal/Gentile Christ?

Overstated if phrased absolutely.

Paul is our earliest surviving major advocate of uncircumcised Gentile mission
on this scale, but Galatians itself records agreements, rivals, and prior
communities.

Use:
`PAULINE_ACCELERATION_AND_RECONFIGURATION`,
not:
`SOLE_INVENTION`.

## Challenge 8 — Does rapid development make the beliefs false?

No.

Speed is evidence about chronology, not truth value.

Rapid development is compatible with:
- genuine extraordinary event;
- visionary experiences;
- charismatic memory;
- scriptural reinterpretation;
- social crisis;
- deliberate invention;
- combinations.

Each mechanism must be tested independently.

## Strongest surviving result

The Yeshua-to-Christ transformation cannot be assigned primarily to:
- John;
- second-century philosophy;
- Constantine.

The core is already present in the first generation.

The most constrained unresolved question is:

> What combination of Yeshua's own claims, post-death experiences, scriptural
> rereading, ritual practice, and early leadership dynamics produced the
> Messiah/Lord/resurrection complex before Paul's surviving writings?

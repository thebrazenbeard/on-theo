## Review question

After revision, does the dossier preserve the difference between:
- probable historical Mary;
- Markan memory of her role;
- later canonical development;
- later revelatory authority;
- prostitute/sinner reception;
- an unproven deliberate suppression theory?

## Findings

### 1. Historical Mary

PASS.

`PROBABLE` is the appropriate R3 ceiling.

Mark is the earliest explicit named source and therefore carries most of the
historical burden. Repeated stable identification makes a real referent the
lower-assumption model without granting Mary Pauline/external attestation she
does not possess.

### 2. Galilean following and passion/tomb prominence

PASS WITH GUARD.

Mark strongly preserves these propositions at narrative/tradition level.

The dossier correctly does not convert:
- following/service;
- crucifixion presence;
- burial-location memory;
- tomb presence
into a historian's direct eyewitness certification.

Formal office remains unresolved.

### 3. Prostitute identity

PASS.

The claim is now correctly formulated:

EARLIEST_EXPLICIT_SOURCES_IDENTIFY_MARY_AS_PROSTITUTE =
NOT_SUPPORTED.

The dossier explicitly refuses the invalid reverse inference:

MARY_NEVER_ENGAGED_IN_SEX_WORK =
PROVED.

This preserves the strongest historical correction without inventing a private
biography.

### 4. Later prostitute/sinner reception

PASS.

The later composite/penitential Mary remains strong reception history.

The dossier properly refuses to infer one coordinated original slander campaign
from the existence of the later conflation.

### 5. Canonical resurrection roles

PASS.

Matthew/Luke/John preserve later canonical Mary/women resurrection-message
traditions, and John gives Mary an especially individualized messenger role.

The dossier now states that extant-source chronology is not itself proof of one
smooth tradition-development path.

Historical first-appearance priority remains unresolved.

### 6. Gospel of Mary identity

PASS.

The authority conflict is now attributed first to:
THE_WORKS_MARY.

The specifically Magdalene interpretation remains:

PROBABLE_DOMINANT_SCHOLARLY_IDENTIFICATION_NOT_TEXTUALLY_AUTOMATIC.

That is sufficiently cautious for downstream reception analysis.

### 7. Gospel of Mary authority conflict

PASS.

The literary propositions are strong:
- revelatory teaching;
- Andrew challenge;
- Peter challenge;
- Levi defense.

The historical propositions remain unpromoted:
- first-century Peter/Magdalene conflict;
- private historical revelation;
- Yeshua preference.

### 8. "Gnosticism"

PASS.

No unified "Gnostic Mary" is inferred.

Later Mary materials remain tractate-specific and composition/witness clocks are
preserved.

### 9. Spouse / sexual partner

PASS.

No current admitted early evidence establishes marriage or sexual partnership.

The dossier does not convert non-attestation into metaphysical or historical
impossibility.

### 10. Superior understanding

PASS.

Later literary authority is not used as a measurement of historical
comprehension.

The project cannot currently rank:

or

## Residual vulnerabilities

1. Mary remains primarily Gospel-attested rather than Pauline/external.
2. Markan women traditions may preserve both historical memory and literary
   construction.
3. Gospel of Mary composition/source relations remain debated.
4. The protagonist's exact Mary identification is probable, not automatic.
5. Gendered authority conflict in later texts may preserve older social
   tensions, but no clean first-century transmission chain has yet been shown.
6. Later penitential conflation can have suppressive effects without proving
   intentional coordinated origin.

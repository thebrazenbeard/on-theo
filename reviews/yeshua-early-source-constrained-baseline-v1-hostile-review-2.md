## Findings

### 1. Source age is no longer converted into historical certainty

PASS.

The revised baseline continues to distinguish:
- Paul as earlier but non-biographical;
- Mark as later but narratively rich;
- Josephus/Tacitus as later external controls with authenticity/dependence
  limitations.

The prose explicitly refuses to treat Mark as an event transcript.

### 2. Jewish embeddedness remains bounded

PASS WITH GUARD.

The claim now stays at:
Yeshua belongs inside first-century Jewish religious life.

It does not reconstruct one specific sectarian or halakhic program.

Downstream R2 must use the expanded Jewish source envelope rather than treating
"Judaism" as one comparator.

### 3. Galilee/Nazareth is properly marked as probable

PASS.

Mark carries most of the admitted burden in R1.
No false multiple-attestation claim remains.

### 4. John the Baptist is correctly decomposed

PASS.

Josephus supports:
JOHN_AND_BAPTISMAL_MOVEMENT_HISTORICAL.

He does not independently support:
JOHN_BAPTIZED_YESHUA.

The latter remains probable at moderate confidence.

### 5. Kingdom claim is no longer prejudging R2

PASS.

The revised confidence is:
`moderate_provisional`.

The historical message may have been:
- apocalyptic;
- social;
- ethical;
- present;
- future;
- inward;

R1 does not decide among them.

### 6. Healer/exorcist claim is bounded to reputation/presentation

PASS WITH GUARD.

The baseline establishes that Mark strongly remembers/presents Yeshua in this
role and treats a historical reputation as plausible.

It does not establish:
- supernatural mechanism;
- diagnosis;
- exact cure;
- every narrated event.

### 7. Follower/family network and Twelve are now separated

PASS.

Paul gives unusually early evidence for:
- Cephas;
- James;
- apostles;
- Lord's brothers;
- a "Twelve" group-label/tradition.

The revision now explicitly refuses to reconstruct the later canonical roster
from that datum.

### 8. Temple causation is correctly unresolved

PASS.

independent control sufficient to promote causal reconstruction.

### 9. Crucifixion / Pilate claims remain strong but bounded

PASS.

- Gospel trial scripts;
- crowd mechanics;
- exact charge;
- Pilate's motives.

### 10. Burial is tradition history, not tomb history

PASS.

Early burial proclamation survives.

Joseph of Arimathea, rock-tomb location and empty-tomb implications remain
separate claims.

### 11. Resurrection is conviction/reporting history

PASS.

The revised proposition now says appearances were **reported**.

- very early vindication/resurrection conviction;
- named appearance traditions.

It does not adjudicate:
- bodily event;
- visionary experience;
- grief mechanism;
- hallucination;
- fraud;
- supernatural resurrection.

## Residual vulnerabilities

1. Historical-existence confidence still depends on interpreting Pauline
   social-network language, especially James, in ordinary kinship/social terms.
   This is strong but should remain reviewable.

2. The John/baptism and Kingdom claims remain consensus-friendly and therefore
   vulnerable to unnoticed methodological comfort. R2/R3 must actively seek
   disconfirming models.

3. Healing/exorcism remains source-thin under the artificial R1 constraint.

4. Pilate is historically strong, but later external convergence is still not
   three clean independent witnesses.

5. The project has strong prior interest in:
   - Judas innocence/frame-up;
   - Mary/Thomas superior understanding;
   - inward mystical teaching;
   - psychological trauma;
   - Roman cultivation.
   None receives positive R1 promotion.

6. R1 is intentionally underpowered for sayings traditions and must not be
   mistaken for the final historical Yeshua.

## Purpose

Prevent either side from cheating with the wider anomaly / prayer literature.

The ritual-interface hypothesis must not argue:

> anomalous cognition has sometimes been reported, therefore ritual parameters
> may be an interface.

And the hostile review must not argue:

> some prayer or precognition studies failed, therefore anomalous information is
> impossible.

Both are invalid transfers.

The only relevant empirical question is:

> Does one source-secure ritual parameter produce a prospective effect that
> survives ordinary channels and beats matched parameter controls?

Generic psi/prayer literature can inform method and prior skepticism.

It cannot substitute for that test.

## 1. Intercessory prayer — objective external-effect negative context

### STEP trial

Benson et al.,
"Study of the Therapeutic Effects of Intercessory Prayer (STEP) in cardiac
bypass patients" (2006).

PubMed PMID:
`16569567`.

Design:
- multicenter randomized trial;
- 1802 CABG patients;
- three groups;
- prayer/no-prayer under uncertainty plus a prayer-certain group;
- objective postoperative outcomes.

Reported result:
among patients uncertain of whether they would receive prayer, actual
intercessory prayer did not improve complication-free recovery.

The group certain it would receive prayer had a higher complication rate than
the uncertain-prayer group.

Hostile use:

`REMOTE_PRAYER`
does not automatically produce a robust objective external health effect in a
large controlled trial.

Do not overread the higher-complication finding as anti-prayer causality.

The study tested a specific intervention and population.

### Cochrane synthesis

Cochrane review:
`Intercessory prayer for the alleviation of ill health`,
review ID `CD000368`.

The review included ten randomized trials / 7646 participants in the reported
version.

For prayer + standard care versus standard care:
- no clear mortality effect;
- no clear coronary-care readmission effect;
- heterogeneous methods/outcomes;
- individual positive findings did not yield a reliable overall recommendation.

Hostile result:

`GENERAL_INTERCESSORY_PRAYER_OBJECTIVE_EFFECT = NOT_RELIABLY_ESTABLISHED`.

This does **not** test:
- exact mantra;
- exact pronunciation;
- exact ritual order;
- exact addressing;
- a hidden-information endpoint.

Therefore it is negative context, not direct falsification of a parameterized
ritual-interface model.

## 2. Ganzfeld literature — positive meta-analytic claims remain contested

A Stage 2 registered meta-analysis of Ganzfeld anomalous-perception studies
reported a small positive pooled effect.

Relevant F1000Research record:
DOI `10.12688/f1000research.51746.4`.

The authors report:
- overall effect approximately 0.08;
- publication-bias tests not indicating obvious contamination;
- moderator differences.

Historical debate is substantial.

Earlier analyses and skeptical/proponent reanalyses disagree over:
- inclusion;
- replication;
- heterogeneity;
- outliers;
- laboratory independence.

Hostile interpretation:

The right statement is not:
`GANZFELD_PROVES_PSI`.

Nor:
`GANZFELD_IS_WORTHLESS`.

It is:

`GANZFELD_LITERATURE_CONTAINS_PERSISTENT_SMALL_ANOMALY_CLAIMS_WITH_LONG_RUNNING_REPLICATION_AND_INTERPRETATION_DISPUTE`.

For this project, its main value is methodological:
- target isolation;
- forced-choice/ranking;
- leakage control;
- preregistration;
- independent replication;
- separation of anomaly from mechanism.

Even a genuine Ganzfeld anomaly would not establish that historical ritual form
is the causal parameter.

## 3. Remote viewing literature — do not borrow somebody else's anomaly

A 1974–2022 systematic review/meta-analysis of remote-viewing studies reports
positive pooled effects.

Published version:
DOI `10.31275/20232931`.

Preprint:
DOI `10.31234/osf.io/r9yw5`.

The literature remains controversial and includes:
- favorable analyses;
- methodological disputes;
- positive and null replications;
- uncertainty about scoring and laboratory dependence.

Hostile rule:

`REMOTE_VIEWING_META_EFFECT`
does not supply evidence for
`RITUAL_PARAMETER_CAUSES_ANOMALOUS_INFORMATION`.

If ritual condition A is hypothesized to improve hidden-target performance, it
must beat its own matched B/C/D/sham arms prospectively.

No citation transfer.

## 4. Bem-style precognition — high-control replication is a major warning

The original 2011 Bem report generated extensive replication work and debate.

A particularly important modern control is the Transparent Psi /
advanced-meta-experimental replication program.

2025 PLOS ONE:
"Metascientific replication project with the advanced meta-experimental
protocol of the transparent psi project procedures for testing the precognitive
effect claimed by Bem."

DOI:
`10.1371/journal.pone.0335330`.

Reported scale:
- 26,483 participants;
- 420,472 critical trials;
- three replication studies.

The original precognition effect was not stably reproduced across the program.

One exploratory opposite-direction signal in an early study did not remain
stable across the subsequent replication sequence.

Other independent replication work includes:
- "Correcting the past: Failures to replicate psi" — seven experiments,
  N=3,289, no successful replication of the target retroactive-recall effect;
- "The future failed: No evidence for precognition in a large scale replication
  attempt of Bem (2011)",
  DOI `10.1037/cns0000342`.

Hostile implication:

Small surprising effects are fragile when:
- hypotheses;
- code;
- exclusions;
- randomization;
- analysis;
- stopping

are frozen and exposed to large independent testing.

That is exactly why the ritual-interface project must not begin with a flexible
free-response "did something happen?" design.

## 5. What the anomaly literature teaches this project

### Lesson 1 — effect claim must be narrower than metaphysical interpretation

A statistical anomaly is not:
- telepathy;
- spirit;
- deity;
- simulator;
- exploit.

Those are mechanism/source attributions.

### Lesson 2 — tiny effects are expensive

If the true effect is small, honest confirmatory power can require hundreds or
thousands of independent trials.

The parent hostile review's 1-of-8 example deliberately assumes a very large
effect:
- chance = 12.5%;
- alternative = 25%.

Even then, with five fixed arms and FWER 0.001:
- 174 trials/arm;
- 39 exact hits required;
- ~81% power.

If the true uplift resembles small disputed psi meta-effects, the required
sample is much larger.

### Lesson 3 — exploratory signal is not confirmation

A novel parameter:
- exact vowel;
- full moon;
- facing east;
- practitioner lineage;
- exact count

cannot be discovered in the same data and then reported as confirmatory.

It becomes a hypothesis for a fresh target stream.

### Lesson 4 — optional stopping is poisonous here

The temptation will be extreme:

> "Keep trying rituals until something uncanny happens."

That destroys the chance model unless the search process itself is fully
accounted for.

The confirmatory run needs:
- frozen N;
- or a predeclared valid sequential design;
- blinded evidence state where practical;
- no post-hoc ritual tuning.

### Lesson 5 — free-response scoring is dangerous

Semantic similarity can introduce:
- judge flexibility;
- hindsight;
- model leakage;
- target-set artifacts;
- evaluator tuning.

Initial confirmatory work should prefer:
- exact forced choice;
- rank scoring fixed in advance;
- automated target generation;
- hidden target custody.

Free response belongs later, with independent frozen scoring.

### Lesson 6 — experimenter effects are not a convenient rescue

If performance varies by experimenter, that is a new variable requiring
preregistered testing.

It cannot be used after a failed replication to declare:
"the wrong person ran it."

### Lesson 7 — anomaly replication must be adversarial

A credible positive ritual anomaly should invite:
- independent custodians;
- skeptical reviewers;
- independent code reimplementation;
- fresh targets;
- fraud/leakage attempts;
- negative replications preserved.

The hypothesis should become harder to save as evidence improves.

## 6. Strongest empirical target for this project

Health outcomes are noisy and mechanistically overloaded.

Subjective mystical experiences are expectation-sensitive.

Physiology is informative about human-state mechanisms but not external contact.

The cleanest first external endpoint is:

`PROSPECTIVE_UNKNOWN_INFORMATION`.

Example:
- independently generated 1-of-K target;
- no participant/operator/model access;
- exact committed response;
- target revealed only after commitment;
- exact scoring;
- fixed arms;
- sham condition;
- independent replication.

This does not require believing in:
- psi;
- spirits;
- simulation;
- God.

It asks only whether the ritual parameter changes information performance above
the frozen chance model.

## 7. Ritual-specific causal burden

Suppose a source-faithful condition A exceeds chance.

That alone does not support the ritual parameter.

Required next comparison:

- A source-faithful ritual;
- B same meaning, altered surface;
- C same surface dynamics, altered sacred meaning;
- D parameter permutation;
- E matched sham.

The ritual-interface model gains incremental credit only if the source-prescribed
parameter predicts the contrast structure.

Example:

If A and D are equal:
order sensitivity fails.

If A and B are equal:
exact surface-form dependence fails.

If A and C are equal:
sacred semantics may be unnecessary.

If all vocal arms improve equally:
respiration/rhythm or generalized task-state effects remain stronger.

If all arms are at chance:
kill the anomaly branch for the tested configuration under the achieved power.

## 8. No generic-psi piggyback rule

The following argument is forbidden:

1. Some meta-analysis reports anomalous cognition.
2. Historical ritual X has unusual exact features.
3. Therefore ritual X may be using the same channel.

That is only:

`SPECULATIVE_COMPATIBILITY`.

Required evidence is:

`RITUAL_PARAMETER -> PROSPECTIVE_DIFFERENTIAL_EFFECT`.

Until then:

`GENERIC_ANOMALY_LITERATURE != RITUAL_INTERFACE_EVIDENCE`.

## Principal objections

### 1. Mary historical-followership confidence is too high

Mark provides the earliest substantial anchor; Matthew/Luke are not cleanly
independent when repeating Mark and John is later.

Required correction:

MARY_HISTORICAL_FOLLOWER =
PROBABLE

rather than:
PROBABLE_TO_STRONG.

Her early passion/tomb **memory** is strong; the historical reconstruction
should remain slightly lower.

### 2. Mary-as-prostitute correction survives

PASS.

The canonical sources do not identify Mary Magdalene as a prostitute. Later
Western conflation belongs to reception history.

But the project must continue to distinguish:

FALSE_LATER_IDENTIFICATION
from
PROVED_INTENTIONAL_SLANDER_CAMPAIGN.

The latter remains unresolved.

### 3. Gospel of Mary is valid for authority-memory, not direct biography

PASS WITH GUARDS.

The packet properly separates physical witnesses from composition.

The identity of the work's "Mary" as Mary Magdalene is the majority/common
scholarly identification but not logically certain.

Required guard:
MARY_IN_GOSPEL_OF_MARY_IDENTIFICATION =
PROBABLE_NOT_ABSOLUTE.

### 4. Gospel of Judas valuation is over-simplified

REVISION REQUIRED.

The work does give Judas distinctive knowledge relative to other disciples.

But scholarship strongly disputes whether Judas is:
- positive hero;
- tragic figure;
- demonic/negative figure;
- ambivalently privileged.

Therefore R3 may say:

JUDAS_DISTINCTIVE_KNOWLEDGE_IN_LATER_TEXT =
SURVIVES.

It may not say:

GOSPEL_OF_JUDAS_STRAIGHTFORWARDLY_EXONERATES_OR_PRAISES_JUDAS.

The text itself still ends with Judas participating in the handing over.

### 5. Judas historical innocence remains unresolved

PASS.

Neither Pauline silence nor the Gospel of Judas proves innocence.

Mark remains the earliest explicit named betrayal narrative.

R4 arrest/logistics analysis remains necessary.

### 6. Privileged-disciple pattern is interpretive

NARROW.

The recurrence of privileged named figures is real.

Calling it a "literary technology" is a useful project model, but should remain:

PROJECT_INFERENCE / RECEPTION_ANALYSIS,

not primary-source fact.

### 7. Peter/Mary conflict must not become historical suppression

PASS.

Later literary conflict is real.
First-century Peter-versus-Mary institutional suppression is not established.

### 8. User-bias asymmetry is correctly recognized but needs stronger enforcement

The project has unusually strong prior interest in:
- Judas innocence/frame-up;
- Mary superior understanding;
- Thomas privileged understanding;
- apostolic betrayal/co-option.

Therefore evidence **supporting** those hypotheses should require at least the
same standard as evidence opposing them.

No inference may receive promotion simply because:
- it reverses later orthodoxy;
- it appears in a suppressed/noncanonical text;
- it feels morally or psychologically satisfying.

## Core correction

The high-interest category cannot be:

`PARAMETER_LOOKS_ARBITRARY_TO_MODERN_RESEARCHER`.

That is too weak.

Use at least:

`APPARENTLY_LOW_SEMANTIC_PARAMETER`

and distinguish:

1. `OUTSIDER_ARBITRARY` —
   researcher does not know why the parameter exists;
2. `SOURCE_INTERPRETED` —
   tradition supplies an explicit causal, symbolic, legal, cosmological,
   historical or identity-based reason;
3. `SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_BUT_PRESCRIBED` —
   source prescribes the feature without recoverable explanation;
4. `SOURCE_RECEIVED_NOT_REASONED` —
   source/practitioner explicitly frames the feature as received, revealed or
   inherited rather than derived;
5. `EMPIRICALLY_PARAMETER_SENSITIVE` —
   prospective controlled outcome changes when the parameter is altered.

Only classes 3–5 are strong candidates for the "Luke in Lakeview" question.

Even class 4 is not exotic evidence by itself.

Class 5 is the first class that can materially discriminate mechanisms.

## 1. Human social learning predicts preservation of useless-looking detail

Modern experimental literature makes "why would anyone preserve an irrelevant
step?" a bad argument for hidden external necessity.

### Overimitation

Whiten and colleagues' overimitation literature shows children and adults can
copy visibly causally irrelevant actions.

Relevant examples:

- "From over-imitation to super-copying: adults imitate causally irrelevant
  aspects of tool use with higher fidelity than young children"
  (2011), DOI: `10.1348/000712610X493115`.

- "The Rationality of (Over)imitation" (2018),
  DOI: `10.1177/1745691618794921`.

- "Social Learning in the Real-World: 'Over-Imitation' Occurs in Both Children
  and Adults..." (2016),
  DOI: `10.1371/JOURNAL.PONE.0159920`.

Hostile implication:

`VISIBLE_CAUSAL_IRRELEVANCE`
does not imply
`DETAIL_WILL_BE_DROPPED`.

Humans can preserve unnecessary-looking detail precisely because:
- social convention matters;
- model identity/status matters;
- normative action structure matters;
- uncertainty about hidden causality favors faithful copying;
- cultural affiliation rewards fidelity.

This is an ordinary mechanism for brittle-looking transmission.

## 2. Arbitrary conventions can propagate

"Getting the ritual exactly right" can itself be the social goal.

Examples from cultural-transmission research include:
- redundant action copying;
- diffusion of conventional behavior;
- sequencing as an organizing cultural principle;
- conformist transmission.

Relevant modern sources:

- "When the Transmission of Culture Is Child's Play" (2012),
  DOI: `10.1371/JOURNAL.PONE.0034066`.

- "The role of redundant information in cultural transmission and cultural
  stabilization" (2016), DOI: `10.1037/A0040094`.

- "Event Sequencing as an Organizing Cultural Principle" (2011),
  DOI: `10.1111/J.1548-1352.2011.01193.X`.

Hostile implication:

`EXACT_SEQUENCE`
can be preserved because exact sequence is culturally legible and
transmissible.

No external execution engine is required.

## 3. Causal opacity is not one settled category

The ritual-cognition literature itself disputes how "causally opaque" rituals
really are.

Relevant:
- "Causal Opacity or Causal Translucence?" (2023),
  DOI: `10.1558/jch.23722`;
- "The ritual stance and the precaution system..." (2017),
  DOI: `10.1080/2153599X.2016.1141792`;
- "Ritualized objects..." (2019),
  DOI: `10.1163/15685373-12340053`;
- "When instrumental inference hides behind seemingly arbitrary conventions"
  (2022), DOI: `10.1017/S0140525X22001340`.

Some rituals that look opaque to an outsider may be supported by:
- source-internal causal theories;
- representativeness/similarity heuristics;
- specialist practical knowledge;
- normative convention;
- hidden or distributed expertise.

Therefore:

`RESEARCHER_CANNOT_SEE_THE_REASON`
is not evidence for
`NO_ORDINARY_REASON_EXISTS`.

## 4. Reclassification of current ON_THEO candidates

### Vedic configuration

Current:
`SOURCE_INTERPRETED + PARTLY_SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED`.

Many exact elements receive cosmological/liturgical explanation in the textual
tradition.

Do not relabel them "arbitrary" merely because the explanation is not accepted
as modern physical causation.

Potential high-value residue:
features that remain prescribed across textual layers while their stated
explanation shifts or disappears.

That requires diachronic source work.

### Buddhist Vinaya

Current:
`SOURCE_INTERPRETED / INSTITUTIONAL`.

Quorum, jurisdiction, motion, announcements and objection rules are not
mysterious low-semantic parameters once the act is understood as communal legal
procedure.

This is a negative control.

### Maya / Mexica calendar counts

Current:
`FORMALLY_GENERATED`.

Thirteen x twenty structures, trecenas, ordered day names and festival cycles
are not unexplained arbitrary parameters.

They are components of calendar identity.

The question is whether the calendar predicts an external effect prospectively,
not why a calendar counts days exactly.

### Ifá 16 / 256 sign architecture

Current:
`FORMALLY_GENERATED + HISTORICALLY_CONTACT-SENSITIVE`.

Four-position binary combinatorics generate sixteen figures.

The number sixteen is therefore not a low-semantic independent clue.

A better candidate would be:
- a specific source-secure feature of figure ordering;
- a specific generation rule;
- a substitution prohibition;
- a parameter claimed to change divinatory result beyond its role as an index.

That has not yet been isolated in the current packet.

### Daoist register / petition identity

Current:
`SOURCE_INTERPRETED / INSTITUTIONAL-BUREAUCRATIC`.

Addressing a named office or possessing a register may look like credentials and
routing.

But the source world explicitly models religious reality through bureaucracy.

The apparent systems semantics are not low-semantic within that world.

### Shinto ritual action

Current:
`MIXED`.

Purification, office, ritual speech and calendrical timing have source-level
religious meanings.

Specific micro-actions or directional gestures may become
`SOURCE_UNEXPLAINED_BUT_PRESCRIBED` candidates, but they require an exact
source packet before admission.

### Olmec material geometry

Current:
`SEMANTICS_UNKNOWN`.

Unknown meaning is not the same as arbitrary meaning.

This case must remain:

`UNRESOLVED`.

Do not convert archaeological silence into "unexplained parameter."

## 5. The strongest truly interesting historical pattern

The most valuable source pattern would be:

1. exact form persists across long time;
2. source explanations materially change;
3. practitioners continue to prohibit substitutions;
4. the parameter does not obviously serve:
   - indexing;
   - law/status;
   - mnemonic stability;
   - calendar identity;
   - social coordination;
   - physiological entrainment;
5. mistakes are said to change a non-social effect;
6. the same parameter survives independent source witnesses;
7. future controlled alteration changes that external effect.

Current ON_THEO corpus has partial candidates for steps 1–6.

It has no case establishing step 7.

Therefore no case currently earns:

`EMPIRICALLY_PARAMETER_SENSITIVE_EXTERNAL_EFFECT`.

## 7. Required hostile controls for arbitrary counts

Exact repetition counts are particularly easy to overread.

A count can arise from:
- meter;
- calendar;
- finger/body counting;
- memorability;
- cosmological number symbolism;
- institutional standardization;
- aesthetic symmetry;
- finite combinatorics;
- later numerological reinterpretation.

Before treating `N repetitions` as exotic evidence:

1. prove the count belongs to the earliest relevant source layer;
2. prove it is not a later harmonization;
3. separate numeric symbolism from empirical necessity;
4. identify whether neighboring counts are explicitly invalid;
5. distinguish ideal/manual prescription from practice;
6. test whether changing N changes a prospective external outcome.

Without #6:
`EXACT_COUNT = HISTORICAL_RITUAL_RULE`.

Not:
`EXACT_COUNT = ARCHITECTURAL_PARAMETER`.

## 8. Required hostile controls for exact names / addressing

A fixed name or title can matter because:
- identity is semantically constitutive;
- social address is indexical;
- legal formula names the office/person;
- memorized liturgy preserves tradition;
- taboo and reverence constrain substitution.

The fact that "semantically similar" names are not interchangeable is therefore
not automatically surprising.

A genuine external-interface result would require:

- source says exact form matters beyond referential identity;
- ordinary listeners cannot distinguish target conditions;
- meaning/reference are controlled;
- exact form predicts a prospective external outcome;
- effect survives blinding and replication.

Current evidence:
`NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

## 9. Required hostile controls for sequence

Order sensitivity can result from ordinary causal structure.

Example:
purify -> enter -> offer -> recite

is not evidentially equivalent to an arbitrary permutation because early steps
change the human/social/ritual state for later steps.

To make sequence evidence interesting, compare sequences where:
- all components occur;
- duration is matched;
- physiological load is matched;
- participants expect both sequences to be valid or expectation is controlled;
- source specifically predicts order dependence;
- external endpoint is frozen.

If only the canonical order produces a prospective unknown-information anomaly,
sequence earns evidentiary weight.

Current evidence:
`NOT_TESTED`.

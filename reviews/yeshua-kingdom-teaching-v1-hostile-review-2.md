## Findings

### 1. Mixed early tradition versus mixed historical system

PASS.

The revised packet now explicitly states:

`MIXED_EARLY_TRADITION != COHERENT_MIXED_HISTORICAL_YESHUA_SYSTEM`.

This is the most important correction.

R2 may now say:
- future Kingdom language is strongly present in early tradition;
- present/inbreaking Kingdom language is materially present;
- social/status reversal is probable;
- the surviving early field is multi-register.

R2 may not yet say:
Yeshua himself consciously integrated every register into one systematic
theology.

### 2. Present-inbreaking confidence

PASS.

Present-inbreaking is reduced to:
`MODERATE_TO_STRONG_EARLY_TRADITIONAL`.

The exorcism/Kingdom tradition carries the principal burden.

Mark 4 is now only supporting interpretive pressure.

### 3. Markan controls

PASS.

Mark 1:15 remains an early Markan programmatic summary, not an independent
verbatim saying.

referent.

Mark 14:25 preserves future Kingdom expectation while remaining passion
tradition requiring later stratification.

### 4. Social/status reversal

PASS WITH GUARD.

- exact political program;
- egalitarian social reconstruction;
- revolutionary intent

unresolved.

### 5. Luke 17:20–21

PASS.

The lexical packet remains balanced:

- "among/in your midst" is viable;

- authenticity would not by itself prove centrality;
- centrality would not by itself prove Source/soul metaphysics.

This is sufficiently guarded for downstream use.

### 6. Thomas chronology and source value

PASS.

second-century-or-later composition field with third-century Greek witness to
relevant material, pending tighter composition/source analysis.

- physical witness;
- composition;
- saying tradition;
- independence.

Thomas remains strong evidence for an inward/present Kingdom trajectory in
Christian reception.

It remains unresolved as independent proof of the historical Yeshua.

### 10. Inward Yeshua hypothesis

PASS.

Current status remains:

`HISTORICAL_INWARD_MYSTICAL_CORE = UNRESOLVED`.

This is the correct state.

- present/nonvisible Kingdom language is real;
- an interior translation of Luke 17 remains viable;
- Thomas later makes inward/self-knowledge language explicit;
- Jewish visionary/wisdom environments prevent "mystical" from being dismissed
  as automatically foreign to Judaism.

But the project still lacks enough independent early evidence to make inward
self-knowledge the historical center of Yeshua's teaching.

## Review question

Does the dossier distinguish what the earliest sources actually establish about
Mary Magdalene from later resurrection, revelatory-authority, sexualized,
penitential and anti-Peter reception?

## Findings

### 1. Historical Mary confidence is slightly too high

REVISION REQUIRED.

Mark is the earliest surviving explicit named source and carries most of the
historical burden.

Mary's repeated naming and stable Magdalene identifier make invention less
economical than a historical referent, but R3 should not state
`PROBABLE_TO_STRONG` as though it possessed first-generation Pauline or
external attestation comparable to James/Cephas.

Required ceiling:

HISTORICAL_MARY_MAGDALENE =
PROBABLE.

This is not skepticism toward women as sources. It is source-clock symmetry.

### 2. Markan prominence must remain memory-level

PASS WITH WORDING GUARD.

Mark strongly preserves Mary in:
- crucifixion;
- burial-location;
- tomb
memory, and retrospectively identifies the named women as Galilean
followers/servers.

R3 may infer historical following as probable.

It may not convert Mark's narrative roles into:
- direct eyewitness certification by the historian;
- formal leadership;
- apostolic rank.

### 3. Prostitute claim is correctly rejected as an early-source claim

PASS WITH SEMANTIC NARROWING.

The canonical texts do not identify Mary Magdalene as a prostitute, and Luke's
unnamed sinful woman is not named Mary Magdalene.

Therefore:

PROSTITUTE_IDENTIFICATION_IN_EARLIEST_SOURCES =
NOT_SUPPORTED.

But this does not establish the biographical negative:

MARY_NEVER_ENGAGED_IN_SEX_WORK =
PROVED_FALSE.

The project has no evidence for that positive historical claim either way.

The dossier should phrase the result as:
EARLIEST_SOURCE_PROSTITUTE_IDENTITY_NOT_SUPPORTED,
not as a recovered private biography.

### 4. Later prostitute/sinner conflation

PASS.

Later Western reception materially fuses Mary Magdalene with other female
figures/sinner traditions.

This is strong reception history.

But motive remains separate.

COORDINATED_DISGRACING_CAMPAIGN =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

The transformation could involve:
- exegesis;
- conflation;
- penitential theology;
- gender ideology;
- liturgy;
- institutional transmission;
without one centrally planned suppression event.

### 5. John 20

PASS.

John strongly portrays Mary as individual resurrection witness/messenger.

R3 correctly treats the historicity of the event and priority of the appearance
as unresolved.

Do not infer a smooth trajectory:

MARK_HAS_NO_NARRATED_APPEARANCE
->
MATTHEW_DEVELOPS
->
JOHN_INDIVIDUALIZES

as though surviving literary order proves the exact history of tradition.

It is only an extant-source sequence unless dependence/development is
demonstrated.

### 6. Gospel of Mary protagonist identity

REVISION REQUIRED.

The dossier is admirably cautious but still sometimes shifts from
"Mary, probably Magdalene" to unqualified "Mary Magdalene" when discussing the
work's authority conflict.

Required formulation:

GOSPEL_OF_MARY_MARY =
PROBABLY_MAGDALENE_IN_DOMINANT_SCHOLARLY_IDENTIFICATION,
NOT_EXPLICITLY_SECURE_ENOUGH_FOR_UNQUALIFIED_HISTORICAL_EQUIVALENCE.

Consequently:

GOSPEL_OF_MARY_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT =
TEXTUALLY_SECURE_FOR_THE_WORK'S_MARY.

MAGDALENE_SPECIFIC_RECEPTION =
PROBABLE_IF_IDENTIFICATION_ACCEPTED.

### 7. Peter–Mary conflict

PASS.

The literary conflict is secure at the Gospel-of-Mary text level.

Historical Peter–Magdalene conflict remains:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

Repeated later Peter/Mary conflict motifs may eventually support an
authority-discourse trajectory.

They cannot be pooled into a first-century quarrel without source genealogy.

### 8. Historical private revelation

PASS.

Later private-revelation scenes do not establish a historical secret-teaching
session between Yeshua and Mary.

This remains:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

### 9. Superior understanding

PASS.

The evidence can rank literary portrayals.

It cannot currently rank the historical disciples' actual comprehension.

Therefore:

MARY_UNDERSTOOD_YESHUA_BETTER_THAN_PETER =
NOT_CURRENTLY_RANKABLE.

### 10. Spouse / sexual partner

PASS.

No admitted early source establishes marriage or sexual partnership.

Later companion/intimacy language must be text-specific.

### 11. "Suppression" needs proposition decomposition

PASS WITH GUARD.

Three propositions remain distinct:

A. MARY_MEMORY_CHANGED_IN_LATER_RECEPTION = STRONG.
B. SOME_LATER_TEXTS_STAGE_GENDERED_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT = STRONG_SOURCE_SPECIFIC.
C. CENTRALIZED_CHURCH_INVENTED_PROSTITUTE_MARY_TO_ERASE_HISTORICAL_LEADER =
   NOT_ESTABLISHED.

A+B do not automatically prove C.

### 12. Project-bias warning

The project begins with a prior that Mary may have been slandered because she
understood Yeshua unusually well.

That hypothesis remains admissible only as a question.

The current evidence supports a narrower statement:

MARY_MAGDALENE_WAS_NOT_IDENTIFIED_AS_A_PROSTITUTE_BY_THE_EARLIEST_EXPLICIT_SOURCES,
AND_HER_LATER_RECEPTION_WAS_MATERIALLY_TRANSFORMED.

It does not yet establish why that transformation occurred or whether it erased
a superior historical authority.

## Required revisions

1. Demote historical Mary from `PROBABLE_TO_STRONG` to `PROBABLE`.
2. Rephrase prostitute conclusion as an early-source identification claim, not
   a proved negative private biography.
3. Explicitly distinguish Markan narrative/tradition prominence from direct
   eyewitness proof.
4. Qualify Gospel-of-Mary authority claims as applying to the work's "Mary";
   Magdalene identification remains probable but not automatic.
5. State that canonical extant-source order does not by itself demonstrate a
   smooth development trajectory.
6. Preserve coordinated suppression as NOT_ESTABLISHED.
7. Preserve historical Peter/Mary conflict, private revelation, spouse status
   and superior-understanding ranking as unresolved/unestablished.

This is not an independent review.

## Review question

Does the dossier preserve the difference between:
- Paul's first-generation Cephas;
- Mark's literary Peter;
- a possible historical denial core;
- later source-specific restoration;
- speculative hidden-betrayal/rehabilitation theories?

## Findings

### 1. Historical Cephas and early authority

PASS.

Paul's personal-contact testimony makes historical Cephas and major
first-generation authority unusually strong.

This creates a powerful chronological guard:

LATER_GOSPELS_DID_NOT_CREATE_CEPHAS_AS_AN_AUTHORITY_FIGURE_FROM_NOTHING.

Any rehabilitation model must begin with an already prominent pre-Gospel
leader.

### 2. Cephas/Peter identification

PASS WITH GUARD.

The conventional one-person identification remains the strongest current
historical reconstruction.

The dossier correctly keeps the identification explicit rather than making it
an invisible premise.

### 3. Antioch conflict

PASS.

Galatians supplies first-person participant testimony that Paul publicly opposed
Cephas.

But Paul is an interested participant, so:
- Paul's judgment that Cephas was blameworthy is Paul's judgment;
- Cephas's own explanation does not survive directly.

The event/conflict is stronger than Paul's interpretation of every motive.

### 4. Markan prominence + misunderstanding

PASS.

Mark's Peter is textually both prominent and deeply fallible.

This alone is enough to reject a crude claim that the earliest Gospel simply
whitewashes Peter.

### 5. Historical denial confidence needs an explicit source-thickness guard

REVISION REQUIRED.

`HISTORICAL_PETER_DENIAL = PLAUSIBLE` can remain.

But Mark is the earliest surviving explicit narrative source and later canonical
versions cannot simply be counted as independent witnesses.

The matrix must state:

HISTORICAL_DENIAL =
PLAUSIBLE_WITH_SINGLE_EARLY_EXPLICIT_NARRATIVE_SOURCE_GUARD.

The exact threefold choreography remains unestablished.

### 6. P0 "low assumption" wording is too favorable

REVISION REQUIRED.

A historical failure remembered despite later authority is certainly plausible.

But calling it `LOW_ASSUMPTION` risks quietly ranking it above literary or
tradition-shaping alternatives without a discriminator.

Required state:

P0_HISTORICAL_FAILURE_WITH_AUTHORITY =
PLAUSIBLE_NOT_YET_SECURE.

P1 didactic/representative shaping =
TEXTUALLY_STRONG_AS_FUNCTION,
HISTORICAL_CORE_UNRESOLVED.

P4 full literary construction =
LIVE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.

R3 does not need to choose among these yet.

### 7. Mark 16:7

PASS.

At text level, "the disciples and Peter" creates genuine post-failure
reinclusion/restoration pressure.

But "reinclusion signal" is the correct ceiling.

It is not:
- a historical restoration event;
- proof of a Petrine office;
- proof that Mark's community accepted one later institutional hierarchy.

### 8. "Rehabilitation" terminology needs decomposition

REVISION REQUIRED.

The word can refer to several distinct claims:

NARRATIVE_REINCLUSION =
already present in Mark.

LITERARY_RESTORATION =
strong in later canonical portrayals, source-specific.

INSTITUTIONAL_PETRINE_AUTHORITY =
already exists in Paul and later develops in diverse ways.

CONSPIRATORIAL_REHABILITATION_OF_A_DISGRACED_TRAITOR =
not established.

These must not be treated as one trajectory.

### 9. Matthew / Luke-Acts / John

PASS WITH SOURCE-SPECIFIC GUARD.

Each develops Peter differently.

The dossier may compare:
- Matthean authority + failure;
- Lukan special prayer/leadership;
- Johannine denial/restoration/Beloved-Disciple comparison.

It should not imply one smooth canonical rehabilitation sequence merely from
extant order.

### 10. Hidden worse betrayal

PASS.

No positive evidence currently supports:
- Peter as the true handover agent;
- Peter as Roman collaborator;
- Peter as Judas-framer;
- denial as a softened memory of treachery.

The hypothesis remains speculative.

### 11. Relationship to Judas

PASS.

The Judas dossier and Peter dossier are independent constraints.

Peter's later authority does not exonerate Judas.

Judas's uncertain motive/function does not incriminate Peter.

### 12. Peter/Mary conflict

PASS.

Later Peter/Mary authority conflicts are reception evidence.

They do not establish first-generation interpersonal conflict without an older
source genealogy.

### 13. Historical understanding ranking

PASS.

Prominence, failure, authority and later office do not measure how accurately
Peter understood Yeshua.

The ranking remains unavailable.

## Required revisions

1. Add the single-early-explicit-source guard to historical denial.
2. Demote P0 from `PLAUSIBLE_LOW_ASSUMPTION` to
   `PLAUSIBLE_NOT_YET_SECURE`.
3. Decompose "rehabilitation" into:
   - MARKAN_NARRATIVE_REINCLUSION;
   - LATER_SOURCE_SPECIFIC_LITERARY_RESTORATION;
   - HISTORICAL/INSTITUTIONAL_AUTHORITY;
   - CONSPIRATORIAL_REHABILITATION.
4. State explicitly that later canonical agreement on denial does not equal
   independent multiple attestation without dependence analysis.
5. State explicitly that extant Matthew/Luke/John development does not prove
   one smooth rehabilitation history.

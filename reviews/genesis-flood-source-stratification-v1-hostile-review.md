## Review question

Does source stratification improve the Mesopotamian comparison, or does it create
false precision by assigning verses to hypothetical documents too confidently?

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER**
>
> The source-stratification layer is useful, but it has one obvious danger:
> turning a contested compositional model into a second set of "facts."
>
> The present Genesis text really does contain fractures: animal-number
> instructions differ, flood chronologies differ, cosmic-water and rain
> emphases differ, and sacrifice/covenant material has distinct profiles.
>
> Those observations are textual.
>
> Assigning them to "P" and "non-P" is scholarly reconstruction.
>
> Carr's model is strong and current, but it is still one model among competing
> redactional/source-critical accounts. Therefore the packet may use P/non-P as
> a controlled analytical framework but must not say the ancient text physically
> survives as two labeled documents.
>
> The more interesting result survives regardless of exact verse boundaries:
> **different compositional strata within Genesis contain different clusters of
> Mesopotamian flood parallels.**
>
> The non-Priestly cluster is especially close in:
> - bird testing;
> - sacrifice;
> - divine smell/response;
> - no-repeat resolve.
>
> The Priestly cluster contains strong parallels in:
> - detailed vessel construction;
> - ordered preservation of living beings;
> - cosmic flood architecture;
> - vessel resting;
> - post-flood world regulation.
>
> That makes a one-time "Genesis copied Gilgamesh" model less adequate than a
> layered reception model.
>
> But do not overstate the next step. "Both layers have Mesopotamian parallels"
> does not prove that both independently accessed Mesopotamian texts. P may have
> inherited Mesopotamian-shaped material through the earlier biblical flood
> tradition while also drawing on broader cultural knowledge.
>
> So the strongest claim is:
>
> `BOTH_MAJOR_BIBLICAL_FLOOD_STRATA_SHOW_MESOPOTAMIAN_TRADITION_RELATIONSHIPS`
>
> not:
>
> `BOTH_AUTHORS_INDEPENDENTLY_READ_ATRAHASIS_OR_GILGAMESH`.
>
> Finally, the final literary Noah can reasonably be called composite under a
> layered model, but "composite" means compositional, not fictional in the
> historical sense. Source criticism alone cannot determine whether a historical
> person stands behind the literary figure.

## RESEARCH RESPONSE

Accepted.

Current ceiling:

- Genesis 6–9 contains strong textual indications of compositional layering.
- P/non-P is a major analytical model, not a physical-manuscript fact.
- Both major reconstructed strata contain meaningful Mesopotamian flood
  parallels.
- This supports layered biblical reception/reworking of older flood tradition.
- It does not establish independent direct Mesopotamian source access for each
  stratum.
- The final Noah is compositionally composite under this model.
- Source criticism does not decide whether any historical person lies behind
  the literary hero.

## Challenge 1 — Did the test mistake a ritual calendar for bad science?

Yes, this was the largest overstatement risk.

The 364-day year can function as an ideal, schematic, sabbatical/liturgical calendar. Its divergence from the tropical year therefore does not prove the authors were incapable of observational astronomy.

Repair:
- do not call the 364-day scheme simply an "error";
- use it only against claims that Enoch preserves unusually accurate external scientific knowledge;
- preserve `SOPHISTICATED_ANCIENT_ASTRONOMY`.

## Challenge 2 — Does known ancient technology disprove ancient external teaching?

No.

An external-teaching hypothesis could always claim that metallurgy, astronomy, cosmetics, or medicine entered human culture long before the Enochic authors wrote.

But that move removes evidential force from the Enochic list unless independent chronology supports the alleged primordial transmission.

Repair:
- classify the list as `NO POSITIVE ANOMALOUS INFORMATION`, not metaphysical disproof;
- archaeological precedence demonstrates that the authors had ordinary cultural access to the named arts.

## Challenge 3 — Is apkallu -> Watchers dependence settled fact?

No.

It is a significant scholarly model, strongly argued by Amar Annus and related work, but exact dependence, pathways, and redactional history remain scholarly reconstruction.

Repair:
- use it as a strong historical competitor;
- do not label exact literary descent as established without a dedicated dependence packet.

## Challenge 4 — Does compositional development disprove revelation?

No.

Composite development disproves describing the extant corpus as one stable primordial technical report.

It cannot rule out:
- visionary experience;
- perceived revelation;
- extraordinary experience behind an individual layer.

Repair:
maintain the distinction.

## Challenge 5 — Did the test treat lack of simulation vocabulary as evidence?

No.

The test correctly avoids this. Ancient authors would necessarily describe unusual experiences through ancient conceptual resources.

The evidential question is instead:
- anomalous information;
- independent corroboration;
- predictive specificity;
- explanatory advantage.

## Challenge 6 — Is the simulation hypothesis falsifiable as currently stated?

Bare metaphysical simulation is not readily falsifiable from an ancient text.

A narrower claim is testable:
`ENOCH_PRESERVES_POSITIVE_INFORMATION_BEST_EXPLAINED_BY_EXTERNAL_SYSTEM ACCESS`.

That narrower claim currently fails to gain support.

## Review question

Did the revised dossier successfully preserve the distinction between:
- kinship;
- authority;
- causal contribution of kinship;
- later family-authority memory;
- informal succession;
- formal hereditary dynasty?

## Findings

### 1. Historical James

PASS.

Paul's personal-contact testimony remains the strongest early anchor.
The historical existence of James as a first-generation figure remains
VERY_STRONG.

### 2. Familial relation

PASS WITH GUARD.

"Brother of the Lord" is still best read as ordinary familial kinship in the
current evidence configuration, reinforced by Markan family memory and
Josephus if Antiquities 20.200 is substantially authentic.

The dossier correctly refuses to claim an exact full/half/step-brother
biological configuration.

### 3. Early Jerusalem authority

PASS.

Galatians 2:9 and the social weight of James's name in Galatians 2:12 support
major early authority.

The dossier does not convert:
- list order;
- Acts's later portrayal;
- Thomas 12;
into proof of supreme or monarchical leadership.

### 4. "From James"

PASS.

The source supports network/social influence.

It does not establish:
- direct personal order;
- full agreement with the visitors;
- categorical opposition to Paul's Gentile mission.

### 5. Appearance tradition

PASS.

1 Corinthians 15:7 strongly supports an early James appearance tradition.

The dossier correctly separates:
- early tradition;
- event mechanism;
- causal explanation of later authority.

### 6. Josephus

PASS WITH GUARD.

Antiquities 20.200 remains:
STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_NOT_UNANIMOUS.

The passage is not made to carry claims about James's theology, office, or
succession.

### 7. Kinship prestige

PASS.

The prior overreach is corrected.

Current state:

KINSHIP_PRESTIGE_CONTRIBUTION =
PLAUSIBLE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.

This is the appropriate ceiling because the first-generation evidence gives us
both kinship and authority but not the causal bridge between them.

### 8. Family-authority trajectory

PASS.

The revised ladder is now source-disciplined:

FAMILY_AUTHORITY_MEMORY =
LATER_SOURCE_BOUND.

REPEATED_KIN_LEADERSHIP_TRADITION =
LATER_SOURCE_BOUND.

INFORMAL_SUCCESSION_HYPOTHESIS =
POSSIBLE_NOT_ESTABLISHED.

FORMAL_HEREDITARY_DYNASTY =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

This prevents multiple later sources from being pooled into a first-generation
succession event.

### 9. Thomas 12

PASS.

Thomas 12 is strong evidence for later James authority reception.

It is not direct evidence that historical Yeshua appointed James.

### 10. Letter of James

PASS.

Authorship remains contested and the letter is not used as unmediated
first-person biography of Yeshua's brother.

### 11. Understanding ranking

PASS.

Authority, kinship, and appearance tradition do not measure who understood
Yeshua "best."

That comparison remains:
NOT_CURRENTLY_RANKABLE.

## Residual vulnerabilities

1. "Ordinary brotherhood" is still a historical semantic judgment rather than
   a genetically demonstrated relation.

2. Josephus remains load-bearing for external kinship/execution control and is
   not literally uncontested.

3. James's exact role in the Antioch dispute remains underdetermined.

4. Later family-leadership traditions may preserve genuine first-generation
   continuity, but the current source chain does not yet demonstrate it.

5. The project should resist a counter-bias in which later Peter/Paul prominence
   causes James's early importance to be minimized.

## Current claim ceiling

The strongest bounded reconstruction is:

JAMES_WAS_A_VERY_STRONGLY_ATTESTED_FIRST_GENERATION_FIGURE_AND_CLOSE_FAMILY_MEMBER_OF_YESHUA_WITH_ORDINARY_BROTHERHOOD_THE_STRONGEST_CURRENT_READING.

JAMES_WAS_A_MAJOR_EARLY_JERUSALEM_AUTHORITY_AND_AN_EARLY_APPEARANCE_TRADITION_WAS_ATTACHED_TO_HIM.

THE_CAUSAL_ROLE_OF_KINSHIP_IN_HIS_AUTHORITY_IS_PLAUSIBLE_BUT_NOT_ESTABLISHED.

LATER_SOURCES_AMPLIFY_JAMES_AND_YESHUA_FAMILY_AUTHORITY_BUT_DO_NOT_ESTABLISH_A_FORMAL_FIRST_GENERATION_APPOINTMENT_OR_HEREDITARY_DYNASTY.

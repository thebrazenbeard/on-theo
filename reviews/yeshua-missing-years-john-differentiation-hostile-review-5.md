> **Hostile reviewer:** You call eschatological continuity "plausible/strong." Strong relative to what? Josephus' John passage does not independently attest the Christian eschatological profile, and Christian sources have obvious reasons to align John with Yeshua.

**Response:** Sustained. Downgrade to:
PLAUSIBLE / SOURCE-DEPENDENT.
The Christian John traditions can support continuity, but not independent external confirmation of its exact form.

> **Hostile reviewer:** Are you using the embarrassment criterion as proof of Yeshua's baptism?

**Response:** No criterion may carry the claim alone. The baptism remains probable because it is deeply embedded in early Gospel tradition and later redaction visibly negotiates John's precedence; "embarrassment" is only one supporting observation and must not be treated as a mechanical authenticity test.

> **Hostile reviewer:** Does the fasting/eating contrast prove Yeshua personally rejected asceticism?

**Response:** No. The packet already limits this to remembered public style and group practice.

> **Hostile reviewer:** Does John-to-Yeshua differentiation imply that Yeshua consciously broke with his teacher?

**Response:** No. Formal discipleship and deliberate break are both unresolved. Differentiation is a comparison of source profiles.

> **Hostile reviewer:** Does the Fourth Gospel prove overlapping baptismal ministries?

**Response:** No. It raises a plausible historical-memory hypothesis at a later-source ceiling.

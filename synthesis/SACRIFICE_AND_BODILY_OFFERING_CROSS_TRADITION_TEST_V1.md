- structured burial.

## 3. Shared axis

Both traditions support the neutral question:

HOW_DOES_BODILY_SACRIFICE_FUNCTION_WITHIN_RITUAL_ORDER?

But the mechanisms are not collapsed.

Mexica current evidence emphasizes:
- central sacred precinct;
- public/state ritual domain;
- war/captive context in selected evidence.

Inca capacocha emphasizes:
- selected children;
- movement across imperial landscape;
- precious offerings;
- mountain/provincial huacas;
- sacrificial burial.

Therefore:

SHARED_HUMAN_SACRIFICE_CATEGORY != SHARED_RITUAL_MECHANISM.

## 4. Why generic offering was insufficient

A human body/life differs analytically from:
- jade;

- food;
- plant offerings;
- ordinary deposited objects.

The bodily axis tracks:
- bodily subject;
- mode of bodily action;
- living/dead status;
- coercion/selection where recoverable;
- political role;
- recipient/context;
- mortuary outcome.

This does not imply modern moral equivalence or historical sameness.

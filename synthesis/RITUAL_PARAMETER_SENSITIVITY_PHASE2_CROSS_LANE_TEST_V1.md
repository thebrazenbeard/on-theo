## 1. What survived expansion

The strongest cross-lane result is no longer:

`RITUALS_USE_EXACT_SOUND`.

It is:

`RELIGIOUS_SYSTEMS_CAN_TREAT_FORMAL_CONFIGURATION_AS_NON_INTERCHANGEABLE`.

The formal configuration can include different combinations of:

- acoustic form;
- written form;
- named recipient;
- operator authorization;
- body mapping;
- sequence;
- repetition;
- timing;
- spatial boundary;
- material carrier;
- practitioner state;
- lineage;
- correction or repair logic.

This is a historical-comparative result.

It is not evidence that one hidden architecture parses those forms.

## 2. One universal sacred language is now strongly disfavored

Phase 2 adds several direct counterexamples.

### Vedic
Accent can matter, but recitational mode is context-, role-, school-, and
rite-sensitive.

### Islamic
Multiple authorized qirāʾāt coexist, and later dhikr can be silent or vocal.

### Buddhist dhāraṇī
The ritual form can be recited, written, worn, or monumentally installed.

### Hindu Tantra
Mantra can involve phonemic form, initiation, body placement, written/oral form,
and school-specific theology.

### Daoism
Talismanic and diagrammatic written form can be primary.

Therefore:

`ONE_ACOUSTIC_CODE = REJECTED_AS_CROSS_TRADITION_MODEL`.

A broader `FORM_IDENTITY` variable survives.

## 3. The strongest new variable is MEDIUM

PR #131 and PR #136 materially change the matrix.

A ritual form can be instantiated through:

- voice;
- script;
- amulet;
- diagram;
- body placement;
- monument;
- petition/document;
- ritual object.

Therefore a source-sensitive model needs:

`FORM_IDENTITY`
and
`FORM_BEARING_MEDIUM`

as separate fields.

The same identity can sometimes cross medium.

That does not establish medium-independence empirically.

## 4. Low ordinary semantics is real, but “meaningless” is too strong

PR #135 and PR #136 supply the strongest cases.

### Tantric mantra
Bīja and compact mantra forms can have low ordinary propositional semantic load
while carrying deity, ritual, cosmological, or phonemic identity.

### Daoist talisman
Selected forms can have low ordinary legibility while carrying ritual authority
and source-claimed efficacy.

### Jewish adjuration
Unusual divine/angelic name strings can be opaque to modern interpreters.

Therefore admit:

`LOW_ORDINARY_PROPOSITIONAL_OR_LEXICAL_ACCESS`.

Do not admit:

`MEANINGLESS_FORM`.

Guards:

`OPAQUE_TO_ANALYST != OPAQUE_TO_PRACTITIONER`.

`LOW_PROPOSITIONAL_SEMANTICS != NO_RITUAL_SEMANTICS`.

## 5. Authorization recurs, but not as one mechanism

Authorization appears in:

- Vedic specialist roles;
- Buddhist monastic legal acts;
- Jewish adjurational preparation/specialist knowledge;
- priestly Israelite office;
- Qurʾanic recitational lineages;
- Sufi orders;
- Tantric initiation;
- Daoist ranks/registers/transmission.

This supports:

`OPERATOR_QUALIFICATION = RECURRENT_PROBLEM_SPACE`.

It does not establish:

`ONE_COSMIC_PERMISSION_SYSTEM`.

Ordinary institutional and pedagogical explanations remain strong.

## 6. Error sensitivity is real but non-deterministic

Phase 2 kills a naive brittle-machine model.

### Vedic
Misaccent/error traditions exist, but acoustic form can be linguistically
meaningful and context-specific.

### Priestly Israel
Unauthorized action can be catastrophic in one narrative, while another
procedural deviation is contextually defended and accepted.

### Tantric mantra
Sources preserve error/impurity and perfection/correction concepts.

### Daoist talisman
Manuals define conditions of efficacy, but efficacy theories themselves differ.

Therefore:

`ERROR_SENSITIVITY = BOUNDED_RECURRENT`.

But:

`ANY_ERROR -> AUTOMATIC_FAILURE`
is not a defensible cross-tradition invariant.

A better field is:

`ERROR_TOLERANCE_AND_REPAIR_LOGIC`.

## 7. Claimed information channels remain rare and high-value

The strongest source-level information-channel candidates remain:

### Jewish revelatory adjuration
Selected sources explicitly seek answers, mysteries, hidden knowledge, or
heavenly response.

### Sanhuang / Daoist talismanic material
Selected traditions claim divinatory or deity-contact outcomes.

### Ifá / Maya / Mexica
Formal divinatory systems already exist in PR #128, but prospective anomalous
information is not established.

These are methodologically more relevant to hidden-target testing than rites
whose outcome is only institutional status.

Still:

`TEXTUALLY_CLAIMED_INFORMATION_CHANNEL != VERIFIED_INFORMATION_CHANNEL`.

## 8. Contact and transmission reduce apparent independence

The new packets strengthen historical contact controls.

- Jewish and Greco-Egyptian adjuration share a late-antique ritual environment.
- Hindu and Buddhist Tantric traditions interacted historically.
- Daoist systems repeatedly combine internal and Buddhist/local ritual elements.
- Islamic qirāʾāt are explicitly transmitted lineages rather than independent
  rediscoveries.

Therefore a cross-cultural recurrence cannot be counted as independent evidence
without a transmission analysis.

## 9. The deeper-interface hypothesis has become more demanding

A simple model:

`EXACT_WORD -> HIDDEN_SYSTEM_RESPONSE`

no longer fits the historical data well.

Any surviving speculative model would have to accommodate:

- multiple media;
- bounded local variants;
- different authorization systems;
- school-specific forms;
- internally plural causal theories;
- context-dependent error tolerance;
- historical recombination.

A more flexible speculative abstraction is:

`FORMAL_CONFIGURATION + QUALIFIED_CONTEXT -> CLAIMED_EFFECT`.

But this flexibility creates a major falsifiability problem.

The broader the abstraction becomes, the easier it is to explain everything
after the fact.

Therefore the next experimental phase must become narrower, not broader.

## 10. Hostile compression

> If every religion can use a different sound, different symbol, different
> medium, different body state, different authorized operator, and different
> failure rule, then “they are all accessing the same architecture” has almost
> no predictive content.
>
> The historical pattern survives.
>
> The universal mechanism does not.
>
> To rescue the deeper-interface hypothesis scientifically, a source must predict
> a parameter before the experiment, and that parameter must beat a matched
> control on a prospectively scored external outcome.

This is the current hostile standard.

## 11. Highest-information surviving candidate classes

### A. Low-propositional formal identity
Examples:
- selected Tantric bīja/mantra forms;
- selected Daoist talismanic forms.

Why useful:
ordinary lexical meaning can be partially separated from formal identity.

Main confound:
ritual/theological semantics remain dense.

### B. Claimed revelatory information
Examples:
- selected Jewish adjurations;
- selected Sanhuang divinatory claims.

Why useful:
the claimed output can potentially be operationalized as information.

Main confound:
textual testimony and post-hoc interpretation.

### C. Authorized variant families
Example:
- qirāʾāt.

Why useful:
tests whether “correctness” means one string or a bounded family.

Main result:
one-string models fail historically.

### D. Cross-medium identity
Example:
- Buddhist dhāraṇī.

Why useful:
tests whether ritual identity attaches to sound, writing, object, or broader
form.

Main confound:
different media may have different traditional effects.

## 12. Experimental consequence

Do not build one giant ritual.

That would maximize researcher degrees of freedom.

Instead select one exact source-defined contrast.

A strong future test should have:

- one historical source family;
- one parameter distinction;
- one primary external endpoint;
- one preregistered contrast;
- matched ordinary controls;
- frozen scoring;
- independent target custody;
- no post-hoc parameter repair.

A null result weakens that candidate.

It does not justify adding a second tradition, extra symbol, new body state, or
different timing after outcome inspection.

## 13. What would now kill the broad hypothesis

The broad interface hypothesis should lose support if repeated source-defined
candidate tests show:

- authentic form equals matched form;
- parameter ablation does not matter;
- effects are confined to subjective/physiological state;
- claimed information outcomes do not beat chance;
- apparent effects vanish under blinding;
- effects vanish in fresh holdouts;
- independent replication fails;
- source-specific parameter predictions fail more often than generic ritual
  controls.

The hypothesis is not allowed to survive indefinitely by becoming more vague.

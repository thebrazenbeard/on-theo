## 5. Political-sacral diversity

The six breadth lanes already prevent one sacred-politics model.

- Maya/Yaxchilan: sacral dynastic court/ruler ritual.
- Mexica/Tenochtitlan: imperial sacred urban center/state ritual.
- Olmec: partial elite monumental-ritual entanglement; divine kingship not
  established.
- Diné: current sovereign law grounded in traditional/customary/natural/common
  law; not a precolonial reconstruction.
- Haudenosaunee: distributed Great-Law Confederacy order across nations, Clan
  Mothers, Chiefs, Faithkeepers, Grand Council.
- Lakota: NOT_ADMITTED for current political-sacralization axis.

Result:

SACRED_POLITICAL_ORDER_HAS_MULTIPLE_INSTITUTIONAL_ARCHITECTURES.

Not:

ONE_SACRED_KINGSHIP_MODEL.

This independently reinforces the prior political-sacralization test.

# Comparison eligibility gate V1

Status: ACTIVE METHODOLOGY CONTROL / NON-CANONICAL

This control exists to prevent `synthesis/commonality` from flattening internally different traditions into a single perennialist pattern merely because a high-level analogy can be drawn.

The existing synthesis lane already has strong anti-harmonization and epistemic cautions. This gate adds an explicit admission rule for new comparative claims.

## Core rule

`STRUCTURAL_SIMILARITY`
does not imply
`HISTORICAL_LINEAGE`,
`SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE`,
`SHARED_METAPHYSICS`,
or
`COMMON_EXTERNAL_SOURCE`.

A tradition may be included in a comparative synthesis only at the level actually supported by its internally reconstructed source lane.

## Required gates before a comparative claim

### G1 — exact source subject

The source tradition must be pinned to an exact branch head or immutable source subject.

A mutable branch name alone is orientation, not sufficient provenance.

### G2 — internal reconstruction first

The tradition must first be represented on its own terms, including where relevant:

- chronology;
- source/witness strata;
- internal plurality;
- ritual/practice/material evidence;
- concept semantics;
- later reception.

Comparison cannot substitute for missing internal reconstruction.

### G3 — source-layer control

Primary text, material evidence, later tradition, modern reconstruction, and scholarly interpretation must remain distinct.

A late witness may be compared as a late witness without being back-projected into an earlier period.

### G4 — comparison-unit type

Every comparison should identify what kind of thing is being compared, such as:

- lexeme;
- formula;
- narrative sequence;
- ritual;
- institution;
- role/title;
- proposition;
- ontology;
- ethical pattern;
- social function;
- broad structural analogy.

A broad structural analogy cannot silently stand in for lexical or source-level dependence.

### G5 — relationship hypothesis

At least one provisional relationship class must be stated:

- `DIRECT_DEPENDENCE_POSSIBLE`
- `SHARED_TRADITION`
- `POLEMICAL_DEPENDENCE`
- `STRUCTURAL_PARALLEL`
- `PROJECT_ANALOGY`
- `EXTERNAL_COMMON_SOURCE_HYPOTHESIS`
- `UNRESOLVED`

These are competing or overlapping hypotheses, not a ladder that automatically ends in a common source.

### G6 — transmission ceiling

Any historical transmission claim stronger than structural parallel requires evidence proportionate to the claim.

For direct or directional transmission, require at minimum:

`chronology + contact + carrier/access + semantic fit + source-specific signal + direction evidence`.

Motif resemblance plus chronological possibility is not enough.

### G7 — negative-search scope

Failure to find a parallel in one corpus/search surface is only a bounded negative result.

`NO_HIT_IN_CURRENT_SEARCH`
does not imply
`HISTORICAL_ABSENCE`.

### G8 — divergence preservation

A comparison must record material disagreements, not only overlaps.

If a synthesis becomes cleaner by deleting distinctions that matter to the source traditions, the synthesis has become too lossy.

### G9 — evidence pointer

A synthesis row or proposition should point back to the exact source-lane file, claim, witness, or registry record that supports it.

Unbound prose is navigation, not high-confidence comparative evidence.

## Current lane status

The existing Judaism, Christianity, and Islam inputs are retained as the original synthesis inputs, now exact-head locked in `input-lock-v1.yaml`.

Sumerian, Buddhist, Hindu, Shinto, Daoist/Taoist, and Norse/Ásatrú master lanes are recorded as available project inputs but are **not automatically admitted** into the commonality matrix merely because those branches now exist.

Each requires its own comparison-readiness audit against the gates above.

## Claim ceiling

This gate does not determine whether any religion is true, false, derivative, convergent, or secretly describing one metaphysical substrate.

It controls only what kinds of comparative statements the current project evidence can responsibly support.

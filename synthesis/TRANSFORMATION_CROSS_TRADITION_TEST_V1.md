# Transformation Cross-Tradition Test V1

Status: FOUR-LANE DECOMPOSED COMPARISON / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

The current TRANSFORMATION_MODEL mixes several different kinds of change.

This test separates:
- form/perspective and life-death process;
- posthumous cult-status transformation;
- world-scale destruction/renewal;
- divine narrative form/state change and substitution/alternation.

Admitted lanes:
- Daoism;
- Shinto;
- Norse;
- Sumerian.

## Cross-lane result

The word transformation covers four different source structures:

1. Daoist processual transformation of form/perspective/life-death.
2. Shinto historical posthumous status transformation.
3. Norse world-order destruction and renewal.
4. Sumerian divine narrative form-change/substitution/alternation.

Therefore:

SHARED_TRANSFORMATION_VOCABULARY != SHARED_TRANSFORMATION_MECHANISM.

The four lanes do not support a single metaphysical proposition such as:
- everything reincarnates;
- identities always persist;
- identities never persist;
- death is always reversible;
- worlds necessarily recur;
- transformation implies resurrection.

## Identity continuity test

Identity continuity must be asked separately from transformation.

- Daoist loci destabilize fixed identity categories and emphasize process.
- Shinto Michizane remains a named historical identity across changing cult
  status.
- Norse renewal preserves some figures/order across world-scale crisis but is
  not an individual identity theory.
- Sumerian Dumuzid remains narratively identifiable across form-change,
  capture, substitution, and alternation.

Therefore:

TRANSFORMATION != LOSS_OF_IDENTITY
and
TRANSFORMATION != PERSISTENCE_OF_AN_UNCHANGED_ESSENCE.

Both questions remain source-specific.

## Reversibility test

- Daoist process: not reduced to a simple reversible/irreversible binary.
- Shinto cult-status history: historically cumulative/reclassificatory, not a
  reversible physical process.
- Norse world renewal: destruction followed by renewed order; simple recurrence
  is not established.
- Sumerian: form-change can be temporary and half-year status alternates, but
  release is not automatically resurrection.

Therefore:

RETURN_OR_RENEWAL != RESURRECTION_BY_DEFAULT.

## Systems-model boundary

State-transition analogies may be useful downstream, but they remain
PROJECT_ANALOGY or SPECULATIVE_MODEL.

A source describing transformation does not thereby describe:
- software state;
- simulation;
- substrate migration;
- information persistence.

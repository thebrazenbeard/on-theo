## 1. Haudenosaunee wampum

Public evidence supports wampum as a material-documentary practice for:
- treaties;
- titles;
- messages;
- historical memory;
- ceremony.

Meaning is stabilized through:
- material form;
- office;
- oral interpretation;
- repeated diplomatic/social use.

WAMPUM != ALPHABETIC_TEXT.

## 2. Inca khipu

Khipu securely support:
- numerical records;
- administration;
- specialist khipukamayuq;
- material information coding.

Colonial sources also claim broader historical-memory uses, while surviving
khipu remain only partially semantically recoverable.

KHIPU != FULLY_DECIPHERED_ALPHABETIC_TEXT.

## 3. Shared axis

Both cases falsify:

DOCUMENTARY_MEMORY = WRITTEN_PROSE.

The comparison instead asks:

HOW_IS_AUTHORITATIVE_MEMORY_STABILIZED_AND_RETRIEVED?

Haudenosaunee mechanism:
MATERIAL_WAMPUM + ORAL_INTERPRETATION + OFFICE + RENEWAL.

Inca mechanism:
CORD_KNOT_STRUCTURE + SPECIALIST_READING + ADMINISTRATIVE_OR_ORAL_CONTEXT.

## 4. Semantic recovery differs

Wampum current living interpretation can remain institutionally available even
when an external written-document claim is disputed.

Khipu material structures survive, but much nonnumeric semantic interpretation
is no longer directly recoverable.

Therefore:

MATERIAL_DOCUMENT_SURVIVAL != EQUAL_SEMANTIC_ACCESS.

## 5. Colonial mediation

Both traditions also enter alphabetic colonial archives through different
routes.

This does not make alphabetic records superior.

It adds another documentary layer.

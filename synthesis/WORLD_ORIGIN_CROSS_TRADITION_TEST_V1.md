# World Origin Cross-Tradition Test V1

Status: FOUR-LANE BOUNDED COMPARISON / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

The neutral question is:

> What does the source claim, narrate, or refuse to claim about cosmic origin
> and the emergence of familiar world-order?

Four current source lanes already support this comparison:

- Rigvedic / Vedic;
- early Daoist;
- classical Shinto;
- Norse.

Buddhism and Sumerian remain `NOT_ADMITTED` at the current exact source heads.

The test does not ask which tradition has the "correct creation story" and does
not presume that every source supplies the same kind of answer.

## Method correction

`WORLD_ORIGIN_MODEL` cannot be treated as one flat mechanism field.

At minimum the comparison must separate:

- `ORIGIN_ASSERTION_MODE`;
- `PREEXISTING_OR_PRIOR_CONDITION`;
- `ORIGIN_PROCESS_MODEL`;
- `ORIGIN_AGENT_MODEL`;
- `ORIGIN_KNOWLEDGE_STATUS`;
- witness/textual layer.

Method schema:

`synthesis/WORLD_ORIGIN_COMPARISON_SCHEMA_V1.yaml`.

This matters especially because Rigveda 10.129 partly interrogates the
knowability of origin instead of simply presenting one settled sequence.

## 1. Rigvedic lane

Exact source branch:

`research/hindu-rigvedic-upanishadic-proposition-v1-20260920@614fa715fcacee0d7ec1ba573061d3e776e708d3`.

Primary locus:

`RV 10.129`.

Current status:

`BOUNDED_READY`.

### Source propositions

The selected hymn:
- begins with neither `asat` nor `sat` in its own formulation;
- denies ordinary death/immortality and night/day distinctions at the relevant
  primordial stage;
- describes darkness and an undifferentiated condition;
- associates emergence with `tapas`;
- introduces `kama` as an early seed associated with mind;
- asks who truly knows the origin;
- places the gods later than the relevant coming-forth;
- ends with the possibility that even the highest overseer may or may not know.

### Neutral classification

`ORIGIN_ASSERTION_MODE = INTERROGATIVE_EPISTEMIC_SUSPENSION`.

`ORIGIN_PROCESS_MODEL = PARTIAL_EMERGENCE_LANGUAGE_WITHOUT_SETTLED_COMPLETE_MECHANISM`.

`ORIGIN_AGENT_MODEL = NOT_SETTLED`.

`ORIGIN_KNOWLEDGE_STATUS = EXPLICITLY_UNCERTAIN`.

### Guard

The selected hymn does not establish:
- atheism;
- one creator-God doctrine;
- one universal Vedic cosmogony;
- later Vedanta ontology;
- a tradition-wide claim that origin is unknowable.

Strongest result:

`ORIGIN_INQUIRY_CAN_BE_SOURCE_INTERNAL_AND_UNRESOLVED`.

## 2. Daoist lane

Exact source branch:

`research/daoism-manuscript-proposition-v1-20260920@708ba10413d9511648c2540ad84be74b6b5f6b5c`.

Primary controlled loci:

- selected Zhuangzi Inner Chapter 6.

Laozi 42 is relevant but is not required for this test because its full

Current status:

`BOUNDED`.

### Source propositions

The controlled early/received lane supports:
- Dao-language prior to heaven and earth;
- Dao as provisionally named;
- a human -> earth -> heaven -> Dao -> ziran relation in Laozi 25;
- myriad things from `you`, and `you` from `wu`, in Laozi 40, with lexical
  controls on `wu`;
- selected Zhuangzi language placing Dao before heaven and earth and describing
  it as its own root/source.

Related Laozi material distinguishes generation/nurture from possession and
dominating rule.

### Neutral classification

`ORIGIN_ASSERTION_MODE = GENERATIVE_PRIORITY_DESCRIPTION`.

`PREEXISTING_OR_PRIOR_CONDITION = DAO_PRIORITY_TO_HEAVEN_AND_EARTH`.

`ORIGIN_PROCESS_MODEL = GENERATIVE_WITH_YOU_WU_AND_TRANSFORMATION_LANGUAGE`.

`ORIGIN_AGENT_MODEL = NON_SOVEREIGN_GENERATIVE_SOURCE_AT_CURRENT_CEILING`.

`ORIGIN_KNOWLEDGE_STATUS = ASSERTIVE_BUT_SEMANTICALLY_AND_TEXTUALLY_CONTROLLED`.

### Guard

The controlled passages do not establish:
- a personal creator;
- creation ex nihilo;
- Dao as an owning sovereign;
- `wu` as absolute metaphysical nothingness by default;
- Dao as a computational substrate or simulator.

Strongest result:

`GENERATIVE_PRIORITY_NE_SOVEREIGN_CREATOR`.

## Cross-lane result

The four lanes do not converge on one origin mechanism.

They instead expose four materially different source architectures:

1. `INTERROGATIVE_EPISTEMIC_SUSPENSION`;
2. `NON_SOVEREIGN_GENERATIVE_PRIORITY`;
3. `VARIANT_DIVINE_GENEALOGICAL_COSMOGRAPHY`;
4. `DIVINE_WORLD_FORMATION_FROM_PRIOR_CONDITION`.

Therefore:

`SHARED_WORLD_ORIGIN_QUESTION != SHARED_WORLD_ORIGIN_ANSWER`.

And:

`SHARED_ORIGIN_VOCABULARY != SHARED_CAUSAL_MECHANISM`.

## Single-creator / ex-nihilo control

At these exact selected loci:

`ONE_SOVEREIGN_CREATOR_EX_NIHILO_MODEL = NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

This statement is deliberately narrow.

It does not mean:
- the traditions as wholes deny such a model;
- later strata cannot develop creator concepts;
- absence in these loci proves philosophical impossibility.

It means the current four comparison cells do not support using
"single sovereign creation from nothing" as their neutral common denominator.

## Prior-condition control

Three lanes directly engage a condition prior to familiar world-order:

- Rigveda 10.129: difficult primordial state-language plus epistemic uncertainty;
- Daoist loci: Dao prior to heaven and earth;
- Voluspa: Ymir/Ginnungagap before earth and upper heaven.

These are not therefore one shared primordial substrate.

Shinto is intentionally excluded from this sub-comparison until an exact
primordial-state packet is bound.

Therefore:

`PRE_FAMILIAR_WORLD_CONDITION_SIMILARITY != ONE_SUBSTRATE`.

## Comparative-problem-space consequence

This test strengthens the project's existing conclusion:

`COMPARATIVE_PROBLEM_SPACE`

is a better synthesis target than:

`ONE_HIDDEN_DOCTRINE`.

The traditions can ask recognizably comparable questions about origin while
preserving radically different:
- assertion modes;
- agent structures;
- process models;
- epistemic confidence;
- witness histories.

The recurring question is real.

A recurring answer is not established.

## Systems-model boundary

Downstream systems analogies could map these to:
- unknown initialization;
- generative substrate;
- branching configuration;
- state formation from prior conditions.

Those are `SPECULATIVE MODEL` or `PROJECT ANALOGY`.

They are not evidence that any source describes computation, simulation, or a
hidden controller.

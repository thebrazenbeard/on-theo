## Purpose

The current synthesis state names `POLITICAL_SACRALIZATION_COMPARISON` as the
next primary frontier and reports four admitted lanes.

This audit checks that count against the latest exact tradition heads before a
cross-tradition comparison is written.

Neutral axis:

This is not a search for one universal doctrine of sacred kingship.

## Method result

The axis remains valuable precisely because the candidate mechanisms are not
the same:

- divine/cosmic grant of kingship;
- ritual consolidation of sovereignty;
- religious-political institutional entanglement;
- genealogical-celestial legitimation;
- court administration of cult.

These must not be collapsed into "sacred kingship."

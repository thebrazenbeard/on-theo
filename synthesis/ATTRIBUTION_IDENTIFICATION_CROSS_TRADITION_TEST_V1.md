## Purpose

Test whether one general source-critical schema can distinguish:
- genuine positive religious self-identification;
- hostile attribution;
- demonological theory;
- colonial semantic translation;
- legal/conspiracy allegation;
- independently corroborated behavior.

## Case 1 — Modern Church of Satan

Label:
SATANIST.

Attribution status:
SELF_IDENTIFIED / INTERNAL_ORGANIZATIONAL_LABEL.

Target voice:
DIRECT_CURRENT_SELF_DESCRIPTION.

Self-identification:
EXPLICIT_POSITIVE.

Satan referent:
SYMBOLIC / NON-THEISTIC in current organizational sources.

Result:
The label can legitimately populate a current identity cell.

But it cannot automatically populate:
- literal Devil belief;
- sacrificial harm;
- every other Satanist group's theology.

## Case 7 — Satanic Ritual Abuse network

Label:

Attribution status:

## Method result 1 — Attribution can be historically real without target identity

An accusation can have real consequences:
- prosecution;
- execution;
- suppression;
- confiscation;
- conversion pressure;
- moral panic;
- institutional reform.

Therefore:

FALSE_OR_UNVERIFIED_TARGET_LABEL
does not mean
HISTORICALLY_UNIMPORTANT_LABEL.

## Method result 2 — Emic voice is necessary but not omnipotent

Self-identification is strongest for:
- present identity;
- internal meaning;
- organizational doctrine.

It does not automatically establish:
- antiquity;
- historical continuity;
- supernatural truth;
- external behavioral claims.

Therefore:

EMIC_PRIORITY_FOR_IDENTITY
!=
EMIC_INFALLIBILITY_FOR_HISTORY.

## Method result 3 — Hostile sources may contain divisible evidence

A hostile source can simultaneously preserve:
- observable action;
- place;
- object;
- office;
- wording;

and impose:
- moral judgment;
- demonological causation;
- hostile identity.

Therefore source criticism should split propositions rather than either:
- accept the whole source;
or
- discard the whole source.

## Method result 4 — Reappropriation creates diachronic inversion

Modern Satanists can positively appropriate a figure/category earlier used as a
hostile label.

Therefore:

EARLIER_ATTRIBUTION
->
LATER_REAPPROPRIATION

is a valid historical mechanism.

But:

LATER_REAPPROPRIATION != EARLIER_ATTRIBUTION_ACCURATE.

GLOBAL_CORPUS_INTENT != GLOBAL_CORPUS_COMPLETION.

## Method rule for Indigenous traditions

"Native American religion" is not an admissible tradition-level analytical
unit except as a modern umbrella label for portfolio navigation.

Research must be nation/community and period specific.

Examples:
- Lakota traditions != Dine traditions;
- Dine traditions != Haudenosaunee traditions;
- Classic Maya religion != colonial K'iche' textual tradition != living Maya
  community religion;
- Mexica imperial religion != all Nahua religion;
- Olmec material culture != recoverable Olmec theology.

## Sacred-knowledge boundary

For living Indigenous traditions:
- prefer community-authored/community-grounded public sources where available;
- distinguish public teaching from restricted ceremonial knowledge;
- do not reconstruct or publish material explicitly treated as closed,
  initiatory, restricted, or inappropriate for public circulation;
- preserve colonial/missionary ethnography as a historically situated evidence
  class rather than treating it as transparent Indigenous self-description;
- preserve living-tradition correction of older ethnography where available.

## Mesoamerican source controls

### Maya

Required separation:
- Preclassic / Classic inscriptional and archaeological evidence;
- Postclassic codices and archaeology;
- colonial-period Maya-language texts;
- K'iche' Popol Wuj / Popol Vuh textual history;
- living Maya traditions.

Guard:
MAYA != ONE_TIMELESS_RELIGION.

### Mexica / Aztec / Nahua

Required separation:
- Mexica imperial ritual/political system;
- broader Nahua language/cultural traditions;
- archaeological and pictorial evidence;
- colonial Nahuatl documentation;
- Spanish missionary descriptions;
- living Nahua communities.

Guard:
FLORENTINE_CODEX != UNMEDIATED_PRECONTACT_TRANSCRIPT.

### Olmec

Current likely evidence ceiling:
- MATERIAL_EVIDENCE;
- ICONOGRAPHIC_ANALYSIS;
- ARCHAEOLOGICAL_CONTEXT;
- SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION.

No surviving Olmec theological text corpus is known to the project.

Therefore:
OLMEC_ICONOGRAPHY != RECOVERED_SYSTEMATIC_THEOLOGY.

Olmec should be treated as a high-value but low-textual-certainty lane.

5. Haudenosaunee public-source packet
   - nation/confederacy-specific historical and living-source controls.

6. Andean / Inca packet
   - imperial Inca, Quechua/Aymara traditions, colonial chroniclers,

7. Olmec material/iconographic packet
   - deliberately low claim ceiling.

8. Further nation-specific North American, Mesoamerican, South American,
   Arctic/Subarctic, and Caribbean lanes as source quality permits.

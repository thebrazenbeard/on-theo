# Contact and Reinterpretation Cross-Tradition Test V1

Status: FOUR-LANE BOUNDED COMPARISON / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Four current source lanes now contain strong enough evidence to compare what
happens when religious systems encounter one another:

- Daoism / Buddhism;
- Shinto / Buddhism;
- Norse religion / Christianity;
- Sumerian-to-Ugaritic transmission testing around Dumuzid/Baal.

The question is not whether contact occurred.

The question is:

> What does contact actually establish, and what transformations follow from it?

## Daoism / Buddhism

The Daoist lane contains several distinct contact mechanisms:

- direct textual adaptation in Shangqing-era material;
- Buddhist-derived forms and salvation structures recoded within Lingbao;
- ritual/institutional adaptation;
- polemical counter-genealogy in huahu discourse;
- Buddhist counter-polemics;
- material/epigraphic hybridization in the Yao Boduo stele.

Neutral summary:

`CONTACT_WITH_ADAPTATION_RECODING_AND_POLEMIC`.

This is the strongest current lane for showing that borrowing can be real while
meaning changes materially in the receiving system.

## Shinto / Buddhism

The Shinto lane shows a different contact pattern:

- progressive shrine-temple combination;
- Buddhist ritual and iconographic influence;
- honji-suijaku identification structures;
- long institutional coexistence;
- later Meiji separation/reclassification.

Neutral summary:

`CONTACT_WITH_LONG_TERM_INSTITUTIONAL_COMBINATION_AND_LATER_SEPARATION`.

The later separation does not recover a provably untouched primordial Shinto.

## Norse religion / Christianity

The Norse lane shows:

- prolonged conversion-period overlap;
- mixed material symbols;
- political conversion;
- delayed ritual change;
- later Christian preservation and systematization of pre-Christian material.

Neutral summary:

`CONTACT_WITH_CONVERSION_COEXISTENCE_AND_PRESERVER_REFRAMING`.

Christian preservation is neither transparent transmission nor automatic
fabrication.

## Sumerian / Ugaritic transmission control

The Dumuzid/Baal lane supplies a crucial negative control.

The evidence supports:
- chronology compatible with east-to-west influence;
- regional contact;
- Mesopotamian scribal presence at Ugarit;
- Dumuzi-specific scholastic exposure;
- plausible carrier-network capacity.

But the controlled evidence does not establish:
- a source-specific Dumuzid narrative reaching the Baal Cycle;
- a distinctive low-base-rate narrative sequence;
- lexical calques;
- a source note;
- a relevant source carrier.

Current result:

`DIRECT_DUMUZID_TO_BAAL_BORROWING = NONE_ESTABLISHED`.

Neutral summary:

`CONTACT_WITHOUT_ESTABLISHED_SOURCE_SPECIFIC_BORROWING`.

## Cross-lane result

The four lanes demonstrate that:

`CONTACT`
can lead to very different outcomes:

- textual adaptation;
- semantic recoding;
- institutional combination;
- polemical origin rewriting;
- political conversion;
- preservation under a new worldview;
- later separation;
- or no demonstrable source-specific borrowing at all.

Therefore:

`CONTACT != TRANSMISSION`.

And even when transmission is established:

`TRANSMISSION != SEMANTIC_IDENTITY`.

## Method consequence

A transmission claim should require, where applicable:

1. exact source unit;
2. chronology;
3. contact opportunity;
4. distinctive correspondence;
5. plausible direction;
6. source/carrier evidence;
7. semantic transformation analysis;
8. competing convergence/circulation models;
9. explicit disconfirmers.

A contact claim can pass while a borrowing claim fails.

The Sumerian/Ugaritic lane demonstrates this directly.

## Systems-model consequence

Downstream analogies such as:
- protocol translation;
- schema migration;
- fork;
- compatibility layer;
- adversarial reclassification

may be useful.

They are not historical evidence.

The controlled sequence remains:

`SOURCE_A -> CONTACT_MECHANISM -> SOURCE_B_CHANGE -> OPTIONAL_SYSTEMS_ANALOGY`.

## Current conclusion

The contact axis strongly supports ON_THEO's larger method:

Similarity is too weak.
Contact is stronger.
Transmission is a separate claim.
Semantic persistence is a further claim still.

Those stages must be represented independently.

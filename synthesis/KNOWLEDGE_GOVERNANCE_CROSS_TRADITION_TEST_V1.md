## Neutral question

WHO_MAY_HOLD_LEARN_TRANSMIT_RECORD_INTERPRET_ACCESS_OR_DISCLOSE_RELIGIOUS_KNOWLEDGE_AND_UNDER_WHAT_CONDITIONS?

The question concerns governance of knowledge.

It does not ask:
HOW_IS_SUPERNATURAL_TRUTH_OBTAINED?

Those are separate dimensions.

## 1. Diné — explicit access/disclosure governance

Current public Navajo Nation / Diné-controlled sources distinguish:
- public knowledge;
- restricted knowledge;
- conditional access;
- information whose existence can be public while content is protected;
- information that should not be collected/disseminated;
- authorized apprenticeship and institutional transmission.

Subtype:

EXPLICIT_ACCESS_AND_DISCLOSURE_GOVERNANCE.

Strongest method result:

RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE != MISSING_DATA.

Comparison does not require disclosure.

## 2. Lakota — community-governed public boundary

Current public-source reconstruction supports:
- high-level public ceremonial/vision categories;
- oral/intergenerational transmission;
- community authority over disclosure;
- explicit project refusal to reproduce restricted sacred detail merely because
  outsider sources recorded it.

Subtype:

COMMUNITY_GOVERNED_DISCLOSURE_BOUNDARY.

The current packet does not reconstruct a single Lakota-wide access code.

## 3. Haudenosaunee — office/material/oral stewardship

Current public evidence supports stabilization through:
- wampum;
- office/title;
- oral interpretation;
- recurrent address;
- Great Law/treaty memory.

Subtype:

OFFICE_AND_MATERIAL_MEMORY_STEWARDSHIP.

Current limitation:

restricted versus public content.

Therefore this cell is:
PARTIAL for access restriction,
BOUNDED for stewardship.

INSTITUTIONAL_SAFEGUARDING
- Ifá and Diné in different forms.

## 6. Expertise versus secrecy

Knowledge can require specialist training without being "secret."

Knowledge can be public in outline while restricted in detail.

Knowledge can be institutionally stewarded without one specialist monopoly.

Therefore:

SPECIALIST != SECRET_BY_DEFINITION.

And:

PUBLIC_EXISTENCE != PUBLIC_CONTENT.

## 7. Comparison ethics is evidence-sensitive method

A research corpus can become more rigorous by preserving an access boundary.

The method therefore rejects:

MAXIMUM_DISCLOSURE = MAXIMUM_EVIDENCE.

Instead:

VALID_PUBLIC_BOUNDARY
can itself be
DOCUMENTARY_EVIDENCE_ABOUT_KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE.

## 8. Modern institutions and historical continuity

Current:
- law;
- educational institutions;
- safeguarding programs;
- tribal programs;
- organizational offices

may be modern institutional forms.

They can still provide valid evidence for:
CURRENT_KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE.

They cannot automatically reconstruct:
PRECOLONIAL_ACCESS_RULES.

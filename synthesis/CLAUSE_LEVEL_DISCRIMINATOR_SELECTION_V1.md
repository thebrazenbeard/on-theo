# Clause-Level Discriminator Selection V1

Status: NON-CANONICAL / SOURCE-FIRST NARROWING GATE

## Question

Which current source clauses provide the cleanest historical A-vs-B distinction
without the analyst inventing the “wrong” condition after the fact?

## Result

Four clause families now clear the strictest historical-brittleness gate.

### 1. Hekhalot Rabbati §§204–205

Source-defined:
- exact count;
- explicit no-add/no-subtract rule;
- distinct source-claimed consequence.

Strength:
very high endogenous specificity.

Weakness:
literal reproduction is not admitted because the source itself frames
deviation as dangerous.

Use:
historical brittleness evidence and safe structural-abstraction logic only.

### 2. Kulārṇavatantra 15.55

Source-defined:
- delivery/form defect;
- source-claimed non-success.

Strength:
clean formal variable.

Weakness:
phonological form can overlap semantic/linguistic form, and “success” remains
generic.

Use:
best current source-defined form/error model.

### 3. Kulārṇavatantra 15.57–58

Source-defined:
- named formal impurity present;
- impurity removed/avoided;
- failure versus claimed efficacy.

Strength:
explicit correction pair.

Weakness:
historical siddhi remains too broad as a laboratory endpoint.

### 4. Baopuzi Inner Chapters 19

Source-defined:
- accurate talisman;
- corrupted/wrongly copied talisman;
- benefit/effect versus failure/adverse source claim.

Strength:
non-acoustic written-form contrast.

Weakness:
practitioner sincerity is also source-relevant, so graphic form is not isolated.

## Best failure/repair logic

`Sefer ha-Razim` solar procedure:

- initial invocation sequence;
- explicit “no answer” state;
- reverse-order fallback sequence.

This is not ordinary A-success/B-failure.

It is a source-defined repair branch.

That is valuable because it shows historical rituals can contain recovery logic
rather than only brittle failure.

## Best source-defined operation with missing endogenous control

`Netra Tantra 6.18–19`.

The text specifies framing/enclosure of a name/mantra and assigns a
source-claimed result.

But the current clause does not explicitly state:

`unframed -> failure`.

Any unframed B is modern.

This keeps it out of Tier 1.

## Most important negative result

`Netra Tantra chapter 21` was initially targeted because it contains rich
mantra theory.

Clause extraction shows that philosophical richness is not the same thing as a
clean discriminator.

Therefore chapter 21 is retained as ontology/context, while chapters 6 and 18
carry the more operational material.

This is exactly why clause-level extraction matters.

## Ranking by historical brittleness versus experiment quality

Historical brittleness and experiment quality are different axes.

Highest historical specificity:
- Hekhalot §§204–205.

Best potentially safe formal abstraction:
- Kulārṇavatantra 15.55 / 15.57–58.

Strongest written-form analogue:
- Baopuzi 19.

Best endogenous failure/repair:
- Sefer ha-Razim solar procedure.

Best formal-operation grammar:
- Netra 6.18–19 / 18.9–12.

No candidate currently has all of:
- exact source-defined A and B;
- safe literal reproduction;
- single isolated parameter;
- narrow externally measurable historical outcome;
- empirical evidence of efficacy.

That missing conjunction is the frontier.

## What the next experimental-selection gate should require

A candidate moves from historical discriminator to modern empirical candidate
only if:

1. the exact source locus is frozen;
2. A and B are source-defined OR B is explicitly labeled modern;
3. the historical claimed outcome is narrow enough to operationalize;
4. the procedure can be translated safely without changing the relevant
   parameter;
5. the ordinary mechanism family can be matched;
6. one primary prospective outcome is chosen;
7. the kill condition is frozen before data;
8. no additional ritual parameter is added after a null result.

## Current conclusion

Clause-level work strengthens the historical claim:

`SOME_RELIGIOUS_TEXTS_EXPLICITLY_DEFINE_PARAMETER_ERROR_AND_ALTERNATE_OUTCOME`.

It weakens any attempt to jump directly from that fact to a modern universal
ritual.

The correct next frontier is:

`SOURCE_DEFINED_OUTCOME_NARROWING`.

Find the clauses where the historical claimed result is specific enough to
score prospectively, not merely “siddhi,” “power,” “protection,” or
“revelation.”

# Human Ontology — Project Inference V1

Status: PROJECT INFERENCE / SPECULATIVE MODEL / NOT HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

## Purpose

State the project's current best higher-order inference about humanity after
broad cross-tradition comparison without promoting convergence, analogy, or
philosophical preference into historical or empirical fact.

This file is intentionally downstream of evidence.

It may be revised freely as the corpus grows.

## 1. What the evidence actually establishes

Across the current corpus, human traditions recurrently organize around a
recognizable problem-space involving:
- self/personhood;
- agency and constraint;
- suffering and disorder;
- moral action;
- social relation;
- relation to nonhuman beings/place;
- death and continuing relation to the dead;
- transformation/liberation;
- knowledge and uncertainty;
- cosmic order/origin.

This does NOT establish:
ONE_HIDDEN_DOCTRINE.

It establishes:
RECURRING_HUMAN_PROBLEM_SPACE.

## 2. My current best inference about the human self

The strongest cross-disciplinary default is:

HUMAN_PERSON =
EMBODIED + COGNITIVE + RELATIONAL + HISTORICAL + SELF-MODELING.

The isolated, self-contained individual is probably an incomplete ontology.

Human identity is materially dependent on:
- body/brain;
- memory;
- language;
- relationships;
- social recognition;
- environment;
- inherited symbolic systems.

This does not prove:
NO_NONMATERIAL_ASPECT_EXISTS.

It means any soul/self theory that ignores embodiment and relation is
empirically underdescribed.

## 3. Consciousness

The evidence strongly supports:
CONSCIOUS_EXPERIENCE_DEPENDS_ON_BRAIN_STATE.

It does not currently establish:
CONSCIOUSNESS_IS_EXHAUSTIVELY_REDUCIBLE_TO_BRAIN_DESCRIPTION.

The hard ontological question remains open:
why physical/informational processes are accompanied by first-person
experience at all.

Current project inference:

REDUCTIVE_MATERIALISM =
PLAUSIBLE_BUT_NOT_CLOSED.

SUBSTANCE_DUALISM =
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

CONSCIOUSNESS_ONTOLOGY =
OPEN.

## 4. Agency

Religious systems repeatedly model human beings as:
NEITHER_FULLY_FREE_NOR_FULLY_DETERMINED.

Constraints are described variously as:
- karma;
- fate;
- sin;
- desire;
- ignorance;
- social obligation;
- ritual disorder;
- ancestral relation;
- cosmic structure.

Modern naturalistic language adds:
- genetics;
- neurobiology;
- development;
- trauma;
- economics;
- social environment.

My current inference:

HUMAN_AGENCY_IS_BOUNDED_BUT_REAL_AT_THE_LEVEL_OF_PERSONAL_AND_SOCIAL_CAUSATION.

This remains compatible with multiple deeper metaphysical accounts.

## 5. Relation may be more fundamental than isolated substance

Many traditions and modern human sciences independently place heavy explanatory
weight on relations.

Persons are formed through:
- kinship;
- recognition;
- language;
- reciprocal obligation;
- institutions;
- place;
- ecological dependence.

Current inference:

RELATIONAL_PERSONHOOD
is probably closer to the lived structure of humanity than
ATOMIZED_SELFHOOD.

This does not require accepting any specific religious ontology.

## 6. Human cosmic centrality

The comparative corpus weakens the assumption that religion universally places
humanity at the center of reality.

Current cases include:
- worlds with multiple classes of conscious beings;
- gods who are themselves within cosmic order;
- humans embedded in ecological/ancestral relations;
- beginningless cosmologies;
- origin accounts where humans are not the reason the cosmos exists.

Current inference:

HUMAN_COSMIC_CENTRALITY =
NOT_A_SAFE_DEFAULT.

Humans may be unusual epistemically and culturally without being the purpose of
the cosmos.

## 7. Theology

A creator deity is not a universal requirement for:
- religion;
- meaning;
- ethics;
- ritual;
- liberation;
- cosmological structure.

Therefore:

GOD_AS_CREATOR =
ONE_THEOLOGICAL_MODEL,
not
THE_NEUTRAL_DEFINITION_OF_RELIGION_OR_ULTIMATE_REALITY.

My current speculative preference, if forced to choose among broad metaphysical
models, is that any real transcendent/ultimate ground would more likely be:
- less anthropomorphic;
- less culturally parochial;
- less institutionally proprietary;
- more fundamental than a humanlike cosmic ruler.

Possible model families include:
- ground-of-being;
- process;
- relational;
- panentheistic;
- nondual;
- impersonal generative order.

This is SPECULATIVE MODEL, not evidence.

## 8. Religious experience

Religious experiences are unquestionably real as experiences.

Their causes can involve:
- neurobiology;
- expectation;
- ritual;
- attention;
- social entrainment;
- altered states;
- grief;
- stress;
- contemplative practice;
- pharmacology;
- possibly factors not yet understood.

Current inference:

EXPERIENCE_REAL
does not imply
INTERPRETATION_METAPHYSICALLY_TRUE.

But neither does:
NEURAL_CORRELATE
imply
EXPERIENCE_METAPHYSICALLY_FALSE.

The ontological interpretation remains underdetermined.

## 9. Death and postmortem existence

The corpus strongly establishes:

DEAD_PERSONS_CAN_REMAIN_SOCIAL_AND_RITUAL_REFERENTS.

It does not currently establish:

CONSCIOUSNESS_SURVIVES_BIOLOGICAL_DEATH.

Current best evidence posture:

POSTMORTEM_CONSCIOUS_SURVIVAL =
OPEN_BUT_NOT_DEMONSTRATED.

I would not use the frequency of afterlife traditions as proof of survival.

I would also not treat cultural universality of death-concern as proof that no
survival is possible.

## 10. Why religions converge at all

The strongest parsimonious explanation for recurrent themes is currently a
combination of:
- shared human neurobiology;
- shared mortality;
- agency under constraint;
- social dependence;
- environmental dependence;
- grief;
- dream/vision phenomena;
- altered states;
- power/institutional dynamics;
- transmission/contact in some cases;
- independent cultural construction in others.

This explains substantial convergence without requiring:
one primordial revelation
or
one hidden metaphysical system.

But it does not logically disprove either possibility.

## 11. The remaining anomaly

The hardest unresolved issue is not:
which religion got the mythology exactly right?

It is:

WHY_IS_THERE_SUBJECTIVE_EXPERIENCE_AT_ALL,
AND
WHY_IS_REALITY_INTELLIGIBLE_ENOUGH_FOR_EMBODIED_BEINGS_INSIDE_IT_TO_MODEL_IT?

That problem survives:
- theism;
- atheism;
- materialism;
- idealism;
- simulation models;
- nondualism.

Any final ontology must explain consciousness, intelligibility, embodiment and
relation without merely renaming them.

## 12. Current best overall position

If forced into a compact label:

CRITICAL_METAPHYSICAL_AGNOSTICISM_WITH_RELATIONAL_AND_ANTI_REDUCTIONIST_LEAN.

Meaning:
- empirical naturalism is the strongest method for public factual claims;
- human minds are deeply embodied;
- the isolated-self model is incomplete;
- consciousness is not yet ontologically explained;
- creator-theism is not required by comparative religion;
- reductive materialism is not yet demonstrated as a complete ontology;
- postmortem survival is not established;
- any deeper reality, if real, is likely less anthropomorphic and less
  tradition-specific than most institutional theologies claim.

## 13. What would change this view

Evidence that should materially update the model would include:
- independently replicable evidence of consciousness operating without
  sufficient biological substrate;
- reliable anomalous-information transfer under controlled conditions;
- strong evidence of postmortem personal continuity;
- a physical theory that genuinely derives first-person consciousness rather
  than correlating with it;
- independently convergent source-specific revelation containing high-information
  content inaccessible through ordinary transmission;
- experimentally reproducible ritual effects with mechanism-sensitive
  discriminators.

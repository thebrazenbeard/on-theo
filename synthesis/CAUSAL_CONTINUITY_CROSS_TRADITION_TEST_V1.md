# Causal Order and Continuity Cross-Tradition Test V1

Status: BOUNDED COMPARISON / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

This is the first ON_THEO comparison built only after all six current
non-Abrahamic lanes acquired exact source-bound entry points.

It does **not** ask:

> Which religions secretly teach the same thing?

It asks:

> When six source traditions are placed on the same neutral questions, do their
> answers actually converge at mechanism level?

Source heads are locked in:
- `synthesis/BOUNDED_NEUTRAL_COMPARISON_MATRIX_V1.yaml`.

The two selected axes are:
- `CAUSAL_ORDER_TYPE`;
- `CONTINUITY_MODEL`.

They were selected because all six lanes currently contain at least bounded or
partial source support.

## 1. Causal order

### Buddhism

Current bounded source model:

`CONDITION -> CONDITIONED_RESULT`.

SN 12.2 / SN 12.15 and parallel controls support dependent/conditioned
explanation without requiring a single sovereign causal agent.

This does not establish one complete Buddhist metaphysics.

It does establish that:

`ROOT_CONTROLLER`

cannot be required as the neutral comparison grammar.

### Hindu / early Upaniṣadic packet

The current Hindu lane does not yield one causal architecture.

Controlled structures include:
- desire -> resolve -> action -> outcome/becoming;
- conduct -> differentiated rebirth;
- self/essence language in Chāndogya with interpretation controls;
- source-level uncertainty in RV 10.129 about ultimate origin.

The correct cell is therefore `PARTIAL`, not a synthetic "Brahman causes all."

### Daoism

Selected Laozi material supplies a markedly different causal picture:
- Dao prior to heaven and earth;
- ziran/self-so;
- wuwei;
- self-transformation;
- generation/nurture without possession or domination.

Later Celestial Masters material then adds:
- revelation;
- divine offices;
- petitions;
- ritual mediation.

So even within one tradition family:

`CAUSAL_ORDER_TYPE`

changes by historical layer.

This is strong evidence against treating a tradition as one static ontology.

### Shinto

The current source lane preserves:
- variant mythic genealogies;
- multiple Nihon shoki alternatives;
- ritual-order evidence independent of mythology;
- kami presence tied to places, objects, rites, and institutions.

No single causal system has been admitted.

The result is not failure.

The plurality is the source result.

### Norse

Völuspá supplies:
- divine actors;
- formation/gifting;
- explicit fate assignment by Norns;
- agents acting within foretold or assigned constraints.

The current model is therefore neither:
- pure sovereign command;
- pure impersonal law;
- pure unconstrained agency.

It is a layered agent-plus-fate architecture at the selected loci.

### Sumerian

The deep chain supplies:
- divine authority;
- city/deity patronage;
- temple administration;
- decreed/granted kingship;
- differentiated divine/mythic action.

But city, period, composition, and pantheon variation block a universal
"Sumerian causal system."

The cell remains partial.

## Causal-order result

At the level of abstraction:

`ALL_SIX_HAVE_SOME_ACCOUNT_OF_ORDER_OR_CAUSATION`

is trivially true and analytically weak.

At mechanism level the current source models include:
- conditioned relational causation;
- action/desire/becoming chains;
- noncoercive generative order;
- variant genealogical plus ritual order;
- divine agency under fate constraints;
- divine/civic/royal order with local and chronological plurality.

These are not semantic equivalents.

### Immediate consequence for the GOD model

A controller architecture may be compared downstream with any of these.

It cannot be treated as the neutral common denominator.

The source evidence currently favors a **model family**, not one architecture:

- sovereign/controller;
- conditioned relational;
- generative/non-dominating;
- fate-constrained agentic;
- ritual/relational;
- layered/mixed.

That is a PROJECT_METHOD conclusion, not a metaphysical conclusion about reality.

## 2. Continuity through death or transformation

### Buddhism

SN 12.38 / SĀ 359 support conditioned future renewed existence.

The current source packet does not assert a permanent entity that simply
travels unchanged.

Neutral summary:

`CAUSAL_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ASSERTED_PERMANENT_SELF`.

### Hindu / early Upaniṣadic packet

Bṛhadāraṇyaka 4.4.2–6 and Chāndogya 5.10 provide:
- death-transition;
- continuity into new form;
- self-language;
- action/desire conditioning;
- multiple postmortem routes;
- return/rebirth.

Neutral summary:

`CONTINUITY_WITH_SELF_LANGUAGE_AND_DIFFERENTIATED_REBIRTH_PATHS`.

This is not reduced to one later Vedānta theory.

### Daoism

Selected Zhuangzi material is more securely about:
- life/death transformation;
- form change;
- perspectival instability;

than about one entity migrating intact.

Neutral summary:

`TRANSFORMATION_FIRST_CONTINUITY_PARTIAL`.

This is exactly why `TRANSFORMATION_MODEL` had to be separated from
`CONTINUITY_MODEL`.

### Shinto

The Yomi cycle provides:
- death realm;
- separation;
- pollution;
- continuing identity of mythic actors.

It does not yet provide a bounded general human continuity doctrine.

Neutral summary:

`MYTHIC_DEATH_CONTINUITY_PARTIAL / GENERAL_HUMAN_MODEL_NOT_ADMITTED`.

### Norse

The current Norse packet is stronger for:
- world renewal;
- survival/return of selected divine figures;
- fate and transformation;

than for one universal human postmortem continuity model.

Neutral summary:

`WORLD_AND_DIVINE_CONTINUITY_PARTIAL / HUMAN_MODEL_UNDERDEVELOPED`.

### Sumerian

The human funerary lane supports:
- deceased-person `gidim` identity;
- differentiated conditions among the dead;
- descendant obligations;
- offerings;
- memory;
- exceptional royal office/status.

Neutral summary:

`SOCIAL_AND_GHOST_CONTINUITY_WITH_DIFFERENTIATED_MORTUARY_STATUS`.

## Continuity result

The coarse statement:

> All these traditions believe something continues after death.

would be an overcompression.

The current evidence instead distinguishes at least:

1. causal renewed existence without permanent-self assertion;
2. self-linked rebirth and differentiated postmortem routes;
3. transformation where persistence is not the primary framing;
4. mythic death-realm continuity without a general human doctrine;
5. world/divine renewal with human continuity underdeveloped;
6. socially maintained ghost/dead identity with offerings and descendants.

Those are not interchangeable mechanisms.

## 3. Re-instantiation stress test

The existing GOD model sometimes uses computational language such as
`re-instantiation`.

This comparison establishes a strict limit:

`RE_INSTANTIATION`
may be a useful downstream analogy for selected source structures.

It is not neutral enough to stand for all six continuity models.

It fits some structures better than others and risks distorting:
- Buddhist non-self controls;
- Daoist transformation;
- Sumerian living-dead social relation;
- Shinto pollution/Yomi structure;
- Norse world renewal.

Therefore any future systems mapping must be per-cell:

`SOURCE_PROPOSITION -> NEUTRAL_AXIS -> CANDIDATE_SYSTEMS_MAPPING`

not:

`TRADITION -> RE_INSTANTIATION`.

## 4. First comparison conclusion

The first properly gated six-lane comparison does **not** reveal one clean common
metaphysical architecture.

It reveals something more useful:

- recurrent questions;
- very different mechanism classes;
- real internal diachronic change;
- evidence modes that cannot be collapsed;
- some genuinely comparable structures;
- substantial non-equivalence.

This means ON_THEO's strongest synthesis target should currently be:

`COMPARATIVE_PROBLEM_SPACE`

rather than:

`ONE_HIDDEN_DOCTRINE`.

The traditions repeatedly confront questions such as:
- what orders reality?
- what constitutes a person?
- what survives change/death?
- what role do actions play?
- how is sacred order mediated?

But the answers are not yet reducible to one source-supported solution.

## 5. What would count as stronger convergence

Future convergence claims should require more than a shared question.

For any two lanes, require:
1. same axis;
2. proposition-level semantic fit;
3. mechanism-level similarity;
4. compatible chronology;
5. independent evidence mode control;
6. transmission test where contact is plausible;
7. ordinary convergence alternative;
8. explicit disconfirmers.

Until those are met:

`SHARED_QUESTION`
!=
`SHARED_ANSWER`
!=
`SHARED_SOURCE`
!=
`SHARED_METAPHYSICAL_REALITY`.
